bauhaus

WEIMAR · DESSAU · BERLIN · 1919–1933
01

Architects, sculptors, painters — we must all return to the crafts. There is no essential difference between the artist and the craftsman.

02

Form follows function. Ornament is not a crime, but it must earn its place on the wall like any load-bearing beam.

03

Together let us desire, conceive and create the new structure of the future, which will embrace everything in one single form.

“The ultimate aim of all creative activity is the building.”— WALTER GROPIUS, BAUHAUS MANIFESTO, APRIL 1919
