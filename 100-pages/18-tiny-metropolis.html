How to play

  • Pick a tool below, then click or drag on the map.
  • Buildings only work when they touch a road. Unconnected ones show a ⚠.
  • Houses bring people. Shops & factories bring jobs.
  • Factories pollute — keep them away from homes, or plant parks.
  • Happiness rises with jobs, parks and shops; unhappy cities shrink.
  • Drag with the right mouse button (or two fingers) to pan. Scroll to zoom.
