How it works

Imagine a wind that blows in a slightly different direction at every point on the canvas. That's a vector field. Here the angle at each point comes from noise(x·scale, y·scale, t) — a smooth random function, so neighbouring points agree with each other.

Thousands of particles are dropped into the field. Each frame, every particle looks up the angle where it stands, takes a small step in that direction, and draws a line from where it was to where it is. Instead of clearing the screen, we paint a nearly-transparent veil over it — that's the trail fade.

Move your mouse over the diagram below to see the field's arrows and a single particle's path.

Curl multiplies the angle so paths twist harder. Evolve slowly moves the noise through time, so the wind changes. Attract mode bends every path toward your cursor; mirror reflects the field into four-fold symmetry.
