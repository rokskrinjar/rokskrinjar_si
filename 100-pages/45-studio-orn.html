Studio Örn is an architecture practice in Reykjavík and Lisbon. We design quiet buildings for loud landscapes — houses, libraries and the occasional lighthouse.

We are eleven people who believe a building should be a little quieter than its site. We draw by hand first, argue about daylight, and visit every site in the worst weather we can find.

01 — Weather first

We design for the worst day of the year. The other 364 take care of themselves.

02 — Local, literally

Stone from the hillside, timber from the valley, builders from the village.

03 — Room to wander

Every building gets one space with no stated purpose. People always find one.

Let's make something quiet.
