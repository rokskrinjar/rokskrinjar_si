What grows in the dark

Twelve common species from European woods and meadows. Some are delicious and some can kill you. The deadly ones often look the most harmless.

Who are you?

A dichotomous key. Answer one question about the mushroom in front of you and follow the branches to a likely name.

Take a spore print

Put the cap gills-down on paper, cover it with a glass and wait overnight. Millions of spores fall and leave a pattern, and its colour is one of the best clues to what the species is.

Tip: pale spores disappear on white paper. Use half black, half white paper so any print colour shows up.
