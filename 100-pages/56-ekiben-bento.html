自分の弁当Pack your own bento.

Drag (or tap) food from the counter into your box. Rice goes in the big compartment. A good bento balances colour, taste and nutrition, and the meters will tell you how you're doing.

名物駅弁Famous ekiben of Japan.

Every station has a speciality. Some come in ceramic pots, some in boxes that heat themselves, and one comes in a Daruma doll.

東海道線Along the Tōkaidō.

Tokyo to Hakata on the Shinkansen, a 5-hour ride with a new bento at every stop. Click a station.

スタンプラリーStation stamp rally.

Japanese stations keep free rubber stamps near the gates. Tap a circle to "visit" the station and ink your book. Collect all eight for a prize.
