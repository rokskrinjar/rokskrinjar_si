Why marbles do what they do

Gravity pulls, ramps redirect, and every bounce loses a little energy. Here are two small experiments.

Slope & speed

A steeper slope gives more speed, but at the bottom it only depends on the height the marble fell: v = √(2gh). Slope changes how quickly it gets there.

Bounciness

The coefficient of restitution is how much speed survives a bounce. Rubber keeps about 0.8 of it. Clay keeps almost none.
