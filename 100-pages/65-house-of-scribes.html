Your name in a cartouche

A cartouche is an oval rope that protected royal names. Type your name and the scribes will spell it out with the sound-signs of the hieroglyphic alphabet.

Counting like an Egyptian

Egyptians used a decimal system with no zero. Each power of ten has its own sign, repeated as often as needed. They usually wrote the biggest signs first.
