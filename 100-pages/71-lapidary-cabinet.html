In 1812 Friedrich Mohs ranked ten minerals by what can scratch what. Each one scratches everything below it. The steps aren't even, though: diamond is about four times harder than corundum.

Every crystal belongs to one of seven systems, set by the lengths of its three axes (a, b, c) and the angles between them.
