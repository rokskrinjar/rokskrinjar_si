Change the Letters

The sign has three rows of plastic letters and a box of spares that is running a little low. Type your own message and the letters clack into place.

Tonight's Double Feature

Gates open at dusk. Tune your car radio to 88.7 FM for the sound. Pick a showing.

Let's All Go to the Lobby

Order from your car and Dottie the carhop skates it over on roller skates. Ketchup is extra, and so is her opinion.

Pick Your Spot

Every spot has a speaker post. The back rows are for sweethearts and the front rows are for families with very big hats.
