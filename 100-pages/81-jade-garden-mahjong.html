Match pairs of free tiles. A tile is free if nothing is on top of it and its left or right side is open.

The tiles

A full set has 144 tiles in three suits, plus honours and bonus tiles. Any two identical tiles make a pair. So does any flower with another flower, and any season with another season.

How to play

Mahjong solitaire, sometimes called Shanghai, was invented in 1981 as a computer game. The tiles came from a four-player game that is centuries old.

  1. Tiles are stacked in layers. Your goal is to clear every tile from the board.
  2. Click two identical tiles to remove them. Both must be free.
  3. A tile is free when no tile sits on top of it (even partly) and it has no neighbour on at least one side, left or right.
  4. Every deal here is guaranteed solvable: it was built by removing pairs in reverse. You can still paint yourself into a corner, though.
  5. Stuck? Hint shows you a legal pair. Shuffle rearranges the remaining faces, but it may break the guarantee.
