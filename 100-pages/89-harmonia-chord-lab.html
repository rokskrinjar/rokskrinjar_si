The circle
of fifths.

Neighbours on the circle share six of their seven notes, which is why songs slide easily between them. Click a key to hear its chords. Each pitch has its own colour, the way Scriabin saw sound.

What chord
am I playing?

Click keys to add or remove them (or use your computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K). Harmonia names the chord as you build it.

Four chords
is plenty.

Most pop songs loop four chords from a single key. Pick a key and a preset, or choose each chord yourself, then press play.

Seven moods,
one set of notes.

Play the white keys starting on different notes and you get the seven modes. Each has its own flavour, from bright Lydian to dark Locrian.
