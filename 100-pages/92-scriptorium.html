An illuminated initial

Incipit. Monks spent weeks on a single capital letter, laying gold leaf over gesso so it would catch the candlelight. Choose a letter and its colours, and the scribe does the rest in an instant.

Devise your arms

Heraldry has its own language, called blazon. Build a shield and we'll write it out as a herald would. Mind the rule of tincture: don't put a colour on a colour, or a metal on a metal.

The bestiary

Medieval bestiaries mixed real animals with marvels, and gave each one a moral lesson. Nobody had seen a unicorn, but everybody knew what it meant.

The labours of the months

Every Book of Hours opened with a calendar showing the work of each month. The current month is framed in gold.
