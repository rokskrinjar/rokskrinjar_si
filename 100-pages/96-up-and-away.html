Steer with the wind

A balloon has no steering wheel. Pilots change altitude to catch winds blowing in different directions. Hold BURN (or the space bar) to rise and let go to sink. Land in the target field.

Design your envelope

A balloon's envelope is sewn from long vertical strips called gores. Choose a pattern and colours, give it a name, and register it for the fiesta.

Night glow

After dark the balloons stay tethered to the ground and fire their burners to light up like giant lanterns. Click a balloon to make it glow, or press the button for a synchronised show.

Fiesta programme

Three days, 120 balloons and a very early alarm clock. All launches depend on the weather: wind below 10 knots, and no rain.
