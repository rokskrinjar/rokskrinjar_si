Chicago, 1926

A plain door in an alley behind a laundry. Word on the street is that you knock the old "shave and a haircut" rhythm: knock, knock-knock, knock, knock… (pause) …knock, knock. Click the door to knock.

Behind the bar

The regulars are thirsty. Read each order, choose the glass and pour the right bottles, then serve it. The recipe card is on the cocktail menu if you get stuck.

The bandstand

The Velvet Four play till 3 a.m. Bring each musician in and out, and they'll find the groove together.
