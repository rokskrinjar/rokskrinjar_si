Voices in the static

Drag the dial or turn the knob from 3 to 30 MHz. Between the hiss you'll find a time signal, a Morse beacon, a far-off concert and one very strange station that only sends numbers. Press Power first, because sound needs a click to start.

The Morse desk

QSL cards

Radio stations send postcards to listeners who report hearing them. Tune in a station on the receiver to unlock its card.

The band plan

The shortwave spectrum is split between broadcasters, radio amateurs, ships, aircraft and the odd mystery. Shortwave signals bounce off the ionosphere, so they can travel right around the planet.
