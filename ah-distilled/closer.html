CLOSERThe CLOSER Framework

Hormozi sales training · CLOSER

Šest korakov prodajnega pogovora, v katerem stranka sama pride do odločitve. Ne gre za trike, ampak za vrstni red: najprej razumeš, potem pokažeš pot, na koncu odpraviš dvome.

Six steps of a sales conversation in which the client reaches the decision themselves. It isn't about tricks but about order: first you understand, then you show the path, finally you clear the doubts.

BistvoThe gist

C · ClarifyZakaj je tukaj?Why are they here?
L · LabelPoimenuj problem, stranka potrdiName the problem, they confirm
O · OverviewKaj je že poskusila in zakaj ni šloWhat they tried and why it failed
S · SellProdaj počitnice, ne letalaSell the vacation, not the plane
E · ExplainRazloži in odpravi pomislekeExplain away concerns
R · ReinforceUtrdi odločitev po nakupuReinforce the decision after buying

Dober prodajni pogovor je diagnoza, ne predstavitev. Prve tri črke (C, L, O) so v celoti posvečene stranki: zakaj je prišla, kaj jo boli in zakaj dosedanje rešitve niso delovale. Šele ko stranka sama izreče problem in prizna, da ga sama ne bo rešila, ima smisel govoriti o tvoji ponudbi. Kdor preskoči na S, prodaja nekomu, ki še ni kupil problema.

A good sales conversation is a diagnosis, not a presentation. The first three letters (C, L, O) are entirely about the client: why they came, what hurts, and why past solutions didn't work. Only once the client has said the problem out loud and admitted they won't solve it alone does it make sense to talk about your offer. Skip straight to S and you are selling to someone who hasn't bought the problem yet.

Šest korakovThe six steps

C · Razjasni, zakaj je tukajC · Clarify why they're here

Prvo vprašanje je preprosto: »Kaj te je spodbudilo, da si se oglasil zdaj?« Ljudje se ne oglasijo brez razloga. Nekaj se je zgodilo: izgubljen kupec, nova zahteva uprave, ponovna zamuda. Ta sprožilec je motor celotnega pogovora. Če stranka ne ve, zakaj je prišla, tudi ne bo vedela, zakaj bi kupila. Clarify pomeni, da prisluhneš in vprašaš naprej, dokler ne slišiš pravega razloga, ne prve vljudne razlage.

The first question is simple: "What made you reach out now?" People don't get in touch for no reason. Something happened: a lost customer, a new demand from the board, yet another delay. That trigger powers the whole conversation. If the client doesn't know why they came, they won't know why they should buy. Clarify means listening and asking further until you hear the real reason, not the first polite explanation.

L · Označi problem (stranka ga potrdi)L · Label them with a problem (they confirm it)

Ko slišiš dovolj, problem povzameš v en stavek in vprašaš, ali drži: »Torej, če prav razumem, je težava v tem, da …, je tako?« Ključno je, da stranka potrdi. Ko ona reče »ja, točno to«, je problem njen, ne tvoj. Label ni diagnoza, ki jo vsiliš; je ogledalo. Če stranka popravi tvoj povzetek, še bolje, zdaj imaš natančnejšo sliko.

Once you have heard enough, you sum up the problem in one sentence and ask if it's right: "So if I understand correctly, the issue is that …, is that it?" The key is that the client confirms. When they say "yes, exactly that", the problem is theirs, not yours. Label isn't a diagnosis you impose; it is a mirror. If the client corrects your summary, even better: now you have a sharper picture.

O · Preglej preteklo bolečinoO · Overview past pain

Vprašaj, kaj je stranka že poskusila, koliko časa in denarja je to stalo in zakaj ni delovalo. Ta korak naredi dve stvari. Prvič, stranka sama prizna, da problema ne more rešiti z dosedanjimi pristopi. Drugič, ti izveš, česa ne smeš ponuditi, ker je že propadlo. Overview je tudi priložnost, da ločiš problem od krivde: rešitev ni delovala, ker ni bila prava, ne zato, ker je stranka nesposobna.

Ask what the client has already tried, how much time and money it cost, and why it didn't work. This step does two things. First, the client admits for themselves that past approaches won't solve the problem. Second, you learn what not to offer, because it has already failed. Overview is also a chance to separate problem from blame: the solution failed because it wasn't the right one, not because the client is incompetent.

S · Prodaj počitniceS · Sell the vacation

Nihče ne kupi počitnic zaradi letala. Ljudi zanima plaža, ne sedež 14C in štiri ure leta. Enako velja za tvojo ponudbo: opiši cilj, kako bo videti stranki teden ali mesec po rešitvi, ne pa korakov, sestankov in tehnologije. Proces omeni le toliko, kolikor je potrebno, da je obljuba verodostojna. Čim več podrobnosti o letalu, tem več stvari, ki se stranki lahko zdijo naporne ali tvegane.

Nobody books a holiday for the plane. People care about the beach, not seat 14C and a four-hour flight. The same goes for your offer: describe the destination, what the client's week or month looks like once it's solved, not the steps, meetings and technology. Mention the process only as much as needed to make the promise believable. The more detail about the plane, the more things the client may find tiring or risky.

E · Razloži in odpravi pomislekeE · Explain away concerns

Pomisleki so normalni in večinoma spadajo v nekaj skupin: čas (»zdaj nimam časa«), denar (»predrago je«), odločevalec ali odlašanje (»moram vprašati šefa«, »bom še premislil«) in dvom vase (»pri nas to ne bo delovalo«, »nisem pravi za to«). Pomislek ne pomeni »ne«; pogosto pomeni »še nisem prepričan«. Najprej ga ponovi in vprašaj, ali je to edini zadržek. Potem se vrni k temu, kar je stranka povedala pri C, L in O: njen razlog, njen problem, njeni neuspeli poskusi. Ne prepiraj se; pomagaj ji, da sama vidi, ali ima pomislek težo.

Concerns are normal and mostly fall into a few groups: time ("I don't have time now"), money ("it's too expensive"), decision-maker or stalling ("I need to ask my boss", "I'll think about it") and self-doubt ("it won't work for us", "it's not right for me"). A concern doesn't mean "no"; often it means "I'm not sure yet". First repeat it back and ask whether it's the only thing holding them back. Then return to what the client said during C, L and O: their reason, their problem, their failed attempts. Don't argue; help them see for themselves whether the concern carries weight.

R · Utrdi odločitevR · Reinforce the decision

Takoj po nakupu se pri mnogih pojavi kesanje kupca (buyer's remorse). Zato pogovor ne konča pri plačilu. Čestitaj, povej, kaj se zgodi naslednje in kdaj, in poskrbi za prvi stik ali prvo zmago čim prej. Reinforce zmanjša odpovedi in vračila, hkrati pa je začetek dobre izkušnje, ki kasneje prinese priporočila.

Right after buying, many people feel buyer's remorse. So the conversation doesn't end at payment. Congratulate them, say what happens next and when, and arrange the first contact or first win as soon as possible. Reinforce cuts cancellations and refunds, and it is the start of a good experience that later brings referrals.

B2B · analitikaB2B · analytics

Klic z direktorjem proizvodnega podjetjaCall with a manufacturing company's CEO

Odlomek pogovora, ki gre skozi vse črke. Prodajalec je svetovalec za poslovno analitiko.

A conversation excerpt that walks through every letter. The seller is a business analytics consultant.

C · Prodajalec: Kaj vas je spodbudilo, da ste se oglasili ravno ta mesec?

C · Seller: What made you get in touch this particular month?

Stranka: Na zadnji seji nadzornega sveta nisem znal povedati, kateri kupci nam dejansko prinašajo maržo. Bilo je neprijetno.

Client: At the last supervisory board meeting I couldn't say which customers actually bring us margin. It was awkward.

L · Prodajalec: Torej problem ni pomanjkanje podatkov, ampak to, da jih ne dobite pravočasno v obliki, ki ji lahko zaupate. Drži?

L · Seller: So the problem isn't a lack of data, it's that you don't get it in time and in a form you can trust. Is that right?

Stranka: Točno. Številke imamo, a vsak oddelek ima svoje.

Client: Exactly. We have the numbers, but every department has its own.

O · Prodajalec: Kaj ste že poskusili?

O · Seller: What have you tried so far?

Stranka: Lani smo kupili orodje za poročanje. Licence plačujemo, uporablja ga en človek, ki je zdaj na porodniški.

Client: Last year we bought a reporting tool. We pay for the licences, one person uses it, and she's now on maternity leave.

S · Prodajalec: Predstavljajte si naslednjo sejo: odprete eno stran, vidite maržo po kupcu za zadnji mesec in na vsako vprašanje odgovorite v nekaj sekundah, brez klicanja računovodstva.

S · Seller: Picture the next board meeting: you open one page, see margin by customer for last month, and answer every question within seconds, without calling accounting.

E · Stranka: Zveni dobro, ampak spet bomo plačali nekaj, česar nihče ne bo uporabljal.

E · Client: Sounds good, but we'll pay again for something nobody uses.

Prodajalec: Razumem, to je prav tisto, kar se je zgodilo lani. Je to edini zadržek? Zato prvih 30 dni vključuje, da poročilo pripravimo mi in ga dvakrat skupaj pregledamo z vami pred sejo. Ne potrebujete nikogar, ki bi se učil orodja.

Seller: I understand, that's exactly what happened last year. Is that the only concern? That's why the first 30 days include us preparing the report and reviewing it with you twice before the meeting. You don't need anyone to learn the tool.

R · Prodajalec (po podpisu): Odlična odločitev. Jutri ob devetih imate uvodni klic z našo analitičarko, do petka pa prvo različico poročila.

R · Seller (after signing): Great decision. Tomorrow at nine you have a kickoff call with our analyst, and the first version of the report by Friday.

Pomislek je spadal v skupino dvoma (»pri nas ne bo delovalo«). Prodajalec se ni prepiral, ampak je uporabil strankino lastno izkušnjo iz koraka O in jo povezal s konkretnim delom ponudbe.

The concern belonged to the self-doubt group ("it won't work for us"). The seller didn't argue; he used the client's own experience from the O step and tied it to a concrete part of the offer.

Lokalna storitev · zobozdravstvoLocal service · dental clinic

Posvet za ortodontski aparat za odrasleConsultation for adult braces

PredBefore

Recepcija takoj razloži vrste aparatov, materiale, trajanje terapije v mesecih in ceno. Pacientka reče, da bo premislila, in se ne vrne.

Reception immediately explains types of braces, materials, treatment length in months and the price. The patient says she'll think about it and never returns.

PoAfter

Najprej vprašanje, zakaj zdaj (poroka sestre čez leto dni). Povzetek: »Ne marate se smejati na fotografijah, je tako?« Pogovor o tem, kaj je že poskusila. Nato slika cilja: nasmeh na poročnih fotografijah. Pomislek o denarju razrešijo z obročnim plačilom.

First the question why now (her sister's wedding in a year). Summary: "You don't like smiling in photos, is that right?" Talk about what she's tried. Then a picture of the destination: smiling in the wedding photos. The money concern is handled with instalments.

Isti izdelek, isti cenik. Razlika je v vrstnem redu: v prvem primeru je klinika prodajala letalo (materiale in mesece), v drugem pa počitnice (fotografije s poroke). Po posvetu jo še isti teden pokličejo in potrdijo prvi termin, kar je korak R.

Same product, same price list. The difference is the order: in the first case the clinic sold the plane (materials and months), in the second the vacation (wedding photos). After the consultation they call her that same week to confirm the first appointment, which is the R step.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Skok naravnost na predstavitevJumping straight to the pitch

    Brez C, L in O nimaš na čem graditi. Stranka sliši ponudbo za problem, ki ga še ni izrekla, in vse zveni kot strošek.

    Without C, L and O you have nothing to build on. The client hears an offer for a problem they haven't voiced yet, and everything sounds like a cost.

  • Problem izrečeš ti, ne strankaYou state the problem, not the client

    Če stranka problema ne potrdi z lastnimi besedami, ga ne čuti kot svojega. Povzetek vedno konča z vprašanjem.

    If the client doesn't confirm the problem in their own words, they don't feel it's theirs. A summary always ends with a question.

  • Prodaja letalaSelling the plane

    Razlaga arhitekture, metodologije in števila delavnic. Tehnični ljudje to radi počnejo, a vsaka podrobnost je nova priložnost za pomislek.

    Explaining architecture, methodology and number of workshops. Technical people love doing this, but every detail is a new chance for a concern.

  • Prepiranje s pomislekomArguing with the concern

    Ko se prepiraš, stranka brani svoje stališče. Bolje je vprašati, ali je to edini zadržek, in se vrniti k njenim besedam.

    When you argue, the client defends their position. Better to ask if it's the only concern and go back to their own words.

  • Tišina po podpisuSilence after signing

    Brez koraka R kesanje kupca raste. Nekaj dni brez stika je dovolj, da stranka začne dvomiti.

    Without the R step buyer's remorse grows. A few days without contact is enough for the client to start doubting.
