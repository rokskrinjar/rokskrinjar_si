Ozko grloThe Constraint

Hormozi talks · based on Goldratt's Theory of Constraints · Constraint / Bottleneck

Podjetje ima v vsakem trenutku eno glavno omejitev, ki določa, kako hitro lahko raste. Vse drugo, kar izboljšuješ, ima malo vpliva, dokler ne odpraviš te.

At any moment a business has one main limit that sets how fast it can grow. Everything else you improve has little effect until you remove that one.

BistvoThe gist

Tok:Flow: PovpraševanjaLeads → ProdajaSales → IzvedbaDelivery ▼ → ZadržanjeRetention

Podjetje je kot cev z več odseki. Skozi celo cev steče le toliko, kolikor prepusti najožji odsek. Če razširiš katerikoli drug del, se ne spremeni nič, ker voda še vedno čaka pri istem zožitvenem mestu. Ideja izhaja iz Goldrattove Theory of Constraints, Hormozi pa jo uporablja kot preprosto pravilo za podjetnike: poišči eno stvar, ki te ta trenutek omejuje, in delaj samo na njej, dokler se omejitev ne premakne drugam.

A business is like a pipe with several sections. Only as much flows through the whole pipe as the narrowest section lets through. Widen any other part and nothing changes, because the water still waits at the same narrow point. The idea comes from Goldratt's Theory of Constraints, and Hormozi uses it as a simple rule for founders: find the one thing limiting you right now, and work only on that until the constraint moves elsewhere.

Kako delujeHow it works

1. Vedno je ena glavna omejitev1. There is always one main constraint

Težav je v podjetju vedno veliko, a omejitev je ena: tista, ki bi ob izboljšavi najbolj dvignila rezultat celote. Najpogostejša mesta so povpraševanja (premalo ljudi izve zate), prodajna konverzija (ljudje pridejo, a ne kupijo), zmogljivost izvedbe (prodaš več, kot zmoreš dostaviti) in zadržanje (stranke odhajajo, churn). Včasih je omejitev tudi denarni tok ali ljudje, na primer manjka en ključen vodja.

A business always has many problems, but only one constraint: the one whose improvement would most raise the result of the whole. The usual places are leads (too few people hear about you), sales conversion (people come but don't buy), delivery capacity (you sell more than you can fulfil) and retention (clients leave, churn). Sometimes the constraint is cash flow or people, for example one missing key manager.

2. Delo drugje ima nizek vzvod2. Work elsewhere is low leverage

Če je omejitev izvedba, dodatna oglaševalska kampanja prinese le več razočaranih strank in daljše čakalne vrste. Če je omejitev zadržanje, nova spletna stran napolni vedro, ki pušča. Takšno delo je videti produktivno, a ne premakne rezultata. Zato pravilo: reci ne vsemu, kar ni ozko grlo, tudi dobrim idejam.

If delivery is the constraint, another ad campaign only brings more disappointed clients and longer queues. If retention is the constraint, a new website fills a leaking bucket. Such work looks productive but doesn't move the result. Hence the rule: say no to everything that isn't the bottleneck, good ideas included.

3. Kako ga najdeš3. How to find it

Najhitrejši test je miselni: »Če bi se mi jutri povpraševanja podvojila, kaj bi se prvo zlomilo?« Če je odgovor »nič, bi jih z veseljem obdelal«, so omejitev povpraševanja. Če bi se zlomila prodaja (ni časa za vse klice), je omejitev prodaja. Če bi se zlomila izvedba, je to izvedba. Drugi način so številke lijaka: za vsak korak izmeri pretvorbo in jo primerjaj z razumnim pričakovanjem za tvojo panogo. Korak, ki najbolj odstopa ali kjer se nabira čakalna vrsta, je kandidat.

The quickest test is a thought experiment: "If my leads doubled tomorrow, what would break first?" If the answer is "nothing, I'd happily handle them", leads are the constraint. If sales would break (no time for all the calls), it's sales. If delivery would break, it's delivery. The other way is funnel numbers: measure conversion at each step and compare it with a reasonable benchmark for your industry. The step that deviates most, or where a queue piles up, is the candidate.

4. Odpravi ga, nato se premakne4. Fix it, then it moves

Ko omejitev odpraviš, se ne izgubi, ampak se premakne. Več povpraševanj naredi prodajo za novo ozko grlo; boljša prodaja preobremeni izvedbo; boljša izvedba razkrije, da stranke ne ostanejo. To je normalno in pomeni napredek. Zato je iskanje omejitve stalna navada, na primer enkrat mesečno, ne enkratni projekt.

When you remove a constraint it doesn't disappear, it moves. More leads make sales the new bottleneck; better sales overload delivery; better delivery reveals that clients don't stay. That is normal and means progress. So finding the constraint is a standing habit, say monthly, not a one-off project.

5. Pet korakov po Goldrattu, poenostavljeno5. Goldratt's five steps, simplified

  1. Poišči omejitev.

    Identify the constraint.

  2. Izkoristi jo do konca z obstoječimi viri (nič ne sme stati v čakanju).

    Exploit it fully with existing resources (nothing should sit idle).

  3. Podredi vse ostalo omejitvi.

    Subordinate everything else to it.

  4. Razširi jo z novimi viri, ljudmi ali sistemi.

    Elevate it with new resources, people or systems.

  5. Ponovi, ko se premakne.

    Repeat once it moves.

B2B · SaaS za analitikoB2B · analytics SaaS

Lijak v enem četrtletjuOne quarter's funnel

Majhno podjetje prodaja analitično platformo proizvodnim podjetjem. Ustanovitelj želi več oglaševati, a pred tem pogleda številke.

Vsi koraki so v mejah pričakovanega, razen zadržanja. Več oglasov bi prineslo več strank, ki bi jih podjetje v pol leta izgubilo več kot polovico. Ozko grlo je zadržanje. Ukrep: mesečni pregled uporabe s stranko in opozorilo, ko nekdo dva tedna ne odpre platforme. Šele ko zadržanje naraste, se splača dvigniti oglaševanje.

Every step is within expectations except retention. More ads would bring more clients, more than half of whom the company would lose within six months. The bottleneck is retention. Action: a monthly usage review with each client and an alert when someone hasn't opened the platform for two weeks. Only once retention rises does it pay to scale ads.

Lokalno podjetje · avtoservisLocal business · car repair shop

Polni termini, prazen računFull calendar, flat revenue

PredBefore

Lastnik misli, da potrebuje več strank, in plača oglase na družbenih omrežjih. Klicev je več, a čakalna doba za termin naraste na tri tedne, nekateri stalni kupci odidejo drugam.

The owner thinks he needs more customers and pays for social ads. Calls increase, but the wait for an appointment grows to three weeks and some regulars go elsewhere.

PoAfter

Test »če bi se povpraševanja podvojila« takoj pokaže: zlomila bi se delavnica. Omejitev je en dvižni most in en diagnostik. Lastnik ustavi oglase, uvede drugo izmeno ob četrtkih in petkih ter vnaprej naroča dele, da avto ne stoji na mostu.

The "if leads doubled" test shows at once: the workshop would break. The constraint is one lift and one diagnostic technician. The owner stops the ads, adds a second shift on Thursdays and Fridays, and pre-orders parts so cars don't sit on the lift.

Pred tem je izboljševal korak, ki ni bil omejitev, in s tem poslabšal izkušnjo strank. Ko izvedba zmore več, je naslednje ozko grlo spet povpraševanje, in takrat imajo oglasi smisel.

Before, he was improving a step that wasn't the constraint, and made the customer experience worse. Once delivery can handle more, the next bottleneck is leads again, and that's when ads make sense.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Delo na vsem hkratiWorking on everything at once

    Deset izboljšav po malem ne premakne ničesar, ker ozko grlo dobi le desetino pozornosti.

    Ten small improvements move nothing, because the bottleneck only gets a tenth of the attention.

  • Privzeto »potrebujemo več strank«Defaulting to "we need more clients"

    Povpraševanja so najpogostejši odgovor, a pogosto napačen. Več strank v podjetje z luknjastim zadržanjem ali preobremenjeno izvedbo škodi.

    Leads are the most common answer, but often the wrong one. More clients into a business with leaky retention or overloaded delivery does harm.

  • Izbira po občutku namesto po številkahChoosing by gut instead of numbers

    Ustanovitelji radi delajo na tistem, kar jih zabava. Brez meritev lijaka je izbira omejitve pogosto izbira hobija.

    Founders like working on what they enjoy. Without funnel metrics, picking the constraint is often picking a hobby.

  • Pozabljanje, da se omejitev premakneForgetting the constraint moves

    Ko je ena omejitev rešena, nekateri nadaljujejo z istim delom. Po vsaki izboljšavi ponovi test.

    Once one constraint is solved, some keep doing the same work. Re-run the test after every improvement.

Vzemi zadnje tri mesece svojega podjetja in odgovori pisno.

Take the last three months of your business and answer in writing.

Test: Če bi se ti jutri povpraševanja podvojila, kaj bi se prvo zlomilo?

Test: If your leads doubled tomorrow, what would break first?

Lijak: Zapiši števila za vsak korak (povpraševanja, klici, posli, uvedba, zadržanje). Kje je največje odstopanje?

Funnel: Write down the numbers for each step (leads, calls, deals, onboarding, retention). Where is the biggest gap?

Čakalne vrste: Kje se delo ali stranke nabirajo in čakajo?

Queues: Where do work or clients pile up and wait?

Fokus: Katere tri projekte, ki ne zadevajo ozkega grla, boš ta mesec ustavil?

Focus: Which three projects that don't touch the bottleneck will you pause this month?

Ukrep: Kaj je en ukrep, ki v 30 dneh razširi omejitev? Kako boš to izmeril?

Action: What one action widens the constraint within 30 days? How will you measure it?
