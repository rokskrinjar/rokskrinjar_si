Core FourThe Core Four

$100M Leads · Core Four

Obstajajo samo štirje načini, kako ljudem sporočiš, da obstajaš. Vse ostalo so različice teh štirih. Ko to vidiš, prenehaš loviti »skrivni kanal« in začneš delati.

There are only four ways to let people know you exist. Everything else is a variation of these four. Once you see it, you stop hunting for a "secret channel" and start working.

BistvoThe gist

Topli kontaktiWarm outreach Te poznajo · ena na enaKnow you · one-to-one
Brezplačna vsebinaPosting free content Te poznajo · ena na mnogoKnow you · one-to-many
Hladni kontaktiCold outreach Te ne poznajo · ena na enaDon't know you · one-to-one
Plačani oglasiPaid ads Te ne poznajo · ena na mnogoDon't know you · one-to-many

Dve osi: ali te človek že pozna (warm) ali ne (cold), in ali mu pišeš osebno (one-to-one) ali govoriš mnogim naenkrat (one-to-many). Vsaka kombinacija je ena od štirih poti. Cilj ni prodaja v prvem koraku, ampak angažiran lead (engaged lead): človek, ki je pokazal zanimanje, ker je nekaj naredil, npr. odgovoril, prenesel lead magnet ali se prijavil na posvet.

Two axes: does the person already know you (warm) or not (cold), and are you messaging them personally (one-to-one) or speaking to many at once (one-to-many). Each combination is one of the four paths. The goal isn't a sale in the first step but an engaged lead: someone who showed interest by doing something, e.g. replying, downloading a lead magnet or booking a call.

Štiri poti, kako priti do leadovFour paths to leads

Najprej: kaj je oglaševanje in kaj je leadFirst: what advertising is and what a lead is

Hormozi oglaševanje razume široko: vsako dejanje, s katerim ljudem sporočiš, kaj ponujaš. Sporočilo prijatelju je oglaševanje, objava na LinkedInu je oglaševanje, plačan oglas tudi. Lead je oseba, s katero lahko stopiš v stik. Angažiran lead je tisti, ki je na tvoje sporočilo odreagiral. Oglašuješ zato, da dobiš angažirane leade; prodaja pride potem, v pogovoru.

Hormozi treats advertising broadly: any action that lets people know what you offer. A message to a friend is advertising, a LinkedIn post is advertising, a paid ad is too. A lead is a person you can contact. An engaged lead is one who responded to your message. You advertise to get engaged leads; the sale comes afterwards, in conversation.

1. Topli kontakti1. Warm outreach warm · 1:1

Kaj: osebna sporočila ljudem, ki te že poznajo: nekdanji sodelavci, stranke, znanci, sošolci. Zakaj: je brezplačno, takoj na voljo in ima najvišjo stopnjo odziva. Je najboljši začetek za vsakogar, ki nima še nobenega sistema.

What: personal messages to people who already know you: former colleagues, clients, acquaintances, classmates. Why: it's free, available today and has the highest response rate. It's the best starting point for anyone without a system yet.

Kako: izvozi vse kontakte (telefon, e-pošta, LinkedIn) v en seznam. Vsakemu napiši osebno sporočilo, ki se nanaša nanj, ne kopiran oglas. Najprej se pogovorita, šele nato omeniš, s čim se ukvarjaš. Namesto »bi kupil?« vprašaj »ali poznaš koga, ki ima ta problem?«. Prošnja za priporočilo zmanjša pritisk, pogosto pa človek odgovori »pravzaprav jaz«. Prve stranke lahko dobiš tudi po znižani ceni ali brezplačno v zameno za referenco.

How: export all your contacts (phone, email, LinkedIn) into one list. Write each person a personal message that refers to them, not a pasted ad. Catch up first, only then mention what you're working on. Instead of "would you buy?" ask "do you know anyone with this problem?". Asking for a referral lowers pressure, and people often reply "actually, me". Your first clients can also come at a discount or free in exchange for a testimonial.

2. Brezplačna vsebina2. Posting free content warm · 1:N

Kaj: objavljaš vsebino (objave, videi, članki, podkast) za občinstvo, ki ti sledi. Zakaj: en kos vsebine doseže mnogo ljudi in deluje dolgo; sčasoma gradi zaupanje, zato topli leadi pridejo sami. Je počasna pot, ki se obrestuje.

What: you publish content (posts, videos, articles, a podcast) for an audience that follows you. Why: one piece reaches many people and keeps working; over time it builds trust, so warm leads come to you. It's a slow path that compounds.

Kako: vsak kos vsebine ima tri dele. Hook pritegne pozornost (naslov, prvi stavek, slika). Retain zadrži človeka do konca (zgodba, seznam, napetost). Reward mu da nekaj uporabnega, zaradi česar je bilo vredno. Daj stran več vrednosti, kot se ti zdi pametno. Občasno dodaj poziv k dejanju, npr. »komentiraj ANALIZA in ti pošljem predlogo«, sicer gradiš občinstvo, ne leadov.

How: every piece has three parts. The hook grabs attention (title, first line, image). Retain keeps the person to the end (story, list, tension). Reward gives them something useful that made it worth it. Give away more value than feels sensible. Regularly add a call to action, e.g. "comment ANALYSIS and I'll send you the template", or you're building an audience, not leads.

3. Hladni kontakti3. Cold outreach cold · 1:1

Kaj: osebna sporočila ljudem, ki te ne poznajo: e-pošta, LinkedIn, telefon. Zakaj: nimaš omejitve na število ljudi, ki jih poznaš, in natančno izbereš, komu pišeš. Odziv je nizek, zato je to igra volumna.

What: personal messages to people who don't know you: email, LinkedIn, phone. Why: you're not capped by how many people you know, and you pick exactly who you contact. Response rates are low, so it's a volume game.

Kako: sestavi seznam idealnih strank (javni registri, LinkedIn, kupljene baze). Personaliziraj prvo vrstico, da je jasno, da sporočilo ni masovno. Ne prodajaj v prvem sporočilu: ponudi nekaj brezplačnega in uporabnega, najbolje lead magnet. Pošiljaj veliko in vztrajno, z več opomniki, ter meri odziv. Avtomatizacija pomaga pri volumnu, a ne sme ubiti osebnega tona.

How: build a list of ideal clients (public registries, LinkedIn, bought databases). Personalise the first line so it's clear the message isn't a blast. Don't sell in the first message: offer something free and useful, ideally a lead magnet. Send a lot and persistently, with several follow-ups, and track replies. Automation helps with volume but must not kill the personal tone.

4. Plačani oglasi4. Paid ads cold · 1:N

Kaj: plačaš platformi (Meta, Google, LinkedIn), da tvoje sporočilo pokaže ljudem, ki te ne poznajo. Zakaj: najhitrejši način za skaliranje, ko veš, da ponudba deluje. Denar kupi doseg, ne pa sporočila: slab oglas samo hitreje zažge proračun.

What: you pay a platform (Meta, Google, LinkedIn) to show your message to people who don't know you. Why: the fastest way to scale once you know the offer works. Money buys reach, not a message: a bad ad just burns budget faster.

Kako: dober oglas ima tri dele. Callout nagovori pravo občinstvo (»vodje proizvodnje, ki ...«). Value pove, kaj dobijo, najbolje lead magnet. Call to action jasno reče, kaj naj naredijo zdaj. Začni z majhnim dnevnim proračunom, primerjaj več različic in spremljaj, koliko te stane en angažiran lead in koliko ti prinese ena stranka.

How: a good ad has three parts. The callout names the right audience ("production managers who..."). Value says what they get, ideally a lead magnet. The call to action says clearly what to do now. Start with a small daily budget, compare several variants, and track what one engaged lead costs you and what one customer brings in.

Začni z enim kanalomStart with one channel

Vseh štirih ne potrebuješ takoj. Izberi enega, ki ustreza tvojim virom: imaš čas in malo denarja, začni s toplimi kontakti ali vsebino; imaš denar in malo časa, so oglasi bolj smiselni. Kanal izvajaj, dokler ne prinaša predvidljivih leadov, šele nato dodaš naslednjega. Večja podjetja sčasoma uporabljajo vse štiri.

You don't need all four right away. Pick one that matches your resources: time and little money, start with warm outreach or content; money and little time, ads make more sense. Run that channel until it produces predictable leads, only then add the next. Larger companies eventually use all four.

B2B · analitikaB2B · analytics

Samostojni BI svetovalec brez novih strankFreelance BI consultant with no new clients

PredBefore

Občasna objava na LinkedInu »Na voljo za nove projekte«, spletna stran brez obiskov in čakanje, da se kdo oglasi. Dva meseca brez povpraševanja.

An occasional LinkedIn post "Available for new projects", a website with no traffic, and waiting for someone to call. Two months without an enquiry.

PoAfter

Izvozi 400 kontaktov, vsak dan napiše 20 osebnih sporočil nekdanjim sodelavcem in strankam: »Kako gre pri vas poročanje po prehodu na nov ERP? Delam brezplačne pregledne analize, poznaš koga, ki bi mu prišla prav?« V treh tednih 9 pogovorov, 2 projekta.

Exports 400 contacts and writes 20 personal messages a day to former colleagues and clients: "How's reporting going since your ERP switch? I'm doing free review analyses, know anyone who'd find one useful?" In three weeks: 9 conversations, 2 projects.

Kaj se je spremenilo: iz pasivnega »na voljo sem« je prešel na aktiven kanal, ki ga obvladuje (topli kontakti). Sporočilo je osebno, ponuja nekaj brezplačnega in prosi za priporočilo, zato ne deluje kot prodaja. Ko bo ta kanal tekel, lahko doda vsebino z istimi temami.

What changed: he moved from a passive "I'm available" to an active channel he controls (warm outreach). The message is personal, offers something free and asks for a referral, so it doesn't feel like selling. Once this channel runs, he can add content on the same themes.

B2B · SaaSB2B · SaaS

Orodje za planiranje proizvodnjeProduction planning tool

Majhna ekipa razvija SaaS za planiranje v kovinski industriji. Ker nima znanih imen v panogi, so topli kontakti hitro izčrpani. Izberejo hladne kontakte: seznam 600 proizvodnih podjetij iz javnih registrov, prva vrstica omeni konkreten stroj ali certifikat s spletne strani podjetja, ponudba pa je brezplačen izračun izgubljenih ur zaradi prestavljanja naročil. Pošiljajo 50 sporočil na dan s tremi opomniki. Odziv je 3 %, a to je dovolj za 6 demo predstavitev na teden.

A small team builds planning SaaS for metalworking. With no known names in the industry, warm contacts run out fast. They choose cold outreach: a list of 600 manufacturers from public registries, a first line mentioning a specific machine or certificate from the company's website, and an offer of a free calculation of hours lost to rescheduled orders. They send 50 messages a day with three follow-ups. The reply rate is 3%, but that's enough for 6 demos a week.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Vsi kanali naenkratAll channels at once

    Malo vsega pomeni, da noben kanal ne dobi dovolj volumna, da bi videl, ali deluje. Izberi enega in ga pelji do rezultatov.

    A little of everything means no channel gets enough volume to tell whether it works. Pick one and drive it to results.

  • Sram pred toplimi kontaktiBeing embarrassed by warm outreach

    Mnogi preskočijo najlažji kanal, ker se jim zdi nerodno pisati znancem. Prošnja za priporočilo ni vsiljiva, je pa najhitrejša pot do prve stranke.

    Many skip the easiest channel because messaging people they know feels awkward. Asking for a referral isn't pushy, and it's the fastest path to a first client.

  • Prodaja v prvem sporočiluSelling in the first message

    Neznanec, ki dobi takoj »kupite«, ne odgovori. Najprej ponudi vrednost in pridobi angažiranega leada, prodaja pride v pogovoru.

    A stranger who gets "buy now" straight away won't reply. Offer value first and get an engaged lead; the sale happens in conversation.

  • Vsebina brez poziva k dejanjuContent without a call to action

    Sledilci niso leadi. Če nikoli ne poveš, kaj naj naredijo naslednje, dobiš všečke, ne pogovorov.

    Followers aren't leads. If you never say what to do next, you get likes, not conversations.

  • Prehitro obupatiQuitting too early

    Ena kampanja ali deset objav ni test kanala. Brez dovolj volumna ne veš, ali je problem kanal ali sporočilo.

    One campaign or ten posts isn't a test of a channel. Without enough volume you can't tell whether the problem is the channel or the message.

Delovni listWorksheet

Odgovori pisno za svoje podjetje. Cilj je izbrati en kanal za naslednjih 30 dni.

Answer in writing for your business. The goal is to pick one channel for the next 30 days.

Inventar: Koliko ljudi te pozna (kontakti, sledilci, nekdanje stranke)? Koliko od njih bi lahko poznalo nekoga z mojim problemom?

Inventory: How many people know you (contacts, followers, past clients)? How many of them might know someone with the problem you solve?

Viri: Imam več časa ali več denarja? Kateri od štirih kanalov se s tem najbolje ujema?

Resources: Do I have more time or more money? Which of the four channels fits that best?

Angažiran lead: Kaj točno mora nekdo narediti, da ga štejem za angažiranega leada (odgovor, prijava, prenos)?

Engaged lead: What exactly must someone do for me to count them as an engaged lead (reply, sign-up, download)?

Sporočilo: Napiši prvo sporočilo za izbrani kanal. Je osebno ali ima jasen callout? Kaj ponuja brezplačno?

Message: Write the first message for your chosen channel. Is it personal, or does it have a clear callout? What does it offer for free?

Poziv: Kaj je en jasen naslednji korak, ki ga povem na koncu vsakega sporočila, objave ali oglasa?

Call to action: What is the one clear next step I state at the end of every message, post or ad?
