Grand Slam ponudbaGrand Slam Offer

$100M Offers · Grand Slam Offer

Ponudba, ki je tako dobra, da se kupec počuti neumno, če reče ne. Ne tekmuješ s ceno, ker te nihče ne more primerjati z nikomer.

An offer so good the buyer feels foolish saying no. You don't compete on price, because nobody can compare you with anyone.

BistvoThe gist

Večina ponudnikov prodaja commodity: isto stvar kot vsi drugi, zato kupec primerja le ceno in zmaga najcenejši. Grand Slam ponudba je paket rešitev za vse ovire, ki kupca ločijo od želenega rezultata, zložen tako, da nima neposredne primerjave. Postaneš category of one: kategorija s samo enim ponudnikom.

Most sellers offer a commodity: the same thing as everyone else, so the buyer only compares price and the cheapest wins. A Grand Slam Offer is a bundle of solutions to every obstacle between the buyer and the result they want, stacked so it has no direct comparison. You become a category of one.

  1. Določi sanjski izidIdentify the dream outcome

    Kam kupec v resnici želi priti?

    Where does the buyer really want to end up?

  2. Naštej vse ovireList every obstacle

    Pred, med in po nakupu.

    Before, during and after the purchase.

  3. Vsako oviro preobrni v rešitevTurn each obstacle into a solution

    »Kako … brez …«

    "How to … without …"

  4. Izberi načine dostaveChoose delivery vehicles

    Kako bo vsaka rešitev prišla do kupca.

    How each solution reaches the buyer.

  5. Oklesti in zložiTrim & stack

    Obdrži poceni-vredno, odreži drago-malo vredno.

    Keep cheap-but-valuable, cut costly-but-low-value.

KorakiThe steps

1. Določi sanjski izid1. Identify the dream outcome

Začneš pri cilju, ne pri svojem izdelku. Kaj bi kupec opisal kot popoln rezultat, in kaj bo to pomenilo zanj pred drugimi (šef, uprava, sosedje, partner)? To je isti Dream Outcome kot v enačbi vrednosti. Bolj ko je konkreten in merljiv, lažje boš v naslednjem koraku našel ovire.

You start at the destination, not at your product. What would the buyer describe as the perfect result, and what will it mean for them in front of others (boss, board, neighbours, partner)? This is the same Dream Outcome as in the value equation. The more concrete and measurable it is, the easier it is to find obstacles in the next step.

Praktično: zapiši ga z besedami stranke. Če imaš posnetke prodajnih klicev ali e-pošto, poišči stavke tipa »rad bi, da …«.

In practice: write it in the client's words. If you have sales call recordings or emails, look for sentences like "I'd love it if …".

2. Naštej vse ovire2. List every obstacle

Prehodi celotno pot kupca in zapiši vse, kar ga lahko ustavi, upočasni ali prestraši. Razdeli na pred (dvomi, ali je to zanj, ali ima čas, ali bo šef odobril), med (kje začeti, kaj, ko se zatakne, kdo mu pomaga) in po (kako ohraniti rezultat, kako ga pokazati drugim). Cilj je dolg seznam, ne kratek. Vsaka ovira je priložnost za kos ponudbe, ki ga konkurenca nima.

Walk the buyer's entire journey and write down everything that could stop, slow or scare them. Split into before (doubts about whether it's for them, whether they have time, whether the boss will approve), during (where to start, what if they get stuck, who helps them) and after (how to keep the result, how to show it to others). Aim for a long list, not a short one. Every obstacle is a chance for a piece of the offer the competition doesn't have.

Pomagaj si z enačbo vrednosti: za vsako oviro se vprašaj, ali znižuje zaznano verjetnost, podaljšuje čas ali povečuje trud.

Use the value equation as a lens: for each obstacle ask whether it lowers perceived likelihood, adds time or increases effort.

3. Vsako oviro preobrni v rešitev3. Turn each obstacle into a solution

Vsako oviro preoblikuješ v obljubo, najlažje v obliki »kako doseči X brez Y« ali »kako X, tudi če Y«. Ovira »ne vem, ali so naši podatki dovolj čisti« postane »kako dobiti zanesljiva poročila, tudi če so podatki v ERP-ju neurejeni«. S tem dobiš seznam rešitev, ki se bere kot seznam koristi, in hkrati besedilo za prodajno stran.

You rephrase each obstacle as a promise, most easily in the form "how to get X without Y" or "how to X even if Y". The obstacle "I don't know if our data is clean enough" becomes "how to get reliable reports even if your ERP data is messy". This gives you a list of solutions that reads like a list of benefits, and copy for your sales page at the same time.

4. Izberi načine dostave4. Choose delivery vehicles

Za vsako rešitev razmisli, kako bi jo lahko dostavil. Ista rešitev ima lahko zelo različno vrednost in strošek glede na obliko:

For each solution, think about how you could deliver it. The same solution can have very different value and cost depending on its form:

Uporaben test: kako bi to dostavil, če bi kupec plačal desetkrat več? In kako, če bi plačal desetkrat manj? Obe skrajnosti odpreta ideje, na katere sicer ne pomisliš.

A useful test: how would you deliver this if the buyer paid ten times more? And how if they paid ten times less? Both extremes surface ideas you wouldn't otherwise think of.

5. Oklesti in zloži5. Trim & stack

Zdaj imaš preveč. Vsak kos oceni po dveh oseh: vrednost za kupca in strošek zate. Odreži tisto, kar je drago in malo vredno. Obdrži poceni in zelo vredno: predloge, posnetke, kontrolne sezname, avtomatizacije. Nekaj dragega in zelo vrednega lahko obdržiš kot jedro ali premium nadgradnjo. Ostanek stack-aš: zložiš v paket in vsak kos poimenuješ, da je vidno, koliko dobi.

Now you have too much. Score each piece on two axes: value to the buyer and cost to you. Cut what's expensive and low-value. Keep what's cheap and highly valuable: templates, recordings, checklists, automations. Something expensive and highly valuable can stay as the core or a premium upgrade. Then you stack the rest into a bundle and name each piece, so it's visible how much they get.

Povezava z enačbo vrednosti: vsak kos v paketu mora premakniti vsaj en vzvod, torej povečati verjetnost, skrajšati čas ali zmanjšati trud. Kos, ki ne premakne ničesar, je samo šum.

Tie-back to the value equation: every piece in the stack must move at least one lever, raising likelihood, cutting time or reducing effort. A piece that moves nothing is just noise.

PrimeriExamples

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Examples are made up for illustration.

B2B · analitika za proizvodnjoB2B · manufacturing analytics

Od »BI projekta« do paketa brez primerjaveFrom "BI project" to an offer with no comparison

PredBefore

»Razvoj Qlik aplikacij po urni postavki. Ocena: 120 ur.« Kupec pridobi tri ponudbe in izbere najnižjo urno postavko.

"Qlik app development at an hourly rate. Estimate: 120 hours." The buyer collects three quotes and picks the lowest rate.

PoAfter

»Nadzor nad OEE in izmetom v 30 dneh«: priklop na MES in ERP, čiščenje šifrantov, tri vnaprej pripravljene aplikacije, 30-minutni tedenski pregled z vodji izmen, kontrolni seznam za upravo, mesec podpore po zagonu.

"OEE and scrap under control in 30 days": MES and ERP connection, master-data clean-up, three ready-made apps, a 30-minute weekly review with shift leads, a board-ready checklist, one month of post-launch support.

Ovire, ki jih je ponudnik našel: »podatki so neurejeni« (čiščenje šifrantov), »ljudje tega ne bodo uporabljali« (tedenski pregled z vodji izmen), »uprava ne bo verjela številkam« (kontrolni seznam), »po predaji bomo sami« (mesec podpore). Pripravljene aplikacije so poceni za ponovno uporabo in zelo vredne za kupca. Urne postavke ni več, zato tudi ni primerjave.

Obstacles the seller found: "our data is messy" (master-data clean-up), "people won't use it" (weekly review with shift leads), "the board won't trust the numbers" (checklist), "we'll be on our own after handover" (a month of support). The ready-made apps are cheap to reuse and highly valuable to the buyer. There's no hourly rate any more, so there's nothing to compare.

Svetovanje · AIConsulting · AI

Avtomatizacija obdelave računovInvoice processing automation

Sanjski izid: računovodstvo zapre mesec dva dni prej. Ovire: strah pred napakami AI, nezaupanje revizorja, ni časa za uvajanje. Rešitve in dostava: dvotedensko vzporedno delovanje (ročno in AI hkrati) s poročilom o odstopanjih, dokumentacija za revizorja kot predloga, pet 15-minutnih posnetkov namesto delavnice, odzivni chat v delovnem času prvi mesec. Odrezano: obsežno šolanje na lokaciji, ki je drago in ga stranka ne potrebuje.

Dream outcome: accounting closes the month two days earlier. Obstacles: fear of AI errors, auditor distrust, no time for onboarding. Solutions and delivery: two weeks of parallel running (manual and AI side by side) with a variance report, auditor documentation as a template, five 15-minute recordings instead of a workshop, responsive chat during business hours for the first month. Cut: extensive on-site training, which is costly and the client doesn't need.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Kopičenje brez ovirStacking without obstacles

    Dodajanje bonusov, ki ne rešujejo nobene resnične ovire, paket le napihne. Kupec čuti, da je to polnilo.

    Adding extras that don't solve any real obstacle just bloats the package. The buyer senses it's filler.

  • Prekratek seznam ovirA list of obstacles that's too short

    Če najdeš le tri ovire, nisi prehodil poti kupca. Prav neočitne ovire (strah pred šefom, nerodnost) so tiste, ki jih konkurenca spregleda.

    If you only find three obstacles, you haven't walked the buyer's journey. The non-obvious ones (fear of the boss, awkwardness) are exactly what competitors overlook.

  • Paket, ki ga ne moreš dostavitiA stack you can't deliver

    Brez koraka »oklesti« obljubiš 1-na-1 pozornost vsem in te pokoplje strošek. Vsak kos mora preživeti test stroška.

    Without the trim step you promise 1-on-1 attention to everyone and the cost buries you. Every piece must survive the cost test.

  • Ostati v primerljivi kategorijiStaying in a comparable category

    Če ponudbo še vedno opišeš kot »razvoj po urah« ali »paket ur«, si nazaj pri commodity. Ime in struktura morata kazati na rezultat.

    If you still describe the offer as "development by the hour" or "a bundle of hours", you're back to commodity. The name and structure must point to the result.

Delovni listWorksheet

Za eno ponudbo izpolni pisno. Ne preskakuj korakov, vrstni red je pomemben.

Fill this in writing for one offer. Don't skip steps, the order matters.

Izid: En stavek, z besedami stranke: kaj želi doseči in kako jo bodo potem videli drugi?

Outcome: One sentence, in the client's words: what do they want to achieve and how will others see them afterwards?

Ovire: Naštej vsaj 15 ovir, razdeljenih na pred, med in po.

Obstacles: List at least 15 obstacles, split into before, during and after.

Rešitve: Prepiši vsako oviro v obliko »kako X brez Y«.

Solutions: Rewrite each obstacle as "how to X without Y".

Dostava: Za pet najpomembnejših rešitev zapiši dva načina dostave: dragega in poceni.

Delivery: For the five most important solutions, write two delivery options: an expensive one and a cheap one.

Oklesti: Kaj odrežeš, ker je drago in malo vredno? Kateri poceni kos (predloga, posnetek, avtomatizacija) ima največjo vrednost?

Trim: What do you cut because it's costly and low-value? Which cheap piece (template, recording, automation) carries the most value?

Primerjava: S kom bi te kupec po novem še lahko neposredno primerjal? Če z nekom, kaj manjka?

Comparison: Who could the buyer still compare you with directly? If anyone, what's missing?
