$100M Offers

Enačba vrednostiThe Value Equation

Vrednost ponudbe v očeh kupca. Dve stvari dvigneš, dve spustiš.

The value of an offer in the buyer's eyes. Push two things up, two things down.

Vrednost =Value =
Sanjski izidDream Outcome × Zaznana verjetnostPerceived Likelihood
Časovni zamikTime Delay × Trud in žrtvovanjeEffort & Sacrifice
BistvoKey point
Večina tekmuje samo na zgornjem delu (večji obljubljeni rezultat). Največ vrednosti je pogosto v imenovalcu: hitreje do prvega rezultata in manj dela za stranko (done-for-you namesto do-it-yourself).
Most compete only on the top (a bigger promised result). The most value is often in the denominator: a faster first result and less work for the client (done-for-you instead of do-it-yourself).

Grand Slam ponudbaGrand Slam Offer

Ponudba, ki je tako dobra, da je kupec ne more primerjati s konkurenco, zato cena ni več glavni kriterij.

An offer so good the buyer can't compare it to competitors, so price stops being the main criterion.

  1. Določi sanjski izid stranke.

    Identify the client's dream outcome.

  2. Naštej vse ovire na poti do njega (pred, med in po nakupu).

    List every obstacle on the way (before, during and after purchase).

  3. Vsako oviro preobrni v rešitev (»kako bi …«).

    Turn each obstacle into a solution ("how to …").

  4. Za vsako rešitev določi način dostave: 1-na-1, skupinsko, done-for-you, orodje, predloga.

    Pick a delivery vehicle per solution: 1-on-1, group, done-for-you, tool, template.

  5. Odreži, kar je drago in malo vredno; zloži ostalo v paket (trim & stack).

    Trim what is expensive and low-value; stack the rest.

UporabiZa svojo najboljšo storitev naredi seznam vsaj 15 ovir, ki jih ima stranka. Večina ponudb rešuje le 2–3.

ApplyFor your best service, list at least 15 obstacles the client faces. Most offers solve only 2–3.

Cena in ojačevalci ponudbePricing & Offer Enhancers

Višja cena ni le več denarja: kupi ti boljše stranke, več pozornosti na stranko in boljše rezultate (virtuous cycle of price).

A higher price isn't just more money: it buys you better clients, more attention per client and better results (the virtuous cycle of price).

  • Pomanjkanje (Scarcity): omejeno število mest ali enot.
  • Nujnost (Urgency): rok, kohorta, sprememba cene.
  • Bonusi (Bonuses): vsak rešuje konkretno oviro.
  • Garancije (Guarantees): brezpogojna, pogojna, anti-garancija, implicitna.
  • Ime (Naming): magnet, ciljna oseba, cilj, časovni okvir, kontejner.
  • Scarcity: limited seats or units.
  • Urgency: deadline, cohort, price change.
  • Bonuses: each one solves a specific obstacle.
  • Guarantees: unconditional, conditional, anti-guarantee, implied.
  • Naming: magnetic reason, avatar, goal, interval, container.

UporabiKatera je največja bojazen kupca in katera pogojna garancija bi jo izničila, ne da bi te ogrozila?

ApplyWhat is the buyer's biggest fear, and which conditional guarantee would neutralise it without endangering you?

$100M Leads

The Core Four

Obstajajo samo štirje načini, kako ljudem poveš, da obstajaš. Dve osi: ali te poznajo in ali govoriš eni osebi ali mnogim.

There are only four ways to let people know you exist. Two axes: do they know you, and are you talking to one person or many.

Lead magnetLead Magnets

Brezplačna stvar, ki v celoti reši ozek problem in razkrije naslednjega, ki ga rešuje plačljiva ponudba.

A free thing that fully solves a narrow problem and reveals the next one, which your paid offer solves.

Trije tipiThree types
Razkrije problem (diagnoza, audit), da vzorec (preizkus, demo), ali en korak večjega procesa (predloga, checklist).
Reveal the problem (diagnosis, audit), give a sample (trial, demo), or one step of a bigger process (template, checklist).
TestTest
Bi zanj kdo plačal? Če ne, ni dovolj dober, da bi bil brezplačen.
Would someone pay for it? If not, it isn't good enough to be free.

Pravilo 100 in Več–Bolje–NovoRule of 100 & More–Better–New

Volumen na začetku premaga talent. Vsak dan 100 primarnih akcij (npr. 100 kontaktov ali 100 minut vsebine) dovolj dolgo, da vidiš podatke.

Early on, volume beats talent. Do 100 primary actions every day (e.g. 100 reach-outs or 100 minutes of content) long enough to see real data.

  1. Več (More): najprej povečaj tisto, kar že dela.

    More: first scale what already works.

  2. Bolje (Better): ko volumen stoji, izboljšaj konverzijo.

    Better: when volume plateaus, improve conversion.

  3. Novo (New): nov kanal šele, ko sta prva dva izčrpana.

    New: a new channel only once the first two are exhausted.

UporabiKoliko primarnih akcij si dejansko naredil prejšnji teden?

ApplyHow many primary actions did you actually do last week?

Monetizacija: stranka financira svoj prihodMoney: the client pays for their own acquisition

$100M Money Models

Money Models

Zaporedje ponudb, ki iz ene stranke naredi več denarja, hitreje. Cilj: bruto dobiček prvih 30 dni pokrije strošek pridobitve stranke, idealno dvakrat, da se rast financira sama (client-financed acquisition).

A sequence of offers that turns one customer into more cash, faster. Goal: 30-day gross profit covers the cost of acquiring the customer, ideally twice, so growth funds itself (client-financed acquisition).

Privabljalna ponudbaAttraction offerVstop: brezplačno, poceni ali »kupi X, dobiš Y«.Entry: free, low-cost or "buy X, get Y".
UpsellTakoj po »da«: naslednji logičen korak.Right after a yes: the next logical step.
DownsellPo »ne«: manjša, cenejša ali obročna verzija.After a no: smaller, cheaper or payment plan.
NaročninaContinuityPonavljajoč prihodek, ki zgradi LTV.Recurring revenue that builds LTV.

Ekonomika strankeCustomer Economics

Dve številki, ki ju moraš poznati na pamet.

Two numbers you should know by heart.

LTGP : CAC
≥ 3 : 1 · bruto dobiček skozi življenjsko dobo stranke (Lifetime Gross Profit) proti strošku pridobitve (Customer Acquisition Cost). Pod tem rast boli.
≥ 3 : 1 · lifetime gross profit per customer versus the cost to acquire them. Below that, growth hurts.
PovračiloPayback
≤ 30 dni · kako hitro se strošek pridobitve vrne.
≤ 30 days · how quickly the acquisition cost comes back.

Prodaja: pogovor, ne pritiskSales: a conversation, not pressure

Predavanja in podcastiTalks and podcasts

The CLOSER Framework

Struktura prodajnega pogovora. Stranka naj sama pove, zakaj potrebuje spremembo.

A structure for the sales conversation. The prospect should say themselves why they need a change.

C

Clarify: zakaj so tu, zakaj zdaj.

Clarify why they're here, why now.

L

Label: problem poimenuj, naj ga potrdijo.

Label them with a problem they confirm.

O

Overview past pain: kaj so že poskusili in zakaj ni delovalo.

Overview past pain: what they tried and why it failed.

S

Sell the vacation: prodaj destinacijo (rezultat), ne letala (proces).

Sell the vacation, not the plane flight.

E

Explain away concerns: ugovori so skoraj vedno čas, denar, odločanje ali dvom vase.

Explain away concerns: usually time, money, decision-making or self-doubt.

R

Reinforce: po nakupu potrdi odločitev.

Reinforce the decision after they buy.

Ozko grloThe Constraint

Podjetje ima v vsakem trenutku eno glavno omejitev: premalo leadov, slaba konverzija, slaba dostava ali odhajanje strank. Delo na čemerkoli drugem ima malo učinka.

At any moment a business has one main constraint: too few leads, weak conversion, poor delivery or customers leaving. Working on anything else has little effect.
