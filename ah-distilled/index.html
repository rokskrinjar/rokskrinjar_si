Grand Slam OfferGrand Slam ponudba

An offer so valuable for its price, and so specific to one market, that the buyer has nothing to compare it with. It is the way out of competing on price.Ponudba, ki je za svojo ceno tako vredna in tako prilagojena enemu trgu, da je kupec nima s čim primerjati. Je izhod iz tekmovanja s ceno.

Commodity → Differentiated → Grand Slam (category of one)Blago (commodity) → diferencirana ponudba → Grand Slam (category of one)

Starving CrowdLačna množica

Pick a market that is already hungry for what you sell. The right market makes an average offer work, and the wrong one makes a great offer fail.Izberi trg, ki je že lačen tega, kar prodajaš. Pravi trg poskrbi, da deluje tudi povprečna ponudba, napačen pa pokoplje odlično.

Charge What It's WorthZaračunaj, kar je vredno

The Core FourCore Four

There are only four ways to let people know about you, set by two questions. Do they already know you, and are you reaching one person or many? Pick one, master it, then add the next.Obstajajo samo štirje načini, kako ljudem sporočiš, da obstajaš, določata pa jih dve vprašanji. Te že poznajo in ali nagovarjaš eno osebo ali mnoge? Izberi enega, ga obvladaj, nato dodaj naslednjega.

Known × one-to-one = warm outreach · known × one-to-many = free content · unknown × one-to-one = cold outreach · unknown × one-to-many = paid adsZnani × ena na ena = topli outreach · znani × ena na mnogo = brezplačna vsebina · neznani × ena na ena = hladni outreach · neznani × ena na mnogo = plačani oglasi

Client-Financed AcquisitionPridobivanje, ki ga financira stranka

When what a new customer pays in the first 30 days covers the cost to acquire and serve them, growth funds itself. At 2×, every customer pays for two more.Ko to, kar nova stranka plača v prvih 30 dneh, pokrije strošek njene pridobitve in izvedbe, se rast financira sama. Pri 2× vsaka stranka plača še dve.

30-day gross profit ≥ CAC (goal ≥ 2× CAC); LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1 as a rule of thumb30-dnevni bruto dobiček ≥ CAC (cilj ≥ 2× CAC); LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1 kot pravilo palca

The ConstraintOzko grlo

At any moment a business has one main constraint that limits its growth. Work on anything else has little effect until that one is fixed, and then the constraint moves.Podjetje ima v vsakem trenutku eno glavno omejitev, ki zavira rast. Delo na čemer koli drugem ima malo učinka, dokler je ne odpraviš, nato pa se omejitev premakne.

Find the one constraint, fix it, find the next one. Test: if leads doubled tomorrow, what breaks first?Najdi eno omejitev, jo odpravi, poišči naslednjo. Test: če bi se ti jutri povpraševanja podvojila, kaj bi se prvo zlomilo?

Pricing DecisionsOdločitve o ceni

Price is the lever Alex reaches for first: it costs nothing to change, falls straight to profit, and most owners are underpriced. He tests it by conversion × price and almost never lowers it.Cena je vzvod, po katerem Alex seže najprej: spremeniti jo ne stane nič, razlika gre naravnost v dobiček, večina lastnikov pa je prepoceni. Preizkuša jo z zmnožkom konverzija × cena in je skoraj nikoli ne zniža.

Raise price until conversion × price (or total gross profit) starts to fall, not until unit sales fall.Ceno dviguj, dokler zmnožek konverzija × cena (ali skupni bruto dobiček) ne začne padati, ne dokler ne pade število prodaj.

Hiring & TalentZaposlovanje in talenti

Past the first stage, the speed of a company is the speed at which it recruits. Alex treats talent as the long-term constraint and runs hiring with the same funnel, metrics and standards as customer acquisition.Ko je prva faza mimo, podjetje raste tako hitro, kot zna zaposlovati. Alex vidi talent kot dolgoročno ozko grlo in zaposlovanje vodi z enakim lijakom, metrikami in standardi kot pridobivanje strank.

Who before how. Hire A-players for the smallest skill gap, pay them well, interview far more than feels reasonable.Najprej kdo, potem kako. Zaposli A-igralce z najmanjšo vrzeljo v veščinah, plačaj jih dobro in opravi veliko več razgovorov, kot se zdi smiselno.
