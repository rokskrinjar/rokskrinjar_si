Lead getterji in vsebinaLead Getters & Content

$100M Leads · Lead Getters, Content

Ko sam ne zmoreš več vsega, naj ti leade prinašajo drugi: stranke, zaposleni, agencije in partnerji. Vsebina pa naj dela zate tudi takrat, ko spiš.

When you can no longer do it all yourself, get others to bring you leads: customers, employees, agencies and partners. And let content work for you even while you sleep.

Core Four (topli in hladni kontakti, vsebina, oglasi) lahko izvajaš sam, a tvoj čas je omejen. Naslednja raven je vzvod: ljudje, ki leade prinašajo namesto tebe (lead getters). Vsak tip potrebuje drugačno spodbudo in drugačno »orodje«. Vsebina je pri tem lepilo: z njo greješ občinstvo, ki ga ti ali tvoji partnerji pripeljete.

You can run the Core Four (warm and cold outreach, content, ads) yourself, but your time is finite. The next level is leverage: people who bring leads instead of you (lead getters). Each type needs a different incentive and a different "tool". Content is the glue: it warms up the audience that you or your partners bring in.

Štirje lead getterji in vsebinaThe four lead getters and content

1. Stranke: priporočila1. Customers: referrals

Najcenejši in najbolj prepričljiv vir. Priporočeni lead pride z že izposojenim zaupanjem, zato hitreje kupi in pogosto ostane dlje. Temelj ni nagrada za priporočilo, temveč izkušnja, o kateri stranka sama želi govoriti. Šele nato pridejo vzvodi: prosi ob pravem trenutku (takoj po prvi občuteni zmagi), olajšaj korak (pripravljeno sporočilo, ki ga lahko posreduje), ponudi nekaj obema strani in javno se zahvali.

The cheapest and most convincing source. A referred lead arrives with borrowed trust, so they buy faster and often stay longer. The foundation isn't the referral reward but an experience the customer wants to talk about. Then come the levers: ask at the right moment (right after the first felt win), make the step easy (a ready-made message they can forward), offer something to both sides and thank them publicly.

2. Zaposleni2. Employees

Zaposleni lahko izvajajo iste aktivnosti kot ti: pišejo, kličejo, objavljajo, vodijo oglase. Pogoj je, da imaš proces, ki ga je mogoče naučiti. Pristop je preprost: najprej to delo naredi sam in ga dokumentiraj, nato ga pokaži, potem naj ga oni delajo ob tebi in na koncu sami, ti pa samo meriš. Merila naj bodo aktivnosti in rezultati (število stikov, dogovorjeni sestanki), ne »trud«.

Employees can run the same activities you do: write, call, post, run ads. The condition is a process you can teach. The approach is simple: do the work yourself first and document it, then show them, then they do it next to you, and finally on their own while you only measure. Measure activity and results (contacts made, meetings booked), not "effort".

3. Agencije3. Agencies

Agencija je bližnjica do znanja, ki ga še nimaš, na primer vodenje plačanih oglasov ali hladni e-poštni nagovor v velikem obsegu. Najboljša uporaba: agencijo najameš, da se učiš od nje. Zahtevaj, da ti pokaže, kaj dela in zakaj, zbiraj podatke na svojih računih in sčasoma znanje prenesi v hišo. Agencija, ki ti noče razložiti, kaj počne, je rdeča zastavica.

An agency is a shortcut to know-how you don't have yet, such as running paid ads or high-volume cold email. The best use: hire the agency to learn from it. Ask it to show you what it does and why, keep data on your own accounts and over time bring the skill in-house. An agency that won't explain what it does is a red flag.

4. Partnerji in affiliati4. Affiliates & partners

Ljudje ali podjetja, ki že imajo tvoje idealne stranke, a jim ne prodajajo tega, kar prodajaš ti. Primeri: računovodja za mala podjetja, implementator ERP sistema, lokalna zbornica. Partner tvega svoj ugled, zato mu daj nekaj, kar njegovim strankam pomaga: tvoj lead magnet, brezplačno delavnico, skupni webinar. Poleg provizije (affiliate) mu olajšaj delo s pripravljenimi besedili in jasno sledljivostjo, od kod je prišel lead.

People or companies that already have your ideal customers but don't sell them what you sell. Examples: an accountant for small firms, an ERP implementer, a local chamber of commerce. A partner risks their reputation, so give them something that helps their customers: your lead magnet, a free workshop, a joint webinar. Besides a commission (affiliate), make their job easy with ready-made copy and clear tracking of where each lead came from.

5. Vsebina: kavelj, zadrži, nagradi5. Content: hook, retain, reward

Vsak kos vsebine ima tri naloge. Hook: v prvih sekundah ali prvi vrstici ujame pravo osebo (naslov, vprašanje, presenetljiva številka). Retain: zadrži jo z zgodbo, strukturo ali obljubo, ki jo izpolniš proti koncu. Reward: na koncu mora bralec dobiti nekaj uporabnega, sicer naslednjič ne bo kliknil. Druga zakonitost: daj bistveno več, kot prosiš (give:ask ratio). Večina objav naj samo daje; poziv k akciji naj bo redek in zato slišan. Tretja: vsebine ne izmišljuj, temveč dokumentiraj svoje dejansko delo. Projekti, napake in rešitve iz tedna so neizčrpen vir, hkrati pa delujejo kot dokaz.

Every piece of content has three jobs. Hook: in the first seconds or first line it catches the right person (headline, question, surprising number). Retain: keep them with a story, structure or a promise you pay off near the end. Reward: at the end the reader must get something useful, or they won't click next time. Second principle: give far more than you ask (give:ask ratio). Most posts should only give; the call to action should be rare and therefore heard. Third: don't invent content, document your actual work. The week's projects, mistakes and fixes are an endless source and double as proof.

Examples are made up for illustration.

B2B · analitikaB2B · analytics

BI svetovalec, ki ne more več sam iskati strankA BI consultant who can't keep hunting alone

PredBefore

Vse nove stranke pridejo iz lastnih klicev in občasnih objav na LinkedInu. Ko je projektov veliko, iskanje strank zamre; ko se projekti končajo, ni ničesar v cevovodu.

Every new client comes from his own calls and the odd LinkedIn post. When projects pile up, prospecting stops; when they end, the pipeline is empty.

PoAfter

Po vsakem uspešnem zagonu nadzorne plošče stranko prosi za eno predstavitev kolegu in ji da pripravljen e-mail. Z dvema ERP implementatorjema ima dogovor: njunim strankam ponudi brezplačen pregled podatkov, onadva dobita provizijo. Vsak teden objavi en zapis »kaj smo ta teden popravili v podatkovnem modelu«.

After every successful dashboard go-live he asks the client for one intro to a peer and hands them a ready email. He has a deal with two ERP implementers: he offers their clients a free data review, they get a commission. Every week he posts one note on "what we fixed in the data model this week".

Kaj se je spremenilo: leadi prihajajo iz treh virov, ki ne zahtevajo njegovega časa za vsak stik. Priporočila izkoriščajo trenutek zadovoljstva, partnerja prinašata tuje zaupanje, dokumentirana vsebina pa prav tem leadom pokaže, da zna, preden sploh pride do sestanka.

What changed: leads arrive from three sources that don't need his time per contact. Referrals use the moment of satisfaction, partners lend their trust, and documented content shows those very leads that he knows his stuff before the first meeting.

Nobena aktivnost ne zahteva velikega proračuna. Ključ je, da je vsak korak predpripravljen: bon, scenarij, predavanje. Ljudje priporočajo, kadar jim je to lahko in kadar s tem pomagajo nekomu, ki ga imajo radi.

None of these needs a big budget. The key is that each step is pre-packaged: voucher, script, talk. People refer when it's easy and when it helps someone they care about.

Svetovanje · AIConsulting · AI

Vsebina iz dejanskega delaContent from actual work

PredBefore

»5 razlogov, zakaj je AI prihodnost poslovanja.« Splošno, brez kavlja, brez nagrade. Vsaka tretja objava se konča s »pišite nam za ponudbo«.

"5 reasons AI is the future of business." Generic, no hook, no reward. Every third post ends with "contact us for a quote".

PoAfter

»Ta teden smo obdelavo reklamacij skrajšali s 3 dni na 4 ure. Tukaj je točen potek in napaka, ki nas je stala en dan.« Sledi shema procesa in predloga, ki jo bralec lahko prenese. Poziv k akciji enkrat mesečno.

"This week we cut complaint handling from 3 days to 4 hours. Here's the exact flow and the mistake that cost us a day." Followed by a process diagram and a template the reader can download. Call to action once a month.

Kavelj je konkretna številka, zadrži ga zgodba z napako, nagrada je predloga. Ker objava dokumentira resnično delo, je hkrati dokaz in je ni treba »izmišljati«. Razmerje med dajanjem in prošnjami je močno na strani dajanja.

The hook is a concrete number, the mistake story retains, the template is the reward. Because it documents real work, the post is also proof and doesn't need to be "invented". The give:ask ratio leans heavily to giving.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Nagrada namesto izkušnjeReward instead of experience

    Program priporočil ne reši povprečne storitve. Če stranke niso navdušene, jih tudi popust ne bo prepričal, da tvegajo svoj ugled.

    A referral programme won't rescue an average service. If customers aren't delighted, no discount will make them risk their reputation.

  • Delegiranje nečesa, česar sam nisi delalDelegating what you never did yourself

    Če ne veš, kako izgleda dober hladni nagovor, ga ne moreš naučiti ne preveriti. Najprej naredi sam, nato predaj.

    If you don't know what good cold outreach looks like, you can't teach or check it. Do it yourself first, then hand it over.

  • Agencija kot črna škatlaThe agency as a black box

    Brez dostopa do podatkov in razlage ostaneš odvisen. Ko odideš, ne odnese le rezultatov, temveč tudi vse naučeno.

    Without access to data and explanations you stay dependent. When you part ways, it takes not only the results but all the learning.

  • Partnerju daš le provizijoGiving partners only a commission

    Partner ne bo tvegal odnosa s svojimi strankami zaradi provizije. Potrebuje nekaj, kar njegove stranke cenijo, in pripravljena orodja.

    A partner won't risk client relationships for a commission alone. They need something their clients value, plus ready-made tools.

  • Vsebina, ki samo prosiContent that only asks

    Če vsaka objava prodaja, te ljudje nehajo brati. Brez nagrade na koncu tudi najboljši kavelj deluje samo enkrat.

    If every post sells, people stop reading. Without a reward at the end even the best hook works only once.

Odgovori pisno in za vsak vir zapiši en korak, ki ga narediš ta teden.

Answer in writing and note one step per source you'll take this week.

Stranke: V katerem trenutku je tvoja stranka najbolj zadovoljna? Kaj točno ji takrat daš, da te lažje priporoči?

Customers: At what moment is your client happiest? What exactly do you hand them then to make referring easy?

Zaposleni: Katero aktivnost pridobivanja leadov delaš sam in bi jo lahko dokumentiral ter predal v naslednjih 30 dneh?

Employees: Which lead-getting activity do you do yourself that you could document and hand over in the next 30 days?

Agencije: Katerega znanja nimaš in kaj želiš od agencije naučiti, ne le kupiti?

Agencies: Which skill are you missing, and what do you want to learn from an agency, not just buy?

Partnerji: Naštej tri podjetja, ki že imajo tvoje idealne stranke. Kaj bi njihovim strankam koristilo in ga lahko ponudiš brezplačno?

Partners: List three companies that already have your ideal clients. What would help their clients that you could offer for free?

Vsebina: Kaj si ta teden naredil, kar bi lahko dokumentiral? Kakšen je kavelj, kaj zadrži bralca in kaj je nagrada?

Content: What did you do this week that you could document? What's the hook, what retains, what's the reward?
