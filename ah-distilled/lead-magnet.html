Lead magnetLead Magnets

$100M Leads · Lead Magnets

Brezplačna ponudba, ki tako dobro reši en ozek problem, da človek takoj vidi naslednjega in da ga lahko rešiš samo ti, za plačilo.

A free offer that solves one narrow problem so well that the person immediately sees the next one, and that you're the one to solve it, for a fee.

BistvoThe gist

Lead magnet je celovita rešitev za ozek problem, ki razkrije naslednji problem, ki ga rešuje tvoja plačljiva ponudba. Ni »brošura« ali »e-knjiga s 50 nasveti«. Je majhna, a resnična zmaga. Ljudje dobijo vrednost, ti pa dobiš angažiranega leada (engaged lead), ki ti že zaupa in ve, kaj potrebuje naprej.

A lead magnet is a complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the next problem your paid offer solves. It isn't a "brochure" or "an ebook with 50 tips". It's a small but real win. People get value; you get an engaged lead who already trusts you and knows what they need next.

Trije tipi lead magnetovThree types of lead magnet

1. Razkrij jim problem1. Reveal their problem

Kaj: diagnoza, pregled (audit), test ali kalkulator, ki človeku pokaže, kje ima težavo in koliko ga stane. Zakaj deluje: mnogi ne kupijo, ker ne vedo, da imajo problem, ali pa ga podcenjujejo. Ko ga vidijo črno na belem, s številko, se problem iz »bomo videli« spremeni v »to moramo rešiti«.

What: a diagnosis, audit, test or calculator that shows a person where they have a problem and what it costs them. Why it works: many people don't buy because they don't know they have a problem, or underestimate it. Once they see it in black and white, with a number, it shifts from "we'll see" to "we have to fix this".

Najbolj ustreza: storitvam, kjer je problem nevidni strošek: slabi podatki, izgubljen čas, puščanje denarja.

Best fit: services where the problem is a hidden cost: bad data, lost time, money leaking out.

2. Vzorec ali preizkus glavne ponudbe2. Samples or trials of the core offer

Kaj: delček tega, kar sicer prodajaš: prvi teden, en modul, ena storitev, omejen dostop. Zakaj deluje: ni boljšega dokaza od izkušnje. Če je vzorec dober, se kupec ne sprašuje več »ali deluje«, temveč »koliko stane več«.

What: a piece of what you normally sell: the first week, one module, one service, limited access. Why it works: there's no better proof than experience. If the sample is good, the buyer stops asking "does it work" and starts asking "how much for more".

Pozor: vzorec mora biti dovolj dober, da navduši, a ne toliko, da reši vse. Tudi brezplačen preizkus programske opreme je vzorec.

Watch out: the sample must be good enough to impress, but not so complete that it solves everything. A free software trial is a sample too.

3. En korak večstopenjskega procesa3. One step of a multi-step process

Kaj: če tvoja rešitev obsega več korakov, enega v celoti podariš, običajno prvega. Zakaj deluje: človek naredi pravi napredek, hkrati pa vidi, koliko korakov je še pred njim. Naravno vprašanje je: »Ali lahko ostalo narediš ti?«

What: if your solution has several steps, you give one away in full, usually the first. Why it works: the person makes real progress while also seeing how many steps are still ahead. The natural question is: "Can you do the rest?"

Kako ga narediš: šest korakovHow to build one: six steps

  1. Izberi ozek problem. Ne »boljše poslovanje«, ampak en konkreten, boleč problem, ki ga ima tvoj idealni kupec in je povezan s tvojo plačljivo ponudbo.

    Pick the narrow problem. Not "better business", but one concrete, painful problem your ideal buyer has, linked to your paid offer.

  2. Izberi tip. Razkritje problema, vzorec ali en korak. Vprašaj se, kateri najbolj naravno vodi do naslednjega problema.

    Choose the type. Reveal the problem, sample, or one step. Ask which leads most naturally to the next problem.

  3. Izberi način dostave. Programska oprema (orodje, kalkulator), informacija (vodnik, video, predloga), storitev (pregled, posvet) ali fizični izdelek (vzorec). Isti problem lahko rešiš na več načinov.

    Choose the delivery. Software (tool, calculator), information (guide, video, template), service (audit, consultation) or physical (a sample). The same problem can be solved in several ways.

  4. Naj bo enostaven za uporabo. Čim manj korakov, branja in čakanja. Raje kratek video ali izpolnjena predloga kot 40 strani PDF-ja, ki ga nihče ne prebere.

    Make it easy to consume. As few steps, as little reading and waiting as possible. A short video or a filled-in template beats a 40-page PDF nobody reads.

  5. Naj bo res dober. Test: bi bili ljudje pripravljeni plačati zanj? Če ne, ni dovolj dober. Brezplačna stvar določa, kaj si ljudje mislijo o tvoji plačljivi.

    Make it really good. Test: would people pay for it? If not, it isn't good enough. Your free thing sets what people think of your paid one.

  6. Dodaj jasen poziv k dejanju. Na koncu povej natančno, kaj naj naredijo naslednje (call to action), in razlog, zakaj zdaj: npr. rezerviraj posvet, kjer skupaj rešimo, kar je pokazal pregled.

    Add a clear call to action. At the end, say exactly what to do next and why now: e.g. book a call where we fix together what the audit showed.

B2B · analitikaB2B · analytics

Pregled kakovosti podatkov v ERPERP data-quality audit

PredBefore

»Prenesite našo e-knjigo: 10 trendov poslovne inteligence v 2026.«

"Download our ebook: 10 business intelligence trends for 2026."

PoAfter

»Pošljite izvoz kupcev in artiklov iz ERP. V 48 urah dobite poročilo: koliko dvojnikov, praznih polj in napačnih šifer imate in koliko faktur je zaradi tega napačno razvrščenih.«

"Send an export of customers and items from your ERP. In 48 hours you get a report: how many duplicates, empty fields and wrong codes you have, and how many invoices are misclassified as a result."

Tip: razkritje problema, dostava kot storitev. E-knjiga ni rešila ničesar in ni pokazala, zakaj bi kupec potreboval svetovalca. Pregled reši ozek problem (»kako slabi so naši podatki?«) in razkrije naslednjega: čiščenje podatkov in zanesljivo poročanje, kar je plačljiva ponudba. Polovica dela (skripte za preverjanje) je avtomatizirana, zato je pregled za ponudnika poceni.

Type: reveal the problem, delivered as a service. The ebook solved nothing and didn't show why the buyer would need a consultant. The audit solves a narrow problem ("how bad is our data?") and reveals the next: data clean-up and reliable reporting, which is the paid offer. Half the work (check scripts) is automated, so the audit is cheap for the provider.

Lokalno · pekarnaLocal · bakery

Pekarna, ki želi naročila za podjetjaA bakery that wants corporate orders

PredBefore

Letak s ceno pogostitev, ki ga pustijo na recepciji okoliških podjetij.

A flyer with catering prices left at the reception desks of nearby companies.

PoAfter

Ob ponedeljkih zjutraj prinesejo brezplačno degustacijsko škatlo za 10 ljudi na sestanek ekipe, s kartico: »Naročite redno petkovo dostavo, prvi mesec 15 % ceneje.«

On Monday mornings they bring a free tasting box for 10 people to a team meeting, with a card: "Order a regular Friday delivery, 15% off the first month."

Tip: vzorec, fizična dostava. Letak je zahteval domišljijo, škatla pa ustvari izkušnjo in hkrati razkrije naslednji problem: »Kdo bo poskrbel za prigrizke vsak teden?« Poziv k dejanju je konkreten in časovno omejen.

Type: sample, physical delivery. The flyer required imagination; the box creates an experience and reveals the next problem: "Who sorts out snacks every week?" The call to action is concrete and time-limited.

Svetovanje · AIConsulting · AI

Zemljevid procesov za avtomatizacijoAutomation process map

AI svetovalna družba ponuja brezplačno 45-minutno delavnico, na kateri z vodjo oddelka popišejo pet najpogostejših ročnih opravil in za vsako ocenijo porabljene ure na mesec in primernost za avtomatizacijo. Stranka odide s tabelo prioritet, ki jo lahko uporabi tudi brez svetovalca. To je prvi korak njihovega večstopenjskega procesa (popis → prototip → produkcija → vzdrževanje). Na koncu: »Prvi proces s seznama vam postavimo v treh tednih.«

An AI consultancy offers a free 45-minute workshop where they list a department head's five most common manual tasks and estimate, for each, the hours spent per month and suitability for automation. The client leaves with a priority table they can use even without the consultant. It's step one of their multi-step process (map → prototype → production → maintenance). At the end: "We'll get the first process on your list live in three weeks."

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Preširok problemToo broad a problem

    »Vse o digitalni preobrazbi« ne reši ničesar. Ozek problem, rešen do konca, je vreden več kot širok, rešen do polovice.

    "Everything about digital transformation" solves nothing. A narrow problem solved completely is worth more than a broad one half-solved.

  • Brez povezave s plačljivo ponudboNo link to the paid offer

    Če lead magnet reši problem, ki ne vodi nikamor, dobiš hvaležne ljudi, ne strank. Naslednji problem mora biti tisti, ki ga prodajaš.

    If the lead magnet solves a problem that leads nowhere, you get grateful people, not clients. The next problem has to be the one you sell.

  • Slaba kakovost, ker je »samo brezplačno«Low quality because it's "only free"

    Ljudje sodijo tvojo plačljivo ponudbo po brezplačni. Povprečen lead magnet sporoča povprečno storitev.

    People judge your paid offer by your free one. An average lead magnet signals an average service.

  • Pretežak za uporaboToo hard to consume

    Dolgi dokumenti, zapleteni obrazci in čakanje pomenijo, da večina nikoli ne pride do vrednosti, in s tem ne do naslednjega koraka.

    Long documents, complicated forms and waiting mean most people never reach the value, and therefore never reach the next step.

  • Manjkajoč poziv k dejanjuMissing call to action

    Človek je navdušen, nato pa ne ve, kaj naj stori. Vedno povej naslednji korak in olajšaj ga.

    The person is impressed, then doesn't know what to do. Always state the next step and make it easy.

Delovni listWorksheet

Zasnuj en lead magnet za svojo glavno ponudbo. Odgovori pisno.

Design one lead magnet for your main offer. Answer in writing.

Ozek problem: Kateri en boleč problem ima moj idealni kupec, tik preden potrebuje mojo plačljivo ponudbo?

Narrow problem: What one painful problem does my ideal buyer have just before they need my paid offer?

Tip: Ali ga bolje rešim z razkritjem problema, vzorcem ali enim korakom procesa? Zakaj?

Type: Is it best solved by revealing the problem, a sample, or one step of the process? Why?

Dostava: Programska oprema, informacija, storitev ali fizično? Kaj je najlažje za kupca in najceneje zame?

Delivery: Software, information, service or physical? What's easiest for the buyer and cheapest for me?

Test kakovosti: Bi kdo zanj plačal? Koliko? Kaj bi moral dodati, da bi bil odgovor »da«?

Quality test: Would anyone pay for it? How much? What would I need to add for the answer to be "yes"?

Naslednji problem: Kaj bo kupec ugotovil, ko bo lead magnet uporabil? Ali to vodi naravnost do moje ponudbe?

Next problem: What will the buyer realise after using the lead magnet? Does it lead straight to my offer?

Poziv: Napiši zadnji stavek lead magneta: kaj naj stori zdaj in zakaj ravno zdaj?

Call to action: Write the lead magnet's last sentence: what should they do now, and why now?
