$100M Leads · Cold Outreach

Contacting strangers one by one, by email, DM, phone or text. Response rates are low, so it works through targeted lists, a big free offer, many follow-ups and volume.Neposredno kontaktiranje neznancev, enega po enega, po e-pošti, sporočilih, telefonu ali SMS-u. Odzivnost je nizka, zato deluje s ciljanimi seznami, veliko brezplačno ponudbo, številnimi opomniki in obsegom.

The gist

Cold outreach is the Core Four channel where you contact people who do not know you, one at a time. Its big advantage is control: you choose exactly who hears from you, and you are not limited by how many people you already know. Its big drawback is that strangers ignore most messages, so it only works with volume, persistence and an offer that makes replying worth it.

Engaged leads from cold outreach = Right people (list) × Reason to reply (personalisation + big fast value) × Touches (follow-ups) × Volume

Strangers have no reason to trust you. You earn a reply by showing you did your homework and by offering something useful for free, often a lead magnet. You do not sell in the first message. You get an engaged lead first, and the sale happens later in conversation.

How it works

Build or buy a targeted list

Everything starts with who is on the list. Define your ideal client first: industry, size, role, the problem they have. Then collect contacts that match. You can build a list yourself from public sources, company websites, professional networks and event lists, or buy one from a data provider. Bought lists save time but need checking: remove wrong roles, dead addresses and companies that do not fit. A smaller list of the right people beats a large list of the wrong ones.

Personalise at scale

A message that could be sent to anyone gets treated like spam. Add one or two details that show it was written for this person: a recent project, a machine or certificate mentioned on their site, a post they wrote, a hire they made. The trick is to do this at scale. Research a few facts per contact in a spreadsheet, then drop them into a template. A rule of thumb: more personal means better replies but fewer messages per hour, so find the balance that still gives you volume.

Lead with big, fast value

The offer in the first message has to be worth a stranger's time. The best cold offers give a real result quickly and for free: a short audit, a calculation of what a problem costs them, a sample on their own data, a useful template. This is where lead magnets do their work. Ask for something small in return, such as a reply or a short call.

Many touches and follow-ups

Most replies come after the first message, not from it. People are busy, miss emails and need several reminders. Plan a sequence of follow-ups over days and weeks, each one short and each one adding a new reason to reply. You can also mix channels: an email, then a connection request, then a call.

Automate after it works

Do it by hand first. When you know which list, message and offer get replies, automate the repetitive parts: sending, follow-up scheduling, tracking. Automating a message that does not work only spreads the failure faster. Keep the tone personal even when software sends it.

Channel options

Channel Strength Watch out for
Email Cheap, scalable, easy to automate Deliverability, spam filters
LinkedIn DM Strong for B2B, profile adds credibility Platform limits on volume
Phone Fast feedback, real conversation Time per contact, rejection
SMS High open rates Strict rules, feels intrusive if cold

Regulations matter. In the EU, GDPR and national rules on electronic marketing apply, and they are stricter for consumers than for businesses. Know the rules for your country and channel before you send, offer an easy way to opt out, and keep a record of where each contact came from.

Deliverability basics for email

Use a separate sending domain so your main domain stays safe. Set up the standard authentication records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Warm up new inboxes gradually. Keep volume per inbox moderate, clean your list to avoid bounces, and write plain messages without heavy links or images.

Volume and testing

Cold outreach is a numbers game. Track sent, replies, engaged leads and booked calls. Change one thing at a time (list, first line, offer, follow-up) and keep what wins.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

ERP data consultant targeting manufacturers (B2B)

Before. 200 identical emails to a bought list: "We offer BI and ERP reporting services. Can we schedule a meeting?" No follow-ups. Two replies, both "unsubscribe".

After. A cleaned list of production managers at manufacturers with 50 to 300 employees. First line mentions their ERP system or a recent expansion. Offer: "I'll calculate how many hours your planners lose to manual reports, free, in 20 minutes on a call." Four follow-ups over three weeks.

With 50 emails per working day for 20 days, that is 1,000 contacts a month. At a 3% reply rate he gets 30 replies, and if a third of those book a call, 10 calls a month. The specific list, the personal first line and a free result make replying worth it, and the follow-ups catch everyone who missed the first message.

Plumber reaching property managers (local business)

A plumbing company builds a list of 80 local property managers and building administrators. The owner calls each one, mentions a building they manage, and offers a free inspection of shared pipes in one building. Every manager who says "not now" gets a short email and a call again a month later. Calls are slow, but each signed manager brings recurring work across several buildings.

Common mistakes

  • Selling in the first message. A stranger who gets "buy now" ignores it. Offer value first and earn a conversation.
  • Wrong list. Perfect copy sent to the wrong people still fails. Most cold outreach problems start with the list.
  • Giving up after one message. Without follow-ups you leave most of your replies on the table.
  • Automating too early. Scaling an untested message spreads a bad result and can damage your sending domain.
  • Ignoring rules and deliverability. Breaking privacy rules or landing in spam can end the channel before it starts.

How Alex would apply it

  • He fixes the list first. If replies are low, he checks whether the right people are being contacted before touching the copy.
  • He makes the offer so good that ignoring it feels like a loss. A big, fast, free result beats a request for a meeting.
  • He expects most results from follow-ups. He plans several touches before the first message goes out.
  • He does it by hand, then automates, then hires. He proves the message works himself, then uses software and people to scale it.
  • He treats it as volume plus testing. He sets a daily number of contacts, sticks to it, and improves one variable at a time.
  • He uses cold outreach to buy speed when warm contacts run out, and he keeps the personal tone even at high volume.
  • Questions he would ask: Who exactly is on the list, and how did you choose them? What do you offer in the first message that is worth a reply? How many touches does each person get? How many messages do you send per day, and what is the reply rate? What did you change last week, and what happened?

Worksheet

Answer in writing before you send anything.

  1. List: Describe your ideal contact in one sentence. Where will you find or buy 500 of them?
  2. Personalisation: Which one or two facts can you find for each contact in under two minutes?
  3. Offer: What big, fast, free result can you give in the first message?
  4. Sequence: Write the first message and three follow-ups. What new reason to reply does each one add?
  5. Rules: Which privacy and marketing rules apply to your country and channel, and how will you handle opt-outs?
  6. Numbers: How many contacts per day will you commit to for the next 30 days?

Bistvo

Hladni outreach (cold outreach) je kanal iz Core Four, v katerem enega po enega kontaktiraš ljudi, ki te ne poznajo. Njegova velika prednost je nadzor: sam izbereš, kdo te bo slišal, in nisi omejen s tem, koliko ljudi že poznaš. Velika slabost je, da neznanci večino sporočil prezrejo, zato deluje samo z obsegom, vztrajnostjo in ponudbo, zaradi katere se odgovor splača.

Zavzeti leadi iz hladnega outreacha = pravi ljudje (seznam) × razlog za odgovor (personalizacija + velika hitra vrednost) × stiki (opomniki) × obseg

Neznanci nimajo razloga, da bi ti zaupali. Odgovor si prislužiš tako, da pokažeš, da si se pripravil, in ponudiš nekaj uporabnega brezplačno, pogosto lead magnet. V prvem sporočilu ne prodajaš. Najprej dobiš zavzet lead (engaged lead), prodaja pa se zgodi kasneje v pogovoru.

Kako deluje

Zgradi ali kupi ciljan seznam

Vse se začne pri tem, kdo je na seznamu. Najprej opredeli idealno stranko: panogo, velikost, vlogo, problem, ki ga ima. Nato zberi kontakte, ki ustrezajo. Seznam lahko zgradiš sam iz javnih virov, spletnih strani podjetij, poslovnih omrežij in seznamov udeležencev dogodkov ali ga kupiš pri ponudniku podatkov. Kupljeni seznami prihranijo čas, a jih moraš preveriti: odstrani napačne vloge, mrtve naslove in podjetja, ki ne ustrezajo. Manjši seznam pravih ljudi premaga velikega z napačnimi.

Personaliziraj v obsegu

Sporočilo, ki bi ga lahko poslal komurkoli, dobi obravnavo kot spam. Dodaj eno ali dve podrobnosti, ki pokažeta, da je napisano za to osebo: nedavni projekt, stroj ali certifikat z njihove spletne strani, objavo, ki jo je napisal, novo zaposlitev. Trik je, da to delaš v obsegu. Za vsak kontakt v preglednico zapiši nekaj dejstev, nato jih vstavi v predlogo. Pravilo palca: bolj osebno pomeni boljše odzive, a manj sporočil na uro, zato poišči ravnovesje, ki ti še da obseg.

Začni z veliko, hitro vrednostjo

Ponudba v prvem sporočilu mora biti vredna neznančevega časa. Najboljše hladne ponudbe dajo resničen rezultat hitro in brezplačno: kratek pregled, izračun, koliko jih problem stane, vzorec na njihovih podatkih, uporabno predlogo. Tu delajo lead magneti. V zameno prosi za nekaj majhnega, na primer odgovor ali kratek klic.

Veliko stikov in opomnikov

Večina odgovorov pride po prvem sporočilu, ne iz njega. Ljudje so zaposleni, spregledajo e-pošto in potrebujejo več opomnikov. Načrtuj zaporedje opomnikov (follow-ups) čez dneve in tedne, vsak naj bo kratek in naj doda nov razlog za odgovor. Kanale lahko tudi mešaš: e-pošta, nato povabilo k povezavi, nato klic.

Avtomatiziraj, ko deluje

Najprej delaj ročno. Ko veš, kateri seznam, sporočilo in ponudba prinesejo odgovore, avtomatiziraj ponavljajoče se dele: pošiljanje, razpored opomnikov, sledenje. Avtomatizacija sporočila, ki ne deluje, samo hitreje razširi neuspeh. Ton naj ostane oseben, tudi ko pošilja programska oprema.

Predpisi so pomembni. V EU veljajo GDPR in nacionalna pravila o elektronskem trženju, ki so strožja za potrošnike kot za podjetja. Preden pošiljaš, poznaj pravila za svojo državo in kanal, ponudi enostavno odjavo in vodi evidenco, od kod je prišel posamezen kontakt.

Osnove dostavljivosti e-pošte

Uporabljaj ločeno domeno za pošiljanje, da glavna domena ostane varna. Nastavi standardne zapise za overjanje (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Nove nabiralnike ogrevaj postopoma. Obseg na nabiralnik naj bo zmeren, seznam očisti, da se izogneš zavrnjenim sporočilom, in piši preprosta sporočila brez veliko povezav ali slik.

Obseg in testiranje

Hladni outreach je igra številk. Spremljaj poslana sporočila, odgovore, zavzete leade in dogovorjene klice. Naenkrat spremeni eno stvar (seznam, prvo vrstico, ponudbo, opomnik) in obdrži, kar zmaga.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Svetovalec za ERP podatke, ki cilja proizvajalce (B2B)

Pred. 200 enakih e-pisem na kupljen seznam: »Ponujamo storitve BI in ERP poročanja. Se lahko dogovorimo za sestanek?« Brez opomnikov. Dva odgovora, oba »odjava«.

Po. Očiščen seznam vodij proizvodnje pri proizvajalcih s 50 do 300 zaposlenimi. Prva vrstica omenja njihov ERP sistem ali nedavno širitev. Ponudba: »Brezplačno izračunam, koliko ur vaši planerji izgubijo z ročnimi poročili, v 20 minutah na klicu.« Štirje opomniki v treh tednih.

S 50 e-pismi na delovni dan v 20 dneh je to 1.000 kontaktov na mesec. Pri 3-odstotni odzivnosti dobi 30 odgovorov, in če tretjina rezervira klic, 10 klicev na mesec. Specifičen seznam, osebna prva vrstica in brezplačen rezultat naredijo odgovor vreden truda, opomniki pa ujamejo vse, ki so prvo sporočilo spregledali.

Vodovodar, ki nagovarja upravnike stavb (lokalni posel)

Vodovodno podjetje zgradi seznam 80 lokalnih upravnikov nepremičnin in stavb. Lastnik pokliče vsakega, omeni stavbo, ki jo upravlja, in ponudi brezplačen pregled skupnih cevi v eni stavbi. Vsak upravnik, ki reče »zdaj ne«, dobi kratko e-pismo in čez mesec ponoven klic. Klici so počasni, a vsak pridobljen upravnik prinese ponavljajoče se delo v več stavbah.

Pogoste napake

  • Prodaja v prvem sporočilu. Neznanec, ki dobi »kupi zdaj«, to prezre. Najprej ponudi vrednost in si prisluži pogovor.
  • Napačen seznam. Popolno besedilo, poslano napačnim ljudem, še vedno ne deluje. Večina težav s hladnim outreachom se začne pri seznamu.
  • Odnehanje po enem sporočilu. Brez opomnikov pustiš večino odgovorov na mizi.
  • Prezgodnja avtomatizacija. Širjenje nepreizkušenega sporočila razširi slab rezultat in lahko poškoduje domeno za pošiljanje.
  • Ignoriranje pravil in dostavljivosti. Kršenje pravil o zasebnosti ali pristanek v spamu lahko kanal ukine, še preden zaživi.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Najprej popravi seznam. Če je odgovorov malo, najprej preveri, ali kontaktiraš prave ljudi, šele nato se loti besedila.
  • Ponudbo naredi tako dobro, da je prezreti občutek izgube. Velik, hiter, brezplačen rezultat premaga prošnjo za sestanek.
  • Večino rezultatov pričakuje iz opomnikov. Več stikov načrtuje, še preden gre ven prvo sporočilo.
  • Najprej ročno, nato avtomatizira, nato zaposli. Sam dokaže, da sporočilo deluje, nato ga z orodji in ljudmi razširi.
  • Jemlje ga kot obseg plus testiranje. Določi dnevno število kontaktov, se ga drži in naenkrat izboljšuje eno spremenljivko.
  • S hladnim outreachom kupi hitrost, ko topli kontakti poidejo, in tudi pri velikem obsegu ohrani oseben ton.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Kdo točno je na seznamu in kako si jih izbral? Kaj ponudiš v prvem sporočilu, kar je vredno odgovora? Koliko stikov dobi vsaka oseba? Koliko sporočil pošlješ na dan in kakšna je odzivnost? Kaj si spremenil prejšnji teden in kaj se je zgodilo?

Delovni list

Odgovori pisno, preden karkoli pošlješ.

  1. Seznam: V enem stavku opiši idealen kontakt. Kje boš našel ali kupil 500 takih?
  2. Personalizacija: Katero eno ali dve dejstvi lahko za vsak kontakt najdeš v manj kot dveh minutah?
  3. Ponudba: Kateri velik, hiter, brezplačen rezultat lahko daš že v prvem sporočilu?
  4. Zaporedje: Napiši prvo sporočilo in tri opomnike. Kateri nov razlog za odgovor doda vsak?
  5. Pravila: Kateri predpisi o zasebnosti in trženju veljajo za tvojo državo in kanal ter kako boš urejal odjave?
  6. Številke: Za koliko kontaktov na dan se zavežeš v naslednjih 30 dneh?
