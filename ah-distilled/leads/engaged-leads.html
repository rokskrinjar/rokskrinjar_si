Leads & Engaged LeadsLeadi in zavzeti leadi

$100M Leads · Engaged Leads

A lead is someone you can contact. An engaged lead is someone who has shown interest in what you sell, and those are the only leads that turn into customers.Lead je nekdo, s komer lahko stopiš v stik. Zavzeti lead je nekdo, ki je pokazal zanimanje za to, kar prodajaš, in samo taki leadi postanejo stranke.

The gist

A lead is a person you can get in touch with: a name with a phone number, an email, a LinkedIn profile. An engaged lead is a lead who has done something that shows interest in your stuff. They replied, booked a call, downloaded a lead magnet, came to an event, or asked a question. The difference is action. A list of 5,000 emails is a list of leads. The 40 people who answered your message are engaged leads.

Advertising simply means making something known. A text to an old colleague, a LinkedIn post, a cold email and a paid ad are all advertising. The point of all of it is the same: turn strangers into engaged leads, then turn engaged leads into customers in a conversation.

Strangers → (advertising) → Leads → (they show interest) → Engaged leads → (sales) → Customers

Every engaged lead is paid for, either with time (writing messages, making content) or with money (ads, agencies, list data). So the number that matters is the cost per engaged lead, and the business grows when you get more of them, cheaper, from sources you control.

How it works

Lead vs engaged lead

Lead Engaged lead
What you have Contact details Contact details plus a signal of interest
Example A name on a list of 600 manufacturers A plant manager who replied "send me the audit"
What you do next Make them aware of you Start a sales conversation
Value to the business Low until they act High, they're halfway to buying

The useful habit is to stop counting leads and start counting engaged leads. A big list feels like progress. The only thing that predicts sales is how many people raised their hand.

What counts as "showing interest"

An engaged lead has taken a step toward you. Typical signals, from weaker to stronger:

  1. Opened, liked or watched (weak, often not counted).
  2. Replied to a message or comment.
  3. Downloaded or requested a lead magnet.
  4. Filled in a form with real details.
  5. Booked a call or showed up to one.

Decide in advance which actions count for your business, write it down, and count the same way every week. Otherwise you can't compare channels or months.

Advertising means making known

If nobody knows your offer exists, nobody can buy it. Many service businesses say "we don't advertise, we get work by word of mouth". In Alex's sense, word of mouth is advertising too, just done by someone else and not under your control. Seeing every message, post and ad as advertising helps you treat it as a system you can measure and improve.

You pay with time or money

There is no free lead. Warm outreach and free content cost hours. Cold outreach costs hours plus tools and data. Paid ads cost money. Early on most people have more time than money, so they buy leads with time. Later they swap time for money to grow faster. Both are fine. Pretending leads are free means you never measure what they cost.

Measure cost per engaged lead

A simple version that works for time and money:

  • Track what you spent on a channel in a period (hours × your hourly value, plus money).
  • Count engaged leads from that channel in the same period.
  • Divide.

Then compare with what an engaged lead is worth: how many of them become customers, and how much profit a customer brings over time. If an engaged lead is worth more than it costs, you buy more of them. That is the whole game.

Why this framing comes first

Lead generation is often treated as a creative problem ("we need better content"). Framed as engaged leads, it becomes a numbers problem: how many hands were raised, at what cost, from which source. Numbers can be improved one step at a time.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik consultant counting the wrong thing (B2B analytics)

Before. "This month I added 300 new LinkedIn connections and my newsletter grew to 1,200 subscribers." No idea how many people are interested in buying.

After. He counts only replies asking for his free reporting audit and booked calls. This month: 14 engaged leads. He spent about 20 hours on outreach, valued at €50/hour, so €1,000 in time. Cost per engaged lead: €1,000 ÷ 14 ≈ €71. Four of the 14 became projects worth €6,000 each.

The vanity numbers said nothing about demand. The engaged-lead count shows that one engaged lead is worth about €6,000 × 4 ÷ 14 ≈ €1,714 in revenue against a cost of about €71. The obvious decision is to do much more of the same.

Dental practice (local business)

Source Spend Engaged leads (booked check-up) Cost per engaged lead
Local ad €600 20 €30
Flyers €400 4 €100
Texting past patients 5 hours of staff time (€100) 25 €4

The practice was about to double the flyer budget because flyers "feel like marketing". Measured as engaged leads, texting past patients is 25 times cheaper per booked check-up than flyers, and the local ad is next best.

Common mistakes

  • Counting leads instead of engaged leads. Followers, connections, list size and impressions are not interest. Count actions that show interest.
  • Thinking leads are free. Hours count. If you don't price your time, you can't compare a channel that costs time with one that costs money.
  • Changing the definition every month. If "engaged" means a reply in March and a booked call in April, your numbers lie. Fix the definition.
  • Not knowing what an engaged lead is worth. Without a close rate and a customer value, you can't tell whether a lead is cheap or expensive.
  • Waiting for word of mouth. Referrals are welcome, but a business that only waits has no way to get more when it needs them.

How Alex would apply it

  • He asks for the number of engaged leads first. Before talking about sales skill or pricing, he wants to know how many people raised their hand last month. Too few is the most common reason a small business isn't growing.
  • He treats lead flow as the default bottleneck for businesses that sell well once they're in a conversation. More engaged leads is usually the fastest fix.
  • He prices time. If you spend your evenings on outreach, he counts those hours as a cost and expects you to know what each engaged lead costs you.
  • He compares cost to value. If an engaged lead is clearly worth more than it costs, his answer is to do more, a lot more, before trying anything new.
  • He wants sources you control. Word of mouth and luck are fine, but he builds at least one channel you can turn up on demand.
  • Questions he would ask: How many engaged leads did you get last month, and from where? What counts as engaged for you? What did each one cost in time or money? How many became customers? What is a customer worth to you?

Worksheet

Look at the last 30 days and answer with real numbers, not estimates where you can avoid them.

  1. Definition: Which actions count as "engaged" in your business? Write one clear rule.
  2. Count: How many engaged leads did you get, and from which sources?
  3. Cost: How many hours and how much money went into each source? Put a price on your hour.
  4. Cost per engaged lead: Divide cost by engaged leads for each source. Which is cheapest?
  5. Value: What share of engaged leads became customers, and what is a customer worth? Is each engaged lead worth more than it costs?
  6. Decision: Which one source will you do more of next month, and by how much?

Bistvo

Lead je oseba, s katero lahko stopiš v stik: ime s telefonsko številko, e-naslovom ali profilom na LinkedInu. Zavzeti lead (engaged lead) je lead, ki je naredil nekaj, kar kaže zanimanje za tvoje stvari. Odgovoril je, rezerviral posvet, prenesel lead magnet, prišel na dogodek ali postavil vprašanje. Razlika je v dejanju. Seznam 5.000 e-naslovov je seznam leadov. 40 ljudi, ki so odgovorili na tvoje sporočilo, so zavzeti leadi.

Oglaševanje (advertising) preprosto pomeni, da nekaj narediš znano. SMS nekdanjemu sodelavcu, objava na LinkedInu, hladno e-sporočilo in plačan oglas so vsi oglaševanje. Namen je vedno isti: neznance spremeniti v zavzete leade, zavzete leade pa v pogovoru v stranke.

Neznanci → (oglaševanje) → leadi → (pokažejo zanimanje) → zavzeti leadi → (prodaja) → stranke

Vsak zavzeti lead je plačan, bodisi s časom (pisanje sporočil, ustvarjanje vsebine) bodisi z denarjem (oglasi, agencije, baze podatkov). Zato je pomembna številka strošek na zavzetega leada, podjetje pa raste, ko jih dobiš več, ceneje in iz virov, ki jih nadziraš.

Kako deluje

Lead ali zavzeti lead

Lead Zavzeti lead
Kaj imaš Kontaktne podatke Kontaktne podatke in znak zanimanja
Primer Ime na seznamu 600 proizvodnih podjetij Vodja obrata, ki je odgovoril »pošljite mi pregled«
Kaj narediš naslednje Poskrbiš, da izve zate Začneš prodajni pogovor
Vrednost za podjetje Nizka, dokler ne ukrepa Visoka, do nakupa je že na pol poti

Koristna navada je, da nehaš šteti leade in začneš šteti zavzete leade. Dolg seznam daje občutek napredka. Prodajo napove samo število ljudi, ki so dvignili roko.

Kaj šteje kot »pokazati zanimanje«

Zavzeti lead je naredil korak proti tebi. Tipični znaki, od šibkejših do močnejših:

  1. Odprl, všečkal ali pogledal (šibko, pogosto se ne šteje).
  2. Odgovoril na sporočilo ali komentar.
  3. Prenesel ali zahteval lead magnet.
  4. Izpolnil obrazec s pravimi podatki.
  5. Rezerviral posvet ali nanj prišel.

Vnaprej določi, katera dejanja štejejo v tvojem podjetju, to zapiši in vsak teden štej enako. Sicer ne moreš primerjati kanalov ali mesecev.

Oglaševanje pomeni narediti znano

Če nihče ne ve, da tvoja ponudba obstaja, je nihče ne more kupiti. Veliko storitvenih podjetij pravi »ne oglašujemo, delo dobimo s priporočili«. V Alexovem smislu so tudi priporočila oglaševanje, le da ga dela nekdo drug in ga ne nadziraš ti. Če vsako sporočilo, objavo in oglas vidiš kot oglaševanje, ga lažje obravnavaš kot sistem, ki ga lahko meriš in izboljšuješ.

Plačaš s časom ali denarjem

Brezplačnega leada ni. Topli outreach in brezplačna vsebina stanejo ure. Hladni outreach stane ure ter orodja in podatke. Plačani oglasi stanejo denar. Na začetku ima večina ljudi več časa kot denarja, zato leade kupujejo s časom. Kasneje čas zamenjajo za denar, da rastejo hitreje. Oboje je v redu. Če se pretvarjaš, da so leadi zastonj, nikoli ne izmeriš, koliko stanejo.

Meri strošek na zavzetega leada

Preprosta različica, ki deluje za čas in denar:

  • Zabeleži, koliko si v obdobju porabil za kanal (ure × vrednost tvoje ure, plus denar).
  • Preštej zavzete leade iz tega kanala v istem obdobju.
  • Deli.

Nato primerjaj s tem, koliko je zavzeti lead vreden: koliko jih postane strank in koliko dobička stranka prinese skozi čas. Če je zavzeti lead vreden več, kot stane, jih kupiš več. To je vsa igra.

Zakaj ta pogled pride prvi

Pridobivanje leadov pogosto obravnavamo kot ustvarjalni problem (»potrebujemo boljšo vsebino«). Ko ga pogledaš skozi zavzete leade, postane problem številk: koliko rok se je dvignilo, po kakšni ceni, iz katerega vira. Številke lahko izboljšuješ korak za korakom.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Qlik svetovalec, ki šteje napačno stvar (B2B analitika)

Pred. »Ta mesec sem dodal 300 novih povezav na LinkedInu, novice pa imajo 1.200 naročnikov.« Nima pojma, koliko ljudi bi želelo kupiti.

Po. Šteje samo odgovore, v katerih ljudje prosijo za brezplačen pregled poročanja, in rezervirane posvete. Ta mesec: 14 zavzetih leadov. Za outreach je porabil približno 20 ur po 50 €, torej 1.000 € v času. Strošek na zavzetega leada: 1.000 € ÷ 14 ≈ 71 €. Štirje od 14 so postali projekti po 6.000 €.

Številke za ego niso povedale nič o povpraševanju. Štetje zavzetih leadov pokaže, da je en zavzeti lead vreden približno 6.000 € × 4 ÷ 14 ≈ 1.714 € prihodka ob strošku okoli 71 €. Očitna odločitev je, da dela veliko več istega.

Ordinacija je nameravala podvojiti proračun za letake, ker se letaki »zdijo kot marketing«. Merjeno v zavzetih leadih je SMS nekdanjim pacientom na rezerviran pregled 25-krat cenejši od letakov, lokalni oglas pa je drugi najboljši.

Pogoste napake

  • Štetje leadov namesto zavzetih leadov. Sledilci, povezave, velikost seznama in prikazi niso zanimanje. Štej dejanja, ki kažejo zanimanje.
  • Prepričanje, da so leadi zastonj. Ure štejejo. Če svojega časa ne ovrednotiš, ne moreš primerjati kanala, ki stane čas, s kanalom, ki stane denar.
  • Spreminjanje definicije vsak mesec. Če »zavzet« marca pomeni odgovor, aprila pa rezerviran posvet, te številke zavajajo. Definicijo določi in je ne spreminjaj.
  • Neznana vrednost zavzetega leada. Brez deleža zaprtih poslov in vrednosti stranke ne veš, ali je lead poceni ali drag.
  • Čakanje na priporočila. Priporočila so dobrodošla, a podjetje, ki samo čaka, nima načina, da bi jih dobilo več, ko jih potrebuje.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Najprej vpraša po številu zavzetih leadov. Preden govori o prodajnih veščinah ali ceni, želi vedeti, koliko ljudi je prejšnji mesec dvignilo roko. Premalo je najpogostejši razlog, da majhno podjetje ne raste.
  • Pretok leadov obravnava kot privzeto ozko grlo pri podjetjih, ki dobro prodajajo, ko so enkrat v pogovoru. Več zavzetih leadov je navadno najhitrejša rešitev.
  • Ovrednoti čas. Če večere porabiš za outreach, te ure šteje kot strošek in pričakuje, da veš, koliko te stane vsak zavzeti lead.
  • Primerja strošek z vrednostjo. Če je zavzeti lead očitno vreden več, kot stane, je njegov odgovor: delaj več, veliko več, preden poskusiš kaj novega.
  • Želi vire, ki jih nadziraš. Priporočila in sreča so v redu, a zgradi vsaj en kanal, ki ga lahko ojačaš, ko želiš.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Koliko zavzetih leadov si dobil prejšnji mesec in od kod? Kaj zate pomeni zavzet? Koliko je vsak stal v času ali denarju? Koliko jih je postalo strank? Koliko ti je vredna stranka?

Delovni list

Poglej zadnjih 30 dni in odgovori z dejanskimi številkami, ne z ocenami, kjer se jim lahko izogneš.

  1. Definicija: Katera dejanja v tvojem podjetju štejejo kot »zavzet«? Zapiši eno jasno pravilo.
  2. Štetje: Koliko zavzetih leadov si dobil in iz katerih virov?
  3. Strošek: Koliko ur in koliko denarja je šlo v vsak vir? Svoji uri določi ceno.
  4. Strošek na zavzetega leada: Za vsak vir deli strošek s številom zavzetih leadov. Kateri je najcenejši?
  5. Vrednost: Kolikšen delež zavzetih leadov je postal strank in koliko je vredna stranka? Je vsak zavzeti lead vreden več, kot stane?
  6. Odločitev: Katerega enega vira boš naslednji mesec delal več in za koliko?
