Posting Free ContentObjavljanje brezplačne vsebine

$100M Leads · Posting Free Content

Publishing valuable content to people who already follow you, so that trust builds and warm leads come to you. One-to-many to people who know you.Objavljanje uporabne vsebine za ljudi, ki ti že sledijo, da se gradi zaupanje in topli leadi pridejo k tebi. Eden-mnogim, ljudem, ki te poznajo.

The gist

Free content is the Core Four channel where you speak to many people at once who already know you: followers, subscribers, readers. You give value in public, they consume it, and over time some of them raise their hand and ask to buy. It is slow to start and compounds, because each piece keeps working long after you post it.

Content that gets leads = Hook (they start) + Retain (they stay) + Reward (they got value, so they come back) + occasional asks (they know what to do next)

The core rule is to give far more than you ask. Most pieces should only give. When you do ask, the ask is clear and specific, and because you rarely ask, people listen. The best raw material is your own real work: what you built, fixed, learned or got wrong this week.

How it works

Hook: get the right person to start

The hook is whatever makes someone stop and pay attention. It has three parts.

  • Topic. What the piece is about. Good topics come from your work, your clients' problems, and things that already performed well for others in your space. Pick a topic your ideal client already cares about.
  • Headline. The first line, title or first seconds. Headlines that work tend to be recent, relevant to the reader, specific, a bit unusual, or about a tension or conflict. A concrete number or result helps.
  • Format. How it is packaged: a short text post, a carousel, a long article, a short video, a long video, a podcast. The same topic can be told in several formats.

Retain: keep their attention to the end

Once they start, you have to keep them. Simple structures do most of the work: a numbered list, step-by-step instructions, or a story with a problem, a struggle and a resolution. Open a question early and answer it near the end. Cut anything that does not serve the promise of the hook.

Reward: pay off the promise

At the end the reader must feel the time was well spent. They learned something, got a template, saw a result or laughed. Reward is what earns the next click. A great hook with no reward works once and then trains people to ignore you.

Give, give, give, ask

Value builds goodwill. Asks spend it. Keep most pieces pure value, then make an occasional ask with a clear call to action: "comment DATA and I'll send the checklist", "book a call via the link", "reply if you want the template". An ask can also live inside a value piece, as a short line at the end. If you never ask, you are building an audience, not leads.

Use your real work as the source

You do not need to invent content. Document what you already do: the project, the problem, the fix, the numbers before and after, the mistake that cost a day. This is endless, it is honest, and it doubles as proof that you can do the job.

Consistency and volume before polish

Early on, output matters more than perfection. You learn what works by posting a lot and watching what gets attention. Polish comes later, once you know which topics and formats your audience responds to. A rule of thumb: post on a fixed schedule you can keep for months.

One platform first

Pick one platform where your ideal clients already spend time and build there until it works. Then repurpose the winning pieces to other platforms. Spreading thin across five channels at the start means none of them gets enough volume to tell you anything.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik consultant on LinkedIn (B2B analytics)

Before. Once a month: "We are a BI partner offering dashboards and analytics. Contact us for a quote." A handful of likes, no conversations.

After. Three posts a week, each documenting real work. "A manufacturer's margin report took 40 minutes to load. Here are the three data model changes that got it to 4 seconds." Ends with a short diagram. Once every two weeks: "Comment MODEL and I'll send the checklist I use to review data models."

The hook is a concrete before/after number, the list of three changes retains, and the diagram is the reward. The ask is rare and specific, and it hands over a lead magnet, so every comment is an engaged lead he can follow up with.

Bakery on Instagram (local business)

Element Weak Strong
Hook "Fresh bread today" "Why our sourdough takes 36 hours"
Retain One photo Short video of each step, start to finish
Reward Nothing to take away One tip for keeping bread fresh at home
Ask Every post says "come visit" Once a week: "order by Thursday for Saturday pickup"

The bakery films what it already does every night. The process itself is the content, and the weekly ask gives followers one clear action.

AI automation consultant (newsletter)

A consultant sends a weekly email with one automation she built that week: the problem, the tools, the time saved and one screenshot. Every fourth issue ends with an offer of a free process review for three companies. The other three issues only give.

Common mistakes

  • Asking in every piece. If every post sells, people stop reading. Goodwill runs out faster than you build it.
  • Never asking. Followers are not leads. Without a clear call to action you collect likes, not conversations.
  • Hook without reward. Clickbait works once. If the piece does not pay off the headline, the next headline gets ignored.
  • Polishing before posting. Spending a week on one perfect video means you learn nothing. Volume teaches you what works.
  • Starting on every platform at once. Each platform gets too little output to show results, and you burn out.

How Alex would apply it

  • He treats content as a long game. He expects it to compound over months and years, and he does not judge it on a few weeks of results.
  • He leans hard on giving. He would rather give away his best ideas for free, because the people who act on them become the best buyers.
  • He builds every piece on Hook, Retain, Reward. If a piece underperforms, he checks which of the three failed instead of blaming the algorithm.
  • He documents instead of inventing. He turns what he and his companies actually do into content, which keeps it honest and credible.
  • He picks one platform, maxes it out, then goes wide. He repurposes pieces that already worked rather than starting from scratch elsewhere.
  • He pushes volume first, then improves. More output, then better output, then new platforms, in that order.
  • Questions he would ask: Who exactly is this for, and what do they care about right now? What did you do this week that they would want to see? What is the hook, what keeps them, and what do they walk away with? How often do you ask, and what exactly do you ask them to do? How many pieces have you posted on this platform so far?

Worksheet

Answer in writing for your main platform.

  1. Platform: Where do your ideal clients already spend time? Commit to that one for the next 90 days.
  2. Topics: List ten things you did or solved at work in the last month that a client would find useful.
  3. Hook, Retain, Reward: For your best topic, write the headline, the structure that keeps people reading, and what they take away.
  4. Ask: What is the one clear action you want readers to take? Which lead magnet will you offer in exchange?
  5. Rhythm: How many pieces per week can you keep up for months? How often will you include an ask?

Bistvo

Brezplačna vsebina je kanal iz Core Four, v katerem hkrati nagovarjaš veliko ljudi, ki te že poznajo: sledilce, naročnike, bralce. Vrednost daješ javno, oni jo uporabljajo, in sčasoma nekateri dvignejo roko in želijo kupiti. Začetek je počasen, učinek pa se sešteva, ker vsak kos deluje še dolgo po objavi.

Vsebina, ki prinaša leade = Hook (začnejo) + Retain (ostanejo) + Reward (dobili so vrednost, zato se vrnejo) + občasne prošnje (vedo, kaj narediti naprej)

Osnovno pravilo je, da daš bistveno več, kot prosiš. Večina kosov naj samo daje. Ko prosiš, je prošnja jasna in konkretna, in ker prosiš redko, te ljudje slišijo. Najboljši material je tvoje resnično delo: kaj si ta teden zgradil, popravil, se naučil ali zamočil.

Kako deluje

Hook: pravo osebo pripravi do začetka

Kavelj (hook) je vse, kar nekoga ustavi in pritegne njegovo pozornost. Ima tri dele.

  • Tema. O čem je kos. Dobre teme pridejo iz tvojega dela, iz problemov tvojih strank in iz stvari, ki so drugim v tvojem prostoru že dobro delovale. Izberi temo, ki tvojo idealno stranko že zanima.
  • Naslov. Prva vrstica, naslov ali prve sekunde. Delujejo naslovi, ki so aktualni, relevantni za bralca, konkretni, malo nenavadni ali govorijo o napetosti oziroma sporu. Pomaga konkretna številka ali rezultat.
  • Format. Kako je zapakirano: kratka besedilna objava, karusel, daljši članek, kratek video, dolg video, podcast. Isto temo lahko poveš v več formatih.

Retain: zadrži pozornost do konca

Ko začnejo, jih moraš zadržati. Večino dela opravijo preproste strukture: oštevilčen seznam, navodila po korakih ali zgodba s problemom, bojem in razpletom. Vprašanje odpri zgodaj in odgovori nanj proti koncu. Izreži vse, kar ne služi obljubi iz kavlja.

Reward: izpolni obljubo

Na koncu mora bralec čutiti, da je bil čas dobro porabljen. Nekaj se je naučil, dobil predlogo, videl rezultat ali se nasmejal. Nagrada (reward) je tisto, kar si zasluži naslednji klik. Odličen kavelj brez nagrade deluje enkrat, potem pa ljudi nauči, da te preskočijo.

Daj, daj, daj, prosi (give, give, give, ask)

Vrednost gradi dobro voljo. Prošnje jo porabljajo. Večina kosov naj bo čista vrednost, občasno pa dodaj prošnjo z jasnim pozivom k akciji (call to action): »napiši PODATKI v komentar in ti pošljem kontrolni seznam«, »rezerviraj klic prek povezave«, »odgovori, če želiš predlogo«. Prošnja lahko živi tudi znotraj vrednostnega kosa, kot kratka vrstica na koncu. Če nikoli ne prosiš, gradiš občinstvo, ne leadov.

Vir naj bo tvoje resnično delo

Vsebine ni treba izmišljati. Dokumentiraj, kar že počneš: projekt, problem, rešitev, številke pred in po, napako, ki te je stala en dan. To je neizčrpno, je pošteno in hkrati dokazuje, da delo znaš opraviti.

Doslednost in obseg pred izpiljenostjo

Na začetku je količina pomembnejša od popolnosti. Kaj deluje, se naučiš tako, da veliko objavljaš in opazuješ, kaj pritegne pozornost. Izpiljenost pride kasneje, ko veš, na katere teme in formate se tvoje občinstvo odziva. Pravilo palca: objavljaj po stalnem urniku, ki ga zmoreš držati mesece.

Najprej ena platforma

Izberi eno platformo, kjer tvoje idealne stranke že preživljajo čas, in tam gradi, dokler ne deluje. Šele nato zmagovalne kose predelaj za druge platforme. Če se na začetku razpršiš na pet kanalov, nobeden ne dobi dovolj obsega, da bi ti kaj povedal.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Qlik svetovalec na LinkedInu (B2B analitika)

Pred. Enkrat mesečno: »Smo BI partner in ponujamo nadzorne plošče in analitiko. Pišite nam za ponudbo.« Nekaj všečkov, nobenega pogovora.

Po. Tri objave na teden, vsaka dokumentira resnično delo. »Poročilo o marži pri proizvajalcu se je nalagalo 40 minut. Tu so tri spremembe podatkovnega modela, s katerimi smo prišli na 4 sekunde.« Na koncu kratka shema. Vsaka dva tedna: »Napiši MODEL v komentar in ti pošljem kontrolni seznam, s katerim pregledujem podatkovne modele.«

Kavelj je konkretna številka pred in po, seznam treh sprememb zadrži bralca, shema je nagrada. Prošnja je redka in konkretna ter preda lead magnet, zato je vsak komentar zavzet lead (engaged lead), ki mu lahko sledi.

Pekarna na Instagramu (lokalni posel)

Element Šibko Močno
Hook »Danes svež kruh« »Zakaj naš kruh z drožmi nastaja 36 ur«
Retain Ena fotografija Kratek video vsakega koraka od začetka do konca
Reward Nič za odnesti En nasvet, kako kruh doma ostane svež
Prošnja Vsaka objava pravi »pridite k nam« Enkrat tedensko: »naroči do četrtka za prevzem v soboto«

Pekarna snema, kar že vsako noč počne. Sam proces je vsebina, tedenska prošnja pa sledilcem da eno jasno dejanje.

Svetovalka za AI avtomatizacijo (e-novice)

Svetovalka vsak teden pošlje e-pismo z eno avtomatizacijo, ki jo je ta teden zgradila: problem, orodja, prihranjen čas in en posnetek zaslona. Vsaka četrta številka se konča s ponudbo brezplačnega pregleda procesa za tri podjetja. Ostale tri številke samo dajejo.

Pogoste napake

  • Prošnja v vsakem kosu. Če vsaka objava prodaja, te ljudje nehajo brati. Dobra volja poide hitreje, kot jo gradiš.
  • Nikoli ne prosiš. Sledilci niso leadi. Brez jasnega poziva k akciji zbiraš všečke, ne pogovorov.
  • Kavelj brez nagrade. Vaba za klike deluje enkrat. Če kos ne izpolni naslova, bo naslednji naslov prezrt.
  • Piljenje pred objavo. Če en teden porabiš za en popoln video, se ne naučiš ničesar. Obseg te nauči, kaj deluje.
  • Začetek na vseh platformah hkrati. Vsaka dobi premalo objav, da bi pokazala rezultate, ti pa izgoriš.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Vsebino jemlje kot dolgo igro. Pričakuje, da se bo seštevala mesece in leta, in je ne ocenjuje po nekaj tednih rezultatov.
  • Močno se nagiba k dajanju. Svoje najboljše ideje raje podari, ker ljudje, ki jih uporabijo, postanejo najboljši kupci.
  • Vsak kos zgradi na Hook, Retain, Reward. Če kos ne deluje, preveri, kateri od treh delov je odpovedal, namesto da krivi algoritem.
  • Dokumentira namesto izmišljuje. V vsebino spremeni to, kar on in njegova podjetja res počnejo, zato ostane poštena in verodostojna.
  • Izbere eno platformo, jo izčrpa, nato gre širše. Predela kose, ki so že delovali, namesto da drugje začne iz nič.
  • Najprej poveča obseg, nato izboljšuje. Več objav, potem boljše objave, potem nove platforme, v tem vrstnem redu (more, better, new).
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Za koga točno je to in kaj ga trenutno skrbi? Kaj si naredil ta teden, kar bi želeli videti? Kaj je kavelj, kaj jih zadrži in kaj odnesejo? Kako pogosto prosiš in kaj točno jim rečeš, naj naredijo? Koliko kosov si na tej platformi do zdaj objavil?

Delovni list

Odgovori pisno za svojo glavno platformo.

  1. Platforma: Kje tvoje idealne stranke že preživljajo čas? Zaveži se tej eni za naslednjih 90 dni.
  2. Teme: Naštej deset stvari, ki si jih v zadnjem mesecu naredil ali rešil pri delu in bi bile stranki koristne.
  3. Hook, Retain, Reward: Za najboljšo temo napiši naslov, strukturo, ki bralca zadrži, in kaj odnese.
  4. Prošnja: Katero eno jasno dejanje želiš od bralcev? Kateri lead magnet ponudiš v zameno?
  5. Ritem: Koliko kosov na teden zmoreš držati mesece? Kako pogosto boš dodal prošnjo?
