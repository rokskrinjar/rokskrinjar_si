The book in one page

Most small businesses don't have a sales problem or a product problem. They have too few people interested in what they sell. $100M Leads is about fixing that. Its central idea is that a business grows by getting more engaged leads: people who have shown interest in your offer by replying, booking, downloading or otherwise raising a hand.

Advertising, in this book, simply means making something known. Every message, post and ad counts. You pay for each engaged lead with time or money, so the work is to get more of them, at a cost that is lower than what a customer is worth.

The book follows the order in which you build lead flow:

  1. Understand leads. Count engaged leads, not contacts. Give a lead magnet: a complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the next problem your paid offer solves.
  2. Pick one of the Core Four. Warm outreach, free content, cold outreach or paid ads. Choose based on whether you have more time or more money.
  3. Do it at volume. The Rule of 100 sets a daily minimum of primary actions until the channel is predictable.
  4. Improve it. Do more of what works, make it better, and only then try something new.
  5. Add lead getters. Once you know what works, get customers, employees, agencies and affiliates to bring leads too.

How Alex would use this book

  • When a business with a good offer isn't growing, he checks lead flow first: how many engaged leads came in last month, and from where.
  • He would start a business with no money on warm outreach and a strong lead magnet, before content or ads.
  • He insists on one channel at a time, run at volume every day, until it produces leads predictably.
  • He measures cost per engaged lead against what a customer is worth, and spends more wherever the first is clearly lower.
  • He adds lead getters only after he can teach the process, because others can only repeat what is already working.

Knjiga na eni strani

Večina malih podjetij nima težav s prodajo ali izdelkom. Ima premalo ljudi, ki jih zanima to, kar prodaja. $100M Leads govori o tem, kako to popraviš. Osrednja ideja je, da podjetje raste, ko dobi več zavzetih leadov (engaged leads): ljudi, ki so pokazali zanimanje za tvojo ponudbo, ker so odgovorili, rezervirali, prenesli ali kako drugače dvignili roko.

Oglaševanje v tej knjigi preprosto pomeni narediti nekaj znano. Šteje vsako sporočilo, objava in oglas. Vsakega zavzetega leada plačaš s časom ali denarjem, zato je naloga, da jih dobiš več in po ceni, ki je nižja od vrednosti stranke.

Knjiga sledi vrstnemu redu, po katerem gradiš pretok leadov:

  1. Razumi leade. Štej zavzete leade, ne kontaktov. Ponudi lead magnet: celovito rešitev ozkega problema, ki razkrije naslednji problem, ki ga rešuje tvoja plačljiva ponudba.
  2. Izberi enega od Core Four. Topli outreach, brezplačna vsebina, hladni outreach ali plačani oglasi. Izbiraj glede na to, ali imaš več časa ali več denarja.
  3. Delaj ga v velikem obsegu. Pravilo 100 (Rule of 100) določa dnevni minimum osnovnih dejanj, dokler kanal ne postane predvidljiv.
  4. Izboljšuj ga. Delaj več tega, kar deluje, naredi to bolje in šele nato poskusi nekaj novega.
  5. Dodaj lead getterje. Ko veš, kaj deluje, naj leade prinašajo tudi stranke, zaposleni, agencije in partnerji.

Kako bi knjigo uporabil Alex

  • Ko podjetje z dobro ponudbo ne raste, najprej preveri pretok leadov: koliko zavzetih leadov je prišlo prejšnji mesec in od kod.
  • Podjetje brez denarja bi začel s toplim outreachom in močnim lead magnetom, pred vsebino ali oglasi.
  • Vztraja pri enem kanalu naenkrat, ki ga delaš vsak dan v velikem obsegu, dokler ne prinaša leadov predvidljivo.
  • Strošek na zavzetega leada primerja z vrednostjo stranke in več porabi povsod, kjer je prvi očitno nižji.
  • Lead getterje doda šele, ko zna proces naučiti druge, ker lahko drugi ponovijo samo to, kar že deluje.
