$100M Leads · Lead Getters

Other people who bring you leads: customers, employees, agencies, and affiliates and partners. They turn the Core Four from something you do into something that runs without your time per lead.Drugi ljudje, ki ti prinašajo leade: stranke, zaposleni, agencije ter affiliati in partnerji. Core Four spremenijo iz nečesa, kar delaš sam, v nekaj, kar teče brez tvojega časa za vsak lead.

The gist

You can run the Core Four yourself: warm outreach, free content, cold outreach and paid ads. But your time is limited, and at some point you are the bottleneck. Lead getters are the people who do the advertising for you or send you their audience. They are how a business keeps getting leads when the founder stops doing it all.

Lead getters = Customers (referrals) + Employees (run the Core Four) + Agencies (rent skills) + Affiliates & partners (other people's audiences)

Each type needs a different incentive and a different setup. Customers refer when they got great results and it is easy to do. Employees need a process they can follow. Agencies are best used to learn a skill. Affiliates and partners need something that helps their audience and a fair reward for sending it to you.

How it works

Customers: referrals

Referred leads arrive with trust borrowed from someone they know, so they tend to buy faster and stay longer. The foundation is results: customers talk about what impressed them. A referral programme cannot save an average service. Once delivery is strong, use these levers:

  • Ask. Most happy customers never refer because nobody asked. Ask at the moment they feel the win, such as right after a first result or a good review.
  • Make it easy. Give them a ready message, a link, or a voucher they can forward in one step.
  • Reward. Offer something to the referrer, the new customer, or both. It can be money, a discount, a bonus, or public recognition.
  • Deliver great results. This keeps the loop going. Each new customer who gets results becomes a source of the next referral.

Employees

Employees can run the same Core Four activities you do: send outreach, make content, run ads. The condition is that you have a process. The usual path is to do the work yourself first and write it down, then show them, then watch them do it, and finally let them run it while you track the numbers. Measure activity and results (contacts made, engaged leads, calls booked), not effort. If you have never done the activity yourself, you cannot train or judge the person doing it.

Agencies

An agency is a way to rent skills you do not have yet, such as running paid ads or high-volume cold email. Alex's view is that the best use of an agency is to learn from it. Ask them to explain what they do and why. Keep the ad accounts, data and assets in your own name. Over time, bring the skill in-house with an employee who learned from the agency. An agency that refuses to show you what it does keeps you dependent.

Affiliates and partners

These are people or businesses who already have your ideal customers but do not sell what you sell. Examples: an accountant for small firms, an ERP implementer, a software vendor, an industry association, an influencer in your niche. They put their reputation on the line when they recommend you, so give them:

  • something that helps their audience, such as your lead magnet, a free workshop or a joint webinar
  • a clear reward, usually a commission per lead or per sale
  • ready-made materials: copy, links, a short description of who is a good fit
  • clean tracking, so everyone knows which lead came from whom

Start with a few partners who fit well and make them successful before adding more.

When to add lead getters

Lead getters multiply what already works. Get leads yourself first, so you know which message and offer convert. Then hand that proven system to others. Adding lead getters to a broken offer just spreads the problem.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

BI consultant who can no longer prospect alone (B2B analytics)

Before. Every new client comes from his own calls and the odd LinkedIn post. When projects pile up, prospecting stops. When they end, the pipeline is empty.

After. After each successful dashboard go-live he asks the client for one intro to a peer and hands over a ready email. Two ERP implementers offer their clients his free data review and get a commission on every signed project. A part-time assistant runs his cold outreach from a documented script.

Leads now come from three sources that do not need his time per contact. Referrals use the moment of satisfaction, partners lend their trust, and the assistant runs a process he already proved himself.

Physiotherapy clinic (local business)

Lead getter How they activate it
Customers After the last session the patient gets two "first assessment for a friend" vouchers. When one is used, the patient gets a free check-up.
Employees The receptionist calls past patients with a short "how is the knee?" script and tracks calls and bookings.
Partners Two local gyms and a running club get a free injury-prevention talk for their members.
Agency A local agency runs the clinic's ads for six months and trains the receptionist on the setup.

None of this needs a big budget. Each step is ready to use: voucher, script, talk. People refer when it is easy and when it helps someone they care about.

Common mistakes

  • Reward before results. A referral bonus does not fix average service. If customers are not impressed, no discount will make them risk their reputation.
  • Delegating what you never did. If you do not know what good outreach looks like, you cannot teach it or check it. Do it first, then hand it over.
  • The agency as a black box. Without access to data and explanations you stay dependent, and when the agency leaves, the learning leaves with it.
  • Giving partners only a commission. Partners protect their relationships. They need something their audience values and tools that make sending leads easy.
  • Never asking. Most customers would refer if asked at the right moment. Not asking leaves the cheapest leads unused.

How Alex would apply it

  • He makes delivery referral-worthy first. He sees referrals as a result of great outcomes, and fixes the product before building a referral programme.
  • He builds the ask into the process. Asking for referrals happens at a fixed point, right after the customer feels the win, every time.
  • He does it, documents it, then delegates it. No activity goes to an employee before he has proven it works and written down how.
  • He hires agencies to learn, then brings skills in-house. He keeps accounts and data in his own name and treats the agency fee as tuition.
  • He goes where the audience already is. Partners and affiliates with his ideal customers are the fastest way to reach many people who will trust him.
  • He judges every lead getter on numbers. Engaged leads, customers and cost per customer by source, so he knows which to grow.
  • Questions he would ask: Do your customers get results good enough to talk about? When do you ask for referrals, and what do you hand them? Which lead-getting activity have you proven and documented well enough to hand over? What are you learning from your agency? Who already has your customers, and what would help their audience?

Worksheet

Answer in writing and note one step per source for this week.

  1. Customers: At what moment is your client happiest? What exactly will you hand them then to make referring easy?
  2. Employees: Which lead-getting activity do you do yourself that you could document and hand over in the next 30 days?
  3. Agencies: Which skill are you missing, and what do you want to learn from an agency, not just buy?
  4. Partners: List three businesses that already serve your ideal clients. What could you offer their clients for free?
  5. Reward: What will each type of lead getter get for sending you a lead, and how will you track it?

Bistvo

Core Four lahko izvajaš sam: topli outreach, brezplačno vsebino, hladni outreach in plačane oglase. A tvoj čas je omejen in v neki točki postaneš ozko grlo. Lead getterji so ljudje, ki oglašujejo namesto tebe ali ti pošljejo svoje občinstvo. Z njimi podjetje še naprej dobiva leade, ko ustanovitelj neha delati vsega sam.

Lead getterji = stranke (priporočila) + zaposleni (izvajajo Core Four) + agencije (najem znanja) + affiliati in partnerji (tuja občinstva)

Vsak tip potrebuje drugačno spodbudo in drugačno postavitev. Stranke priporočajo, ko so dobile odlične rezultate in je priporočanje enostavno. Zaposleni potrebujejo proces, ki mu lahko sledijo. Agencije so najbolj koristne, ko se od njih naučiš veščino. Affiliati in partnerji potrebujejo nekaj, kar pomaga njihovemu občinstvu, in pošteno nagrado, da ga pošljejo k tebi.

Kako deluje

Stranke: priporočila (referrals)

Priporočeni leadi pridejo z zaupanjem, izposojenim od nekoga, ki ga poznajo, zato navadno kupijo hitreje in ostanejo dlje. Temelj so rezultati: stranke govorijo o tem, kar jih je navdušilo. Program priporočil ne more rešiti povprečne storitve. Ko je izvedba močna, uporabi te vzvode:

  • Prosi. Večina zadovoljnih strank nikoli ne priporoči, ker jih nihče ni prosil. Prosi v trenutku, ko občutijo zmago, na primer takoj po prvem rezultatu ali dobri oceni.
  • Olajšaj. Daj jim pripravljeno sporočilo, povezavo ali bon, ki ga posredujejo v enem koraku.
  • Nagradi. Ponudi nekaj priporočitelju, novi stranki ali obema. Lahko je denar, popust, bonus ali javno priznanje.
  • Dostavi odlične rezultate. To ohranja krog. Vsaka nova stranka, ki dobi rezultate, postane vir naslednjega priporočila.

Zaposleni

Zaposleni lahko izvajajo iste aktivnosti Core Four kot ti: pošiljajo outreach, ustvarjajo vsebino, vodijo oglase. Pogoj je, da imaš proces. Običajna pot je, da delo najprej narediš sam in ga zapišeš, nato jim ga pokažeš, nato jih opazuješ, ko ga delajo, in na koncu ga vodijo sami, ti pa spremljaš številke. Meri aktivnosti in rezultate (število stikov, zavzeti leadi, dogovorjeni klici), ne truda. Če aktivnosti nikoli nisi delal sam, osebe, ki jo dela, ne moreš ne izučiti ne oceniti.

Agencije

Agencija je način, da najameš veščine, ki jih še nimaš, na primer vodenje plačanih oglasov ali hladno e-pošto v velikem obsegu. Alex meni, da je agencijo najbolje uporabiti za učenje. Prosi jih, naj razložijo, kaj delajo in zakaj. Oglasne račune, podatke in gradiva imej na svoje ime. Sčasoma veščino prenesi v hišo z zaposlenim, ki se je naučil od agencije. Agencija, ki ti noče pokazati, kaj počne, te drži v odvisnosti.

Affiliati in partnerji

To so ljudje ali podjetja, ki že imajo tvoje idealne stranke, a ne prodajajo tega, kar prodajaš ti. Primeri: računovodja za mala podjetja, implementator ERP sistema, ponudnik programske opreme, panožno združenje, vplivnež v tvoji niši. Ko te priporočijo, tvegajo svoj ugled, zato jim daj:

  • nekaj, kar pomaga njihovemu občinstvu, na primer tvoj lead magnet, brezplačno delavnico ali skupni webinar
  • jasno nagrado, navadno provizijo na lead ali na prodajo
  • pripravljena gradiva: besedila, povezave, kratek opis, kdo je primerna stranka
  • čisto sledljivost, da vsi vedo, kateri lead je prišel od koga

Začni z nekaj partnerji, ki se dobro ujemajo, in jih naredi uspešne, preden dodaš nove.

Kdaj dodati lead getterje

Lead getterji pomnožijo tisto, kar že deluje. Najprej leade pridobivaj sam, da veš, katero sporočilo in ponudba prodajata. Šele nato ta preizkušen sistem predaj drugim. Če lead getterje dodaš pokvarjeni ponudbi, problem samo razširiš.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

BI svetovalec, ki ne more več sam iskati strank (B2B analitika)

Pred. Vse nove stranke pridejo iz lastnih klicev in občasnih objav na LinkedInu. Ko je projektov veliko, iskanje strank zamre. Ko se končajo, je cevovod prazen.

Po. Po vsakem uspešnem zagonu nadzorne plošče stranko prosi za eno predstavitev kolegu in ji izroči pripravljen e-mail. Dva ERP implementatorja svojim strankam ponudita njegov brezplačen pregled podatkov in dobita provizijo za vsak podpisan projekt. Asistentka s krajšim delovnim časom vodi njegov hladni outreach po dokumentiranem scenariju.

Leadi zdaj prihajajo iz treh virov, ki ne zahtevajo njegovega časa za vsak stik. Priporočila izkoristijo trenutek zadovoljstva, partnerja posodita svoje zaupanje, asistentka pa izvaja proces, ki ga je sam že dokazal.

Fizioterapevtska ambulanta (lokalni posel)

Lead getter Kako ga aktivirajo
Stranke Po zadnji terapiji pacient dobi dva bona »prvi pregled za prijatelja«. Ko je eden unovčen, dobi pacient brezplačno kontrolo.
Zaposleni Receptorka kliče bivše paciente s kratkim scenarijem »kako je koleno?« in spremlja klice in naročila.
Partnerji Dva lokalna fitnesa in tekaški klub dobijo brezplačno predavanje o preprečevanju poškodb za svoje člane.
Agencija Lokalna agencija šest mesecev vodi oglase ambulante in receptorko nauči nastavitev.

Nič od tega ne zahteva velikega proračuna. Vsak korak je pripravljen za uporabo: bon, scenarij, predavanje. Ljudje priporočajo, kadar je to enostavno in kadar s tem pomagajo nekomu, ki jim je mar zanj.

Pogoste napake

  • Nagrada pred rezultati. Bonus za priporočilo ne popravi povprečne storitve. Če stranke niso navdušene, jih noben popust ne prepriča, da tvegajo svoj ugled.
  • Delegiranje nečesa, česar nisi nikoli delal. Če ne veš, kako izgleda dober outreach, ga ne moreš naučiti ne preveriti. Najprej naredi sam, nato predaj.
  • Agencija kot črna škatla. Brez dostopa do podatkov in razlag ostaneš odvisen, in ko agencija odide, odide z njo tudi vse naučeno.
  • Partnerju daš samo provizijo. Partnerji varujejo svoje odnose. Potrebujejo nekaj, kar njihovo občinstvo ceni, in orodja, ki pošiljanje leadov olajšajo.
  • Nikoli ne prosiš. Večina strank bi priporočila, če bi jih prosil ob pravem trenutku. Brez prošnje ostanejo najcenejši leadi neizkoriščeni.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Najprej poskrbi, da je izvedba vredna priporočila. Priporočila vidi kot posledico odličnih izidov, zato najprej popravi izdelek, šele nato gradi program priporočil.
  • Prošnjo vgradi v proces. Prošnja za priporočilo se zgodi na stalni točki, takoj ko stranka občuti zmago, vsakič.
  • Naredi, dokumentira, nato delegira. Nobena aktivnost ne gre k zaposlenemu, preden ni sam dokazal, da deluje, in zapisal, kako.
  • Agencije najame za učenje, nato veščine prenese v hišo. Račune in podatke ima na svoje ime, plačilo agenciji pa jemlje kot šolnino.
  • Gre tja, kjer občinstvo že je. Partnerji in affiliati z njegovimi idealnimi strankami so najhitrejša pot do veliko ljudi, ki mu bodo zaupali.
  • Vsakega lead getterja oceni po številkah. Zavzeti leadi, stranke in cena na stranko po viru, da ve, katerega naj razvija.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Ali tvoje stranke dobijo rezultate, o katerih je vredno govoriti? Kdaj prosiš za priporočila in kaj jim izročiš? Katero aktivnost pridobivanja leadov si dokazal in dokumentiral dovolj dobro, da jo lahko predaš? Kaj se učiš od svoje agencije? Kdo že ima tvoje stranke in kaj bi pomagalo njegovemu občinstvu?

Delovni list

Odgovori pisno in za vsak vir zapiši en korak, ki ga narediš ta teden.

  1. Stranke: V katerem trenutku je tvoja stranka najbolj zadovoljna? Kaj točno ji takrat izročiš, da te lažje priporoči?
  2. Zaposleni: Katero aktivnost pridobivanja leadov delaš sam in bi jo lahko dokumentiral ter predal v naslednjih 30 dneh?
  3. Agencije: Katere veščine nimaš in kaj se želiš od agencije naučiti, ne le kupiti?
  4. Partnerji: Naštej tri podjetja, ki že služijo tvojim idealnim strankam. Kaj bi lahko brezplačno ponudil njihovim strankam?
  5. Nagrada: Kaj bo vsak tip lead getterja dobil za poslan lead in kako boš to spremljal?
