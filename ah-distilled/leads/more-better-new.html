More, Better, NewVeč, bolje, novo

$100M Leads · More, Better, New

The order in which to grow lead flow. Do more of what works, then make it work better, and only then add something new.Vrstni red, po katerem večaš pritok leadov. Delaj več tega, kar deluje, nato to delaj bolje, šele potem dodaj nekaj novega.

The gist

Once a channel starts producing leads, there are three ways to get more out of it. You can do more of it, do it better, or try something new. The order matters. Each step is more expensive and less certain than the one before, so you only move on when the previous one has stopped paying.

More (raise volume) → Better (raise conversion) → New (add channels, platforms, offers)

Most founders get the order backwards. They get bored with what works and jump to a new platform, a new funnel or a new offer, which puts them back at zero. Shiny new things feel like progress. Usually they are a way to avoid the repetitive work that is actually producing results.

How it works

More: do more of what already works

What. Raise the volume of the activity that is producing leads. From 100 reach-outs a day to 200. From one video a week to three. A higher ad budget on the ad that already converts.

Why first. It is the cheapest and most predictable growth. You are repeating something already proven, so there is little new to learn and little risk.

How. Increase in steps and watch whether results per unit hold, such as replies per 100 messages or cost per lead. Keep going until one of these happens:

  1. Results per unit drop noticeably (you are running out of good prospects, or the audience is saturated).
  2. You run out of your own time, and the next step is hiring or delegating to keep volume growing.
  3. You hit a real limit of the platform or the market.

Getting help to keep doing more (an assistant who builds lists, a second person sending messages, an editor for your content) still counts as More.

Better: improve conversion at each step

What. Get more out of the same volume by improving each step of the path from first contact to sale.

Why second. Better is harder than More. It takes testing and patience, and many tests fail. But once volume is at its limit, conversion is where the growth is.

How. Map the steps and their rates, then improve the weakest one.

Step Typical metric
Contact → response Reply rate, click rate, hook retention
Response → engaged lead Share who show interest in the offer
Engaged lead → booked call Booking rate
Booked call → showed up Show rate
Call → sale Close rate

Change one thing at a time: the first line, the offer, the hook, the audience, the reminder sequence. Compare it with the previous version on a large enough sample. Keep the winner and test the next change. Because the steps multiply, a modest gain at two or three steps compounds into a large gain at the end.

New: only then add something new

What. A new channel from the Core Four, a new platform within the same channel, a new audience or a new lead magnet or offer.

Why last. New means starting over. You face a new learning curve, a new period of weak results and a new set of things to track. It also splits your attention from the channel that already pays.

How. Treat it as a fresh start. Apply the Rule of 100 to the new channel, then run More, Better, New on it as well. Keep the existing channel running while you do. It is what funds the experiment.

Why the order protects you

Each step produces the information and cash the next step needs. More gives you volume and data. Better uses that data to raise conversion. The cash and skill from both pay for New. Skip a step and you are guessing with less data and less money.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

AI automation consultant for manufacturers (B2B)

Before. Cold outreach to production managers works: 100 messages a day bring about 5 calls a week. After a month he gets restless and starts planning a podcast, a webinar series and a LinkedIn ad campaign at once.

After. More: an assistant builds lists and volume goes to 200 a day, bringing about 10 calls a week. Better: he tests first lines and finds that naming the prospect's ERP system doubles replies. New: after four months, with client stories in hand, he starts posting case studies on LinkedIn.

More doubled calls with no new skills. Better doubled replies on top of that. By the time he added content, he had stories worth posting and a channel paying for the time. Starting the podcast in month two would have put all three at risk.

Dental practice (local business)

Phase What they do Result
More The whitening ad that works goes from €100 to €200 a day Leads double at a similar cost per lead
Better A text reminder the day before each appointment Show-ups rise from 60% to 80%
New Only now they test ads for dental implants A second offer, built on what they learned

The Better step had nothing to do with the ad. Lifting show-ups from 60% to 80% means a third more patients from the same leads (80 ÷ 60 ≈ 1.33). That is just as much a conversion gain as a better headline.

Common mistakes

  • Jumping to New because you're bored. Repeating what works feels dull. That dullness is usually a sign it works. Keep going.
  • Optimising before you have volume. Testing headlines on 30 messages is noise. Better only works once More has given you enough data.
  • Stopping More too early. Most people quit raising volume long before results per unit actually drop. Check the numbers before deciding you've hit the ceiling.
  • Changing many things at once. If you change offer, copy and audience together, you can't tell what helped, and you can't repeat it.
  • Dropping the old channel for the new one. The working channel funds the experiment. Turn it off and the new channel has to pay for itself from day one.

How Alex would apply it

  • His default answer to "what should I do next?" is more. He first asks whether the current activity can simply be doubled, and usually it can.
  • He sees boredom as the enemy of growth. Many owners abandon what works because it's repetitive. He keeps the boring thing going and often scales it further.
  • He moves to Better only when volume stalls, and then works on the weakest step in the funnel, one change at a time.
  • He is sceptical of new platforms, funnels and offers while an existing channel still has room to grow. New comes last.
  • He buys volume with people before he buys novelty. Hiring someone to do more of the proven work comes before adding a channel.
  • When he does go New, he restarts the Rule of 100 on it and keeps the old channel running.
  • Questions he would ask: What's working right now? Can you do twice as much of it next month? What happens to results per unit as you scale? Which step in your funnel converts worst? What's the one change you'll test there first? Why do you want to add something new, and what will it cost the channel that works?

Worksheet

Use your last 30 days of numbers. Where you have none, start with the Rule of 100 first.

  1. What works: Which one activity brings most of your leads today, and what are its numbers per 100 actions?
  2. More: What would it take to double that volume next month (time, money, an assistant)? What would make you stop?
  3. Funnel: Write down each step from contact to sale with its conversion rate. Which is the weakest?
  4. Better: What single change will you test on the weakest step, and how large a sample do you need before you decide?
  5. New: What new thing are you tempted by? Write down what must be true about More and Better before you're allowed to start it.

Bistvo

Ko kanal začne prinašati leade, lahko iz njega dobiš več na tri načine. Lahko delaš več (More), lahko delaš bolje (Better) ali poskusiš nekaj novega (New). Vrstni red je pomemben. Vsak korak je dražji in manj gotov od prejšnjega, zato se premakneš naprej šele, ko se prejšnji neha izplačevati.

Več (dvigni volumen) → Bolje (dvigni konverzijo) → Novo (dodaj kanale, platforme, ponudbe)

Večina podjetnikov vrstni red obrne. Naveliča se tega, kar deluje, in skoči na novo platformo, nov lijak ali novo ponudbo, kar jih vrne na nič. Bleščeče novosti dajejo občutek napredka. Navadno so način, kako se izogniti ponavljajočemu se delu, ki v resnici prinaša rezultate.

Kako deluje

Več: delaj več tega, kar že deluje

Kaj. Povečaj volumen aktivnosti, ki prinaša leade. S 100 kontaktov na dan na 200. Z enega videa na teden na tri. Večji proračun za oglas, ki že konvertira.

Zakaj prvo. To je najcenejša in najbolj predvidljiva rast. Ponavljaš nekaj, kar je že dokazano, zato je novega učenja malo in tveganja prav tako.

Kako. Povečuj postopoma in opazuj, ali se rezultati na enoto ohranjajo, na primer odgovori na 100 sporočil ali cena na lead. Nadaljuj, dokler se ne zgodi nekaj od tega:

  1. Rezultati na enoto opazno padejo (zmanjkuje dobrih potencialnih strank ali pa je občinstvo nasičeno).
  2. Zmanjka ti lastnega časa in je naslednji korak zaposlitev ali predaja dela, da volumen še raste.
  3. Dosežeš resnično mejo platforme ali trga.

Pomoč, s katero še naprej delaš več (asistent, ki pripravlja sezname, druga oseba, ki pošilja sporočila, montažer za tvojo vsebino), še vedno spada pod Več.

Bolje: izboljšaj konverzijo na vsakem koraku

Kaj. Iz istega volumna dobiš več, ker izboljšaš vsak korak poti od prvega stika do prodaje.

Zakaj drugo. Bolje je težje kot Več. Zahteva testiranje in potrpežljivost, veliko testov pa ne uspe. A ko je volumen na meji, je rast v konverziji.

Kako. Zapiši korake in njihove deleže, nato izboljšaj najšibkejšega.

Korak Tipičen kazalnik
Stik → odziv Delež odgovorov, delež klikov, zadržanje pri hooku
Odziv → zavzeti lead Delež tistih, ki pokažejo zanimanje za ponudbo
Zavzeti lead → dogovorjen posvet Delež rezervacij
Dogovorjen posvet → prihod Delež prihodov (show rate)
Posvet → prodaja Delež zaključenih prodaj (close rate)

Spremeni eno stvar naenkrat: prvo vrstico, ponudbo, hook, ciljno skupino, zaporedje opomnikov. Primerjaj s prejšnjo različico na dovolj velikem vzorcu. Ohrani zmagovalca in preizkusi naslednjo spremembo. Ker se koraki med sabo množijo, se zmerno izboljšanje na dveh ali treh korakih na koncu sešteje v veliko rast.

Novo: šele nato dodaj nekaj novega

Kaj. Nov kanal iz Core Four, nova platforma znotraj istega kanala, nova ciljna skupina ali nov lead magnet oziroma ponudba.

Zakaj zadnje. Novo pomeni začeti znova. Čaka te nova krivulja učenja, novo obdobje šibkih rezultatov in nov nabor stvari za merjenje. Poleg tega razdeli tvojo pozornost s kanalom, ki se že izplača.

Kako. Obravnavaj ga kot nov začetek. Na novem kanalu uporabi Pravilo 100 (Rule of 100), nato tudi na njem izpelji Več, bolje, novo. Obstoječi kanal medtem teče naprej. Prav on financira poskus.

Zakaj te vrstni red ščiti

Vsak korak ustvari informacije in denar, ki ju potrebuje naslednji. Več ti da volumen in podatke. Bolje te podatke uporabi za višjo konverzijo. Denar in veščina iz obeh plačata Novo. Če korak preskočiš, ugibaš z manj podatki in manj denarja.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Svetovalec za AI avtomatizacijo v proizvodnji (B2B)

Pred. Hladni outreach vodjem proizvodnje deluje: 100 sporočil na dan prinese približno 5 posvetov na teden. Po mesecu postane nemiren in začne hkrati načrtovati podcast, serijo webinarjev in oglaševalsko kampanjo na LinkedInu.

Po. Več: asistent pripravlja sezname in volumen naraste na 200 na dan, kar prinese približno 10 posvetov na teden. Bolje: preizkuša prve vrstice in ugotovi, da omemba ERP sistema potencialne stranke podvoji odgovore. Novo: po štirih mesecih, ko ima zgodbe strank, začne na LinkedInu objavljati primere.

Več je podvojil posvete brez novih veščin. Bolje je na to podvojil še odgovore. Ko je dodal vsebino, je imel zgodbe, vredne objave, in kanal, ki je plačeval njegov čas. Če bi podcast začel v drugem mesecu, bi ogrozil vse troje.

Zobozdravstvena ordinacija (lokalno podjetje)

Faza Kaj naredijo Rezultat
Več Oglas za beljenje, ki deluje, dvignejo s 100 € na 200 € na dan Leadi se podvojijo ob podobni ceni na lead
Bolje SMS opomnik dan pred vsakim terminom Prihodi zrastejo s 60 % na 80 %
Novo Šele zdaj preizkusijo oglase za zobne vsadke Druga ponudba, zgrajena na tem, kar so se naučili

Korak Bolje ni imel nič z oglasom. Dvig prihodov s 60 % na 80 % pomeni tretjino več pacientov iz istih leadov (80 ÷ 60 ≈ 1,33). To je enako pravo izboljšanje konverzije kot boljši naslov.

Pogoste napake

  • Skok na Novo iz dolgčasa. Ponavljanje tega, kar deluje, je videti dolgočasno. Ta dolgčas je navadno znak, da deluje. Nadaljuj.
  • Optimiziranje, preden imaš volumen. Testiranje naslovov na 30 sporočilih je šum. Bolje deluje šele, ko ti Več da dovolj podatkov.
  • Prezgodnja ustavitev Več. Večina ljudi neha večati volumen precej preden rezultati na enoto res padejo. Preden se odločiš, da si dosegel strop, preveri številke.
  • Spreminjanje več stvari naenkrat. Če hkrati spremeniš ponudbo, besedilo in ciljno skupino, ne veš, kaj je pomagalo, in tega ne moreš ponoviti.
  • Opustitev starega kanala zaradi novega. Kanal, ki deluje, financira poskus. Če ga izklopiš, se mora novi kanal izplačati že od prvega dne.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Njegov privzeti odgovor na »kaj naj naredim naslednje?« je več. Najprej vpraša, ali je trenutno aktivnost mogoče preprosto podvojiti, in navadno jo je.
  • Dolgčas vidi kot sovražnika rasti. Mnogi lastniki opustijo, kar deluje, ker je ponavljajoče se. On dolgočasno stvar ohrani in jo pogosto še skalira.
  • Na Bolje preide šele, ko volumen zastane, in takrat dela na najšibkejšem koraku lijaka, eno spremembo naenkrat.
  • Do novih platform, lijakov in ponudb je skeptičen, dokler ima obstoječi kanal še prostor za rast. Novo pride zadnje.
  • Volumen kupi z ljudmi, preden kupi novost. Zaposlitev nekoga, ki dela več dokazanega dela, pride pred dodajanjem kanala.
  • Ko gre v Novo, na njem znova zažene Pravilo 100 in stari kanal pusti teči.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Kaj trenutno deluje? Ali lahko naslednji mesec narediš dvakrat toliko? Kaj se zgodi z rezultati na enoto, ko večaš? Kateri korak v lijaku najslabše konvertira? Katero eno spremembo boš tam najprej preizkusil? Zakaj želiš dodati nekaj novega in koliko bo to stalo kanal, ki deluje?

Delovni list

Uporabi številke zadnjih 30 dni. Če jih nimaš, najprej začni s Pravilom 100.

  1. Kaj deluje: Katera ena aktivnost ti danes prinese največ leadov in kakšne so njene številke na 100 dejanj?
  2. Več: Kaj bi potreboval, da naslednji mesec ta volumen podvojiš (čas, denar, asistenta)? Kaj bi te ustavilo?
  3. Lijak: Zapiši vsak korak od stika do prodaje z njegovim deležem konverzije. Kateri je najšibkejši?
  4. Bolje: Katero eno spremembo boš preizkusil na najšibkejšem koraku in kako velik vzorec potrebuješ, preden se odločiš?
  5. Novo: Katera novost te mika? Zapiši, kaj mora veljati za Več in Bolje, preden jo smeš začeti.
