$100M Leads · Paid Ads

Paying a platform to show your message to many strangers at once. The fastest way to scale leads, but only once your offer works and your money model can pay back the cost of each customer.Plačaš platformi, da tvoje sporočilo hkrati pokaže veliko neznancem. Najhitrejša pot do več leadov, a šele ko ponudba deluje in ko money model povrne strošek vsake stranke.

The gist

Paid ads are the Core Four channel where you reach many people who do not know you, by paying a platform for their attention. Of the four channels it scales the fastest: once you find an ad that works, more money buys more leads. It is also the channel where mistakes cost the most, because a bad ad simply burns budget faster.

Ad = Callout (who it is for) + Value (what they get) + Call to Action (what to do now)

An ad is a hook attached to an offer. The hook gets the right stranger to stop. The offer, often a lead magnet, gives them a reason to act. Success is measured in money: what one engaged lead costs, what one customer costs, and whether your money model earns that back fast enough to keep buying more.

How it works

Callout: tell the right people it is for them

The callout makes the right person feel addressed. It can name a group ("production managers in metalworking"), a situation ("still building month-end reports in Excel?"), a place ("dog owners in Ljubljana") or a desire. Strangers scroll fast, so the callout has to work in the first line or the first seconds. A clear callout also filters out the wrong people, which keeps your cost per engaged lead honest.

Value: what they get

Say what they get if they respond, in the result they want. This is usually a lead magnet or an attraction offer: a free audit, a guide, a trial, a calculator, a low-priced first step. The stronger the value relative to what you ask, the cheaper your leads.

Call to Action: what to do now

Tell them exactly what to do next: book a call, download the guide, claim the offer, send a message. One action per ad. Reasons to act now, such as limited spots or a deadline, help when they are real.

Ads are hooks plus offers

The same Hook, Retain, Reward thinking as in free content applies. Most of the work is testing hooks: headlines, images, the first seconds of video. A strong hook on a weak offer gets clicks and no leads. A strong offer with a weak hook never gets seen. Test both.

Start small, find a winner, scale

  1. Start with a small budget you can afford to lose while learning.
  2. Run several variants of hook and creative against the same offer.
  3. Track cost per engaged lead, then cost per customer.
  4. Turn off losers, keep winners, and make new variants of the winners.
  5. Raise spend on what works step by step, and watch whether cost per customer holds.

Expect most ads to lose. You are looking for the few that win.

Measure what matters

Track the full chain: spend, engaged leads, booked calls, customers, and the gross profit each customer brings. Cost per click is a means; cost to acquire a customer (CAC) is what counts. Platform costs vary by market, audience and season, so measure your own numbers instead of relying on general benchmarks.

Can your money model pay for it?

Ads work long term only if each customer earns back what it cost to get them. The best case is client-financed acquisition: the customer's early purchases pay for the ad spend that found them, so you can keep spending without running out of cash. Alex's rule of thumb in Money Models is that profit in the first 30 days should be at least twice the cost to acquire the customer. If your money model does not recover CAC quickly, fix the offer and the upsells before you raise the budget.

Speed to lead

Strangers from ads are cold and distracted. Contact them fast after they respond, and have a follow-up sequence ready. Slow follow-up wastes leads you already paid for.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

SaaS for production planning (B2B)

Before. A LinkedIn ad with the company logo: "Smart planning software for modern manufacturing. Learn more." One version, a large monthly budget from day one. Plenty of clicks, almost no demos.

After. Five variants on a small budget, each with a different callout: "Planners in metalworking: how many hours did rescheduled orders cost you last month?" Value: a free calculator that shows lost hours using their own order numbers. Call to action: "Get your number in 5 minutes." After two weeks the best variant gets its budget raised, the rest are turned off.

Suppose the winning variant brings an engaged lead for 40 €, one in ten leads becomes a customer, so CAC is 400 €. If a customer pays 300 € setup plus 200 € per month and delivery costs are close to zero, the first 30 days bring about 500 € of gross profit. That covers CAC once, not twice, so before scaling the team adds a paid onboarding package to push 30-day gross profit past 800 €.

Dental clinic (local business)

Part Weak Strong
Callout "Dental clinic Smile" "Parents in Kranj with kids aged 6 to 12"
Value "Quality care for the whole family" "Free check-up and fluoride treatment for kids this month"
Call to action "Visit us" "Book a slot online in 30 seconds"

The strong version targets one group, offers a clear free result, and gives a single fast action. The clinic earns back the cost through the families who stay for regular care.

Common mistakes

  • Scaling before you have a winner. A big budget on an untested ad buys expensive lessons. Find the winner small first.
  • Running ads before the offer works. If you cannot convert warm leads, cold paid leads will convert worse.
  • Measuring clicks instead of customers. Cheap clicks that never buy are expensive. Track cost per engaged lead and per customer.
  • No callout. An ad for everyone is an ad for no one. The right people do not stop.
  • Ignoring payback. If customers take a year to repay CAC, ads drain cash. Fix the money model before adding spend.

How Alex would apply it

  • He wants a proven offer before paid traffic. He gets results with warm outreach or content first, then pours money on what already converts.
  • He tests many hooks against one offer. He expects most creatives to fail and treats finding the winners as the job.
  • He judges ads on customer economics. His question is whether the customer pays back the ad cost fast, ideally inside the first 30 days.
  • He fixes the money model to afford more ads. Upsells and a stronger front-end offer let him outspend competitors on acquisition.
  • He scales winners step by step, watching cost per customer, and cuts anything that does not pay back.
  • He insists on fast follow-up, because a paid lead that waits goes cold.
  • Questions he would ask: Who is the ad for, and does the first line say so? What do they get for responding? What does one engaged lead cost, and what does one customer cost? How much gross profit does a customer bring in the first 30 days? How many variants have you tested, and which one won?

Worksheet

Answer in writing before you spend the first euro.

  1. Callout: Who exactly is this ad for? Write three different first lines that would make them stop.
  2. Value: What lead magnet or attraction offer will they get for responding?
  3. Action: What is the single next step, and how fast can you follow up after it?
  4. Budget: What small test budget can you afford to lose while you find a winner?
  5. Economics: What is the most you can pay for one customer, given their 30-day gross profit?
  6. Proof: Has this offer already converted with warm leads? If not, test it there first.

Bistvo

Plačani oglasi (paid ads) so kanal iz Core Four, v katerem dosežeš veliko ljudi, ki te ne poznajo, tako da platformi plačaš za njihovo pozornost. Od štirih kanalov se najhitreje širi: ko najdeš oglas, ki deluje, več denarja kupi več leadov. Je pa tudi kanal, kjer napake stanejo največ, ker slab oglas preprosto hitreje kuri proračun.

Oglas = callout (za koga je) + vrednost (kaj dobijo) + poziv k akciji (kaj naj naredijo zdaj)

Oglas je kavelj, pripet na ponudbo. Kavelj ustavi pravega neznanca. Ponudba, pogosto lead magnet, mu da razlog, da ukrepa. Uspeh se meri v denarju: koliko stane en zavzet lead, koliko stane ena stranka in ali money model ta strošek povrne dovolj hitro, da lahko kupuješ naprej.

Kako deluje

Callout: pravim ljudem povej, da je zanje

Callout poskrbi, da se prava oseba počuti nagovorjeno. Lahko poimenuje skupino (»vodje proizvodnje v kovinski industriji«), situacijo (»mesečna poročila še vedno delate v Excelu?«), kraj (»lastniki psov v Ljubljani«) ali željo. Neznanci hitro drsijo, zato mora callout delovati v prvi vrstici ali prvih sekundah. Jasen callout tudi odfiltrira napačne ljudi, zato cena na zavzet lead ostane poštena.

Vrednost: kaj dobijo

Povej, kaj dobijo, če se odzovejo, v obliki rezultata, ki si ga želijo. Navadno je to lead magnet ali privabljalna ponudba (attraction offer): brezplačen pregled, vodič, preizkus, kalkulator, poceni prvi korak. Čim močnejša je vrednost v primerjavi s tem, kar zahtevaš, tem cenejši so leadi.

Poziv k akciji (call to action): kaj naj naredijo zdaj

Natančno jim povej, kaj naj naredijo naprej: rezervirajo klic, prenesejo vodič, prevzamejo ponudbo, pošljejo sporočilo. En oglas, eno dejanje. Razlogi za takojšnje ukrepanje, na primer omejeno število mest ali rok, pomagajo, če so resnični.

Oglasi so kavlji plus ponudbe

Velja enako razmišljanje Hook, Retain, Reward kot pri brezplačni vsebini. Večina dela je testiranje kavljev: naslovov, slik, prvih sekund videa. Močan kavelj na šibki ponudbi prinese klike brez leadov. Močna ponudba s šibkim kavljem ni nikoli videna. Testiraj oboje.

Začni majhno, najdi zmagovalca, povečaj

  1. Začni z majhnim proračunom, ki si ga lahko privoščiš izgubiti med učenjem.
  2. Za isto ponudbo poženi več različic kavlja in kreative.
  3. Spremljaj ceno na zavzet lead, nato ceno na stranko.
  4. Poražence izklopi, zmagovalce obdrži in naredi nove različice zmagovalcev.
  5. Postopoma povečuj porabo za to, kar deluje, in opazuj, ali cena na stranko zdrži.

Pričakuj, da bo večina oglasov izgubila. Iščeš tistih nekaj, ki zmagajo.

Meri, kar šteje

Spremljaj celotno verigo: porabo, zavzete leade, dogovorjene klice, stranke in bruto dobiček, ki ga prinese posamezna stranka. Cena na klik je sredstvo, šteje strošek pridobitve stranke (CAC). Stroški platform se razlikujejo po trgu, občinstvu in sezoni, zato meri svoje številke, namesto da se zanašaš na splošna merila.

Ali tvoj money model to plača?

Oglasi dolgoročno delujejo samo, če vsaka stranka povrne, kar je stala njena pridobitev. Najboljši primer je pridobivanje, ki ga financira stranka (client-financed acquisition): zgodnji nakupi stranke plačajo oglase, ki so jo našli, zato lahko porabljaš naprej, ne da bi ti zmanjkalo denarja. Alexovo pravilo palca iz Money Models je, da naj bo dobiček v prvih 30 dneh vsaj dvakrat večji od stroška pridobitve stranke. Če tvoj money model CAC ne povrne hitro, popravi ponudbo in upselle, preden povečaš proračun.

Hitrost odziva (speed to lead)

Neznanci iz oglasov so hladni in raztreseni. Ko se odzovejo, jih kontaktiraj hitro in imej pripravljeno zaporedje opomnikov. Počasno nadaljevanje zapravi leade, ki si jih že plačal.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

SaaS za planiranje proizvodnje (B2B)

Pred. LinkedIn oglas z logotipom podjetja: »Pametna programska oprema za planiranje sodobne proizvodnje. Izvedite več.« Ena različica, velik mesečni proračun od prvega dne. Veliko klikov, skoraj nobenega dema.

Po. Pet različic z majhnim proračunom, vsaka z drugačnim calloutom: »Planerji v kovinski industriji: koliko ur so vas prejšnji mesec stala prestavljena naročila?« Vrednost: brezplačen kalkulator, ki izgubljene ure pokaže na njihovih številkah naročil. Poziv k akciji: »Svojo številko dobite v 5 minutah.« Po dveh tednih najboljša različica dobi večji proračun, ostale so izklopljene.

Recimo, da zmagovalna različica prinese zavzet lead za 40 €, vsak deseti lead postane stranka, torej je CAC 400 €. Če stranka plača 300 € za vzpostavitev in 200 € na mesec, stroški izvedbe pa so skoraj nič, prvih 30 dni prinese približno 500 € bruto dobička. To CAC pokrije enkrat, ne dvakrat, zato ekipa pred povečanjem doda plačljiv paket uvajanja, da 30-dnevni bruto dobiček preseže 800 €.

Zobna ordinacija (lokalni posel)

Del Šibko Močno
Callout »Zobna ordinacija Nasmeh« »Starši v Kranju z otroki od 6 do 12 let«
Vrednost »Kakovostna oskrba za vso družino« »Ta mesec brezplačen pregled in fluoridacija za otroke«
Poziv k akciji »Obiščite nas« »Rezervirajte termin na spletu v 30 sekundah«

Močna različica cilja eno skupino, ponudi jasen brezplačen rezultat in eno hitro dejanje. Ordinacija strošek povrne prek družin, ki ostanejo za redno oskrbo.

Pogoste napake

  • Povečevanje, preden imaš zmagovalca. Velik proračun na nepreizkušenem oglasu kupi drage lekcije. Zmagovalca najprej najdi v malem.
  • Oglasi, preden ponudba deluje. Če ne znaš spremeniti toplih leadov v stranke, se bodo hladni plačani leadi spreminjali še slabše.
  • Merjenje klikov namesto strank. Poceni kliki, ki nikoli ne kupijo, so dragi. Spremljaj ceno na zavzet lead in na stranko.
  • Brez callouta. Oglas za vse je oglas za nikogar. Pravi ljudje se ne ustavijo.
  • Ignoriranje povračila. Če stranke CAC povrnejo v enem letu, oglasi praznijo blagajno. Pred večjo porabo popravi money model.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Pred plačanim prometom želi preizkušeno ponudbo. Najprej doseže rezultate s toplim outreachom ali vsebino, nato zlije denar na to, kar že prodaja.
  • Proti eni ponudbi testira veliko kavljev. Pričakuje, da bo večina kreativ odpovedala, in iskanje zmagovalcev vidi kot svoje delo.
  • Oglase ocenjuje po ekonomiki strank. Njegovo vprašanje je, ali stranka strošek oglasa povrne hitro, idealno v prvih 30 dneh.
  • Money model popravi, da si lahko privošči več oglasov. Upselli in močnejša vstopna ponudba mu omogočijo, da za pridobivanje porabi več od konkurence.
  • Zmagovalce povečuje postopoma, spremlja ceno na stranko in odreže vse, kar se ne povrne.
  • Vztraja pri hitrem odzivu, ker se plačan lead, ki čaka, ohladi.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Za koga je oglas in ali to pove prva vrstica? Kaj dobijo, če se odzovejo? Koliko stane en zavzet lead in koliko ena stranka? Koliko bruto dobička prinese stranka v prvih 30 dneh? Koliko različic si testiral in katera je zmagala?

Delovni list

Odgovori pisno, preden porabiš prvi evro.

  1. Callout: Za koga točno je ta oglas? Napiši tri različne prve vrstice, zaradi katerih bi se ustavili.
  2. Vrednost: Kateri lead magnet ali privabljalno ponudbo dobijo, če se odzovejo?
  3. Dejanje: Kateri je en sam naslednji korak in kako hitro se lahko po njem odzoveš?
  4. Proračun: Kateri majhen testni proračun si lahko privoščiš izgubiti, medtem ko iščeš zmagovalca?
  5. Ekonomika: Koliko največ lahko plačaš za eno stranko glede na njen 30-dnevni bruto dobiček?
  6. Dokaz: Je ta ponudba že prodajala toplim leadom? Če ne, jo najprej preizkusi tam.
