The Rule of 100Pravilo 100

$100M Leads · Rule of 100

Do 100 primary lead-getting actions every day for 100 days. Early on, volume teaches you more than talent or planning ever will.Vsak dan opravi 100 osnovnih dejanj za pridobivanje leadov, 100 dni zapored. Na začetku te volumen nauči več kot talent ali načrtovanje.

The gist

Most new businesses don't fail because they picked the wrong channel. They fail because they never did enough of anything to find out. The Rule of 100 fixes that with a simple commitment: every day, for 100 days, do 100 of your primary lead-getting actions.

100 primary actions a day × 100 days, done first, tracked daily

What counts as "100" depends on the channel. In outreach it is 100 reach-outs. In content it is 100 minutes spent making and posting. In paid ads it is commonly described as $100 a day. The number is a rule of thumb. It is big enough to produce real data and concrete enough that you can't negotiate your way out of it.

How it works

Pick one channel from the Core Four

The Core Four are the ways to let people know about your stuff: warm outreach, posting free content, cold outreach and paid ads. Choose one to start. Warm outreach is usually the easiest first step because the people already know you. Cold outreach and ads cost more effort or money but scale further. Pick the one you can do today with the time and money you have.

Translate "100" into a countable action

The action has to be something you can count at the end of the day without arguing with yourself.

Channel Your daily 100
Warm outreach 100 personal reach-outs to people you know
Cold outreach 100 personalised reach-outs to strangers on a list
Free content 100 minutes making and posting content
Paid ads About $100 (or €100) a day in ad spend

Count actions you control, such as messages sent or minutes worked. Don't count replies or sales. Those are results, and early on they are too small to steer by.

Do it first, every day, for 100 days

The 100 actions happen before email, delivery, admin and everything else. If lead-getting gets the leftover time, it doesn't happen. One hundred days matters because results in any channel lag. The first weeks are mostly noise and learning. Quitting after two bad weeks means you pay the full cost of learning and never collect the benefit.

Volume beats talent, early on

At the start you don't know which message, headline, offer or audience works, and you can't work it out from theory. Volume gives you two things at once. It gives you enough attempts to see patterns, and enough repetitions to get better at the skill itself. Someone who sends a thousand average messages learns more than someone who polished ten perfect ones. Skill comes out of volume.

Track every day

The real purpose of the 100 days is data. Without numbers you can't tell whether your problem is too little activity or poor conversion. Keep a simple sheet with at least:

  1. Actions done (messages, minutes, money spent).
  2. Responses (replies, comments, clicks).
  3. Engaged leads (people who show interest in your offer).
  4. Calls or meetings booked, and sales, reviewed weekly.

After 100 days you know your numbers for that channel. That knowledge is what lets you grow it on purpose, using More, Better, New.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik analytics consultant (B2B, cold outreach)

Before. A LinkedIn post when there's time, a newsletter once a month, a few cold emails on quiet days and a small Google Ads test. A bit of everything, nothing measured. After three months nobody can say what works.

After. Cold outreach only. Every morning before 10:00, 100 personalised messages to finance and operations managers at manufacturers, offering a free review of one manual Excel report. Every number logged in one sheet.

Week Messages Replies Calls Sales
1 500 15 (3%) 3 0
2 500 20 (4%) 5 1
3 500 35 (7%) 8 2

Volume stays fixed at 100 a day, five days a week, so any change in replies comes from the messages themselves. The reply rate more than doubles in three weeks because the consultant had enough attempts to see which first lines worked. At ten messages a week, the same 1,500 data points would have taken almost three years.

Dental practice (local business, paid ads)

Before. The practice runs a whitening ad for a week, sees an expensive cost per booking and switches it off. Next month it tries a different platform for a week. Same result.

After. €100 a day for 100 days on one platform, five ad variations, weekly review of cost per lead and show-up rate. Week one is expensive, and they keep going.

Over 100 days they spend €10,000. In week one cost per lead is €50 (€700 for 14 leads). By week six one patient-video ad brings leads at €25, half the starting cost. They only found it because they kept running long enough for the winner to show up.

Common mistakes

  • Quitting after two weeks. Switching channels at the first bad result keeps you a beginner everywhere. Every switch restarts the learning curve.
  • Perfecting instead of doing. Hours spent polishing one message or video feel productive but produce one data point. Early on, a hundred sent beats one perfect.
  • Doing it with leftover time. If the 100 happens after everything else, it stops happening by week two. Lead-getting goes first.
  • Counting results instead of actions. "I'll stop when I get five calls" gives you nothing to control. Count what you did.
  • Not tracking. Without numbers you can't tell a volume problem from a conversion problem, so you won't know what to fix next.

How Alex would apply it

  • He diagnoses volume before anything else. When someone says a channel doesn't work, his first move is to ask how much they actually did. Usually the answer is far too little to judge.
  • He insists on one channel at a time. Spreading effort across four channels at low volume means none of them gets enough attempts to work.
  • He makes it non-negotiable and first. The lead-getting block goes at the start of the day and is protected like a client meeting.
  • He treats the first 100 days as paid learning. Poor early results are expected. The goal of this phase is enough data to see what works.
  • He would rather see bad work at high volume than good work at low volume. Volume can be improved later. Low volume gives you nothing to improve.
  • He wants the numbers written down daily, because the next step (More, Better or New) depends entirely on them.
  • Questions he would ask: How many reach-outs did you do yesterday, exactly? What's your daily 100, and when in the day does it happen? Which one channel are you committing to? What do your numbers show after the first two weeks? What would you drop to make room for this?

Worksheet

Write your 100-day plan and set up the tracking sheet before you start.

  1. Channel: Which one Core Four channel will you commit to for the next 100 days, and why that one?
  2. Your 100: What exactly is the daily action, and how do you count it (messages, minutes, euros)?
  3. Schedule: At what time does it happen every day, before anything else? What will you drop to make that time?
  4. Tracking: Which 3 to 5 numbers will you log daily and weekly, and where?
  5. Review: On which dates will you review the numbers (for example days 14, 30, 60 and 100), and what will you look for each time?

Bistvo

Večina novih podjetij ne propade zato, ker bi izbrala napačen kanal. Propade, ker nikoli ni naredila dovolj ničesar, da bi to ugotovila. Pravilo 100 (Rule of 100) to reši s preprosto zavezo: vsak dan, 100 dni zapored, opravi 100 svojih osnovnih dejanj za pridobivanje leadov.

100 osnovnih dejanj na dan × 100 dni, opravljeno najprej, z dnevnim beleženjem

Kaj šteje kot »100«, je odvisno od kanala. Pri outreachu je to 100 kontaktov. Pri vsebini je to 100 minut ustvarjanja in objavljanja. Pri plačanih oglasih se navadno navaja 100 $ na dan. Število je pravilo palca. Dovolj je veliko, da da prave podatke, in dovolj konkretno, da se iz njega ne moreš izgovoriti.

Kako deluje

Izberi en kanal iz Core Four

Core Four so štirje načini, kako ljudem sporočiš, da obstajaš: topli outreach (warm outreach), objavljanje brezplačne vsebine (free content), hladni outreach (cold outreach) in plačani oglasi (paid ads). Za začetek izberi enega. Topli outreach je navadno najlažji prvi korak, ker te ljudje že poznajo. Hladni outreach in oglasi zahtevajo več truda ali denarja, a se bolj skalirajo. Izberi tistega, ki ga lahko začneš danes s časom in denarjem, ki ga imaš.

Prevedi »100« v dejanje, ki ga lahko prešteješ

Dejanje mora biti nekaj, kar ob koncu dneva prešteješ, ne da bi se moral prepričevati sam s sabo.

Kanal Tvojih dnevnih 100
Topli outreach 100 osebnih sporočil ljudem, ki jih poznaš
Hladni outreach 100 personaliziranih sporočil neznancem s seznama
Brezplačna vsebina 100 minut ustvarjanja in objavljanja
Plačani oglasi Približno 100 $ (ali 100 €) na dan za oglase

Štej dejanja, ki jih nadzoruješ, na primer poslana sporočila ali minute dela. Ne štej odgovorov ali prodaj. To so rezultati in na začetku so premajhni, da bi se lahko po njih ravnal.

Opravi jih najprej, vsak dan, 100 dni

Teh 100 dejanj pride pred e-pošto, izvedbo, administracijo in vsem ostalim. Če pridobivanje leadov dobi le ostanek časa, se ne zgodi. Sto dni je pomembnih, ker rezultati v vsakem kanalu zamujajo. Prvi tedni so večinoma šum in učenje. Kdor odneha po dveh slabih tednih, plača celoten strošek učenja in nikoli ne pobere koristi.

Volumen premaga talent, na začetku

Na začetku ne veš, katero sporočilo, naslov, ponudba ali ciljna skupina deluje, in tega iz teorije ne moreš ugotoviti. Volumen ti da dve stvari hkrati: dovolj poskusov, da vidiš vzorce, in dovolj ponovitev, da postaneš boljši v sami veščini. Kdor pošlje tisoč povprečnih sporočil, se nauči več kot nekdo, ki je izpilil deset popolnih. Veščina zraste iz volumna.

Beleži vsak dan

Pravi namen 100 dni so podatki. Brez številk ne veš, ali je težava v premajhni aktivnosti ali v slabi konverziji. Vodi preprosto tabelo z vsaj temi podatki:

  1. Dejanja, ki si jih opravil (sporočila, minute, porabljen denar).
  2. Odzivi (odgovori, komentarji, kliki).
  3. Zavzeti leadi (engaged leads): ljudje, ki pokažejo zanimanje za tvojo ponudbo.
  4. Dogovorjeni posveti ali sestanki in prodaje, pregledani tedensko.

Po 100 dneh poznaš svoje številke za ta kanal. To znanje ti omogoči, da ga načrtno razvijaš po zaporedju Več, bolje, novo (More, Better, New).

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Svetovalec za Qlik analitiko (B2B, hladni outreach)

Pred. Objava na LinkedInu, ko je čas, novice enkrat na mesec, nekaj hladnih e-mailov v mirnih dneh in manjši test Google oglasov. Vsega po malem, nič izmerjeno. Po treh mesecih nihče ne zna povedati, kaj deluje.

Po. Samo hladni outreach. Vsako jutro do 10. ure 100 personaliziranih sporočil vodjem financ in operative v proizvodnih podjetjih, s ponudbo brezplačnega pregleda enega ročnega poročila v Excelu. Vse številke v eni tabeli.

Teden Sporočila Odgovori Posveti Prodaje
1 500 15 (3 %) 3 0
2 500 20 (4 %) 5 1
3 500 35 (7 %) 8 2

Volumen ostaja enak, 100 na dan pet dni na teden, zato vsaka sprememba v odgovorih izvira iz samih sporočil. Delež odgovorov se v treh tednih več kot podvoji, ker je imel svetovalec dovolj poskusov, da je videl, katere prve vrstice delujejo. Z desetimi sporočili na teden bi do istih 1.500 podatkov potreboval skoraj tri leta.

Zobozdravstvena ordinacija (lokalno podjetje, plačani oglasi)

Pred. Ordinacija teden dni vrti oglas za beljenje zob, vidi drago ceno na rezervacijo in ga izklopi. Naslednji mesec teden dni preizkuša drugo platformo. Enak rezultat.

Po. 100 € na dan, 100 dni, na eni platformi, pet različic oglasa, tedenski pregled cene na lead in deleža prihodov na termin. Prvi teden je drag, a nadaljujejo.

V 100 dneh porabijo 10.000 €. Prvi teden je cena na lead 50 € (700 € za 14 leadov). Do šestega tedna en oglas z videom pacientke prinaša leade po 25 €, kar je polovica začetne cene. Našli so ga samo zato, ker so vztrajali dovolj dolgo, da se je zmagovalec pokazal.

Pogoste napake

  • Odnehanje po dveh tednih. Menjava kanala ob prvem slabem rezultatu te povsod ohrani na ravni začetnika. Vsaka menjava znova zažene krivuljo učenja.
  • Piljenje namesto dela. Ure za izpopolnjevanje enega sporočila ali videa so videti produktivne, a dajo en sam podatek. Na začetku sto poslanih premaga eno popolno.
  • Delo v ostanku časa. Če 100 dejanj pride na vrsto po vsem ostalem, se do drugega tedna nehajo dogajati. Pridobivanje leadov gre prvo.
  • Štetje rezultatov namesto dejanj. »Nehal bom, ko dobim pet posvetov« ti ne da ničesar, kar bi nadzoroval. Štej, kar si naredil.
  • Brez beleženja. Brez številk ne ločiš težave z volumnom od težave s konverzijo, zato ne veš, kaj popraviti naslednje.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Najprej preveri volumen. Ko nekdo reče, da kanal ne deluje, ga Alex najprej vpraša, koliko je v resnici naredil. Navadno precej premalo za kakršnokoli oceno.
  • Vztraja pri enem kanalu naenkrat. Če trud razpršiš na štiri kanale z nizkim volumnom, noben ne dobi dovolj poskusov, da bi zaživel.
  • Poskrbi, da je delo nepogajljivo in prvo. Blok za pridobivanje leadov gre na začetek dneva in je zaščiten kot sestanek s stranko.
  • Prvih 100 dni obravnava kot plačano učenje. Slabi zgodnji rezultati so pričakovani. Cilj te faze so podatki, ki pokažejo, kaj deluje.
  • Raje vidi slabo delo z velikim volumnom kot dobro delo z majhnim. Volumen lahko pozneje izboljšaš. Majhen volumen ti ne da ničesar, kar bi lahko izboljšal.
  • Hoče številke zapisane vsak dan, ker je naslednji korak (več, bolje ali novo) v celoti odvisen od njih.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Koliko kontaktov si včeraj naredil, natančno? Kaj je tvojih dnevnih 100 in kdaj v dnevu se zgodi? Kateremu enemu kanalu se zavezuješ? Kaj kažejo številke po prvih dveh tednih? Kaj boš opustil, da narediš prostor za to?

Delovni list

Napiši svoj 100-dnevni načrt in pred začetkom pripravi tabelo za beleženje.

  1. Kanal: Kateremu enemu kanalu iz Core Four se zavezuješ za naslednjih 100 dni in zakaj prav temu?
  2. Tvojih 100: Kaj točno je dnevno dejanje in kako ga šteješ (sporočila, minute, evri)?
  3. Urnik: Ob kateri uri se zgodi vsak dan, pred vsem ostalim? Kaj boš opustil, da dobiš ta čas?
  4. Beleženje: Katere 3 do 5 številk boš beležil dnevno in tedensko, in kje?
  5. Pregled: Na katere dneve boš pregledal številke (na primer 14., 30., 60. in 100. dan) in kaj boš vsakič iskal?
