Warm OutreachTopli outreach

$100M Leads · Warm Outreach

Personal, one-to-one messages to people who already know you. It's free, works today and is where almost everyone should start getting engaged leads.Osebna sporočila ena na ena ljudem, ki te že poznajo. Je brezplačen, deluje že danes in skoraj vsakdo bi moral z njim začeti pridobivati zavzete leade.

The gist

Warm outreach is reaching out, one person at a time, to people who already know you: friends, former colleagues, past clients, classmates, people in your phone. It's the top-left box of the Core Four: known audience, one-to-one. It costs no money, the reply rate is the highest of the four channels, and you can start in the next hour.

The method is simple. Go through every contact list you have. Send each person a personal message. Start a real conversation before you mention what you do. Keep it going with ACA. Then make an offer, or ask whether they know anyone who could use it. Do this at volume and follow up.

Contact list → personal message → conversation (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask) → offer or referral ask → follow up

Warm outreach also teaches you to sell. You hear real objections, real words for the problem and real reactions to your offer, from people who are kind enough to answer. That makes every other channel better later.

How it works

1. Build the list

Collect everyone you can contact in one place: phone contacts, email, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp groups, old client lists, alumni groups. Most people have far more than they think, often several hundred to a few thousand. Don't filter too hard. Someone who would never buy may know someone who would.

2. Personalise the first message

Each message refers to the person, not to you. Mention something you know about them, something you saw them post, or the last time you spoke. A message that could be sent to anyone reads like an ad and gets ignored. Short and specific beats long and polished.

3. Start a conversation before you offer

The first goal is a reply, not a sale. Ask how they are, what they've been up to, how the thing they posted turned out. Only once you're talking do you mention what you're working on.

4. Keep it going with ACA

ACA is a framework commonly associated with $100M Leads for keeping a conversation natural:

Step What you do Why
Acknowledge Repeat back or respond to what they said They feel heard
Compliment Say something genuinely positive about them or what they did It builds goodwill and lowers guard
Ask Ask another question that moves the conversation on It keeps them talking, and steers toward the problem you solve

You repeat ACA until the conversation naturally reaches their situation, their work, or a problem you can help with.

5. Offer, or ask for a referral

When the moment fits, tell them what you do and offer something valuable, often your lead magnet, for free. If they don't need it, ask whether they know anyone who does. The referral ask lowers the pressure because you aren't asking them to buy. A common result is that the person says they could use it themselves.

For your very first clients, a free or heavily discounted version in exchange for feedback and a testimonial is a reasonable trade. You are buying proof and practice.

6. Volume

Warm outreach works when you do a lot of it. Alex ties it to the Rule of 100: 100 primary actions a day, which here means reaching out to 100 people a day. That number is a rule of thumb. The principle is that a handful of messages a week tells you nothing, while hundreds tell you what works.

7. Follow up

Many people don't reply the first time because they are busy, not uninterested. Follow up politely after a few days, and again later. Track who you contacted, when, and where each conversation stands, in a simple spreadsheet or CRM.

When warm outreach runs out

Your list is finite. That's fine: warm outreach gets you the first clients, testimonials and a tested offer. Then you add another channel, typically free content or cold outreach, using what you learned.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik consultant starting his own practice (B2B analytics)

Before. A LinkedIn post: "I've gone independent, available for Qlik projects, DM me." Twelve likes, no enquiries.

After. He exports 600 contacts and messages 100 a day. To a former colleague: "Saw your plant got the new line running, congrats. How's reporting holding up since?" She replies that month-end takes days. He acknowledges it, compliments how her team handled the rollout, and asks what takes longest. Then he offers a free two-hour reporting review, and asks if anyone else in her network has the same problem.

The post broadcast an ad. The messages started real conversations about a real problem. Personal, a free offer, a referral ask, at volume: over three weeks that kind of outreach can produce several calls from people who already trust him.

Personal trainer filling a new class (local business)

Step What she does
List Goes through 800 phone and Instagram contacts
Message "Hey, saw your marathon photos, how did the knee hold up?"
ACA Acknowledges the injury, compliments the finish, asks what they're training for next
Offer Two free strength sessions for runners, ask if a running buddy wants to join
Volume 100 messages a day for two weeks
Follow-up A short reminder after four days to everyone who didn't answer

If 1,400 messages get a 20% reply rate, that's 280 conversations. Even if only 5% of those conversations take the free sessions, that's 14 people trying the class, and the first ones become her testimonials.

Common mistakes

  • Pitching in the first message. It reads like spam from a friend. Start a conversation first.
  • Copy-paste messages. Generic messages get generic silence. Mention something about them.
  • Too few messages. Ten messages and two replies proves nothing. Volume is the method.
  • Skipping people out of embarrassment. The people who know you are the most likely to help. Asking for a referral is not pushy.
  • No follow-up and no tracking. Without a list of who's where, warm leads go cold and you message the same people twice.

How Alex would apply it

  • He makes it the default first channel. For anyone with no money and no leads, he would start here before content, cold outreach or ads.
  • He sets a daily number and holds to it. His rule of thumb is 100 reach-outs a day, every day, until it's a habit and the numbers are clear.
  • He leads with value, not a pitch. He'd offer the lead magnet or the service itself for free to the first people, to get proof and practice.
  • He uses the referral ask because it removes pressure and opens doors to people you don't know yet.
  • He follows up more than feels comfortable, because most replies come after the first message.
  • He treats it as sales training. Every conversation tells you how people describe the problem and what they object to. He'd feed that back into the offer.
  • Questions he would ask: How many people are on your combined contact lists? How many did you message today? What's your reply rate? What do you offer them for free? Are you asking for referrals? Who did you follow up with this week?

Worksheet

Plan your first two weeks of warm outreach. Answer in writing.

  1. List: Where are all your contacts? Export them into one sheet. How many do you have in total?
  2. Opener: Write three personal first lines for three different people. What do you know about each of them?
  3. ACA: For one likely reply, write an Acknowledge, a Compliment and an Ask.
  4. Offer: What free thing will you offer, and what's your referral question?
  5. Volume: How many messages a day will you send, and at what time each day?
  6. Tracking: Where will you log who you contacted, replies and follow-up dates?

Bistvo

Topli outreach (warm outreach) pomeni, da posamično pišeš ljudem, ki te že poznajo: prijateljem, nekdanjim sodelavcem, preteklim strankam, sošolcem, ljudem v tvojem telefonu. To je zgornje levo polje Core Four: znano občinstvo, ena na ena. Ne stane denarja, odziv je med vsemi štirimi kanali najvišji, začneš pa lahko že v naslednji uri.

Metoda je preprosta. Pojdi skozi vse sezname kontaktov, ki jih imaš. Vsakemu pošlji osebno sporočilo. Začni pravi pogovor, preden omeniš, s čim se ukvarjaš. Pogovor ohranjaj z ACA. Nato daj ponudbo ali vprašaj, ali poznajo koga, ki bi jo potreboval. Delaj to v velikem obsegu in se oglasi znova.

Seznam kontaktov → osebno sporočilo → pogovor (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask) → ponudba ali prošnja za priporočilo → ponovni stik

Topli outreach te nauči tudi prodajati. Slišiš prave ugovore, prave besede za problem in prave odzive na svojo ponudbo, od ljudi, ki so dovolj prijazni, da odgovorijo. Zaradi tega so pozneje boljši tudi vsi drugi kanali.

Kako deluje

1. Sestavi seznam

Vse, s katerimi lahko stopiš v stik, zberi na enem mestu: kontakti v telefonu, e-pošta, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, skupine na WhatsAppu, stari seznami strank, skupine alumnov. Večina ljudi jih ima veliko več, kot misli, pogosto nekaj sto do nekaj tisoč. Ne filtriraj preveč. Nekdo, ki sam nikoli ne bi kupil, morda pozna nekoga, ki bi.

2. Prilagodi prvo sporočilo

Vsako sporočilo govori o osebi, ne o tebi. Omeni nekaj, kar veš o njej, nekaj, kar je objavila, ali kdaj sta se nazadnje slišala. Sporočilo, ki bi ga lahko poslal komurkoli, deluje kot oglas in ga ljudje prezrejo. Kratko in konkretno premaga dolgo in zloščeno.

3. Začni pogovor, preden ponudiš

Prvi cilj je odgovor, ne prodaja. Vprašaj, kako so, kaj počnejo, kako se je izteklo tisto, kar so objavili. Šele ko se pogovarjata, omeniš, na čem delaš.

4. Pogovor ohranjaj z ACA

ACA je okvir, ki ga običajno povezujejo s knjigo $100M Leads, za naraven tok pogovora:

Korak Kaj narediš Zakaj
Acknowledge (priznaj) Ponoviš ali se odzoveš na to, kar so rekli Počutijo se slišane
Compliment (pohvali) Iskreno pohvališ njih ali to, kar so naredili Gradi naklonjenost in zniža obrambo
Ask (vprašaj) Postaviš novo vprašanje, ki pogovor pelje naprej Ohranja pogovor in ga usmerja k problemu, ki ga rešuješ

ACA ponavljaš, dokler pogovor naravno ne pride do njihovega položaja, dela ali problema, pri katerem lahko pomagaš.

5. Ponudi ali prosi za priporočilo

Ko je pravi trenutek, povej, s čim se ukvarjaš, in brezplačno ponudi nekaj vrednega, pogosto svoj lead magnet. Če tega ne potrebujejo, vprašaj, ali poznajo koga, ki potrebuje. Prošnja za priporočilo zniža pritisk, ker jih ne prosiš za nakup. Pogost izid je, da človek reče, da bi to prišlo prav njemu.

Za čisto prve stranke je brezplačna ali močno znižana različica v zameno za povratne informacije in referenco razumna menjava. Kupuješ dokaze in vajo.

6. Volumen

Topli outreach deluje, ko ga delaš veliko. Alex ga poveže s pravilom 100: 100 osnovnih dejanj na dan, kar tukaj pomeni, da vsak dan stopiš v stik s 100 ljudmi. Ta številka je pravilo palca. Načelo je, da peščica sporočil na teden ne pove ničesar, stotine pa pokažejo, kaj deluje.

7. Ponovno se oglasi

Veliko ljudi prvič ne odgovori, ker so zaposleni, ne pa nezainteresirani. Čez nekaj dni se vljudno oglasi znova in pozneje še enkrat. Beleži, komu si pisal, kdaj in kje je vsak pogovor, v preprosti preglednici ali CRM.

Ko topli kontakti zmanjkajo

Tvoj seznam je končen. To je v redu: topli outreach ti prinese prve stranke, reference in preizkušeno ponudbo. Nato dodaš drug kanal, navadno brezplačno vsebino ali hladni outreach, in uporabiš, kar si se naučil.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Qlik svetovalec, ki začenja samostojno pot (B2B analitika)

Pred. Objava na LinkedInu: »Postal sem samostojen, na voljo za Qlik projekte, pišite mi.« Dvanajst všečkov, nobenega povpraševanja.

Po. Izvozi 600 kontaktov in jih vsak dan kontaktira 100. Nekdanji sodelavki: »Videl sem, da vam je nova linija stekla, čestitke. Kako se od takrat drži poročanje?« Odgovori, da zaključek meseca traja več dni. On to prizna, pohvali, kako je njena ekipa izpeljala zagon, in vpraša, kaj traja najdlje. Nato ponudi brezplačen dvourni pregled poročanja in vpraša, ali ima kdo v njeni mreži isti problem.

Objava je razposlala oglas. Sporočila so začela prave pogovore o pravem problemu. Osebno, brezplačna ponudba, prošnja za priporočilo, v velikem obsegu: v treh tednih tak outreach lahko prinese več klicev z ljudmi, ki mu že zaupajo.

Osebna trenerka, ki polni novo skupino (lokalno podjetje)

Korak Kaj naredi
Seznam Pregleda 800 kontaktov v telefonu in na Instagramu
Sporočilo »Hej, videla sem fotke z maratona, kako se je držalo koleno?«
ACA Prizna poškodbo, pohvali uvrstitev, vpraša, za kaj trenirajo naslednje
Ponudba Dve brezplačni vadbi moči za tekače, vpraša, ali bi se pridružil kak tekaški prijatelj
Volumen 100 sporočil na dan dva tedna
Ponovni stik Kratek opomnik po štirih dneh vsem, ki niso odgovorili

Če 1.400 sporočil dobi 20-odstotni odziv, je to 280 pogovorov. Tudi če brezplačni vadbi sprejme le 5 % teh pogovorov, je to 14 ljudi, ki preizkusijo skupino, prvi med njimi pa postanejo njene reference.

Pogoste napake

  • Prodaja v prvem sporočilu. Deluje kot spam od prijatelja. Najprej začni pogovor.
  • Kopirana sporočila. Splošna sporočila dobijo splošno tišino. Omeni nekaj o osebi.
  • Premalo sporočil. Deset sporočil in dva odgovora ne dokažeta ničesar. Volumen je metoda.
  • Izpuščanje ljudi iz sramu. Ljudje, ki te poznajo, ti najverjetneje pomagajo. Prošnja za priporočilo ni vsiljiva.
  • Brez ponovnega stika in beleženja. Brez seznama, kdo je kje, se topli leadi ohladijo, istim ljudem pa pišeš dvakrat.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • To je njegov privzeti prvi kanal. Za vsakogar brez denarja in brez leadov bi začel tukaj, pred vsebino, hladnim outreachom ali oglasi.
  • Določi dnevno številko in se je drži. Njegovo pravilo palca je 100 stikov na dan, vsak dan, dokler to ne postane navada in so številke jasne.
  • Začne z vrednostjo, ne s prodajo. Prvim ljudem bi lead magnet ali kar storitev ponudil brezplačno, da dobi dokaze in vajo.
  • Uporabi prošnjo za priporočilo, ker odstrani pritisk in odpre vrata k ljudem, ki jih še ne poznaš.
  • Oglasi se znova pogosteje, kot je prijetno, ker večina odgovorov pride po prvem sporočilu.
  • Na to gleda kot na prodajni trening. Vsak pogovor pove, kako ljudje opisujejo problem in kaj jih zadržuje. To bi vrnil v ponudbo.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Koliko ljudi je na tvojih združenih seznamih kontaktov? Koliko si jih danes kontaktiral? Kolikšen je odziv? Kaj jim ponudiš brezplačno? Prosiš za priporočila? Komu si se ta teden oglasil znova?

Delovni list

Načrtuj prva dva tedna toplega outreacha. Odgovori pisno.

  1. Seznam: Kje so vsi tvoji kontakti? Izvozi jih v eno preglednico. Koliko jih imaš skupaj?
  2. Uvod: Napiši tri osebne prve vrstice za tri različne ljudi. Kaj veš o vsakem od njih?
  3. ACA: Za en verjeten odgovor napiši Acknowledge, Compliment in Ask.
  4. Ponudba: Kaj boš ponudil brezplačno in kakšno je tvoje vprašanje za priporočilo?
  5. Volumen: Koliko sporočil na dan boš poslal in ob kateri uri vsak dan?
  6. Beleženje: Kje boš beležil, komu si pisal, odgovore in datume ponovnih stikov?
