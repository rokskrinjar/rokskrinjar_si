Money ModelMoney Models

$100M Money Models · Money Model

Ne ena ponudba, temveč zaporedje ponudb, ki iz ene stranke hitreje ustvari več denarja. Cilj: da stranka sama financira lastno pridobitev.

Not one offer but a sequence of offers that turns one customer into more cash, faster. The goal: the customer pays for their own acquisition.

BistvoThe gist

  1. Privabljalna ponudbaAttraction offer

    Pripelje stranko noter.

    Gets the customer in the door.

  2. NadgradnjaUpsell

    Takoj po »da«: več, boljše, hitreje.

    Right after a "yes": more, better, faster.

  3. Znižana ponudbaDownsell

    Po »ne«: manjše, cenejše ali na obroke.

    After a "no": smaller, cheaper or in instalments.

  4. Ponavljajoča ponudbaContinuity

    Prihodek, ki se ponavlja vsak mesec.

    Revenue that recurs every month.

Večina podjetij ima eno ponudbo in čaka, da se strošek pridobitve stranke (CAC) povrne v nekaj mesecih. Money model obrne logiko: z zaporedjem ponudb v prvih 30 dneh zbereš toliko bruto dobička, da pokrije CAC, idealno dvakrat. Takrat vsaka nova stranka financira naslednji dve, in rast ni več omejena z gotovino na računu (client-financed acquisition).

Most businesses have a single offer and wait months for customer acquisition cost (CAC) to pay back. A money model flips this: a sequence of offers in the first 30 days collects enough gross profit to cover CAC, ideally twice. Then each new customer funds the next two, and growth is no longer capped by cash in the bank (client-financed acquisition).

Štiri vrste ponudbThe four offer types

1. Privabljalna ponudba1. Attraction offer

Naloga ni zaslužek, temveč pridobitev prave stranke ob čim nižjem ali celo negativnem neto strošku. Oblike so različne: brezplačna ali zelo poceni vstopna storitev, »kupi X, dobi Y«, vabljiva cenejša različica ob dražji (decoy), izziv z vračilom denarja ob uspehu. Dobra privabljalna ponudba je dovolj zanimiva, da jo sprejmejo, in dovolj povezana z glavnim produktom, da jo naravno nadaljujejo. Ni pa namenjena lovcem na zastonj stvari.

Its job isn't profit but getting the right customer at a low or even negative net cost. Forms vary: a free or very cheap entry service, "buy X, get Y", a cheaper decoy next to a premium option, a challenge with money back on success. A good attraction offer is interesting enough to accept and close enough to the core product that continuing is natural. It is not meant for freebie hunters.

2. Nadgradnja2. Upsell

Ponudiš jo takoj po tem, ko je kupec rekel da, ker je takrat zaupanje najvišje in je odločitev že sprejeta. Nadgradnja reši naslednjo težavo, ki jo prvi nakup razkrije: kupil si analizo, zdaj potrebuješ implementacijo; kupil si program vadbe, zdaj potrebuješ prehrano. Logika »ne moreš imeti X brez Y« deluje bolje kot splošno »želite še kaj?«.

You make it right after the buyer said yes, because trust is highest and the decision is already made. An upsell solves the next problem the first purchase reveals: you bought an analysis, now you need implementation; you bought a training plan, now you need nutrition. The logic "you can't have X without Y" beats a generic "anything else?".

3. Znižana ponudba3. Downsell

Ko kupec reče ne, to pogosto ni »ne« izidu, temveč ceni, obsegu ali tveganju. Znižana ponudba odstrani oviro: manjši obseg, cenejša različica brez dela storitev, plačilo na obroke ali preizkus za omejen čas. Pomembno: ne zniži cene iste stvari brez razloga, ker s tem kaznuješ tiste, ki so plačali polno ceno. Odvzemi nekaj ali spremeni pogoje.

When a buyer says no, it's often not a no to the outcome but to the price, scope or risk. A downsell removes the obstacle: smaller scope, a cheaper version without part of the service, a payment plan or a time-limited trial. Important: don't just cut the price of the same thing, which punishes those who paid full price. Remove something or change the terms.

4. Ponavljajoča ponudba4. Continuity

Naročnina, vzdrževanje, članstvo ali stalna podpora. Prinaša predvidljiv prihodek in daje strankam razlog, da ostanejo. Najbolje deluje, ko se vrednost ponavlja (novi podatki vsak mesec, redne vadbe, mesečni pregled). Pogosto jo pospešiš z bonusom za prijavo ali s tem, da prvi mesec vključiš v vstopno ponudbo.

Subscription, maintenance, membership or ongoing support. It brings predictable revenue and gives customers a reason to stay. Works best where value recurs (new data each month, regular sessions, a monthly review). Often you accelerate it with a sign-up bonus or by bundling the first month into the entry offer.

Merilo: 30-dnevni bruto dobiček proti CACThe yardstick: 30-day gross profit vs CAC

Model presojaš z enim vprašanjem: koliko bruto dobička (prihodek minus neposredni stroški izvedbe) povprečna nova stranka ustvari v prvih 30 dneh, v primerjavi s tem, koliko je stala njena pridobitev. Pod 1× rast črpa gotovino. Pri 1× se pridobitev plača sama. Pri 2× ali več vsaka stranka financira še eno. Ker vse ponudbe ne sprejmejo vsi, računaš s pričakovanim deležem sprejetja.

You judge the model with one question: how much gross profit (revenue minus direct delivery costs) does the average new customer generate in the first 30 days, compared with what acquiring them cost? Below 1× growth drains cash. At 1× acquisition pays for itself. At 2× or more each customer funds another. Since not everyone accepts every offer, you calculate with expected take rates.

Lokalna storitev · fitnes studioLocal service · fitness studio

Od članarine do zaporedja ponudbFrom a membership to a sequence of offers

Prej: samo članarina 59 € na mesec, bruto dobiček 45 € na mesec. CAC iz oglasov je 140 €. Povrnitev traja več kot tri mesece (140 / 45 ≈ 3,1), zato studio ne more pospešiti oglaševanja.

Before: only a membership at €59/month, gross profit €45/month. CAC from ads is €140. Payback takes over three months (140 / 45 ≈ 3.1), so the studio can't scale ads.

Potem: zaporedje ponudb, vse v prvih 30 dneh. Pričakovani bruto dobiček = bruto dobiček na kupca × delež kupcev.

After: a sequence of offers, all within the first 30 days. Expected gross profit = gross profit per buyer × share of customers who buy.

B2B · AI svetovanjeB2B · AI consulting

Model za AI avtomatizacijo procesovA model for AI process automation

  1. Privabljalna: plačana delavnica, 2 dniAttraction: paid workshop, 2 days

    Pregled procesov in seznam treh avtomatizacij z izračunom prihranka. Cena se odšteje od implementacije, če jo naročijo v 30 dneh.

    Process review and a list of three automations with savings estimates. The fee is credited against implementation if ordered within 30 days.

  2. Nadgradnja: implementacija prvega procesaUpsell: implement the first process

    Ponujena na koncu delavnice, ko je prihranek še na tabli.

    Offered at the end of the workshop while the savings are still on the whiteboard.

  3. Znižana: pilot na enem oddelku ali plačilo v treh obrokihDownsell: single-department pilot or three instalments

    Za tiste, ki se ustrašijo obsega ali proračuna.

    For those put off by scope or budget.

  4. Ponavljajoča: mesečno vzdrževanje in izboljšaveContinuity: monthly maintenance and improvements

    Spremljanje kakovosti, prilagoditve modelov, en nov mini proces na četrtletje.

    Quality monitoring, model tweaks, one new mini-process per quarter.

Delavnica že sama pokrije velik del stroška prodaje, zato pridobitev stranke ne stoji na enem velikem projektu. Kdor reče ne implementaciji, ne odide praznih rok: pilot ohrani odnos in dokaže vrednost.

The workshop alone covers much of the sales cost, so acquisition doesn't hinge on one big project. Whoever says no to implementation doesn't leave empty-handed: the pilot keeps the relationship and proves value.

B2B · SaaS / analitikaB2B · SaaS / analytics

Naročnina na poročilaReporting subscription

PredBefore

14-dnevni brezplačni preizkus, nato mesečna naročnina. Večina preizkusov se izteče brez nakupa; tisti, ki kupijo, povrnejo CAC po več mesecih.

14-day free trial, then a monthly subscription. Most trials lapse without buying; those who do pay back CAC after several months.

PoAfter

Plačana nastavitev na njihovih podatkih (privabljalna), takoj ponujen paket letne naročnine s popustom (nadgradnja), mesečna različica za neodločene (znižana), dodatni moduli po prvem mesecu (ponavljajoča širitev).

Paid setup on their own data (attraction), annual plan at a discount offered immediately (upsell), a monthly plan for the undecided (downsell), add-on modules after month one (continuity expansion).

Plačana nastavitev in letno predplačilo prineseta gotovino na začetku, ko jo podjetje najbolj potrebuje za nove pridobitve.

Paid setup and annual prepayment bring cash up front, exactly when the company needs it most to fund new acquisition.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Računanje s prihodkomCalculating with revenue

    CAC pokriva samo bruto dobiček. Prihodek, ki ga poje izvedba, ne financira ničesar.

    Only gross profit covers CAC. Revenue eaten by delivery funds nothing.

  • Privabljalna ponudba, ki pritegne napačne ljudiAn attraction offer that pulls the wrong people

    Če vstopna ponudba ni povezana z glavnim produktom, dobiš lovce na popuste, ki nikoli ne nadaljujejo.

    If the entry offer isn't tied to the core product, you get bargain hunters who never continue.

  • Nadgradnja ob napačnem časuUpselling at the wrong time

    Teden dni pozneje je navdušenje mimo. Ponudi jo takoj po »da«, dokler je odločitev sveža.

    A week later the excitement is gone. Offer it right after the "yes" while the decision is fresh.

  • »Ne« kot konec pogovoraTreating "no" as the end

    Brez znižane ponudbe izgubiš vse, ki so imeli težavo s ceno ali obsegom, ne z izidom.

    Without a downsell you lose everyone whose issue was price or scope, not the outcome.

  • Preveč ponudb naenkratToo many offers at once

    Zaporedje pomeni eno ponudbo za drugo, ob pravem trenutku. Meni z desetimi možnostmi zmede in upočasni odločitev.

    A sequence means one offer after another, at the right moment. A menu of ten options confuses and slows the decision.
