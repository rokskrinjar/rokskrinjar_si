$100M Money Models · Attraction Offer

The first offer a new customer sees. Its job is to bring the right people in and, ideally, cover what it cost to get them.Prva ponudba, ki jo vidi nova stranka. Njena naloga je pripeljati prave ljudi in po možnosti pokriti strošek njihove pridobitve.

The gist

An attraction offer is the front door of your money model. It is the thing you advertise, the thing a stranger buys first. Its main job is to turn interest into a first transaction with the right kind of customer, at a cost you can afford.

Attraction offer = easy first yes + filters for the right buyer + recovers acquisition cost (fully or partly)

Most businesses advertise their core product at full price and hope. Alex's view is that the first offer should be designed for getting customers, and the money is made by what follows it. A strong attraction offer is either cheap or free to the buyer in a way that still brings cash in, or it is structured so the buyer pays now and earns value back later.

How it works

What makes a good attraction offer

  • Low friction. The buyer can say yes quickly, without a big decision.
  • Relevant. It solves a real piece of the same problem your core offer solves, so the next step feels natural.
  • Self-funding where possible. It brings in enough gross profit to cover a good part of the cost of acquiring the customer.
  • Filters. It attracts people who will buy more, and puts off freebie hunters.

Win Your Money Back

The customer pays up front and gets their money back, or a credit toward the next purchase, if they hit a defined goal. Example goals: attend every session, complete every task, reach a target number. The buyer feels the offer is risk-free if they do the work. You collect cash now, the people who follow through get results and become your best customers, and crediting the money toward the next purchase turns the refund into a sale.

How to set it up: 1. Pick a goal that tracks the actions that produce results (attendance, homework), not just the outcome. 2. Decide refund or credit. Credit toward the next offer is usually stronger for you. 3. Make the conditions clear and written.

Giveaways

You offer a prize worth having, and people enter by giving their contact details. One person wins. Everyone else receives a follow-up offer, often a discount on the same or a similar thing. The prize gathers a large list of interested people, and the consolation offer turns many of them into buyers.

Decoy offer

You present two options side by side: a basic, cheap option and a much better premium option. The cheap one exists to make an attractive headline price and to make the premium one look like the obvious choice. Many buyers who came for the cheap price end up choosing the better version once they see the difference.

Buy X Get Y Free

The buyer pays for one thing and gets something extra included. The extra is often cheap for you to deliver but feels valuable to the buyer. "Free" is a strong word in advertising, and attaching it to something real makes the main purchase easier.

Pay Less Now or Pay More Later

The buyer can start cheaply or for free now, and either pays more later or pays a fee if certain conditions are not met. A typical shape is a free or low-cost trial where the buyer agrees to a fee if they miss the conditions, or a low starting price that rises after an introductory period. This lowers the barrier to starting while protecting you from people who never intended to engage.

Choosing between them

Offer Best when Watch out for
Win Your Money Back Results depend on the buyer showing up Vague conditions cause disputes
Giveaway You need a big list fast Leads are cold until the follow-up offer
Decoy Price shoppers dominate your market A decoy so bad it looks dishonest
Buy X Get Y Free You have cheap-to-deliver extras Giving away something that devalues the core
Pay Less Now, More Later Starting is the main barrier Unclear terms that feel like a trap

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik reporting audit (B2B analytics)

Before. "We offer Qlik development at €90 per hour. Contact us for a quote." Leads trickle in, and each one needs a long sales process.

After. "Reporting audit for €1,500. In two weeks you get a list of your five slowest reports and a plan to fix them. If you start the implementation within 30 days, the full €1,500 is credited toward it."

The audit is cheap enough to approve without a board decision, relevant to the core project, and brings in cash that covers most of the sales effort. The credit is a Win Your Money Back variant that pulls buyers into the implementation.

Dental clinic (local business)

Before. "Whitening, €350." The ad competes on price with every clinic in town.

After. "Check-up and cleaning for €49 (decoy, shown first), or our Bright Smile package: check-up, cleaning and whitening for €290, with a second cleaning in six months included free."

The €49 option creates a low headline price and brings in people who were not ready to spend more. Next to it, the package looks like better value, and the free second cleaning (Buy X Get Y Free) books the patient's next visit.

Bakery (local business)

A bakery runs a giveaway: a year of free bread for one winner, entered by leaving an email. 800 people enter. One wins. The other 799 get an offer for a ten-loaf card at €25 instead of €35. If 10% buy, that is about 80 cards and €2,000 in revenue from one campaign, plus a list of people who already showed interest.

Common mistakes

  • Advertising the core offer cold. A full-price, high-commitment offer is a hard first step for a stranger. Lead with something easier.
  • Attracting the wrong people. A free thing unrelated to your core offer fills your list with people who will never buy again.
  • Treating the attraction offer as the whole model. Its job is to start the relationship. Without an upsell, downsell or continuity offer after it, the cheap front end loses money.
  • Unclear conditions. Money-back goals, trial fees and price rises must be written down and explained up front, or they feel like tricks.
  • A dishonest decoy. The cheap option must be a real product you will deliver happily.

How Alex would apply it

  • He designs the first offer to get customers, and makes money on the back end. He would accept thin profit at the front if what follows covers the acquisition cost quickly.
  • He wants the front end to pay for itself where possible. His rule of thumb is that the attraction offer plus what follows in the first 30 days should cover the cost of acquiring the customer.
  • He prefers cash now with value back later. Win Your Money Back and credits toward the next purchase are favourite moves, because they collect money and still feel risk-free.
  • He ties the conditions to the behaviours that create results. That way the offer filters out uncommitted buyers and produces success stories.
  • He tests one attraction offer at a time and keeps the one that brings in the best buyers at the lowest net cost, not the one with the most sign-ups.
  • Questions he would ask: What does it cost you to get one customer today? What is the smallest real result you can sell first? Who do you want to attract, and who do you want to repel? What do buyers purchase next, and how quickly?

Worksheet

Answer in writing for your main product or service.

  1. Cost: What does it cost you today to acquire one new customer?
  2. First step: What is a small, real result a stranger could buy from you quickly?
  3. Type: Which of the five attraction offers fits your market best, and why?
  4. Filter: What condition or price would put off people who will never buy more?
  5. Next step: What do you offer immediately after the attraction offer, and how much of the acquisition cost does it recover?

Bistvo

Privabljalna ponudba (attraction offer) so vhodna vrata tvojega money modela. To je tisto, kar oglašuješ, in tisto, kar neznanec kupi prvo. Njena glavna naloga je, da zanimanje spremeni v prvi nakup prave vrste stranke, po strošku, ki si ga lahko privoščiš.

Privabljalna ponudba = lahek prvi da + filter za pravega kupca + povrnitev stroška pridobitve (v celoti ali delno)

Večina podjetij oglašuje glavni izdelek po polni ceni in upa. Alex meni, da mora biti prva ponudba zasnovana za pridobivanje strank, denar pa prinese tisto, kar sledi. Močna privabljalna ponudba je za kupca poceni ali brezplačna na način, ki vseeno prinese gotovino, ali pa je zgrajena tako, da kupec plača zdaj in vrednost dobi nazaj pozneje.

Kako deluje

Kaj naredi privabljalno ponudbo dobro

  • Malo trenja. Kupec lahko hitro reče da, brez velike odločitve.
  • Relevantnost. Reši resničen kos istega problema, ki ga rešuje glavna ponudba, zato je naslednji korak naraven.
  • Samofinanciranje, kjer je mogoče. Prinese dovolj bruto dobička, da pokrije dober del stroška pridobitve stranke.
  • Filter. Pritegne ljudi, ki bodo kupili še kaj, in odbije lovce na zastonj stvari.

Dobi denar nazaj (Win Your Money Back)

Stranka plača vnaprej in dobi denar nazaj ali dobropis za naslednji nakup, če doseže določen cilj. Primeri ciljev: udeležba na vseh srečanjih, opravljene vse naloge, dosežena ciljna številka. Kupec ponudbo doživlja kot brez tveganja, če opravi svoje delo. Ti dobiš gotovino zdaj, tisti, ki vztrajajo, dobijo rezultate in postanejo tvoje najboljše stranke, dobropis za naslednji nakup pa vračilo spremeni v prodajo.

Kako jo postaviš: 1. Izberi cilj, ki meri dejanja, ki prinašajo rezultate (udeležba, domače naloge), ne le sam izid. 2. Odloči se za vračilo ali dobropis. Dobropis za naslednjo ponudbo je zate navadno močnejši. 3. Pogoje jasno zapiši.

Nagradne igre (Giveaways)

Ponudiš nagrado, ki jo je vredno imeti, ljudje pa sodelujejo tako, da pustijo kontaktne podatke. Zmaga en sam. Vsi ostali dobijo nadaljnjo ponudbo, pogosto popust na isto ali podobno stvar. Nagrada zbere velik seznam zainteresiranih, tolažilna ponudba pa mnoge od njih spremeni v kupce.

Vaba (Decoy offer)

Predstaviš dve možnosti drugo ob drugi: osnovno, poceni in veliko boljšo premium možnost. Poceni možnost obstaja zato, da ustvari privlačno izhodiščno ceno in da premium možnost deluje kot očitna izbira. Veliko kupcev, ki so prišli zaradi nizke cene, ob primerjavi izbere boljšo različico.

Kupi X, dobi Y zastonj (Buy X Get Y Free)

Kupec plača eno stvar in dobi nekaj dodatnega. Dodatek je zate pogosto poceni, kupcu pa se zdi vreden. »Zastonj« je v oglaševanju močna beseda, in ko je pripeta na nekaj resničnega, olajša glavni nakup.

Plačaj manj zdaj ali več pozneje (Pay Less Now or Pay More Later)

Kupec lahko začne poceni ali brezplačno zdaj in pozneje plača več ali pa plača pristojbino, če ne izpolni določenih pogojev. Tipična oblika je brezplačen ali poceni preizkus, pri katerem kupec pristane na pristojbino, če pogojev ne izpolni, ali nizka začetna cena, ki se po uvodnem obdobju zviša. To zniža prag za začetek in te zaščiti pred ljudmi, ki se nikoli niso nameravali zares vključiti.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Revizija Qlik poročil (B2B analitika)

Pred. »Ponujamo razvoj v Qliku po 90 € na uro. Pišite nam za ponudbo.« Leadi prihajajo počasi, vsak pa zahteva dolg prodajni proces.

Po. »Revizija poročil za 1.500 €. V dveh tednih dobite seznam petih najpočasnejših poročil in načrt, kako jih popraviti. Če implementacijo začnete v 30 dneh, se celotnih 1.500 € odšteje od njene cene.«

Revizija je dovolj poceni, da jo odobrijo brez odločitve uprave, povezana je z glavnim projektom in prinese gotovino, ki pokrije večino prodajnega truda. Dobropis je različica »dobi denar nazaj«, ki kupce potegne v implementacijo.

Zobna ambulanta (lokalno podjetje)

Pred. »Beljenje zob, 350 €.« Oglas tekmuje v ceni z vsemi ambulantami v mestu.

Po. »Pregled in čiščenje za 49 € (vaba, prikazana prva) ali paket Bel nasmeh: pregled, čiščenje in beljenje za 290 €, z drugim čiščenjem čez šest mesecev zastonj.«

Možnost za 49 € ustvari nizko izhodiščno ceno in pripelje ljudi, ki še niso pripravljeni porabiti več. Ob njej je paket videti kot boljša vrednost, brezplačno drugo čiščenje (kupi X, dobi Y zastonj) pa že rezervira pacientov naslednji obisk.

Pekarna (lokalno podjetje)

Pekarna organizira nagradno igro: leto dni kruha zastonj za enega zmagovalca, sodeluje se z e-poštnim naslovom. Prijavi se 800 ljudi. Eden zmaga. Ostalih 799 dobi ponudbo za kartico desetih hlebcev za 25 € namesto 35 €. Če jih kupi 10 %, je to približno 80 kartic in 2.000 € prihodka iz ene akcije, poleg tega pa še seznam ljudi, ki so že pokazali zanimanje.

Pogoste napake

  • Hladno oglaševanje glavne ponudbe. Ponudba po polni ceni z veliko zavezo je za neznanca težak prvi korak. Začni z nečim lažjim.
  • Privabljanje napačnih ljudi. Brezplačna stvar, ki ni povezana z glavno ponudbo, ti napolni seznam z ljudmi, ki nikoli več ne bodo kupili.
  • Privabljalna ponudba kot celoten model. Njena naloga je začeti odnos. Brez upsella, downsella ali naročnine za njo poceni vstopna ponudba izgublja denar.
  • Nejasni pogoji. Cilje za vračilo, pristojbine pri preizkusu in dvige cen moraš vnaprej zapisati in razložiti, sicer delujejo kot zvijača.
  • Nepoštena vaba. Poceni možnost mora biti resničen izdelek, ki ga z veseljem dostaviš.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Prvo ponudbo zasnuje za pridobivanje strank, zasluži pa v nadaljevanju. Na začetku sprejme tanek dobiček, če tisto, kar sledi, hitro pokrije strošek pridobitve.
  • Želi, da se vstopna ponudba po možnosti plača sama. Njegovo pravilo palca je, da naj privabljalna ponudba skupaj s tem, kar sledi v prvih 30 dneh, pokrije strošek pridobitve stranke.
  • Raje ima gotovino zdaj in vrednost nazaj pozneje. »Dobi denar nazaj« in dobropisi za naslednji nakup so njegove priljubljene poteze, ker poberejo denar in kupcu vseeno delujejo brez tveganja.
  • Pogoje veže na vedenje, ki ustvarja rezultate. Tako ponudba izloči nezavzete kupce in ustvarja zgodbe o uspehu.
  • Testira eno privabljalno ponudbo naenkrat in obdrži tisto, ki pripelje najboljše kupce po najnižjem neto strošku, ne tiste z največ prijavami.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Koliko te danes stane ena stranka? Kateri je najmanjši resnični rezultat, ki ga lahko prodaš najprej? Koga želiš pritegniti in koga odbiti? Kaj kupci kupijo naslednje in kako hitro?

Delovni list

Odgovori pisno za svoj glavni izdelek ali storitev.

  1. Strošek: Koliko te danes stane pridobitev ene nove stranke?
  2. Prvi korak: Kateri majhen, resničen rezultat bi neznanec lahko hitro kupil pri tebi?
  3. Tip: Katera od petih privabljalnih ponudb najbolj ustreza tvojemu trgu in zakaj?
  4. Filter: Kateri pogoj ali cena bi odbila ljudi, ki nikoli ne bodo kupili več?
  5. Naslednji korak: Kaj ponudiš takoj po privabljalni ponudbi in kolikšen del stroška pridobitve to povrne?
