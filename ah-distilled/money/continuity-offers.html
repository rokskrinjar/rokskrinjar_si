Continuity OffersPonudbe naročnine

$100M Money Models · Continuity Offer

Offers that bill the customer again and again for ongoing value. They turn one sale into recurring revenue, and the work is as much about keeping customers as getting them to join.Ponudbe, ki stranki znova in znova zaračunajo stalno vrednost. Eno prodajo spremenijo v ponavljajoči se prihodek, delo pa je enako v ohranjanju strank kot v tem, da se pridružijo.

The gist

A continuity offer is anything the customer pays for on a recurring basis: a membership, a subscription, a retainer, maintenance, ongoing support. It is the last piece of the money model, and often the one that makes the business stable. Each month that a customer stays adds gross profit without another acquisition cost.

Continuity = recurring value + a reason to join now (bonus, discount or waived fee) + a reason to stay (commitment and ongoing results)

Continuity has two problems to solve. First, getting people to sign up for something that never ends feels like a big commitment. Second, people cancel. Alex's three continuity offers address both: they reward the buyer for joining, and they tie the reward to staying.

How it works

What makes continuity work

  • The value recurs. Something new or useful happens every billing period: new data, a session, a review, an improvement.
  • The customer feels the loss of leaving. They would lose access, progress or a benefit.
  • The commitment is rewarded. Longer commitments get something extra, and short ones are still possible.

Continuity bonus

You give a bonus to people who join the recurring offer. The bonus should be valuable, related to the outcome, and ideally something the buyer receives right away. It gives a concrete reason to join today, and a reason to stay if the bonus is unlocked over time or kept only by members.

How to set it up: 1. Pick a bonus that helps them get the result faster. 2. Make it exclusive to members. 3. Give it on joining, or release it in parts to reward staying.

Continuity discount

You give a discount for committing longer, often expressed as free months. For example, pay for a year and get some months free. The buyer saves money, and you get cash up front and a customer who has committed for a longer period.

You can also offer the discount only to people who prepay, or apply it as a credit at the end of the term.

Waived fee

You charge a setup or joining fee, and waive it for buyers who commit to a minimum term. If they cancel before the term ends, the fee becomes due. The fee is often real: onboarding, setup or equipment costs you money. Waiving it makes joining easy, and the fee on early cancellation discourages people from leaving early.

Longer billing reduces churn

As a general pattern, customers on longer billing intervals tend to cancel less than those billed monthly. Every billing event is a moment when the customer reconsiders. Fewer billing events mean fewer moments to quit, and a bigger upfront commitment usually comes with stronger intent. This is why the continuity discount and the waived fee both push toward longer terms.

Offer Main effect Typical use
Continuity bonus Reason to join now Memberships, subscriptions
Continuity discount Longer commitment, cash up front Annual plans
Waived fee Low barrier with a cost to leaving early Services with setup cost

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Managed analytics service (B2B SaaS/BI)

Before. "Support at €800 per month, cancel any time. Setup is €2,400." Clients hesitate at the setup cost, and some cancel after two or three months.

After. "Managed analytics at €800 per month. Setup (€2,400) is waived with a 12-month commitment, and due if you cancel earlier. Pay annually and get two months free: €8,000 instead of €9,600. Members also get a quarterly board-ready KPI review."

The waived fee removes the joining barrier, the annual option gives cash up front (12 × €800 = €9,600, minus two months = €8,000), and the quarterly review is a continuity bonus that recurs.

Gym (local business)

A gym charges €60 per month. New members who sign up today get a free body-composition scan and a nutrition plan (continuity bonus). The €99 joining fee is waived for anyone who commits to six months, and charged if they leave before. Those who prepay a year pay €600 instead of €720, which is two months free.

Bakery (local business)

A bakery sells a bread subscription: one loaf every Friday for €18 per month. Subscribers get a free pastry on their first pickup and a 10% discount on all cakes. The bakery knows its Friday baking volume in advance, and the customers come in every week, which also drives extra purchases.

Common mistakes

  • No recurring value. Charging every month for something delivered once leads to cancellations. The value has to repeat.
  • Discounting without commitment. A cheaper monthly price with no longer term gives away margin and keeps churn the same.
  • Hiding the terms. Commitment periods and early-cancellation fees must be clear at signup, or they damage trust and invite disputes.
  • Ignoring retention. Most of the value of continuity comes from how long people stay. Track churn as closely as signups.
  • Bonuses unrelated to the outcome. A bonus should help them succeed with the core offer, which also helps them stay.

How Alex would apply it

  • He looks for continuity in every business. His default question is what the customer could keep receiving, because recurring revenue makes acquisition cheaper over time.
  • He rewards joining and staying. He stacks a bonus for joining with a discount or waived fee tied to a commitment.
  • He pushes toward longer terms. As a rule of thumb, he prefers annual or longer commitments where the market allows, because they bring cash up front and tend to churn less.
  • He uses upfront cash to fund acquisition. Prepaid continuity is one of the ways a business can pay for new customers with customers' money.
  • He keeps the terms clean and visible. Fees and commitments are explained up front so nobody feels trapped.
  • Questions he would ask: What value can you deliver every month? Why do people cancel, and when? What does it cost you to onboard a customer? What would make a customer commit for a year?

Worksheet

Answer for your business, even if you sell one-off projects today.

  1. Recurring value: What could a customer receive from you every month or quarter?
  2. Bonus: What member-only bonus would help them get results faster?
  3. Discount: What would you give for a 12-month commitment or prepayment?
  4. Fee: What setup cost could you waive in exchange for a minimum term?
  5. Churn: When and why do customers leave today, and what would make them stay?

Bistvo

Ponudba naročnine (continuity offer) je vse, kar stranka plačuje ponavljajoče: članarina, naročnina, mesečni pavšal (retainer), vzdrževanje, stalna podpora. Je zadnji del money modela in pogosto tisti, ki podjetje stabilizira. Vsak mesec, ko stranka ostane, doda bruto dobiček brez novega stroška pridobitve.

Naročnina = ponavljajoča se vrednost + razlog, da se pridruži zdaj (bonus, popust ali odpuščena pristojbina) + razlog, da ostane (zaveza in stalni rezultati)

Naročnina mora rešiti dva problema. Prvič, prijava na nekaj, kar se nikoli ne konča, deluje kot velika zaveza. Drugič, ljudje odpovedujejo. Alexove tri ponudbe naročnine naslovijo oboje: kupca nagradijo za pridružitev in nagrado vežejo na to, da ostane.

Kako deluje

Kaj naredi naročnino uspešno

  • Vrednost se ponavlja. V vsakem obračunskem obdobju se zgodi nekaj novega ali koristnega: novi podatki, srečanje, pregled, izboljšava.
  • Stranka občuti izgubo ob odhodu. Izgubila bi dostop, napredek ali ugodnost.
  • Zaveza je nagrajena. Daljše zaveze dobijo nekaj dodatnega, krajše pa so še vedno mogoče.

Bonus za naročnino (Continuity bonus)

Ljudem, ki se pridružijo ponavljajoči se ponudbi, daš bonus. Bonus naj bo vreden, povezan z izidom in po možnosti nekaj, kar kupec dobi takoj. Da konkreten razlog, da se pridruži danes, in razlog, da ostane, če se bonus odklepa postopoma ali ga obdržijo le člani.

Kako ga postaviš: 1. Izberi bonus, ki jim pomaga hitreje priti do rezultata. 2. Naj bo na voljo samo članom. 3. Daj ga ob pridružitvi ali ga sprostiš po delih, da nagradiš ostajanje.

Popust za naročnino (Continuity discount)

Daš popust za daljšo zavezo, pogosto izražen kot brezplačni meseci. Na primer: plačaj za leto in dobi nekaj mesecev zastonj. Kupec prihrani, ti pa dobiš gotovino vnaprej in stranko, ki se je zavezala za daljše obdobje.

Popust lahko ponudiš tudi samo tistim, ki plačajo vnaprej, ali ga uveljaviš kot dobropis ob koncu obdobja.

Odpuščena pristojbina (Waived fee)

Zaračunaš pristojbino za nastavitev ali pristop in jo odpustiš kupcem, ki se zavežejo za minimalno obdobje. Če odpovedo pred koncem obdobja, pristojbina zapade v plačilo. Pristojbina je pogosto resnična: uvajanje, nastavitev ali oprema te stanejo denar. Odpustitev olajša pristop, pristojbina ob predčasni odpovedi pa ljudi odvrača od zgodnjega odhoda.

Daljše obračunsko obdobje zmanjša odhode

Splošen vzorec je, da stranke z daljšimi obračunskimi obdobji odpovedujejo redkeje kot tiste z mesečnim obračunom. Vsak obračun je trenutek, ko stranka ponovno premisli. Manj obračunov pomeni manj priložnosti za odhod, večja zaveza vnaprej pa navadno pomeni tudi močnejšo namero. Zato tako popust za naročnino kot odpuščena pristojbina potiskata k daljšim obdobjem.

Ponudba Glavni učinek Tipična uporaba
Bonus za naročnino Razlog za pridružitev zdaj Članarine, naročnine
Popust za naročnino Daljša zaveza, gotovina vnaprej Letni paketi
Odpuščena pristojbina Nizek prag s stroškom za predčasen odhod Storitve s stroškom nastavitve

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Upravljana analitika (B2B SaaS/BI)

Pred. »Podpora za 800 € na mesec, odpoved kadar koli. Nastavitev stane 2.400 €.« Stranke oklevajo zaradi stroška nastavitve, nekatere pa odpovedo po dveh ali treh mesecih.

Po. »Upravljana analitika za 800 € na mesec. Nastavitev (2.400 €) je odpuščena ob 12-mesečni zavezi in zapade, če odpoveste prej. Ob letnem plačilu dobite dva meseca zastonj: 8.000 € namesto 9.600 €. Člani dobijo tudi četrtletni pregled KPI, pripravljen za upravo.«

Odpuščena pristojbina odstrani oviro za pristop, letna možnost prinese gotovino vnaprej (12 × 800 € = 9.600 €, minus dva meseca = 8.000 €), četrtletni pregled pa je bonus za naročnino, ki se ponavlja.

Fitnes (lokalno podjetje)

Fitnes zaračuna 60 € na mesec. Novi člani, ki se prijavijo danes, dobijo brezplačno meritev telesne sestave in prehranski načrt (bonus za naročnino). Pristopnina 99 € je odpuščena vsakomur, ki se zaveže za šest mesecev, in zaračunana, če odide prej. Kdor plača leto vnaprej, plača 600 € namesto 720 €, kar sta dva meseca zastonj.

Pekarna (lokalno podjetje)

Pekarna prodaja naročnino na kruh: en hlebec vsak petek za 18 € na mesec. Naročniki ob prvem prevzemu dobijo brezplačno pecivo in 10 % popusta na vse torte. Pekarna vnaprej pozna količino peke za petek, stranke pa prihajajo vsak teden, kar spodbuja tudi dodatne nakupe.

Pogoste napake

  • Brez ponavljajoče se vrednosti. Mesečno zaračunavanje za nekaj, kar je bilo dostavljeno enkrat, vodi v odpovedi. Vrednost se mora ponavljati.
  • Popust brez zaveze. Nižja mesečna cena brez daljšega obdobja podari maržo, odhodi pa ostanejo enaki.
  • Skriti pogoji. Obdobja zaveze in pristojbine za predčasno odpoved morajo biti jasna ob prijavi, sicer škodijo zaupanju in vabijo k sporom.
  • Zanemarjanje ohranjanja strank. Večina vrednosti naročnine izhaja iz tega, kako dolgo ljudje ostanejo. Odhode spremljaj enako pozorno kot prijave.
  • Bonusi, ki niso povezani z izidom. Bonus naj jim pomaga uspeti z glavno ponudbo, kar jim pomaga tudi ostati.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Naročnino išče v vsakem podjetju. Njegovo privzeto vprašanje je, kaj bi stranka lahko dobivala naprej, ker ponavljajoči se prihodek sčasoma poceni pridobivanje.
  • Nagradi pridružitev in ostajanje. Bonus za pridružitev zloži s popustom ali odpuščeno pristojbino, vezano na zavezo.
  • Potiska k daljšim obdobjem. Pravilo palca: kjer trg dopušča, ima raje letne ali daljše zaveze, ker prinesejo gotovino vnaprej in navadno pomenijo manj odhodov.
  • Gotovino vnaprej uporabi za financiranje pridobivanja. Vnaprej plačana naročnina je eden od načinov, kako podjetje nove stranke plača z denarjem strank.
  • Pogoje ohranja čiste in vidne. Pristojbine in zaveze razloži vnaprej, da se nihče ne počuti ujetega.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Katero vrednost lahko dostaviš vsak mesec? Zakaj in kdaj ljudje odpovedujejo? Koliko te stane uvajanje stranke? Kaj bi stranko spodbudilo, da se zaveže za leto?

Delovni list

Odgovori za svoje podjetje, tudi če danes prodajaš enkratne projekte.

  1. Ponavljajoča se vrednost: Kaj bi stranka lahko dobivala od tebe vsak mesec ali četrtletje?
  2. Bonus: Kateri bonus samo za člane bi jim pomagal hitreje do rezultatov?
  3. Popust: Kaj bi dal za 12-mesečno zavezo ali plačilo vnaprej?
  4. Pristojbina: Kateri strošek nastavitve bi lahko odpustil v zameno za minimalno obdobje?
  5. Odhodi: Kdaj in zakaj stranke danes odidejo in kaj bi jih spodbudilo, da ostanejo?
