$100M Money Models · Downsell Offer

What you offer after someone says no. A smaller, cheaper or easier version that removes the real obstacle, so you keep the customer instead of losing them.Kar ponudiš, ko nekdo reče ne. Manjša, cenejša ali lažja različica, ki odstrani pravo oviro, da stranko obdržiš, namesto da jo izgubiš.

The gist

A no is rarely a no to the outcome. More often it is a no to the price, the payment timing, the size of the commitment or the risk. A downsell is a second offer, made right after the no, that removes that specific obstacle.

Downsell = find the real objection + offer a version that removes it (payment terms, trial, or fewer features) + stop at the first yes

Without a downsell, everyone who couldn't or wouldn't take the main offer leaves with nothing, and you lose all the money you spent getting them to the table. With one, many of them become customers, and some of them later move up to the full offer.

How it works

Find the real objection

Ask why, and listen. The answers usually fall into a few groups: - Money now. They want it but can't pay the full amount today. - Risk. They are not sure it will work for them. - Size. It is more than they need right now.

Each needs a different downsell. Lowering the price of the same thing answers none of them well and teaches buyers to wait for discounts.

Payment plans

When the problem is cash flow, change when and how they pay, not what they get. Alex describes stepping down through options and stopping as soon as the buyer says yes. A typical sequence:

  1. Pay in full with a discount or a bonus for paying up front.
  2. Financing or card, so a third party or their credit card carries the cost.
  3. Half now, half later, for example half today and half in 30 days.
  4. Check priority, then three payments. Ask how important solving the problem is to them on a scale of 1 to 10. A high score (summaries mention 8 or more) means the problem is real and it is worth offering to split the amount in three.
  5. Spread monthly, the smallest payment per month over a longer term.

Present one step at a time. Do not show the full list, because people will jump straight to the easiest option.

Trials with a penalty

When the problem is risk, let them try before they fully commit. A free or cheap trial comes with conditions, such as attending, completing tasks or using the product, and a fee if they do not meet them or do not cancel in time. The buyer gets low risk, and you get people who actually engage.

Feature downsells

When the problem is size, take parts out and lower the price accordingly. Fewer sessions, a group instead of one-to-one, do-it-yourself instead of done-for-you, one department instead of the whole company. The buyer gets a real, smaller product. The full offer stays at its price and stays more valuable.

Be transparent about fees

If paying by card or in instalments costs you a fee, say so. Offer the choice: pay the card fee, or pay by bank transfer without it. Clear terms protect trust, and many buyers will pick the cheaper option for you.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

ERP-integration project (B2B manufacturing)

Before. A consultant quotes €24,000 to connect the ERP to production data and build reports. The plant manager says the budget for this year is gone. The deal dies.

After. The consultant asks what blocks it. Answer: budget this year. Offer 1: half now, half in the new budget year. Still no. Offer 2: a pilot on one production line for €6,000, with the fee credited if they roll out to the whole plant.

The first downsell changed timing. The second removed scope and also lowered risk. The plant gets proof on one line, and the consultant keeps the relationship and a clear path to the full project.

Gym (local business)

A gym sells a 12-week programme for €600. For those who say no:

Step Offer Price per payment
1 Pay in full, nutrition guide included €600 once
2 Half now, half in 30 days €300 × 2
3 Three monthly payments €200 × 3
4 12 weekly payments €50 × 12

All steps total €600, and the gym stops at the first yes. A buyer who refuses every plan is offered a two-week trial for €49, with the €49 credited if they join the full programme.

Plumber (local business)

A customer balks at a €3,200 bathroom renovation. The plumber offers the critical part only: replace the leaking pipes and the shower valve for €900 now, and keep the rest of the quote valid for six months. The customer agrees, and the plumber is first in line for the rest.

Common mistakes

  • Discounting the same thing. It punishes those who paid in full and trains buyers to say no first.
  • Showing every option at once. People jump to the cheapest. Step down one level at a time.
  • Not asking why. A payment plan does not solve a risk objection, and a trial does not solve a cash objection.
  • Hidden fees and conditions. Trial penalties and card fees must be stated up front and in writing.
  • Downsells that cost you more to deliver. A smaller version must have lower delivery cost, or you lose margin on every no.

How Alex would apply it

  • He never lets a no end the conversation. His default is to have a downsell ready for every offer in the sequence.
  • He diagnoses before he downsells. He wants to know whether the objection is money, risk or size, and picks the downsell to match.
  • He changes terms before he changes the product. Payment plans come first, because the buyer still gets the full offer and you keep full price.
  • He steps down one rung at a time and stops at the first yes.
  • He removes features rather than cutting price. As a rule of thumb, a lower price should always come with less delivered.
  • He keeps a path back up. Credits and pilots give downsold customers a reason to buy the full offer later.
  • Questions he would ask: What are the top three reasons people say no? What happens to them today after they say no? What is your payment plan sequence? What could you remove and still deliver a real result?

Worksheet

Take your main offer and the last ten people who said no.

  1. Reasons: Sort their objections into money, risk and size. Which is most common?
  2. Payment ladder: Write your step-down sequence of payment options, from pay in full to monthly.
  3. Trial: What trial could you offer, and what conditions and fee would make people engage?
  4. Smaller version: What could you remove to make a real, cheaper product with lower delivery cost?
  5. Way back up: How would a downsold customer move up to the full offer later?

Bistvo

»Ne« je redko »ne« izidu. Pogosteje je »ne« ceni, času plačila, velikosti zaveze ali tveganju. Downsell je druga ponudba, ki jo daš takoj po »ne« in ki odstrani prav to oviro.

Downsell = poišči pravi ugovor + ponudi različico, ki ga odstrani (plačilni pogoji, preizkus ali manj funkcij) + ustavi se pri prvem da

Brez downsella vsi, ki glavne ponudbe niso mogli ali hoteli vzeti, odidejo praznih rok, ti pa izgubiš ves denar, ki si ga porabil, da so prišli do pogovora. Z njim mnogi postanejo stranke, nekateri pa se pozneje premaknejo navzgor do polne ponudbe.

Kako deluje

Poišči pravi ugovor

Vprašaj zakaj in poslušaj. Odgovori navadno padejo v nekaj skupin: - Denar zdaj. Želijo, a danes ne morejo plačati celotnega zneska. - Tveganje. Niso prepričani, da bo pri njih delovalo. - Obseg. To je več, kot trenutno potrebujejo.

Vsaka skupina potrebuje drugačen downsell. Nižja cena za isto stvar ne odgovori dobro na nobenega in kupce nauči čakati na popuste.

Plačilni načrti (payment plans)

Ko je problem denarni tok, spremeni, kdaj in kako plačajo, ne tega, kar dobijo. Alex opisuje stopnjevanje navzdol po možnostih in ustavitev takoj, ko kupec reče da. Tipično zaporedje:

  1. Plačilo v celoti s popustom ali bonusom za plačilo vnaprej.
  2. Financiranje ali kartica, da strošek nosi tretja oseba ali njihova kreditna kartica.
  3. Pol zdaj, pol pozneje, na primer polovica danes in polovica čez 30 dni.
  4. Preveri prioriteto (priority check), nato tri plačila. Vprašaj, kako pomembna jim je rešitev problema na lestvici od 1 do 10. Visoka ocena (povzetki omenjajo 8 ali več) pomeni, da je problem resničen in da je smiselno ponuditi razdelitev zneska na tri dele.
  5. Mesečni obroki, najmanjše plačilo na mesec v daljšem obdobju.

Predstavi en korak naenkrat. Ne pokaži celega seznama, ker bodo ljudje takoj skočili na najlažjo možnost.

Preizkus s pogoji (trials with penalty)

Ko je problem tveganje, jim pusti, da poskusijo, preden se v celoti zavežejo. Brezplačen ali poceni preizkus ima pogoje, na primer udeležbo, opravljene naloge ali uporabo izdelka, in pristojbino, če jih ne izpolnijo ali ne prekličejo pravočasno. Kupec dobi nizko tveganje, ti pa ljudi, ki se res vključijo.

Downsell funkcij (feature downsells)

Ko je problem obseg, odstrani dele in temu primerno znižaj ceno. Manj srečanj, skupina namesto individualno, »naredi sam« namesto »naredimo namesto vas«, en oddelek namesto celega podjetja. Kupec dobi resničen, manjši izdelek. Polna ponudba ostane pri svoji ceni in ostane vrednejša.

Bodi pregleden glede provizij

Če te plačilo s kartico ali na obroke stane provizijo, to povej. Ponudi izbiro: plača provizijo za kartico ali plača z bančnim nakazilom brez nje. Jasni pogoji varujejo zaupanje, veliko kupcev pa bo izbralo cenejšo možnost zate.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Projekt integracije ERP (B2B proizvodnja)

Pred. Svetovalec ponudi 24.000 € za povezavo ERP s proizvodnimi podatki in izdelavo poročil. Vodja obrata reče, da je letošnji proračun porabljen. Posel propade.

Po. Svetovalec vpraša, kaj ga ustavlja. Odgovor: letošnji proračun. Ponudba 1: pol zdaj, pol v novem proračunskem letu. Še vedno ne. Ponudba 2: pilot na eni proizvodni liniji za 6.000 €, znesek pa se odšteje, če razširijo na cel obrat.

Prvi downsell je spremenil čas plačila. Drugi je zmanjšal obseg in hkrati znižal tveganje. Obrat dobi dokaz na eni liniji, svetovalec pa obdrži odnos in jasno pot do celotnega projekta.

Fitnes (lokalno podjetje)

Fitnes prodaja 12-tedenski program za 600 €. Za tiste, ki rečejo ne:

Korak Ponudba Znesek na plačilo
1 Plačilo v celoti, vključen prehranski vodnik 600 € enkrat
2 Pol zdaj, pol čez 30 dni 300 € × 2
3 Tri mesečna plačila 200 € × 3
4 12 tedenskih plačil 50 € × 12

Vsi koraki skupaj znašajo 600 €, fitnes pa se ustavi pri prvem da. Kupcu, ki zavrne vse načrte, ponudi dvotedenski preizkus za 49 €, ki se odšteje, če se vpiše v celoten program.

Vodovodar (lokalno podjetje)

Stranko odbije prenova kopalnice za 3.200 €. Vodovodar ponudi le ključni del: zamenjavo puščajočih cevi in ventila za tuš za 900 € zdaj, preostanek ponudbe pa ostane veljaven šest mesecev. Stranka se strinja, vodovodar pa je prvi v vrsti za preostalo delo.

Pogoste napake

  • Popust na isto stvar. Kaznuje tiste, ki so plačali v celoti, in kupce nauči, da najprej rečejo ne.
  • Vse možnosti naenkrat. Ljudje skočijo na najcenejšo. Stopaj navzdol stopnico za stopnico.
  • Ne vprašaš zakaj. Plačilni načrt ne reši ugovora glede tveganja, preizkus pa ne reši ugovora glede denarja.
  • Skrite provizije in pogoji. Pristojbine pri preizkusu in provizije za kartico moraš navesti vnaprej in pisno.
  • Downselli, ki te stanejo več za izvedbo. Manjša različica mora imeti nižji strošek izvedbe, sicer izgubljaš maržo pri vsakem »ne«.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Nikoli ne dovoli, da »ne« konča pogovor. Privzeto ima pripravljen downsell za vsako ponudbo v zaporedju.
  • Najprej diagnoza, nato downsell. Želi vedeti, ali je ugovor denar, tveganje ali obseg, in temu prilagodi downsell.
  • Pogoje spremeni, preden spremeni izdelek. Plačilni načrti so prvi, ker kupec še vedno dobi polno ponudbo, ti pa obdržiš polno ceno.
  • Stopa navzdol po eno stopnico in se ustavi pri prvem da.
  • Raje odstrani funkcije, kot da zniža ceno. Pravilo palca: nižja cena mora vedno pomeniti manj dostavljenega.
  • Ohrani pot nazaj navzgor. Dobropisi in piloti dajo strankam z downsellom razlog, da pozneje kupijo polno ponudbo.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Kateri so trije glavni razlogi, da ljudje rečejo ne? Kaj se z njimi danes zgodi po »ne«? Kakšno je tvoje zaporedje plačilnih možnosti? Kaj bi lahko odstranil in še vedno dostavil resničen rezultat?

Delovni list

Vzemi svojo glavno ponudbo in zadnjih deset ljudi, ki so rekli ne.

  1. Razlogi: Razvrsti njihove ugovore v denar, tveganje in obseg. Kateri je najpogostejši?
  2. Plačilna lestev: Zapiši svoje zaporedje plačilnih možnosti, od plačila v celoti do mesečnih obrokov.
  3. Preizkus: Kateri preizkus bi lahko ponudil in kateri pogoji in pristojbina bi ljudi spodbudili, da se vključijo?
  4. Manjša različica: Kaj bi lahko odstranil, da dobiš resničen, cenejši izdelek z nižjim stroškom izvedbe?
  5. Pot navzgor: Kako bi se stranka z downsellom pozneje premaknila do polne ponudbe?
