The book in one page

Most businesses sell one thing at one price and wait months for a new customer to pay back what it cost to acquire them. Growth is then limited by cash in the bank: you can only buy as many customers as you can afford to wait for. $100M Money Models is about removing that limit. The book focuses on how you sell to a customer once you have them, and leaves lead generation to $100M Leads.

A money model is a deliberate sequence of offers around a few core products. When it works, the gross profit an average customer generates in the first 30 days exceeds their acquisition cost. That is client-financed acquisition: each customer pays for the next one, so you can spend more on marketing without new capital. The goal is to make more money from each customer, and to make it sooner.

The book follows the order in which a customer meets the offers:

  1. Attraction offers bring the right customer in at a low or even negative net cost.
  2. Upsell offers come right after the yes, solving the next problem the first purchase reveals.
  3. Downsell offers catch the no with a smaller, cheaper or differently structured step.
  4. Continuity offers bill again and again, smoothing cash flow and raising lifetime value.
  5. Assemble and test them one at a time around your core offer, measured by 30-day gross profit against CAC.

How Alex would use this book

  • He reaches for it when a business has a decent offer and leads but can't afford to scale ads, because payback is too slow.
  • He measures CAC and 30-day gross profit per customer first, and judges every change by whether it moves that ratio.
  • He keeps the product list short and creates new offers by changing price, terms, bundling and timing.
  • He adds one offer at a time, starting with whichever piece closes the biggest gap in the first 30 days.
  • He treats covering CAC within 30 days as the threshold, and roughly twice CAC as the point where growth stops being capped by cash (a rule of thumb).

Knjiga na eni strani

Večina podjetij prodaja eno stvar po eni ceni in mesece čaka, da nova stranka povrne strošek svoje pridobitve. Rast je tako omejena z gotovino na računu: kupiš lahko le toliko strank, kolikor si lahko privoščiš čakati. $100M Money Models govori o tem, kako to omejitev odpraviš. Knjiga se ukvarja s tem, kako stranki prodajaš, ko jo že imaš, pridobivanje leadov pa prepusti knjigi $100M Leads.

Money model je premišljeno zaporedje ponudb okoli nekaj glavnih izdelkov. Ko deluje, bruto dobiček, ki ga povprečna stranka ustvari v prvih 30 dneh, preseže strošek njene pridobitve. To je pridobivanje, ki ga financira stranka (client-financed acquisition): vsaka stranka plača naslednjo, zato lahko več vlagaš v marketing brez novega kapitala. Cilj je, da od vsake stranke zaslužiš več in prej.

Knjiga sledi vrstnemu redu, v katerem stranka ponudbe sreča:

  1. Privabljalne ponudbe (attraction offers) pripeljejo pravo stranko ob nizkem ali celo negativnem neto strošku.
  2. Upsell ponudbe pridejo takoj po »da« in rešijo naslednji problem, ki ga razkrije prvi nakup.
  3. Downsell ponudbe ujamejo »ne« z manjšim, cenejšim ali drugače zastavljenim korakom.
  4. Ponudbe naročnine (continuity offers) se zaračunavajo znova in znova, gladijo denarni tok in dvigujejo življenjsko vrednost.
  5. Sestavi in testiraj jih po eno okoli glavne ponudbe, merilo pa je 30-dnevni bruto dobiček v primerjavi s CAC.

Kako bi knjigo uporabil Alex

  • Poseže po njej, ko ima podjetje solidno ponudbo in leade, a si ne more privoščiti večanja oglaševanja, ker se strošek prepočasi povrne.
  • Najprej izmeri CAC in 30-dnevni bruto dobiček na stranko, vsako spremembo pa presoja po tem, ali premakne to razmerje.
  • Seznam izdelkov ohranja kratek, nove ponudbe pa ustvarja s spreminjanjem cene, pogojev, paketov in časa.
  • Ponudbe dodaja po eno, začenši s tisto, ki zapre največjo vrzel v prvih 30 dneh.
  • Pokritje CAC v 30 dneh razume kot prag, približno dvakratnik CAC pa kot točko, kjer rasti ne omejuje več gotovina (pravilo palca).

Continuity OffersPonudbe naročnine

Offers that bill the customer again and again for ongoing value. They turn one sale into recurring revenue, and the work is as much about keeping customers as getting them to join.Ponudbe, ki stranki znova in znova zaračunajo stalno vrednost. Eno prodajo spremenijo v ponavljajoči se prihodek, delo pa je enako v ohranjanju strank kot v tem, da se pridružijo.

Get them to join, then get them to stay. Reward commitment, and longer commitments usually churn less.Najprej, da se pridružijo, nato, da ostanejo. Nagradi zavezo, daljše zaveze pa navadno pomenijo manj odhodov.
