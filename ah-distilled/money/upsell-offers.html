$100M Money Models · Upsell Offer

The offer you make right after someone buys. It solves the next problem the first purchase creates, when trust and buying intent are highest.Ponudba, ki jo daš takoj po nakupu. Reši naslednji problem, ki ga ustvari prvi nakup, takrat, ko sta zaupanje in pripravljenost na nakup najvišja.

The gist

An upsell is a second offer made immediately after the first yes. The moment someone buys is the moment they trust you most and are most ready to act. Their wallet is out and their decision is fresh. Waiting a week throws that away.

Upsell = the next problem the first purchase reveals + offered right after the yes + framed as what makes the first purchase work

Good upsells are not random extras. Every solution creates a new problem: you bought a training plan, now you need to eat right; you bought a data warehouse, now you need reports on it. The upsell sells the answer to that next problem. Alex describes four ways to do it: the classic, the menu, the anchor and the rollover upsell.

How it works

Classic upsell

The logic is "you can't have X without Y". You offer the natural complement to what they just bought, the thing that makes it work or makes it last. The buyer already accepted the problem and the solution, so the complement is an easy next step.

How to do it: 1. List what customers struggle with right after buying your core offer. 2. Package the solution to the most common one. 3. Offer it at the point of sale, framed as completing the first purchase.

Menu upsell

You walk the buyer through a menu of add-ons and act like a guide, not a salesperson. - Unsell first. Tell them what they do not need. This builds trust and makes your recommendations credible. - Prescribe. Tell them what you recommend for their situation and why. - Offer A or B. Ask which of two options they prefer, rather than whether they want anything at all. - Use the card on file. If they already paid, ask if you should add it to the same card. This removes a second payment decision.

Anchor upsell

You show a premium option first, priced well above the core offer (Alex describes something in the range of five to ten times the base price). The buyer reacts to the high price. After that reaction, the core offer looks reasonable by comparison. Some buyers take the premium option, which makes the anchor profitable on its own. The rest buy the core offer with less price resistance.

The premium option must be real and worth its price. It is a genuine offer for the buyers who want the most.

Rollover upsell

You credit what the buyer already spent toward a bigger purchase. "You paid €500 for the starter. Upgrade to the full programme within 14 days and the €500 counts toward it." The buyer does not feel they lose the first payment, so moving up is easier. This also works for customers who bought a while ago and are ready for the next level.

When to use which

Upsell Best when
Classic There is one obvious next problem
Menu There are several add-ons and buyers vary
Anchor Price resistance is the main objection
Rollover Buyers start small and many could go bigger

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik Cloud migration (B2B analytics)

Before. The client signs a fixed-price migration to Qlik Cloud. After go-live the consultant leaves. Three months later the client's reports break after a source change, and they hire someone else.

After. At signing: "Once you are in the cloud, someone has to watch the reloads and fix them when sources change. Most clients add our monitoring and support package. Should I add it to the same invoice, quarterly or yearly?"

This is a classic upsell (you can't run cloud reports without keeping reloads healthy), offered as a choice between two options with the existing invoice. It solves a problem the migration itself creates.

Hair salon (local business)

A client books a cut and colour for €120. At the chair, the stylist says: "You don't need the deep-conditioning treatment, your hair is in good shape. To keep this colour from fading, I'd take the colour-safe shampoo and conditioner. Do you want the set for €38, or just the shampoo for €22? I can add it to your card."

The stylist unsells one item, prescribes another, offers A or B and uses the payment already on file. That is a menu upsell. If 40% of clients take the €38 set, the average ticket rises by about €15 (0.4 × 38 = 15.20).

Business coaching (anchor)

The coach first presents a one-year private programme at €30,000. Then: "Most people start with the six-month group programme at €4,000." The group programme now looks modest. Six months later, a rollover offer credits part of the €4,000 toward the private programme.

Common mistakes

  • Waiting too long. An upsell offered weeks later competes with a cooled decision. Offer it at the point of sale or right after.
  • Random add-ons. An extra that does not solve the next problem feels like a cash grab and lowers trust.
  • Asking open questions. "Anything else?" invites "no". Offer a specific recommendation or two concrete options.
  • A fake anchor. If the premium option is not real or not worth its price, buyers sense the trick.
  • Never unselling. Recommending everything makes every recommendation worthless.

How Alex would apply it

  • He maps the next problem first. Before designing an upsell, he asks what goes wrong for the customer right after they buy, and sells the fix to that.
  • He upsells at the moment of the yes. His default is to make the next offer immediately, while trust and intent are at their peak.
  • He frames it as completing the purchase. The upsell is positioned as what makes the first purchase succeed, not as a second sale.
  • He removes the second payment decision. Using the card on file or the same invoice is one of his standard moves.
  • He judges upsells by contribution to 30-day gross profit. As a rule of thumb, an upsell that many buyers take is often what turns a break-even front end into one that funds growth.
  • Questions he would ask: What problem shows up right after they buy? What do your best customers buy next, and when? What do you tell them they don't need? What is your most expensive option, and do you show it first?

Worksheet

Use your core offer and a list of your last ten customers.

  1. Next problem: What problem does a customer face in the first weeks after buying from you?
  2. Classic: What is the "you can't have X without Y" product or service you could offer?
  3. Menu: Which add-ons would you unsell, and which would you prescribe?
  4. Anchor: What would a premium option at five to ten times your base price include?
  5. Rollover: How could past purchases count toward a bigger next step?
  6. Timing: At which exact moment in your sales process will you present the upsell?

Bistvo

Upsell je druga ponudba, ki jo daš takoj po prvem »da«. Trenutek nakupa je trenutek, ko ti kupec najbolj zaupa in je najbolj pripravljen ukrepati. Denarnica je odprta, odločitev sveža. Če čakaš teden dni, to zapraviš.

Upsell = naslednji problem, ki ga razkrije prvi nakup + ponujen takoj po da + predstavljen kot tisto, zaradi česar prvi nakup deluje

Dobri upselli niso naključni dodatki. Vsaka rešitev ustvari nov problem: kupil si program vadbe, zdaj se moraš pravilno prehranjevati; kupil si podatkovno skladišče, zdaj potrebuješ poročila na njem. Upsell proda odgovor na ta naslednji problem. Alex opiše štiri načine: klasični, meni, sidrni in rollover upsell.

Kako deluje

Klasični upsell (Classic upsell)

Logika je »ne moreš imeti X brez Y«. Ponudiš naravno dopolnilo k temu, kar je kupec pravkar kupil, tisto, kar poskrbi, da deluje ali traja. Kupec je problem in rešitev že sprejel, zato je dopolnilo lahek naslednji korak.

Kako to narediš: 1. Naštej, s čim se stranke mučijo takoj po nakupu glavne ponudbe. 2. Zapakiraj rešitev za najpogostejšo težavo. 3. Ponudi jo ob nakupu, predstavljeno kot dokončanje prvega nakupa.

Meni upsell (Menu upsell)

Kupca vodiš skozi meni dodatkov in se obnašaš kot vodnik, ne kot prodajalec. - Najprej odsvetuj. Povej mu, česa ne potrebuje. To gradi zaupanje in naredi tvoja priporočila verodostojna. - Predpiši. Povej, kaj priporočaš za njegov položaj in zakaj. - Ponudi A ali B. Vprašaj, katero od dveh možnosti ima raje, namesto ali sploh želi kaj. - Uporabi shranjeno kartico (card on file). Če je že plačal, vprašaj, ali dodaš na isto kartico. S tem odpraviš drugo odločitev o plačilu.

Sidrni upsell (Anchor upsell)

Najprej pokažeš premium možnost z ceno precej nad glavno ponudbo (Alex opisuje nekaj v razponu od pet- do desetkratnika osnovne cene). Kupec se odzove na visoko ceno. Po tem odzivu je glavna ponudba v primerjavi videti razumna. Nekateri kupci vzamejo premium možnost, zato je sidro donosno že samo po sebi. Ostali kupijo glavno ponudbo z manj odpora do cene.

Premium možnost mora biti resnična in vredna svoje cene. Je prava ponudba za kupce, ki želijo največ.

Rollover upsell

Kar je kupec že porabil, mu priznaš kot dobropis za večji nakup. »Za začetni paket ste plačali 500 €. Če v 14 dneh preidete na celoten program, se 500 € odšteje.« Kupec nima občutka, da prvo plačilo izgubi, zato je prehod navzgor lažji. Deluje tudi pri strankah, ki so kupile pred časom in so pripravljene na naslednjo stopnjo.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Selitev v Qlik Cloud (B2B analitika)

Pred. Stranka podpiše selitev v Qlik Cloud po fiksni ceni. Po zagonu svetovalec odide. Tri mesece pozneje se stranki po spremembi vira poročila pokvarijo, in najame nekoga drugega.

Po. Ob podpisu: »Ko boste v oblaku, mora nekdo spremljati osveževanja in jih popraviti, ko se viri spremenijo. Večina strank doda naš paket nadzora in podpore. Ga dodam na isti račun, četrtletno ali letno?«

To je klasični upsell (oblačnih poročil ne moreš uporabljati, če ne skrbiš za osveževanja), ponujen kot izbira med dvema možnostma na obstoječem računu. Reši problem, ki ga ustvari že sama selitev.

Frizerski salon (lokalno podjetje)

Stranka rezervira striženje in barvanje za 120 €. Na stolu frizerka reče: »Globinske nege ne potrebujete, lasje so v dobrem stanju. Da barva ne zbledi, bi vzela šampon in balzam za barvane lase. Želite komplet za 38 € ali samo šampon za 22 €? Lahko dodam na vašo kartico.«

Frizerka eno stvar odsvetuje, drugo predpiše, ponudi A ali B in uporabi plačilo, ki je že na voljo. To je meni upsell. Če komplet za 38 € vzame 40 % strank, se povprečni račun zviša za približno 15 € (0,4 × 38 = 15,20).

Poslovni coaching (sidro)

Coach najprej predstavi enoletni zasebni program za 30.000 €. Nato: »Večina začne s šestmesečnim skupinskim programom za 4.000 €.« Skupinski program je zdaj videti skromen. Šest mesecev pozneje rollover ponudba del od 4.000 € prizna kot dobropis za zasebni program.

Pogoste napake

  • Predolgo čakanje. Upsell, ponujen tedne pozneje, tekmuje z ohlajeno odločitvijo. Ponudi ga ob nakupu ali takoj po njem.
  • Naključni dodatki. Dodatek, ki ne reši naslednjega problema, deluje kot grabljenje denarja in zniža zaupanje.
  • Odprta vprašanja. »Še kaj?« vabi k »ne«. Ponudi konkretno priporočilo ali dve konkretni možnosti.
  • Lažno sidro. Če premium možnost ni resnična ali ni vredna svoje cene, kupci zvijačo začutijo.
  • Nikoli ne odsvetuješ. Če priporočaš vse, je vsako priporočilo brez vrednosti.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Najprej popiše naslednji problem. Preden zasnuje upsell, vpraša, kaj gre stranki narobe takoj po nakupu, in proda rešitev za to.
  • Upsell ponudi v trenutku »da«. Privzeto naslednjo ponudbo da takoj, dokler sta zaupanje in namera na vrhuncu.
  • Predstavi ga kot dokončanje nakupa. Upsell je postavljen kot tisto, zaradi česar prvi nakup uspe, ne kot druga prodaja.
  • Odpravi drugo odločitev o plačilu. Uporaba shranjene kartice ali istega računa je ena njegovih standardnih potez.
  • Upselle presoja po prispevku k 30-dnevnemu bruto dobičku. Pravilo palca: upsell, ki ga vzame veliko kupcev, je pogosto tisto, kar vstopno ponudbo na pragu rentabilnosti spremeni v takšno, ki financira rast.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Kateri problem se pojavi takoj po nakupu? Kaj tvoje najboljše stranke kupijo naslednje in kdaj? Kaj jim odsvetuješ? Katera je tvoja najdražja možnost in ali jo pokažeš prvo?

Delovni list

Uporabi svojo glavno ponudbo in seznam zadnjih desetih strank.

  1. Naslednji problem: S katerim problemom se stranka sooči v prvih tednih po nakupu pri tebi?
  2. Klasični: Kateri izdelek ali storitev »ne moreš imeti X brez Y« bi lahko ponudil?
  3. Meni: Katere dodatke bi odsvetoval in katere predpisal?
  4. Sidro: Kaj bi vključevala premium možnost za pet- do desetkratnik osnovne cene?
  5. Rollover: Kako bi pretekli nakupi šteli k večjemu naslednjemu koraku?
  6. Čas: V katerem točnem trenutku prodajnega procesa boš predstavil upsell?
