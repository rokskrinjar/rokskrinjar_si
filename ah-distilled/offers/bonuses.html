$100M Offers · Bonuses

Extras added to the core offer, each built to remove one specific reason the buyer might not buy. Done well, the bonuses alone are worth more than the price.Dodatki k jedru ponudbe, od katerih je vsak narejen za odstranitev enega konkretnega razloga, zakaj kupec morda ne bi kupil. Dobro narejeni bonusi so sami vredni več od cene.

The gist

A bonus is a sales tool with one job. Every hesitation a buyer has ("my team won't use it", "I don't have time", "what if it breaks") is a problem you can solve with a separate, named piece of the offer. When each worry has its own answer, the buyer runs out of reasons to say no.

Obstacle → bonus that solves it → name + stated value · Sum of bonus values > price of the core offer

Bonuses also change how the offer is perceived. One offer with ten features is one thing to evaluate. One core offer followed by five bonuses, each with its own value, feels like the buyer gets far more than they pay. The same contents, split and presented this way, are worth more in the buyer's head.

How it works

Start from the problems list

In the offer-building process, you list every problem and obstacle the buyer faces before, during and after buying. The core offer solves the main problem. The bonuses come from the rest of that list. For each remaining obstacle ask: what small thing could I add that makes this worry go away?

Obstacle Bonus type
"I won't know how to start" Quick-start checklist, onboarding call
"My team won't adopt it" Training kit, internal launch email templates
"I don't have time" Done-for-you setup, templates, scripts
"What if I get stuck?" Support channel, office hours, Q&A recordings
"I can't convince my boss" Business-case template, ROI calculator

One bonus per obstacle

Several smaller bonuses usually beat one large bonus. Each one speaks to its own worry, and each one adds its own value to the stack. When you present a bonus, say which obstacle it solves: "Because most teams worry that users won't log in after week two, you also get the adoption kit."

Name every bonus

A named bonus feels like a real thing with a real price. "Templates" is vague. "The Board-Ready Report Pack" is a product. Use the same naming logic as for the main offer: say who it is for and what result it gives.

State the value of every bonus

Give each bonus a value, and make it believable. Good anchors are what you would charge for it separately, what it cost to produce, or what a client would pay someone else to do it. Values without any basis lower trust. If you can, sell the bonus separately somewhere, so the value is real.

The bonuses together should outweigh the core

A rule of thumb from the book: the total value of the bonuses should be greater than the core offer itself. The buyer should feel that the bonuses alone would justify the price. This is what makes an offer feel unfair in the buyer's favour.

Present bonuses after the core offer

The order matters. First present the core offer and its price, so the buyer evaluates the main thing. Then reveal the bonuses one at a time, each with its obstacle, name and value. Each addition raises the perceived value while the price stays the same. If you mix bonuses into the core description, the stacking effect is lost.

Cheap for you, valuable to them

The best bonuses cost little to deliver again: templates, checklists, recordings, scripts, software you already built. Their value to the buyer comes from saving time and effort, so they can be very high value at almost no marginal cost.

Bonuses from partners

You can include other businesses' products or services as bonuses. A partner gives your buyers a free session, a discount or an audit. The partner gets new customers, you get a bonus with a real market value at no cost, and the buyer gets more. Choose partners whose offer solves one of your buyer's obstacles.

Bonuses and urgency or scarcity

Bonuses are a natural place for time and quantity limits. "This bonus is included for orders this month" or "the first ten get the setup call" creates a reason to act without changing the price of the core.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik month-end close programme (B2B)

Core offer: the Month-End in 3 Days programme for CFOs, fixed price €9,800.

Obstacle Bonus Value
"The board won't understand new reports" Board-Ready Report Pack €1,200
"We still rely on old Excel reports" Legacy Report Rescue (5 reports rebuilt) €3,000
"My team won't use it" Adoption Kit: training videos and launch emails €1,500
"What if something breaks after go-live?" 90-Day Support Line €2,400
"Our data is messy" Data Quality Check, delivered by a partner firm €2,500
Total bonus value €10,600

The bonuses add up to €10,600, which is more than the €9,800 core price. Each answers a real objection CFOs raise. The Data Quality Check comes from a partner and costs Rok nothing to deliver.

Gym 12-week programme (local business)

Before. "12-week programme, €600. Includes training plan, nutrition advice, app access and a free friend pass."

After. "12-week programme, €600. Plus, because most people don't know what to eat: the 12-Week Meal Plan (€200). Because many fear injury: three check-ins with our partner physiotherapist (€180). Because it's easier with company: the Bring-a-Friend Pass (€120). Because people forget the technique: the Form-Check Video Library (€150). Total bonus value €650."

The contents are almost the same. The difference is that each piece now answers a fear, has a name and a value, and comes after the price.

Common mistakes

  • Random bonuses. A bonus that solves no obstacle adds noise. If you cannot finish the sentence "because many worry that…", drop it.
  • Unnamed, unvalued extras. "Plus some templates" adds almost nothing to perceived value. Name it and price it.
  • Inflated values. A €5,000 checklist is not believed and lowers trust in the whole offer. Anchor values in something real.
  • Bonuses in the core description. Mixing them in removes the stacking effect. Price first, bonuses after.
  • Bonuses that add work. A bonus that requires more effort from the buyer (another course to watch) can lower value. Prefer bonuses that save time.

How Alex would apply it

  • He builds bonuses from objections. He collects the reasons people gave for not buying and turns each one into a bonus.
  • He stacks many small bonuses rather than one big one, and presents them one by one after the price.
  • He makes the bonuses worth more than the core. His rule of thumb is that the buyer should feel the bonuses alone justify the purchase.
  • He favours bonuses with low delivery cost and high perceived value, such as templates, scripts, recordings and tools that save the buyer time.
  • He uses partners for bonuses because it adds real value without cost, and it builds relationships that can send leads back.
  • He attaches urgency or scarcity to bonuses instead of discounting the core price.
  • Questions he would ask: Why did the last ten people who didn't buy say no? Which of those reasons does your core offer not address? What do you already have that solves it? What would each bonus cost the buyer to get elsewhere? Which partner already sells something your buyers need?

Worksheet

Take your core offer and its price. Answer in writing.

  1. Obstacles: List every reason a buyer might hesitate before, during and after buying. Aim for at least ten.
  2. Match: For the top five, write one bonus that removes each obstacle.
  3. Name and value: Give each bonus a name and a value you could defend. Add them up. Is the total higher than the core price?
  4. Partners: Which business could provide one bonus in exchange for access to your clients?
  5. Script: Write how you would present the stack: core and price first, then each bonus with "because…", its name and its value.

Bistvo

Bonus je prodajno orodje z eno nalogo. Vsak kupčev pomislek (»ekipa tega ne bo uporabljala«, »nimam časa«, »kaj, če se pokvari«) je problem, ki ga lahko rešiš z ločenim, poimenovanim delom ponudbe. Ko ima vsaka skrb svoj odgovor, kupcu zmanjka razlogov za ne.

Ovira → bonus, ki jo reši → ime + navedena vrednost · Vsota vrednosti bonusov > cena jedra ponudbe

Bonusi spremenijo tudi to, kako kupec ponudbo dojema. Ena ponudba z desetimi funkcijami je ena stvar za presojo. Jedro ponudbe, ki mu sledi pet bonusov, vsak s svojo vrednostjo, pa daje občutek, da kupec dobi veliko več, kot plača. Ista vsebina, razdeljena in predstavljena tako, je v kupčevi glavi vredna več.

Kako deluje

Začni pri seznamu problemov

Pri gradnji ponudbe naštej vse probleme in ovire, s katerimi se kupec sreča pred nakupom, med njim in po njem. Jedro ponudbe reši glavni problem. Bonusi pridejo iz preostanka seznama. Pri vsaki preostali oviri se vprašaj: kaj majhnega lahko dodam, da ta skrb izgine?

Ovira Vrsta bonusa
»Ne bom vedel, kako začeti« Kontrolni seznam za hiter začetek, klic za uvajanje
»Ekipa tega ne bo sprejela« Komplet za usposabljanje, predloge e-pošte za interni zagon
»Nimam časa« Nastavitev namesto vas, predloge, skripte
»Kaj, če se zataknem?« Kanal za podporo, termini za vprašanja, posnetki Q&A
»Šefa ne bom prepričal« Predloga poslovnega primera, kalkulator donosnosti

En bonus za eno oviro

Več manjših bonusov navadno premaga en velik bonus. Vsak nagovori svojo skrb in vsak doda svojo vrednost v sklad. Ko bonus predstaviš, povej, katero oviro reši: »Ker se večina ekip boji, da se uporabniki po drugem tednu ne bodo več prijavljali, dobite tudi komplet za uvajanje.«

Poimenuj vsak bonus

Poimenovan bonus deluje kot prava stvar s pravo ceno. »Predloge« je nejasno. »Paket poročil za upravo« je izdelek. Uporabi isto logiko kot pri imenu glavne ponudbe (glej MAGIC): povej, za koga je in kakšen rezultat da.

Navedi vrednost vsakega bonusa

Vsakemu bonusu daj vrednost, in naj bo verjetna. Dobra sidra so: koliko bi zanj zaračunal posebej, koliko je stala izdelava ali koliko bi stranka plačala nekomu drugemu, da to naredi. Vrednosti brez podlage znižajo zaupanje. Če lahko, bonus nekje prodajaj tudi posebej, da je vrednost resnična.

Bonusi skupaj naj odtehtajo jedro

Pravilo palca iz knjige: skupna vrednost bonusov naj bo večja od samega jedra ponudbe. Kupec naj čuti, da bi že bonusi sami upravičili ceno. Zaradi tega ponudba deluje nepošteno v kupčevo korist.

Bonuse predstavi po jedru ponudbe

Vrstni red je pomemben. Najprej predstavi jedro ponudbe in ceno, da kupec oceni glavno stvar. Nato razkrivaj bonuse enega za drugim, vsakega z oviro, imenom in vrednostjo. Vsak dodatek dvigne zaznano vrednost, cena pa ostane enaka. Če bonuse pomešaš v opis jedra, se učinek zlaganja izgubi.

Poceni zate, dragoceno zanje

Najboljši bonusi te stanejo malo, ko jih dostaviš znova: predloge, kontrolni seznami, posnetki, skripte, programska oprema, ki si jo že zgradil. Njihova vrednost za kupca izhaja iz prihranka časa in truda, zato imajo lahko zelo visoko vrednost ob skoraj ničelnem dodatnem strošku.

Bonusi partnerjev

Kot bonus lahko vključiš izdelke ali storitve drugih podjetij. Partner tvojim kupcem podari brezplačen termin, popust ali pregled. Partner dobi nove stranke, ti dobiš bonus z resnično tržno vrednostjo brez stroškov, kupec pa dobi več. Izberi partnerje, katerih ponudba reši eno od kupčevih ovir.

Bonusi ter nujnost in pomanjkanje

Bonusi so naravno mesto za časovne in količinske omejitve. »Ta bonus je vključen za naročila ta mesec« ali »prvih deset dobi klic za nastavitev« ustvari razlog za ukrepanje, ne da bi spremenil ceno jedra.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Qlik program za mesečno zapiranje (B2B)

Jedro ponudbe: program Zaprt mesec v 3 dneh za finančne direktorje, fiksna cena 9.800 €.

Ovira Bonus Vrednost
»Uprava novih poročil ne bo razumela« Paket poročil za upravo 1.200 €
»Še vedno se zanašamo na stara Excel poročila« Reševanje starih poročil (5 poročil na novo) 3.000 €
»Ekipa tega ne bo uporabljala« Komplet za uvajanje: video usposabljanja in e-pošta za zagon 1.500 €
»Kaj, če se po zagonu kaj pokvari?« 90-dnevna linija za podporo 2.400 €
»Naši podatki so neurejeni« Pregled kakovosti podatkov, ki ga izvede partnersko podjetje 2.500 €
Skupna vrednost bonusov 10.600 €

Bonusi skupaj znašajo 10.600 €, kar je več od cene jedra 9.800 €. Vsak odgovori na resničen ugovor, ki ga finančni direktorji izrečejo. Pregled kakovosti podatkov pride od partnerja in Roka ne stane nič.

12-tedenski program v fitnesu (lokalni posel)

Pred. »12-tedenski program, 600 €. Vključuje plan vadbe, prehranske nasvete, dostop do aplikacije in brezplačno vstopnico za prijatelja.«

Po. »12-tedenski program, 600 €. Poleg tega, ker večina ne ve, kaj jesti: 12-tedenski jedilnik (200 €). Ker se mnogi bojijo poškodb: trije pregledi pri našem partnerskem fizioterapevtu (180 €). Ker je v družbi lažje: vstopnica Pripelji prijatelja (120 €). Ker ljudje pozabijo tehniko: knjižnica videov za pravilno izvedbo (150 €). Skupna vrednost bonusov 650 €.«

Vsebina je skoraj enaka. Razlika je v tem, da vsak del zdaj odgovori na strah, ima ime in vrednost ter pride po ceni.

Pogoste napake

  • Naključni bonusi. Bonus, ki ne reši nobene ovire, doda šum. Če ne moreš dokončati stavka »ker se mnogi bojijo, da …«, ga odstrani.
  • Dodatki brez imena in vrednosti. »Pa še nekaj predlog« zaznani vrednosti doda skoraj nič. Poimenuj ga in mu določi vrednost.
  • Napihnjene vrednosti. Kontrolnemu seznamu za 5.000 € nihče ne verjame, in to zniža zaupanje v celotno ponudbo. Vrednosti zasidraj v nekaj resničnega.
  • Bonusi v opisu jedra. Če jih pomešaš, izgubiš učinek zlaganja. Najprej cena, potem bonusi.
  • Bonusi, ki dodajo delo. Bonus, ki od kupca zahteva več truda (še en tečaj za ogled), lahko vrednost zniža. Raje izberi bonuse, ki prihranijo čas.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Bonuse gradi iz ugovorov. Zbere razloge, ki so jih ljudje navedli, ko niso kupili, in vsakega spremeni v bonus.
  • Zloži veliko majhnih bonusov namesto enega velikega in jih predstavi enega za drugim po ceni.
  • Poskrbi, da so bonusi vredni več od jedra. Njegovo pravilo palca je, da mora kupec čutiti, da bi že bonusi sami upravičili nakup.
  • Ima raje bonuse z nizkim stroškom dostave in visoko zaznano vrednostjo, kot so predloge, skripte, posnetki in orodja, ki kupcu prihranijo čas.
  • Za bonuse uporabi partnerje, ker to doda resnično vrednost brez stroškov in zgradi odnose, ki lahko pošiljajo leade nazaj.
  • Nujnost ali pomanjkanje pripne na bonuse, namesto da bi znižal ceno jedra.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Zakaj je zadnjih deset ljudi, ki niso kupili, reklo ne? Katerih od teh razlogov tvoje jedro ponudbe ne naslovi? Kaj že imaš, kar to reši? Koliko bi kupca vsak bonus stal drugje? Kateri partner že prodaja nekaj, kar tvoji kupci potrebujejo?

Delovni list

Vzemi jedro svoje ponudbe in njegovo ceno. Odgovori pisno.

  1. Ovire: Naštej vse razloge, zakaj bi kupec lahko oklevel pred nakupom, med njim in po njem. Cilj je vsaj deset.
  2. Ujemanje: Za prvih pet zapiši po en bonus, ki odstrani vsako oviro.
  3. Ime in vrednost: Vsakemu bonusu daj ime in vrednost, ki bi jo lahko zagovarjal. Seštej. Je vsota višja od cene jedra?
  4. Partnerji: Katero podjetje bi lahko prispevalo en bonus v zameno za dostop do tvojih strank?
  5. Scenarij: Zapiši, kako bi predstavil sklad: najprej jedro in cena, nato vsak bonus s »ker …«, imenom in vrednostjo.
