$100M Offers · MAGIC Naming Formula

A checklist for naming an offer so the name alone tells the right buyer that it is for them and what they will get. A good name attracts better leads and lets you refresh the same offer when ads get tired.Kontrolni seznam za ime ponudbe, da že samo ime pravemu kupcu pove, da je zanj in kaj bo dobil. Dobro ime privabi boljše leade in omogoča, da isto ponudbo osvežiš, ko se oglasi obrabijo.

The gist

The name is the first part of the offer a buyer sees, often before any price or detail. A generic name ("Consulting Package B", "Monthly Membership") puts you back in the commodity trap, because it looks like everything else. A specific name tells the right person, at a glance, that this was made for them and what it will do.

Magnetic reason why · Announce the avatar · Give them a goal · Indicate a time interval · Complete with a container word

MAGIC is a checklist. You do not need every element. Most strong names use three to five of the five. Names also wear out. The same core offer can be renamed and repackaged for a new season or a new audience, which keeps ads and outreach feeling fresh without rebuilding the offer.

How it works

M: Make a magnetic reason why

A reason for the offer to exist right now. It answers the buyer's unspoken question "why is this being offered?". Good reasons: a season ("Spring"), a launch ("Grand Opening"), an anniversary, a holiday, a new year, a change in the market. A reason why also creates a natural deadline, so it pairs well with urgency.

A: Announce the avatar

Say who it is for. "For CFOs", "for new mums", "for manufacturers", "for dentists". Naming the avatar makes the right people feel seen and tells the wrong people to move on. That is the point: you want fewer, better-fit leads. The narrower the avatar, the stronger the pull on the people inside it.

G: Give them a goal

The dream outcome in a few words. "Clean Reports", "Pain-Free Back", "Month-End in 3 Days". Use the buyer's language and the result they want, not the method you use. Be careful with claims: in regulated areas (health, finance) or on ad platforms, specific promises may not be allowed, so phrase the goal so that it is both appealing and compliant.

I: Indicate a time interval

How long it takes. "30-Day", "6-Week", "in 90 Days". A time frame makes the outcome feel concrete and achievable, and it lowers perceived time delay in the value equation. Only state a time frame your delivery can actually meet.

C: Complete with a container word

A word that turns a list of things into a product. Examples: programme, system, blueprint, challenge, accelerator, bootcamp, intensive, masterclass, sprint, club. The container word also sets expectations: a "challenge" sounds short and intense, a "club" sounds ongoing, a "blueprint" sounds like a plan you follow.

Putting it together

Element Example
Magnetic reason why Autumn
Avatar for Production Managers
Goal Clean Reports
Interval 30-Day
Container System
Name Autumn 30-Day Clean Reports System for Production Managers

Then cut. Long names are fine for a landing page headline. For an ad or an email subject, keep the two or three strongest elements: "30-Day Clean Reports System".

Rename to refresh

Ad audiences get tired of seeing the same message. When response drops, a new name and angle often bring it back while the delivery stays exactly the same. Rotate the reason why with the season, try a different avatar for a different segment, or change the container. Track which names bring the best leads, not just the most clicks.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

BI consulting for CFOs (B2B)

Before. "Qlik Consulting Package, Level 2."

After. "The Month-End in 3 Days Programme for CFOs." In January the same offer runs as "New Year Fast-Close Sprint for Mid-Size Manufacturers".

The first name uses avatar (CFOs), goal and interval together (month-end in three days) and container (programme). The January version adds a reason why (New Year) and targets a narrower avatar, without changing what is delivered.

Hair salon (local business)

Before. "Hair treatment package."

After. "Summer Frizz-Free 6-Week Programme for Curly Hair."

Reason why (summer humidity), goal (frizz-free), interval (6-week), container (programme) and avatar (curly hair). Someone with straight hair skips it, which is fine. Someone with curly hair feels it was made for them.

Common mistakes

  • Naming the process. "Package B", "Premium Tier", "Implementation Service" say nothing about who it is for or what they get.
  • Trying to fit everyone. Leaving out the avatar to avoid excluding anyone makes the name weaker for everyone.
  • Forcing all five elements. A name with every element can become unreadable. Keep what is strongest and cut the rest.
  • Promising what you can't deliver. An interval or a goal in the name is a claim. If "30-Day" isn't true, the name becomes a source of refunds.
  • Never renaming. Running the same name for years while ad response falls, instead of testing a fresh angle.

How Alex would apply it

  • He treats the name as part of the offer, because it decides who stops to look at all.
  • He leads with the avatar and the goal, since those two elements do most of the filtering and attracting.
  • He uses the reason why to create a natural deadline, so naming and urgency work together.
  • He drafts many names and picks the one buyers would repeat to a friend or colleague, not the one that sounds clever.
  • He renames instead of rebuilding. When ads fatigue, his first move is a new name and angle on the same delivery.
  • He checks platform and legal rules before putting a specific claim in a name, and adjusts wording to stay compliant.
  • Questions he would ask: Who exactly is this for? What result do they want, in their words? Why is this being offered now? How long does it take? What would you call it if you had to fit it in a headline? How long has the current name been running?

Worksheet

Take your main offer and name it in writing.

  1. Fill in MAGIC: Write one option for each letter: reason why, avatar, goal, interval, container.
  2. Draft five names: Combine the elements in different ways. At least one should use only three elements.
  3. Test: Read each name to someone in your target audience. Which one do they understand fastest and repeat back?
  4. Short version: Cut your best name to the two or three strongest words for an ad or email subject.
  5. Refresh plan: Write the names you would use for the next three seasons or segments, keeping the same delivery.

Bistvo

Ime je prvi del ponudbe, ki ga kupec vidi, pogosto še pred ceno ali podrobnostmi. Splošno ime (»Svetovalni paket B«, »Mesečna članarina«) te vrne v past blaga, ker je videti kot vse drugo. Konkretno ime pravi osebi na prvi pogled pove, da je bilo narejeno zanjo in kaj bo naredilo.

Magnetic reason why · Announce the avatar · Give them a goal · Indicate a time interval · Complete with a container word

Kratica MAGIC ostaja v angleščini. Pomeni: M magnetičen razlog, zakaj ponudba obstaja zdaj; A povej, za koga je (avatar); G daj jim cilj; I navedi časovni okvir; C zaključi z besedo za paket (vsebnik). MAGIC je kontrolni seznam. Ni treba uporabiti vseh elementov. Večina močnih imen uporabi tri do pet od petih. Imena se tudi obrabijo. Isto jedro ponudbe lahko preimenuješ in prepakiraš za novo sezono ali novo občinstvo, tako da oglasi in outreach delujejo sveže, ne da bi ponudbo gradil na novo.

Kako deluje

M: magnetičen razlog (Make a magnetic reason why)

Razlog, da ponudba obstaja ravno zdaj. Odgovori na kupčevo neizrečeno vprašanje »zakaj mi to ponujajo?«. Dobri razlogi: sezona (»Pomladni«), otvoritev, obletnica, praznik, novo leto, sprememba na trgu. Razlog ustvari tudi naraven rok, zato se dobro ujema z nujnostjo (urgency).

A: povej, za koga je (Announce the avatar)

Povej, komu je namenjena. »Za finančne direktorje«, »za mamice«, »za proizvajalce«, »za zobozdravnike«. Ko imenuješ avatarja, se pravi ljudje počutijo opažene, napačnim pa poveš, naj gredo naprej. Prav to je namen: želiš manj leadov, ki se bolje ujemajo. Ožji kot je avatar, močnejša je privlačnost za ljudi znotraj njega.

G: daj jim cilj (Give them a goal)

Sanjski izid v nekaj besedah. »Čista poročila«, »Hrbet brez bolečin«, »Zaprt mesec v 3 dneh«. Uporabi kupčev jezik in rezultat, ki ga želi, ne metode, ki jo uporabljaš ti. Previdno s trditvami: na reguliranih področjih (zdravje, finance) ali na oglaševalskih platformah konkretne obljube morda niso dovoljene, zato cilj oblikuj tako, da je privlačen in skladen s pravili.

I: navedi časovni okvir (Indicate a time interval)

Koliko časa traja. »30-dnevni«, »6-tedenski«, »v 90 dneh«. Časovni okvir naredi izid konkreten in dosegljiv ter zniža zaznani časovni zamik v enačbi vrednosti. Navedi le okvir, ki ga tvoja izvedba res zmore.

C: zaključi z besedo za paket (Complete with a container word)

Beseda, ki seznam stvari spremeni v izdelek. Primeri: program, sistem, načrt (blueprint), izziv, pospeševalnik, bootcamp, intenziv, masterclass, sprint, klub. Beseda za paket postavi tudi pričakovanja: »izziv« zveni kratko in intenzivno, »klub« zveni trajno, »načrt« zveni kot pot, ki ji slediš.

Preimenuj za osvežitev

Občinstvo oglasov se naveliča istega sporočila. Ko odziv pade, novo ime in kot pogosto vrneta odziv, izvedba pa ostane popolnoma enaka. Razlog zamenjaj s sezono, preizkusi drugega avatarja za drug segment ali spremeni besedo za paket. Spremljaj, katera imena prinesejo najboljše leade, ne le največ klikov.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

BI svetovanje za finančne direktorje (B2B)

Pred. »Qlik svetovalni paket, stopnja 2.«

Po. »Program Zaprt mesec v 3 dneh za finančne direktorje.« Januarja ista ponudba teče kot »Novoletni sprint za hitro zapiranje za srednje velike proizvajalce«.

Prvo ime združi avatarja (finančni direktorji), cilj in časovni okvir (zaprt mesec v treh dneh) ter besedo za paket (program). Januarska različica doda razlog (novo leto) in cilja na ožjega avatarja, ne da bi spremenila, kaj se dostavi.

Frizerski salon (lokalni posel)

Pred. »Paket nege las.«

Po. »Poletni 6-tedenski program za kodraste lase brez našopirjenosti.«

Razlog (poletna vlaga), cilj (brez našopirjenosti), časovni okvir (6-tedenski), beseda za paket (program) in avatar (kodrasti lasje). Nekdo z ravnimi lasmi ga preskoči, in to je v redu. Nekdo s kodrastimi lasmi začuti, da je narejen zanj.

Pogoste napake

  • Poimenovanje procesa. »Paket B«, »Premium raven«, »Storitev implementacije« ne povedo nič o tem, za koga je in kaj dobi.
  • Poskus, da bi ustrezalo vsem. Če izpustiš avatarja, da ne bi koga izključil, je ime šibkejše za vse.
  • Vsiljevanje vseh petih elementov. Ime z vsemi elementi lahko postane neberljivo. Obdrži najmočnejše in odreži ostalo.
  • Obljubljanje nečesa, česar ne zmoreš. Časovni okvir ali cilj v imenu je trditev. Če »30-dnevni« ni res, ime postane vir vračil.
  • Nikoli preimenovati. Isto ime leta in leta, medtem ko odziv na oglase pada, namesto da bi preizkusil svež kot.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Ime obravnava kot del ponudbe, ker odloči, kdo se sploh ustavi in pogleda.
  • Začne z avatarjem in ciljem, ker ta dva elementa opravita večino filtriranja in privabljanja.
  • Z razlogom ustvari naraven rok, tako da poimenovanje in nujnost delujeta skupaj.
  • Napiše veliko imen in izbere tisto, ki bi ga kupci ponovili prijatelju ali sodelavcu, ne tistega, ki zveni pametno.
  • Preimenuje namesto da gradi na novo. Ko se oglasi obrabijo, je njegova prva poteza novo ime in kot na isti izvedbi.
  • Preveri pravila platform in zakonodaje, preden v ime da konkretno trditev, in besede prilagodi, da ostanejo skladne.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Za koga točno je to? Kakšen rezultat želijo, z njihovimi besedami? Zakaj se to ponuja zdaj? Koliko časa traja? Kako bi to imenoval, če bi moralo biti v naslovu? Kako dolgo že teče trenutno ime?

Delovni list

Vzemi svojo glavno ponudbo in jo pisno poimenuj.

  1. Izpolni MAGIC: Za vsako črko zapiši eno možnost: razlog, avatar, cilj, časovni okvir, beseda za paket.
  2. Pet osnutkov imen: Elemente kombiniraj na različne načine. Vsaj eno ime naj uporabi le tri elemente.
  3. Test: Vsako ime preberi nekomu iz ciljne skupine. Katerega najhitreje razume in ga ponovi?
  4. Kratka različica: Najboljše ime skrajšaj na dve ali tri najmočnejše besede za oglas ali zadevo e-pošte.
  5. Načrt osvežitve: Zapiši imena, ki bi jih uporabil za naslednje tri sezone ali segmente, z enako izvedbo.
