Commit to a NicheZaveza niši

$100M Offers · Commit to a Niche

Narrow your market until the offer sounds made for one specific buyer, then stay there. A narrower offer is more relevant, more believable and can charge more.Zoži trg, dokler ponudba ne zveni, kot da je narejena za enega specifičnega kupca, nato tam ostani. Ožja ponudba je bolj relevantna, bolj verjetna in lahko stane več.

The gist

A narrower market looks like fewer customers. In practice it is the opposite of a loss. "Accounting services" is a commodity that competes with every bookkeeper in town. "Accounting for dental practices" speaks to one owner's exact situation, so it sounds more relevant, feels more likely to work and supports a higher price. The product inside can be almost the same. The positioning changes what it is worth.

Narrower market → more relevant offer → higher perceived likelihood → higher price

The second half of the idea is commitment. Picking a niche is easy. Staying with it long enough to learn its language, collect proof and become known is what makes it pay. When results are slow, Alex's default is to keep the market and fix the offer, not to jump to a new market and start from zero.

How it works

Why narrowing raises value

A specific offer moves several levers of the Value Equation at once:

  • Perceived likelihood rises. A buyer believes something built for people like them will work for them. Case studies from the same industry are far more convincing than general ones.
  • Effort drops. You already know their systems, their regulations and their typical problems, so they explain less and you deliver faster.
  • Comparison disappears. A generalist is compared with every other generalist. A specialist in one niche has few direct competitors, which is the start of a Grand Slam Offer.

How to narrow

Start from a broad starving crowd and add one qualifier at a time. Typical qualifiers:

Qualifier Example
Industry Manufacturers, dental practices, gyms
Size 50 to 200 employees, single location, franchise owners
Role CFO, production manager, practice owner
Situation Moving to a new ERP, opening a second location, preparing for an audit
Problem Month-end close takes too long, scrap is too high

Each step makes the message sharper and the list of prospects shorter but easier to find. Rule of thumb: you have gone far enough when you can name the buyer, describe their problem in their own words, and list a hundred real prospects.

Pick one and commit

Many people treat a niche like an experiment they abandon after a few weeks of weak results. Each switch resets the proof, the reputation and the market knowledge you built. Alex's rule of thumb is to stay with the market and treat poor results as a signal about the offer: change the promise, the price, the guarantee, the bonuses or the delivery before you change who you sell to. A market is worth leaving only if it fails the basic tests of pain, money, reach or growth.

What commitment buys you

  • Language. You learn the words buyers use for their problems, which improves every ad and sales call.
  • Proof. Every client becomes a case study that is directly relevant to the next one.
  • Reusable assets. Templates, checklists and ready-made solutions built for one client fit the next client in the same niche, so delivery gets cheaper as value goes up.
  • Reputation. Niches are small worlds. People talk, and referrals come faster.

You can widen later

A niche is a starting point, not a prison. Once you dominate one segment, you can expand to an adjacent one with similar problems, carrying your proof and assets with you. Narrowing first and widening later is far easier than starting broad and trying to stand out.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik consulting (B2B analytics)

Before. "Qlik development and consulting for all industries." Rate €70 per hour. Every project starts from scratch, and each prospect compares three quotes.

After. "Production analytics for mid-sized plastics and metal manufacturers": a fixed package with ready-made OEE, scrap and delivery apps that connect to the ERP systems common in that industry. Fixed price €18,000.

The same skills are now aimed at one buyer. Because most of the apps are reused, a project that used to take 200 hours takes 90. At €70 per hour, 200 hours would have earned €14,000. The niche package earns €18,000 for 90 hours, which is €200 per hour.

Bookkeeping for dental practices (local service)

A bookkeeper switches from "accounting for small businesses" to "accounting for dental practices". She learns the practice software, the typical cost structure and the questions owners ask at year-end. She builds a monthly report showing revenue per chair and per dentist. Owners refer her to each other at professional events. Her fee rises from €250 to €400 per month, and she spends less time per client because every month looks familiar.

Hopping versus iterating

Month Market hopper Committed
1 Gyms, no sales Gyms, no sales
2 Switches to dentists Adds a guarantee
3 Switches to restaurants Changes the price and the name
4 Starting over again First three clients and a case study

Common mistakes

  • Fear of missing out on other customers. Narrowing your message does not mean refusing other work. It means your marketing speaks to one buyer.
  • Hopping markets after a few weeks. Each switch resets learning and proof. Change the offer first.
  • A niche defined by you, not by the buyer. "Companies that value data" is not a group anyone identifies with. Use industry, role or situation.
  • Narrowing into a market with no money. A niche still has to pass the starving crowd tests.
  • Keeping generic delivery. If the niche is only in the headline and the delivery is unchanged, the buyer notices.

How Alex would apply it

  • He narrows until the offer feels personal. The test is whether the buyer reads it and thinks it was made for them.
  • He raises the price after narrowing. A specific offer is worth more, so he expects to charge more for the same core work.
  • He keeps the market and changes the offer. Weak results are a reason to rework the promise, price, guarantee or bonuses, not to start over in a new market.
  • He uses the niche to build reusable assets so that delivery cost falls with every new client.
  • He widens only after winning. Expansion goes to adjacent markets once the first one is clearly working.
  • Questions he would ask: Who is the one buyer this is for? What do you know about them that a generalist doesn't? How long have you actually stayed with this market? What have you changed in the offer before blaming the market?

Worksheet

Answer in writing for your current business.

  1. Current market: How do you describe your target customer today? Could a competitor use the same sentence?
  2. Narrowing: Add industry, size, role and situation. Write the narrowest version you could still build a list of 100 prospects for.
  3. Advantage: What do you already know about this niche (systems, regulations, problems) that a generalist doesn't?
  4. Commitment: For how long will you stay with this market? Write the date.
  5. Iteration plan: List three offer changes you will test before you consider changing the market.
  6. Next market: Which adjacent niche could you widen into later with the same proof and assets?

Bistvo

Ožji trg je videti kot manj strank. V praksi je ravno nasprotno od izgube. »Računovodske storitve« so blago (commodity), ki tekmuje z vsakim knjigovodjo v mestu. »Računovodstvo za zobozdravstvene ordinacije« nagovori točno situacijo enega lastnika, zato zveni bolj relevantno, kupec bolj verjame, da bo delovalo, in prenese višjo ceno. Izdelek v ozadju je lahko skoraj enak. Pozicioniranje spremeni njegovo vrednost.

Ožji trg → bolj relevantna ponudba → višja zaznana verjetnost → višja cena

Druga polovica ideje je zaveza (commitment). Izbrati nišo je lahko. Da se izplača, moraš z njo ostati dovolj dolgo, da se naučiš njenega jezika, zbereš dokaze in postaneš znan. Ko rezultati zamujajo, je Alexova privzeta poteza, da trg obdrži in popravi ponudbo, ne da skoči na nov trg in začne od nič.

Kako deluje

Zakaj zoženje dvigne vrednost

Specifična ponudba hkrati premakne več vzvodov enačbe vrednosti:

  • Zaznana verjetnost zraste. Kupec verjame, da bo nekaj, kar je narejeno za ljudi, kot je on, delovalo tudi pri njem. Primeri strank iz iste panoge so veliko prepričljivejši od splošnih.
  • Trud pade. Njihove sisteme, predpise in tipične težave že poznaš, zato manj razlagajo, ti pa hitreje dostaviš.
  • Primerjava izgine. Generalista primerjajo z vsakim drugim generalistom. Specialist za eno nišo ima malo neposrednih tekmecev, kar je začetek Grand Slam ponudbe.

Kako zožiti

Začni pri široki lačni množici in dodajaj po en kvalifikator naenkrat. Tipični kvalifikatorji:

Kvalifikator Primer
Panoga Proizvajalci, zobozdravstvene ordinacije, fitnesi
Velikost 50 do 200 zaposlenih, ena lokacija, lastniki franšiz
Vloga Finančni direktor, vodja proizvodnje, lastnik ordinacije
Situacija Prehod na nov ERP, odpiranje druge lokacije, priprava na revizijo
Problem Mesečno zapiranje traja predolgo, izmet je previsok

Vsak korak naredi sporočilo ostrejše, seznam potencialnih strank pa krajši, a lažje najdljiv. Pravilo palca: dovolj daleč si, ko lahko kupca poimenuješ, opišeš njegov problem z njegovimi besedami in našteješ sto resničnih potencialnih strank.

Izberi eno in se zaveži

Mnogi obravnavajo nišo kot poskus, ki ga opustijo po nekaj tednih šibkih rezultatov. Vsaka menjava ponastavi dokaze, ugled in znanje o trgu, ki si ga zgradil. Alexovo pravilo palca je, da ostaneš pri trgu, slabe rezultate pa bereš kot signal o ponudbi: spremeni obljubo, ceno, garancijo, bonuse ali način dostave, preden spremeniš, komu prodajaš. Trg je vredno zapustiti le, če pade na osnovnih testih bolečine, denarja, dosegljivosti ali rasti.

Kaj ti prinese zaveza

  • Jezik. Naučiš se besed, s katerimi kupci opisujejo svoje probleme, kar izboljša vsak oglas in vsak prodajni klic.
  • Dokazi. Vsaka stranka postane primer, ki je neposredno relevanten za naslednjo.
  • Ponovno uporabna sredstva. Predloge, kontrolni seznami in pripravljene rešitve, zgrajene za eno stranko, ustrezajo naslednji v isti niši, zato je dostava cenejša, vrednost pa višja.
  • Ugled. Niše so majhni svetovi. Ljudje se pogovarjajo in priporočila pridejo hitreje.

Pozneje lahko razširiš

Niša je izhodišče, ne zapor. Ko obvladaš en segment, se lahko razširiš na sosednjega s podobnimi problemi in s seboj neseš dokaze in sredstva. Najprej zožiti in pozneje razširiti je veliko lažje, kot začeti široko in nato poskušati izstopati.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Qlik svetovanje (B2B analitika)

Pred. »Qlik razvoj in svetovanje za vse panoge.« Postavka 70 € na uro. Vsak projekt se začne od začetka, vsak potencialni kupec pa primerja tri ponudbe.

Po. »Proizvodna analitika za srednje velike proizvajalce plastike in kovin«: fiksen paket s pripravljenimi aplikacijami za OEE, izmet in dobave, ki se povežejo z ERP sistemi, običajnimi v tej panogi. Fiksna cena 18.000 €.

Isto znanje je zdaj usmerjeno v enega kupca. Ker se večina aplikacij ponovno uporabi, projekt, ki je prej trajal 200 ur, traja 90. Pri 70 € na uro bi 200 ur prineslo 14.000 €. Paket za nišo prinese 18.000 € za 90 ur, kar je 200 € na uro.

Knjigovodstvo za zobozdravstvene ordinacije (lokalna storitev)

Knjigovodkinja preide z »računovodstva za mala podjetja« na »računovodstvo za zobozdravstvene ordinacije«. Nauči se programa, ki ga ordinacije uporabljajo, tipične strukture stroškov in vprašanj, ki jih lastniki postavljajo ob koncu leta. Pripravi mesečno poročilo s prihodkom na stol in na zobozdravnika. Lastniki jo priporočajo drug drugemu na strokovnih srečanjih. Njena mesečna cena zraste z 250 € na 400 €, na stranko pa porabi manj časa, ker je vsak mesec podoben.

Skakanje ali izboljševanje

Mesec Skakač med trgi Zavezan
1 Fitnesi, brez prodaje Fitnesi, brez prodaje
2 Preklopi na zobozdravnike Doda garancijo
3 Preklopi na restavracije Spremeni ceno in ime
4 Spet začenja od začetka Prve tri stranke in primer uspeha

Pogoste napake

  • Strah, da boš zamudil druge stranke. Zoženje sporočila ne pomeni, da zavrneš drugo delo. Pomeni, da tvoj marketing nagovarja enega kupca.
  • Skakanje med trgi po nekaj tednih. Vsaka menjava ponastavi učenje in dokaze. Najprej spremeni ponudbo.
  • Niša, ki jo definiraš ti, ne kupec. »Podjetja, ki cenijo podatke« ni skupina, s katero bi se kdo poistovetil. Uporabi panogo, vlogo ali situacijo.
  • Zoženje v trg brez denarja. Niša mora še vedno prestati teste lačne množice.
  • Splošna dostava ostane. Če je niša samo v naslovu, izvedba pa je nespremenjena, kupec to opazi.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Zožuje, dokler ponudba ne deluje osebno. Test je, ali kupec ob branju pomisli, da je narejena zanj.
  • Po zoženju dvigne ceno. Specifična ponudba je vredna več, zato za isto jedro dela pričakuje višjo ceno.
  • Obdrži trg in spremeni ponudbo. Šibki rezultati so razlog za predelavo obljube, cene, garancije ali bonusov, ne za nov začetek na novem trgu.
  • Nišo izkoristi za ponovno uporabna sredstva, da strošek dostave z vsako novo stranko pada.
  • Razširi šele po zmagi. Širitev gre na sosednje trge, ko prvi očitno deluje.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Kdo je tisti en kupec, za katerega je to? Kaj veš o njem, česar generalist ne ve? Kako dolgo si dejansko ostal pri tem trgu? Kaj si spremenil v ponudbi, preden si za vse okrivil trg?

Delovni list

Odgovori pisno za svoje trenutno podjetje.

  1. Trenutni trg: Kako danes opišeš svojo ciljno stranko? Bi lahko isti stavek uporabil konkurent?
  2. Zoženje: Dodaj panogo, velikost, vlogo in situacijo. Zapiši najožjo različico, za katero bi še lahko sestavil seznam 100 potencialnih strank.
  3. Prednost: Kaj o tej niši (sistemi, predpisi, problemi) že veš, česar generalist ne ve?
  4. Zaveza: Kako dolgo boš ostal pri tem trgu? Zapiši datum.
  5. Načrt izboljšav: Naštej tri spremembe ponudbe, ki jih boš preizkusil, preden razmisliš o menjavi trga.
  6. Naslednji trg: V katero sosednjo nišo bi se pozneje lahko razširil z istimi dokazi in sredstvi?
