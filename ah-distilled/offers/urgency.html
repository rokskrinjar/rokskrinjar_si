UrgencyNujnost

$100M Offers · Urgency

A real time limit on your offer, price or bonus. It gives buyers a reason to decide now instead of drifting into "maybe later".Resnična časovna omejitev ponudbe, cene ali bonusa. Kupcu da razlog, da se odloči zdaj, namesto da odločitev odplava v »morda kasneje«.

The gist

Most lost sales end with "not now", and "not now" rarely comes back. Urgency fixes this by putting a clock on the decision: the offer, the price or a bonus is only available until a set moment. When a buyer knows that waiting costs them something, they decide.

Scarcity = limited quantity (seats, units, slots) · Urgency = limited time (dates, windows, deadlines)

Urgency only works if it is honest. A deadline you move or a countdown that resets teaches buyers to ignore you. The best urgency already exists: you find the real deadlines that already exist in your business and in the buyer's world, and you make them visible.

How it works

Urgency vs scarcity

Both raise demand by making an offer harder to get, and they are often used together. The difference is the dimension. Scarcity says "only five spots". Urgency says "only until Friday". Scarcity can sell out early. Urgency ends on schedule whether or not anyone bought. Using both at once (a limited number of spots in a cohort that starts on a fixed date) is stronger than either alone.

Rolling cohorts

You start new clients in groups on fixed dates, for example every first Monday of the month. Anyone who misses this start waits for the next one. This creates a natural, recurring deadline without any discount. It also makes delivery easier, because onboarding happens in batches. Tell buyers the honest consequence of waiting: "The next group starts in four weeks."

Rolling seasonal urgency

Tie the offer to a season or calendar moment: New Year, summer, back to school, quarter end, the start of the fiscal year. Each season gets its own promotion with its own end date. When one season ends, the next one begins, so there is always a current deadline. The reason has to fit the season, otherwise it looks like a pretext.

Pricing or bonus deadlines

The core offer stays available, but something about it changes after a date:

  • Price deadline: the price goes up on a known date, and you actually raise it.
  • Bonus deadline: a bonus is only included for those who decide by a date or within the first call.
  • Terms deadline: a payment plan or a fee waiver ends on a date.

Bonus deadlines are often the safest choice, because the buyer can still buy later. They just get less. This keeps urgency without punishing late buyers too hard.

Exploding opportunity

An offer that is valid for a very short time after it is made: the end of the call, 24 hours, or the end of the week. It works when the opportunity really cannot be held open, for example a slot in your calendar that will be offered to the next person on the list. Use it sparingly and only when the reason is true. Exploding offers that are always re-extended destroy trust fast.

B2B: use the real windows

In B2B, artificial countdowns usually backfire. The buyer's world already has plenty of real deadlines. Use them:

Real window How it creates urgency
Budget cycle Budget not committed by year end is often lost
Project start date Your team can only start on a date if the contract is signed by an earlier date
Capacity planning Your next free implementation slot is in a specific month
Compliance or system change A regulation or software end-of-support date sets a hard limit
Seasonality of their business Changes must be live before their busy season

Work backwards from their deadline to yours. "If you want this live before the January close, we need to start on 15 November, so we need a decision by 8 November."

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

Qlik implementation for a manufacturer (B2B)

Before. "Let us know when you're ready and we'll schedule the project." The proposal sits in the CFO's inbox for three months.

After. "We start two implementations per quarter. The next start is 3 February, which puts your margin reports live before the Q1 close. To hold that slot we need a signed order by 20 January. Orders signed this month also include the budget-planning app at no cost."

The deadline is tied to their own goal (the Q1 close), to real capacity, and to a bonus. Nothing is fake, so the deadline can be stated plainly and kept.

Dental clinic (local business)

Before. "Teeth whitening available all year."

After. "Summer smile: book whitening by 31 May and get a free check-up and cleaning. Results ready before wedding and holiday season."

Seasonal urgency with a clear reason (summer events) and a bonus deadline. In June the clinic switches to a back-to-school or autumn promotion with its own end date.

Online course (rolling cohort)

Element Without urgency With urgency
Start Start any time Cohort starts on the 1st of each month
Price Same forever Early sign-ups by the 20th pay less
Bonus None Live onboarding call only for this cohort

Common mistakes

  • Fake deadlines. Timers that reset, "last chance" emails every week, extensions for everyone. Buyers notice, and from then on no deadline you set is believed.
  • No reason given. A deadline without a "because" feels like pressure. A deadline with a real reason (start date, season, capacity) feels like information.
  • Confusing urgency with discounting. Urgency does not require a lower price. A start date or a bonus deadline creates urgency while keeping the price high.
  • Not keeping the deadline. If you raise the price on the 1st, raise it. Make an exception once and you have trained every future buyer to wait.
  • Ignoring the buyer's own clock. In B2B, your deadline is weaker than theirs. Their budget, their go-live date and their season matter more than your promotion.

How Alex would apply it

  • He uses urgency on almost every offer, because without a reason to act now, most buyers will postpone even when they want it.
  • He prefers rolling structures: cohorts and seasonal promotions that renew themselves, so there is always a fresh, honest deadline and he never has to invent one.
  • He puts the deadline on the bonus before the price. The buyer who waits still can buy, they just lose the extra. It drives decisions without making late buyers feel punished.
  • He combines urgency with scarcity where both are real: limited spots in a group that starts on a date.
  • He refuses fake urgency. His rule of thumb is that the long-term cost of being caught lying is far bigger than the short-term sales it brings.
  • He states the cost of waiting in plain terms: what the buyer loses each week they delay, and when the next chance comes.
  • Questions he would ask: What real deadline already exists here? When does the next group or slot start? What happens to the buyer's problem if they wait three months? Which bonus could expire without hurting the core offer? Have you ever broken a deadline, and do your buyers know it?

Worksheet

Take your main offer and write down the honest time limits around it.

  1. Your deadlines: What real limits exist on your side: start dates, capacity, price changes planned for next year?
  2. Their deadlines: What dates matter to the buyer: budget year, season, go-live, regulation, a busy period?
  3. Structure: Could you sell in rolling cohorts or seasonal promotions? Write the next three start or end dates.
  4. Bonus deadline: Which bonus could be included only for decisions made by a date?
  5. The sentence: Write one sentence that states the deadline and its real reason, for example "To start on X, we need a decision by Y, because Z."

Bistvo

Večina izgubljenih prodaj se konča z »ne zdaj«, in »ne zdaj« se redko vrne. Nujnost (urgency) to reši tako, da na odločitev postavi uro: ponudba, cena ali bonus velja le do določenega trenutka. Ko kupec ve, da ga čakanje nekaj stane, se odloči.

Pomanjkanje (scarcity) = omejena količina (mesta, kosi, termini) · Nujnost (urgency) = omejen čas (datumi, okna, roki)

Nujnost deluje le, če je poštena. Rok, ki ga premikaš, ali odštevalnik, ki se ponastavi, kupce nauči, da te ignorirajo. Najboljša nujnost že obstaja: poiščeš resnične roke, ki so že prisotni v tvojem poslu in v kupčevem svetu, in jih narediš vidne.

Kako deluje

Nujnost in pomanjkanje

Oboje dvigne povpraševanje, ker ponudbo naredi težje dosegljivo, in pogosto ju uporabljamo skupaj. Razlika je v dimenziji. Pomanjkanje pravi »samo pet mest«. Nujnost pravi »samo do petka«. Pomanjkanje se lahko razproda prej. Nujnost se konča po urniku, ne glede na to, ali je kdo kupil. Oboje hkrati (omejeno število mest v skupini, ki začne na določen datum) je močnejše od vsakega posebej.

Tekoče kohorte (rolling cohorts)

Nove stranke začenjajo v skupinah na fiksne datume, na primer vsak prvi ponedeljek v mesecu. Kdor zamudi ta začetek, čaka na naslednjega. To ustvari naraven, ponavljajoč se rok brez kakršnega koli popusta. Hkrati olajša izvedbo, ker uvajanje poteka v serijah. Kupcu povej pošteno posledico čakanja: »Naslednja skupina začne čez štiri tedne.«

Tekoča sezonska nujnost (rolling seasonal urgency)

Ponudbo poveži s sezono ali trenutkom v koledarju: novo leto, poletje, začetek šole, konec kvartala, začetek poslovnega leta. Vsaka sezona dobi svojo akcijo s svojim rokom. Ko se ena sezona konča, se začne naslednja, zato vedno obstaja aktualen rok. Razlog se mora ujemati s sezono, sicer deluje kot izgovor.

Roki za ceno ali bonus

Jedro ponudbe ostane na voljo, po datumu pa se nekaj spremeni:

  • Rok za ceno: cena se na znan datum dvigne, in jo res dvigneš.
  • Rok za bonus: bonus dobijo le tisti, ki se odločijo do datuma ali že na prvem klicu.
  • Rok za pogoje: plačilo na obroke ali odpoved pristojbine velja le do datuma.

Roki za bonus so pogosto najvarnejša izbira, ker lahko kupec kupi tudi kasneje. Dobi pa manj. Tako ohraniš nujnost, ne da bi preveč kaznoval tiste, ki se odločijo pozneje.

Eksplozivna priložnost (exploding opportunity)

Ponudba, ki velja zelo kratek čas po tem, ko je dana: do konca klica, 24 ur ali do konca tedna. Deluje, ko priložnosti res ni mogoče držati odprte, na primer termin v tvojem koledarju, ki ga ponudiš naslednjemu na seznamu. Uporabljaj jo redko in le, ko je razlog resničen. Eksplozivne ponudbe, ki jih vedno znova podaljšaš, hitro uničijo zaupanje.

B2B: uporabi resnična okna

V B2B umetni odštevalniki navadno škodijo. Kupčev svet ima že veliko resničnih rokov. Uporabi jih:

Resnično okno Kako ustvari nujnost
Proračunski cikel Proračun, ki do konca leta ni porabljen, je pogosto izgubljen
Datum začetka projekta Tvoja ekipa lahko začne na datum le, če je pogodba podpisana do zgodnejšega datuma
Planiranje kapacitet Naslednji prosti termin za implementacijo je v konkretnem mesecu
Regulativa ali sprememba sistema Predpis ali konec podpore programske opreme postavi trdo mejo
Sezonskost njihovega posla Spremembe morajo delovati pred njihovo glavno sezono

Od njihovega roka štej nazaj do svojega. »Če želite, da deluje pred januarskim zaprtjem, moramo začeti 15. novembra, zato potrebujemo odločitev do 8. novembra.«

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Qlik implementacija za proizvajalca (B2B)

Pred. »Sporočite, ko boste pripravljeni, pa bomo projekt uvrstili v urnik.« Ponudba tri mesece leži v nabiralniku finančnega direktorja.

Po. »Na kvartal začnemo dve implementaciji. Naslednji začetek je 3. februarja, kar pomeni, da so vaša poročila o marži v uporabi pred zaprtjem prvega kvartala. Za ta termin potrebujemo podpisano naročilo do 20. januarja. Naročila, podpisana ta mesec, brezplačno vključujejo tudi aplikacijo za planiranje proračuna.«

Rok je vezan na njihov lasten cilj (zaprtje prvega kvartala), na resnično kapaciteto in na bonus. Nič ni lažnega, zato rok lahko poveš naravnost in ga držiš.

Zobozdravniška ordinacija (lokalni posel)

Pred. »Beljenje zob vse leto.«

Po. »Poletni nasmeh: rezervirajte beljenje do 31. maja in dobite brezplačen pregled in čiščenje. Rezultat pripravljen pred sezono porok in dopustov.«

Sezonska nujnost z jasnim razlogom (poletni dogodki) in rok za bonus. Junija ordinacija preklopi na šolsko ali jesensko akcijo s svojim rokom.

Spletni tečaj (tekoča kohorta)

Začetek Začni kadarkoli Kohorta začne vsakega 1. v mesecu
Cena Vedno enaka Kdor se prijavi do 20., plača manj

Pogoste napake

  • Lažni roki. Odštevalniki, ki se ponastavijo, e-pošta »zadnja priložnost« vsak teden, podaljšanja za vse. Kupci to opazijo, in od takrat ne verjamejo nobenemu tvojemu roku več.
  • Brez razloga. Rok brez »ker« deluje kot pritisk. Rok z resničnim razlogom (datum začetka, sezona, kapaciteta) deluje kot informacija.
  • Mešanje nujnosti s popustom. Nujnost ne zahteva nižje cene. Datum začetka ali rok za bonus ustvari nujnost, cena pa ostane visoka.
  • Neupoštevanje roka. Če ceno dvigneš 1. v mesecu, jo dvigni. Naredi izjemo enkrat, in vse prihodnje kupce si naučil čakati.
  • Spregledana kupčeva ura. V B2B je tvoj rok šibkejši od njihovega. Njihov proračun, njihov datum zagona in njihova sezona štejejo več kot tvoja akcija.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Nujnost uporabi skoraj pri vsaki ponudbi, ker bo brez razloga za ukrepanje zdaj večina kupcev odlašala, tudi ko si stvar želi.
  • Ima raje tekoče strukture: kohorte in sezonske akcije, ki se same obnavljajo, tako da vedno obstaja svež, pošten rok in mu ga ni treba izmišljevati.
  • Rok postavi na bonus, preden ga postavi na ceno. Kupec, ki čaka, lahko še vedno kupi, izgubi le dodatek. To spodbudi odločitve, ne da bi se pozni kupci počutili kaznovane.
  • Nujnost združi s pomanjkanjem, kjer sta obe resnični: omejena mesta v skupini, ki začne na datum.
  • Lažno nujnost zavrne. Njegovo pravilo palca je, da je dolgoročna cena tega, da te ujamejo pri laži, veliko večja od kratkoročne prodaje, ki jo prinese.
  • Ceno čakanja pove preprosto: kaj kupec izgubi vsak teden odlašanja in kdaj pride naslednja priložnost.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Kateri resnični rok tu že obstaja? Kdaj začne naslednja skupina ali termin? Kaj se zgodi s kupčevim problemom, če počaka tri mesece? Kateri bonus bi lahko potekel, ne da bi škodil jedru ponudbe? Si kdaj prelomil rok, in ali tvoji kupci to vedo?

Delovni list

Vzemi svojo glavno ponudbo in zapiši poštene časovne omejitve okoli nje.

  1. Tvoji roki: Katere resnične omejitve obstajajo na tvoji strani: datumi začetka, kapaciteta, spremembe cen, načrtovane za naslednje leto?
  2. Njihovi roki: Kateri datumi so pomembni za kupca: proračunsko leto, sezona, zagon, regulativa, obdobje največjega dela?
  3. Struktura: Bi lahko prodajal v tekočih kohortah ali sezonskih akcijah? Zapiši naslednje tri datume začetka ali konca.
  4. Rok za bonus: Kateri bonus bi lahko vključil le za odločitve do določenega datuma?
  5. Stavek: Napiši en stavek, ki pove rok in njegov resnični razlog, na primer »Za začetek X potrebujemo odločitev do Y, ker Z.«
