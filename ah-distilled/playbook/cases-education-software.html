Alex's Playbook · Hotseat cases

Real hotseat situations from coaches, course and community businesses, event and creator brands, and software companies, with what Alex diagnosed and told the owner to do.Resnične situacije s hotseatov coachev, podjetij s tečaji in skupnostmi, dogodkov in ustvarjalcev ter programskih podjetij, z Alexovo diagnozo in nasveti lastniku.

The gist

Coaching, course and creator businesses tend to bring Alex the same three complaints: ads stopped scaling, customers churn, and the founder has run out of hours. Underneath, he usually finds that the owner wants to switch something big (platform, avatar, B2B, a new product) when the cheaper fix is inside the current funnel. Creative volume is too low, the weakest funnel step has never been tested, value per customer is too low to absorb rising acquisition costs, and the back end is sold too late or not at all. Many founders also over-deliver: Slack access, long unstructured calls and group sessions nobody attends, which caps capacity without raising retention.

His prescriptions are consistent. Keep the channel that works and feed it far more creative. Raise lifetime value first, through upsells, renewal pitches timed to milestones and events, annual prepay, performance components or a diagnostic sale of the outcome. Fix churn at the start: qualify for the customers who activate, onboard one-on-one, and deliver an early win. On price he moves both ways, but he warns hard against cutting price to fix churn, citing his own costly mistakes. When a founder is the bottleneck, he adds structure (a scripted webinar, a self-serve checkout, clipped content) before adding people.

Software and sellability form a separate thread. For software he treats retention as the whole game: find the cohort that stays, then grow. For education businesses that want an exit, he notes that founder-led education is hard to sell, and points owners to the sellable assets their students or audiences create.

Couples-coaching program · ~$480k/yr, ~$206k profit · founders at capacity, challenge attendance falling

  • Situation. Two 12-month offers ($5k group, $25k high-touch with chat access), a five-day challenge funnel on paid social, slow payback.
  • Diagnosis. A supply constraint caused by over-delivery (chat access, custom plans, group calls nobody valued). The cheap tier was built instead of fixing capacity, and event renewals failed because nobody pitched.
  • Advice. One ~$25k offer: deep one-on-one onboarding, quarterly calls in groups of four, two or three in-person getaways, a curated community. Kill chat access and weekly calls; sunset the $5k tier. Collect ~$9k up front with layaway as a downsell. Test a short date-night event in place of the challenge, run ~100 image ads, and pitch renewals in person with a fast-action incentive.
  • Why. Capacity rises from ~60 to 120–150 clients without hiring; the first payment drives conversion more than total price.

Online program for parents of neurodivergent children · ~$770k/yr, ~$555k profit · founder spends ~30 hours a week in DMs

  • Situation. $2,500/year offer, 9–10% conversion of live attendees, 3–4 hour unstructured live sessions, selling only to a warm audience.
  • Diagnosis. The founder is the bottleneck, held back by beliefs that selling and higher prices are bad; the one-on-one tier is mispriced.
  • Advice. $2,500 per six months, recurring. A structured 45-minute webinar with slides and FAQ. Checkout at $2,500 plus bonus versus $3,000 to talk to someone first. A three-day guarantee and next-day onboarding. Reprice coaching ~5x or drop it. Clip Q&As into 30–50 posts a week, all pointing to DMs; winners become ads.
  • Why. Structure lifts conversion and frees founder time. Follow-up: DM time fell about 90%.

Online tech courses for teens · from ~$700k to ~$150k a month · ROAS fell from 2–3x to ~1x

  • Situation. Sold to mothers through paid social: $297 front end plus a $189 upsell taken by 30–40%. The owner was considering a high-ticket coaching offer.

  • Why. Even a 10% take on $1,500 adds ~$150 per order, more than the current upsell, without building a heavy back end.

Online craft-skills membership · ~$800k/yr, 30–35% profit · owner self-limits ad spend

  • Situation. $27/month or ~$217/year membership sold through a $10 five-day bootcamp three times a year; ~$37 cost per ticket, 22% of buyers join, LTV ~$300. Only four ads running.
  • Diagnosis. Pure demand constraint: too little creative, hooks aimed at already-aware buyers, low front-end cash.

Follow the launch spend cadence; produce 80+ ads (re-shoots, member content unlocked by a secret bonus, clipped lives, AI statics); add pain-aware hooks and call-outs; accept up to ~$100 cost per buyer; sell annual-only during launch with a physical starter kit, monthly after cart close; then test the bootcamp as a $10 seven-day trial with tool upsells.
  • Why. If clicks cost less than they earn, it scales; ~55% trial conversion beats 22%.

Executive career coaching · ~$7.2M/yr, ~$2M profit · wants to add enterprise B2B

  • Advice. Stay B2C and aim to scale spend 10–20x. Fix ROAS levers one at a time. If he needs something new, add a channel and keep the avatar.

Coaching for affiliates who want brand deals · ~$1.5M/yr · a price jump crushed close rate

Front-end sales for new real-estate wholesalers · CAC ~$5k, LTV ~$35k · prospects cannot afford the first payment

  • Situation. Close rate very low; an unqualified lead flow drains the sales team.

Patterns

  • Keep the working channel and feed it (about 10 cases). Owners want a new platform, avatar or B2B line; he says more creative, more spend, more volume on what already returns, and adds a channel before he adds an avatar.
  • The new thing is an escape (about 6 cases). B2B, apparel and supplements, a business school, a new niche, SaaS: the plateau comes from a skill gap in the core, and a new line will not close it.
  • Raise value per customer before scaling ads (about 9 cases). Upsells, ~$1,500 one-time offers, performance back ends, diagnostic sales of the goal, renewal pitches, annual prepay and memberships for graduates.
  • Churn is fixed at the start (about 6 cases). Qualify for the avatar that activates, onboard one-on-one, book the next session, deliver an early win; he would rather lose sales than keep poor-fit buyers.
  • Never cut price to fix churn (about 3 cases). His own price cuts lost millions with churn unchanged; he raises or restructures price instead, splitting upfront and back-end when velocity suffers.
  • Sell at events and milestones (about 4 cases). Events and challenges pay off only when renewals and upgrades are pitched in the room.
  • Over-delivery caps capacity (about 3 cases). Chat access, long unstructured calls and unused group sessions; he cuts them and adds structure and self-serve checkout.
  • Fix the weakest funnel step (about 3 cases). Opt-in rate, pixel placement, cold traffic sent straight to checkout.
  • Education is hard to sell (about 4 cases). Founder dependency and single channels cap exit value; he points to the sellable assets students or audiences create.
  • Software lives on retention and fit (about 4 cases). Find the cohort that stays, sell to the real decision maker, commit to one path, and choose a growing market.

How Alex would apply it

First questions. 1. What does a customer pay you over their lifetime, what does one cost to acquire, and how long until you are paid back? 2. Which funnel step sits furthest below benchmark? 3. How many ad creatives are live, and when did you last add new ones? 4. Which customers activate and stay, and what do they have in common? 5. What do customers buy after the first offer, and when do you pitch it? 6. How many hours does the founder spend on delivery and DMs, and which of those do customers actually value? 7. What new avatar, product or platform are you considering, and is the current one exhausted?

Default moves by situation. - Ads stopped scaling: check creative volume and pixel setup first, then raise LTV with an upsell or back end; leave platform and model alone until those fail. - LTV:CAC thin: add a performance or renewal component, sell the goal over its timeframe, push annual prepay; only then spend more. - High churn: segment churners from stayers, qualify the front door to the stayers' profile, add one-on-one onboarding and an early win, and pitch renewals at milestones and events. - Price questions: treat very high close rates as a sign of underpricing; when a price jump stalls sales, shift part of the price to the back end; never cut price to fix churn. - Founder at capacity: strip over-delivery, structure the sale (scripted webinar, self-serve checkout), clip content for reach, then hire. - Itch for something new: add a channel inside the current business; refuse a new avatar or product until the core is clearly exhausted. - Wanting an exit: remove founder dependency and single-channel risk, or build around the sellable assets your students or audience create. - Software: prove retention in one cohort first, sell to whoever really decides, and commit to one path before spending on acquisition.

Bistvo

Podjetja s coachingom, tečaji in ustvarjalci Alexu navadno prinesejo iste tri pritožbe: oglasi se ne skalirajo več, stranke odhajajo in ustanovitelju je zmanjkalo ur. Pod tem pogosto najde lastnika, ki bi rad zamenjal nekaj velikega (platformo, ciljnega kupca, prehod v B2B, nov izdelek), čeprav je cenejša rešitev znotraj obstoječega lijaka. Oglasnih kreativ je premalo, najšibkejši korak lijaka ni bil nikoli testiran, vrednost na kupca je prenizka, da bi prenesla rastoče stroške pridobivanja, back end pa se prodaja prepozno ali sploh ne. Veliko ustanoviteljev tudi daje preveč: dostop do klepeta, dolgi nestrukturirani klici in skupinska srečanja, ki se jih nihče ne udeležuje, kar omeji zmogljivost, zadrževanja pa ne izboljša.

Njegovi recepti so dosledni. Obdrži kanal, ki deluje, in ga nahrani z bistveno več kreativami. Najprej dvigni življenjsko vrednost kupca: upselli, ponudbe za podaljšanje ob mejnikih in na dogodkih, letno predplačilo, komponenta, vezana na rezultat, ali diagnostična prodaja cilja. Odhajanje strank reši na začetku: kvalificiraj za stranke, ki se aktivirajo, uvajaj ena na ena in poskrbi za zgodnjo zmago. Pri ceni se premika v obe smeri, a odločno svari pred nižanjem cene zaradi odhajanja strank, in pri tem navaja lastne drage napake. Ko je ozko grlo ustanovitelj, najprej doda strukturo (scenarij za webinar, samopostrežno blagajno, izrezane vsebine), šele nato ljudi.

Programska oprema in prodajljivost sta ločena nit. Pri programski opremi je zanj zadrževanje strank vse: najdi kohorto, ki ostane, nato rasti. Pri izobraževalnih podjetjih, ki želijo izstop, opozarja, da je izobraževanje, vezano na ustanovitelja, težko prodati, in lastnike usmeri k prodajljivim premoženjem, ki jih ustvarijo njihovi učenci ali občinstvo.

Vzorci

  • Obdrži kanal, ki deluje, in ga nahrani (približno 10 primerov). Lastniki želijo novo platformo, ciljnega kupca ali B2B linijo; on reče več kreativ, več porabe, več obsega na tistem, kar že vrača, in prej doda kanal kot ciljnega kupca.
  • Nova stvar je beg (približno 6 primerov). B2B, oblačila in dopolnila, poslovna šola, nova niša, SaaS: zastoj izvira iz vrzeli v znanju v jedru, nova linija pa je ne zapre.
  • Dvigni vrednost na kupca pred skaliranjem oglasov (približno 9 primerov). Upselli, enkratne ponudbe za ~1.500 $, back end, vezan na rezultat, diagnostična prodaja cilja, ponudbe za podaljšanje, letno predplačilo in članstva za diplomante.
  • Odhajanje se reši na začetku (približno 6 primerov). Kvalificiraj za ciljnega kupca, ki se aktivira, uvajaj ena na ena, rezerviraj naslednje srečanje, poskrbi za zgodnjo zmago; raje izgubi prodajo, kot da obdrži neustrezne kupce.
  • Nikoli ne nižaj cene zaradi odhajanja (približno 3 primeri). Njegova lastna znižanja so stala milijone, odhajanje pa se ni spremenilo; ceno raje dvigne ali preoblikuje in jo razdeli na vstopni del in back end, ko trpi hitrost prodaje.
  • Prodajaj na dogodkih in ob mejnikih (približno 4 primeri). Dogodki in izzivi se izplačajo šele, ko se podaljšanja in nadgradnje ponudijo v prostoru.
  • Prevelika dostava omeji zmogljivost (približno 3 primeri). Dostop do klepeta, dolgi nestrukturirani klici in neuporabljena skupinska srečanja; ukine jih ter doda strukturo in samopostrežno blagajno.
  • Popravi najšibkejši korak lijaka (približno 3 primeri). Stopnja prijav, postavitev piksla, hladen promet naravnost v blagajno.
  • Izobraževanje je težko prodati (približno 4 primeri). Odvisnost od ustanovitelja in en sam kanal omejita vrednost ob izstopu; usmeri k prodajljivemu premoženju, ki ga ustvarijo učenci ali občinstvo.
  • Programska oprema živi od zadrževanja in ustreznosti (približno 4 primeri). Najdi kohorto, ki ostane, prodajaj tistemu, ki res odloča, zaveži se eni poti in izberi rastoč trg.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

Prva vprašanja. 1. Koliko vam stranka plača v življenjski dobi, koliko stane njena pridobitev in kdaj se vam vložek vrne? 2. Kateri korak lijaka je najbolj pod mejnikom? 3. Koliko oglasnih kreativ teče in kdaj ste nazadnje dodali nove? 4. Katere stranke se aktivirajo in ostanejo ter kaj jim je skupno? 5. Kaj stranke kupijo po prvi ponudbi in kdaj jim to ponudite? 6. Koliko ur porabi ustanovitelj za dostavo in DM-je in katere od teh stranke resnično cenijo? 7. O katerem novem ciljnem kupcu, izdelku ali platformi razmišljate in ali je sedanji res izčrpan?

Privzete poteze glede na situacijo. - Oglasi se ne skalirajo več: najprej preveri število kreativ in nastavitev piksla, nato dvigni LTV z upsellom ali back endom; platformo in model pusti pri miru, dokler to ne odpove. - Tanek LTV:CAC: dodaj komponento, vezano na rezultat, ali podaljšanje, prodajaj cilj v njegovem časovnem okviru, potiskaj letno predplačilo; šele nato porabi več. - Visoko odhajanje: loči tiste, ki odidejo, od tistih, ki ostanejo, vhod kvalificiraj po profilu tistih, ki ostanejo, dodaj uvajanje ena na ena in zgodnjo zmago ter ponudi podaljšanja ob mejnikih in na dogodkih. - Vprašanja o ceni: zelo visoko stopnjo zaključkov beri kot znak prenizke cene; ko skok cene ustavi prodajo, del cene prestavi v back end; nikoli ne nižaj cene zaradi odhajanja. - Ustanovitelj na meji zmogljivosti: odreži preveliko dostavo, strukturiraj prodajo (webinar po scenariju, samopostrežna blagajna), izreži vsebine za doseg, nato zaposli. - Želja po nečem novem: dodaj kanal znotraj obstoječega posla; novega ciljnega kupca ali izdelka ne začni, dokler jedro ni jasno izčrpano. - Želja po izstopu: odpravi odvisnost od ustanovitelja in tveganje enega kanala ali gradi okrog prodajljivega premoženja, ki ga ustvarijo učenci ali občinstvo. - Programska oprema: najprej dokaži zadrževanje v eni kohorti, prodajaj tistemu, ki res odloča, in se zaveži eni poti, preden porabiš za pridobivanje.
