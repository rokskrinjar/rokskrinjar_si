Alex's Playbook · Hotseat cases

Forty-two real situations from gyms, clinics, dental and chiropractic practices, salons, restaurants and home-service contractors, with what Alex diagnosed and what he told the owner to do.Dvainštirideset resničnih situacij iz fitnesov, klinik, zobnih in kiropraktičnih ordinacij, salonov, restavracij in obrtnikov na domu, s tem, kakšno diagnozo je postavil Alex in kaj je lastniku svetoval.

The gist

Local owners almost always arrive saying they need more leads. When Alex looks at the numbers, the leads are rarely the first problem. Close rates of 70 to 90 percent, show rates near 100 percent and ads returning 10:1 or better tell him the business is underpriced and spending far too little on a channel that already works. His first moves are usually a price increase of 10 to 40 percent, a dedicated landing page and more ad spend on the channel that is already profitable. Small changes per ticket (menu order, an attached drink, a rebooking step) go straight to profit in a high-volume business.

The second pattern is capacity. Many local businesses are capped by one doctor, one founder in the van or a shortage of technicians. Alex treats recruiting as a core business function with its own funnel and budget, and he works out what one practitioner or rep is worth in gross profit to show the owner how cheap a large hiring bonus really is. He moves the expert to the hardest work and gives the simpler half to associates.

The third pattern is cash timing. Quarterly billing, pay-per-visit models and fronted equipment starve growing local businesses. He restructures the offer so more cash comes in on day one: a front-end assessment with the card on file, a prepaid year, an enrolment fee, a first quarter paid up front. Underneath all of it sits focus. He cuts extra avatars, side ventures and partner deals so the owner can push one model in one market before expanding.

Cases

Dental practice, multi-location · ~$2M/yr · one expert does every high-ticket case

  • Situation. About 47% profit. The founder-doctor handles every high-ticket case (cap of ~25–30 patients a month), bills per visit and closes 80–90%; patients wait two to four weeks.
  • Diagnosis. Supply constraint created by a single expert doing every step. The very high close rate signals underpricing.
  • Advice. Associates take the simpler half of visits; the founder becomes architect and teacher. Payment up front, card on file at check-in. Raise price about 20%. A VSL before the treatment presentation and faster booking.
  • Why. Halving the expert's load doubles capacity. In the follow-up the average ticket rose from about $28K to $44K and booking dropped to within a week, with no new locations.

Residential trash collection · ~$640K/yr, losing ~$150K · growth starved of cash

  • Situation. Under two years old, 2,500 customers, two avatars (homes and HOAs), five channels. Bins and commissions are paid out before quarterly billing brings cash in.
  • Diagnosis. Too many avatars and channels, and an offer that collects too little cash up front to fund growth.
  • Advice. Pick single homes as the avatar. Go all in on door-to-door with commission-only reps, trained by shadowing and role play. Restructure the offer: bin plus first quarter up front, free months spread later, and an anchor of a prepaid year with the bin free.
  • Why. Each sale needs about $208 cash to cover labour, bin and commission. If about one in five takes the prepaid year, average cash per sale clears that bar and growth funds itself.

Online psychiatry practice, multi-state · ~$1.5M/yr · keeps 1.3% of it

Assisted living and memory care · private pay · profit arrives too slowly

  • Situation. Sales are fine, residents stay a year or more, but profit only appears after two to three months.

  • Advice. Three options: an up-front enrolment fee, the first three months paid up front, or a move-in bundle of services residents need anyway (transport, hair and beauty, furniture or bed upgrades) sold up front.

  • Underpriced, then a price rise first (about 10 cases). Close rates of 70–90% and full calendars read to him as underpricing. Typical moves: 10–40% increases, VIP tiers, weekend or same-day surcharges, priority fees. He watches the close rate and holds the price while it stays healthy.
  • Underspending on a channel that already works (about 8 cases). Search or social returning 5:1 to 13:1 on a tiny budget, an untapped door-to-door team or networking. He scales the winner before testing anything new, and judges success by total profit.
  • Capacity capped by one expert or too few staff (about 9 cases). Founder-doctors, owners in the van, missing technicians, cleaners and practitioners. He prices out what one hire is worth in gross profit and makes recruiting a funnel with a real budget.
  • Cash collected too late (about 7 cases). Quarterly billing, pay-per-visit, slow resident profit. He moves cash forward with card-on-file assessments, prepaid years, enrolment fees and up-front bundles.
  • Too many avatars, channels or ventures (about 8 cases). Trash with two avatars and five channels, clinics with many services, owners with side ventures. He cuts to one avatar, one channel and one model before expanding.
  • Weak front door and slow selling (about 6 cases). No dedicated landing page, sales delayed to day two, leads called back late. He adds a cheap front-end offer, same-day selling and an instant call-back.
  • Missing back end and rebooking (about 6 cases). No membership, no rebook, no maintenance plan, no second visit for first-timers. He adds a default membership or plan and an ask at every visit.
  • Owner, partner or leadership gap (about 8 cases). Misaligned partners, missing finance function, no senior operators. He fixes partnerships first and pays up for experienced leaders, accepting lower short-term profit.
  • Leave a loved product alone (2–3 cases, from his own gyms). When customers love it, replicate or raise price; spend where customers see value.

How Alex would apply it

First questions. 1. What are your show rate, close rate and return on ad spend? Very high numbers mean you are underpriced and underspending. 2. Are you limited by demand (empty chairs, open slots) or by supply (waiting list, owner or expert fully booked)? 3. How much cash do you collect on day one per customer, and does it cover the cost of getting and serving them? 4. How many avatars, services, locations and side ventures are you running, and which one makes the money? 5. What does one more technician, practitioner or rep bring in gross profit per year?

Default moves by situation. - High close rate, open capacity: raise price 10–20%, build a dedicated landing page, then increase spend on the channel that already works until returns drop toward about 5:1. - Waiting list or expert at capacity: move simple work to associates, make recruiting a funnel with a budget sized to a hire's gross profit, then raise price. - Cash squeeze: restructure the offer so more cash comes up front (assessment with card on file, prepaid year, enrolment fee, first quarter up front), cut the lowest-margin payers or services, and stop new initiatives. - Lots of leads, poor conversion: call leads within a minute, sell the same day after the assessment, add a VIP tier and name the differentiators. - One-time sale: add a membership or maintenance plan, rebook before the customer leaves, and ask for a referral at each visit. - Owner wants a new venture or national expansion: first dominate the current market and fix the current constraint; a new business rarely fixes the skill gap behind the plateau. - Multiple locations: fix profit and payback per location before opening the next, and bring in a finance lead and a seasoned operator.

Bistvo

Lokalni lastniki skoraj vedno pridejo z besedami, da potrebujejo več leadov. Ko Alex pogleda številke, so leadi le redko prvi problem. Stopnje zaključka 70 do 90 odstotkov, udeležba na terminih blizu 100 odstotkov in oglasi z donosom 10:1 ali več mu povedo, da ima podjetje prenizke cene in porabi veliko premalo na kanalu, ki že deluje. Njegove prve poteze so običajno dvig cene za 10 do 40 odstotkov, namenska pristajalna stran in več denarja za oglase na kanalu, ki je že dobičkonosen. Majhne spremembe na posamezen račun (vrstni red jedilnika, priložena pijača, korak ponovne rezervacije) gredo v podjetju z velikim obsegom naravnost v dobiček.

Drugi vzorec je kapaciteta. Veliko lokalnih podjetij omejuje en zdravnik, ustanovitelj, ki je še vedno v kombiju, ali pomanjkanje tehnikov. Alex obravnava zaposlovanje kot osrednjo funkcijo podjetja z lastnim lijakom in proračunom. Izračuna, koliko bruto dobička prinese en strokovnjak ali prodajalec, in lastniku tako pokaže, kako poceni je v resnici velik bonus za zaposlitev. Strokovnjaka premakne na najzahtevnejše delo, preprostejšo polovico pa preda sodelavcem.

Tretji vzorec je časovni zamik denarja. Četrtletno obračunavanje, plačilo po obisku in vnaprej plačana oprema izčrpajo rastoče lokalno podjetje. Ponudbo preoblikuje tako, da prvi dan pride več denarja: uvodni pregled s kartico na datoteki, vnaprej plačano leto, vpisnina, prvo četrtletje vnaprej. Pod vsem tem leži fokus. Odreže odvečne avatarje, stranske projekte in partnerske dogovore, da lahko lastnik potisne en model na enem trgu, preden se širi.

  • Prenizke cene, zato najprej dvig cene (okoli 10 primerov). Stopnje zaključka 70–90 % in polni urniki zanj pomenijo prenizko ceno.   • Premalo porabe na kanalu, ki že deluje (okoli 8 primerov). Iskalni ali družbeni oglasi z donosom 5:1 do 13:1 na drobnem proračunu, neizkoriščena ekipa od vrat do vrat ali mreženje. Zmagovalca poveča, preden preizkuša karkoli novega, uspeh pa meri v skupnem dobičku.
  • Kapaciteto omejuje en strokovnjak ali premalo ljudi (okoli 9 primerov). Zdravniki ustanovitelji, lastniki v kombiju, manjkajoči tehniki, čistilci in terapevti. Izračuna, koliko bruto dobička prinese en zaposleni, in zaposlovanje spremeni v lijak s pravim proračunom.

  • Preveč avatarjev, kanalov ali projektov (okoli 8 primerov). Odvoz smeti z dvema avatarjema in petimi kanali, klinike s preveč storitvami, lastniki s stranskimi projekti. Pred širitvijo zreže na en avatar, en kanal in en model.
  • Šibka vhodna vrata in počasna prodaja (okoli 6 primerov). Brez namenske pristajalne strani, prodaja preložena na drugi dan, leadi poklicani pozno. Doda poceni uvodno ponudbo, prodajo isti dan in takojšen povratni klic.
  • Manjka nadaljnja ponudba in ponovna rezervacija (okoli 6 primerov). Brez članstva, brez ponovne rezervacije, brez vzdrževalnega paketa, brez drugega obiska za nove goste. Doda privzeto članstvo ali paket in prošnjo ob vsakem obisku.

Prva vprašanja. 1. Kakšna je tvoja udeležba na terminih, stopnja zaključka in donos na oglase? Zelo visoke številke pomenijo prenizke cene in premalo porabe. 2. Te omejuje povpraševanje (prazni stoli, prosti termini) ali ponudba (čakalna vrsta, lastnik ali strokovnjak povsem zaseden)? 3. Koliko denarja zbereš od stranke prvi dan in ali pokrije strošek, da jo pridobiš in postrežeš? 4. Koliko avatarjev, storitev, lokacij in stranskih projektov imaš in kateri prinaša denar? 5. Koliko bruto dobička na leto prinese en dodaten tehnik, terapevt ali prodajalec?

Privzete poteze po situaciji. - Visok zaključek, prosta kapaciteta: cena gor za 10–20 %, namenska pristajalna stran, nato več porabe na kanalu, ki že deluje, dokler donos ne pade proti okoli 5:1. - Čakalna vrsta ali strokovnjak na meji: preprostejše delo predaj sodelavcem, zaposlovanje spremeni v lijak s proračunom, ki ustreza bruto dobičku enega zaposlenega, nato dvigni ceno. - Stiska z denarjem: preoblikuj ponudbo, da pride več denarja vnaprej (pregled s kartico na datoteki, vnaprej plačano leto, vpisnina, prvo četrtletje vnaprej), odreži plačnike ali storitve z najnižjo maržo in ustavi nove pobude. - Veliko leadov, slaba konverzija: pokliči lead v minuti, prodaj isti dan po pregledu, dodaj VIP raven in poimenuj razlike. - Enkratna prodaja: dodaj članstvo ali vzdrževalni paket, ponovno rezerviraj, preden stranka odide, in ob vsakem obisku prosi za priporočilo. - Lastnik želi nov projekt ali nacionalno širitev: najprej obvladaj sedanji trg in odpravi sedanjo omejitev; nov posel le redko odpravi vrzel v veščinah, ki je povzročila zastoj. - Več lokacij: pred odprtjem naslednje uredi dobiček in vračilno dobo na lokacijo ter pripelji finančnega vodjo in izkušenega operativca.
