Alex's Playbook · Pull cash forward (30-day payback)

Alex treats up-front cash as the master variable. If the first 30 days of a customer pay for acquiring and serving them, growth stops being limited by money, so he fixes cash flow through offer design before anyone talks about debt or investors.Alex obravnava denar vnaprej kot glavno spremenljivko. Če prvih 30 dni stranke plača njeno pridobitev in izvedbo, rast ni več omejena z denarjem, zato denarni tok popravi z obliko ponudbe, preden kdor koli omeni posojilo ali investitorje.

The gist

In the notes, cash flow problems are almost never solved with a loan or a funding round. Alex solves them by changing how and when the customer pays: prepay discounts, setup fees, quarterly or annual billing, layaway, card on file, a planned ladder of payment options. The pattern recurs across hotseats, lectures and sales trainings, and up-front cash works as the master variable behind much of his advice.

The reasoning is simple. If a new customer returns more cash in the first month than they cost to acquire and serve, every customer funds the next one. He ties the 30-day window to the interest-free period on a credit card: it is about as long as a small business can carry the cost of acquisition without its own money.

30-day rule: cash collected in the first 30 days > fully loaded delivery cost + CAC. Stronger version (one keynote): 30-day gross profit from one customer > cost to acquire and serve two customers.

How it works

The 30-day rules

  • Target CAC payback within 30 days. With that in place he says there is no fixed marketing budget: you spend as long as it stays profitable. A fixed budget, in his framing, means you lose money up front.
  • Know your payback curve. In one talk he asks for cash returned per dollar of ad spend at day 15, 30 and 60. Without it, marketing decisions are guesswork.
  • Testing budget. Let a new campaign spend up to about twice the allowable CAC (based on 30-day cash) before switching it off.
  • When payback is too long, he looks at setup fees, onboarding fees, bundled physical products, longer prepaid durations, buy-one-get-two offers, or last month paid up front.

Prepay and pay-in-full discounts

He wants at least a 10% discount for paying in full, and calls 10–20% worth it because a small business earns more on its capital than the markets do. A ladder he uses in one sales training: about 20% off for full prepay, about 10% for half down, list price on the payment plan. Two framing rules:

  • Show the highest price first (the payment plan), then offer discounts for paying sooner. You become the one giving a benefit, not the one adding interest.
  • Say "prepayment discount", not "paid in full". The second is seller language with nothing in it for the buyer.

He often prefers perks to discounts: faster delivery (skip the line), less risk (a longer guarantee), more ease (concierge). He quotes rough uptake in one lecture: buy-10-get-2 gets 15–20% to prepay, a moderate discount plus perks 30–40%.

Up-front fees

Use something up front as the vehicle for cash: a setup fee, onboarding fee or equipment. Build the first payment from hard costs up (delivery, hardware, commission) so it covers what you have to pay before the customer has been with you long. A waived setup fee works as an A/B close: waive it for an annual commitment, charge it for quarter-to-quarter flexibility.

Quarterly and annual prepay

Ask new customers, not existing ones, to prepay a quarter to offset CAC; changing billing for newcomers doesn't push current clients away. Annual can be the default or even the only option, with a status perk or a physical bonus attached only to annual. A typical annual discount is about 16% (pay ten months, get twelve). In one lecture he argues that even at half the conversion, annual prepay can bring in several times more cash up front, which funds aggressive acquisition. Quarterly is the middle ground when annual is too much for the buyer.

Layaway and payment ladders

Layaway means the buyer pays first and delivery starts when they are paid up. Buyers then speed up their own payments, collection is easier, and you carry no delivery risk. His standard step-down order on a sales call:

  1. Full prepay with a discount
  2. Third-party financing
  3. Partial prepay with a smaller discount
  4. Payment plan (with automatic renewal at the end, which he finds stickier than month-to-month)
  5. A smaller commitment, or just booking the next visit

Financing can lift sales (he cites about 35% in one lecture) but won't save a bad product.

Card on file

A heavily discounted assessment or a small paid ticket (around $99 in one hotseat) mainly serves to get a card on file. He says a small up-front fee typically gives 70–80% show rates and makes the later sale and upsell smoother: the next purchase is charged to the card already stored.

When cash is short right now

His order for cash in 48 hours, from one live Q&A: current customers first (prepay, preferably for extra service rather than a discount), then past customers (an "I owe you" credit toward a bigger package), then the lead list (an attraction offer). In a cash crisis he picks one bet with the biggest cash potential in the window and sells the strongest prepaid offer to the existing list, then recycles the cash into ads. Short-term cash plays are acceptable if they fund fixing the real constraint.

Examples

Cases are paraphrased and anonymised from Alex's videos. Any example marked as invented is for illustration only.

Residential waste-collection startup, losing money

Before. Monthly service fee only. Every signup costs labour, a bin and a sales commission before the customer has paid much.

After. First payment built from hard costs: roughly $42 labour + $116 bin + $50 commission ≈ $208 at signup. An A/B offer: prepay a year and get the bin free, or the core plan with a bin fee. Equipment financing as a fallback, with a yearly price increase built in.

Cash at signup covers the cost of each customer, so commission-only door-to-door reps can scale without debt. He notes that even if only 20% take the anchor, average cash per sale goes up.

Indoor family park, weeks from running out of cash

Revenue around $4.8M, unprofitable, heavily indebted. Alex chose one bet only: a prepaid summer camp offer sold to the existing list through a giveaway with a claim deadline and phone follow-up, with the cash recycled into ads.

Couples coaching, slow payback

Two 12-month offers and ad-driven challenges with slow payback. He asked for about $9K (the first quarter) up front, offered layaway as the downsell, and tied fast-action bonuses to the up-front payment. His expectation was that the first payment, more than the total price, drives conversion.

With monthly billing the agency waits almost four months to recover the cost of each client. With an onboarding fee and the first quarter prepaid, the client funds the next acquisition in month one.

Common mistakes

  • Reaching for debt or investors first. He treats a cash gap as an offer-design problem. If a business with strong returns can't raise money, he blames the deal terms, not the pitch.
  • Discounting without asking for cash. A discount that isn't tied to prepayment or commitment gives away margin and gets nothing back.
  • Calling it "paid in full". Seller language. Frame it as a prepayment discount the buyer receives.
  • Changing billing for existing clients. Apply new payment terms to new customers.
  • Paying sales on revenue booked. He says pay rarely changes rep behaviour, with one exception: paying on cash collected raises cash collected.

How Alex would apply it

  • He asks for the 30-day number first. What is a customer worth in gross profit within 30 days, and what does it cost to acquire and serve them?
  • He redesigns the offer before the financing. Setup fees, prepay, annual default, layaway. Debt and capital come after the offer cannot be improved.
  • He anchors high and steps down. Payment-plan price first, prepay discount second, then financing, partial prepay, plan, smaller commitment. Everyone buys something.
  • He uses perks before percentages. Faster, safer, easier, or a bonus worth more to the buyer than it costs you.
  • He goes to existing customers when cash is urgent. Prepay for extra service, credits toward bigger packages, one strong offer to the list.
  • He removes the money ceiling, then spends. Once acquisition funds itself, the limit should be operational, not financial.
  • Questions he would ask: How much cash do you collect in the first 30 days? What is your CAC, and how long until it's paid back? Do you offer prepay or financing, and what share takes it? Do you have a card on file? How much cash do you have, and can you deliver what you've already sold with it?

Worksheet

Fill in with your last ten new clients.

  1. 30-day cash: What did each client pay in their first 30 days, on average?
  2. 30-day cost: What did delivery cost in those 30 days, and what was CAC per client?
  3. Gap: Is cash collected above or below cost? By how much?
  4. Vehicles: Which up-front item fits your offer: a setup fee, onboarding, first quarter prepaid, annual default?
  5. Ladder: Write your step-down sequence from full prepay to the smallest commitment, with exact prices.
  6. Urgent cash: If you needed cash in 48 hours, what would you offer current and past clients?

Bistvo

V zapiskih Alex težav z denarnim tokom skoraj nikoli ne rešuje s posojilom ali krogom financiranja. Rešuje jih tako, da spremeni, kako in kdaj stranka plača: popusti za predplačilo, pristojbine za nastavitev, četrtletno ali letno zaračunavanje, plačilo pred dobavo (layaway), shranjena kartica, načrtovana lestev plačilnih možnosti. Vzorec se ponavlja v hotseatih, predavanjih in prodajnih treningih, denar vnaprej pa deluje kot glavna spremenljivka za velikim delom njegovih nasvetov.

Logika je preprosta. Če nova stranka v prvem mesecu vrne več denarja, kot je stala njena pridobitev in izvedba, vsaka stranka financira naslednjo. Okno 30 dni poveže z brezobrestnim obdobjem kreditne kartice: približno toliko časa lahko malo podjetje nosi strošek pridobivanja brez lastnega denarja.

Pravilo 30 dni: denar, pobran v prvih 30 dneh > polni strošek izvedbe + CAC. Strožja različica (eno predavanje): bruto dobiček ene stranke v 30 dneh > strošek pridobitve in izvedbe za dve stranki.

Kako deluje

Pravila 30 dni

  • Cilj je povračilo CAC v 30 dneh. Ko to dosežeš, po njegovem ni več fiksnega marketinškega proračuna: trošiš, dokler se izplača. Fiksen proračun v njegovem okviru pomeni, da na začetku izgubljaš denar.
  • Poznaj krivuljo povračila. V enem predavanju sprašuje, koliko denarja se vrne na dolar oglaševanja na 15., 30. in 60. dan. Brez tega so marketinške odločitve ugibanje.
  • Proračun za teste. Nova kampanja lahko porabi do približno dvakratnik dovoljenega CAC (izračunanega iz denarja v 30 dneh), preden jo ugasneš.
  • Ko je povračilo predolgo, pogleda pristojbine za nastavitev, uvajanje, priložene fizične izdelke, daljša predplačila, ponudbe »kupi enega, dobiš dva« ali plačilo zadnjega meseca vnaprej.

Popusti za predplačilo

Za plačilo v celoti želi vsaj 10 % popusta, 10–20 % pa se mu zdi vredno, ker malo podjetje s kapitalom zasluži več kot trgi. Lestev iz enega prodajnega treninga: okoli 20 % popusta za celotno predplačilo, okoli 10 % za polovico vnaprej, polna cena na obroke. Dve pravili predstavitve:

  • Najprej pokaži najvišjo ceno (obročno), nato ponudi popust za hitrejše plačilo. Tako si ti tisti, ki daje ugodnost, in ne tisti, ki dodaja obresti.
  • Reci »popust za predplačilo«, ne »plačilo v celoti« (paid in full). Drugo je prodajalski jezik, v katerem za kupca ni ničesar.

Pogosto ima raje ugodnosti kot popuste: hitrejša izvedba (preskok vrste), manj tveganja (daljša garancija), več udobja (osebni skrbnik). V enem predavanju navede okviren delež: »kupi 10, dobiš 2« pripelje 15–20 % kupcev do predplačila, zmeren popust z ugodnostmi pa 30–40 %.

Pristojbine vnaprej

Za pobiranje denarja uporabi nekaj, kar se plača vnaprej: pristojbino za nastavitev, za uvajanje ali opremo. Prvo plačilo sestavi od trdih stroškov navzgor (izvedba, oprema, provizija), da pokrije vse, kar moraš plačati, preden je stranka s tabo dolgo. Odpis pristojbine za nastavitev deluje kot zapiranje A/B: odpišeš jo ob letni zavezi, zaračunaš pa, če stranka želi fleksibilnost iz četrtletja v četrtletje.

Četrtletno in letno predplačilo

Nove stranke, ne obstoječih, prosi za predplačilo četrtletja, da pokriješ CAC; sprememba plačevanja za nove ne odžene obstoječih. Letni paket je lahko privzeta ali celo edina možnost, s statusno ugodnostjo ali fizičnim bonusom samo za letni paket. Tipičen letni popust je okoli 16 % (plačaš deset mesecev, dobiš dvanajst). V enem predavanju trdi, da lahko letno predplačilo celo pri polovični konverziji prinese večkrat več denarja vnaprej, kar financira agresivno pridobivanje strank. Četrtletje je srednja pot, ko je letno preveč za kupca.

Plačilo pred dobavo in plačilne lestve

Layaway pomeni, da kupec najprej plača, izvedba pa se začne, ko je plačano. Kupci nato sami pospešijo plačila, izterjava je lažja, ti pa ne nosiš tveganja izvedbe. Njegov standardni vrstni red korakov navzdol na prodajnem klicu:

  1. Celotno predplačilo s popustom
  2. Financiranje prek tretje osebe
  3. Delno predplačilo z manjšim popustom
  4. Obročno plačilo (s samodejnim podaljšanjem na koncu, ki se mu zdi trajnejše od mesečne naročnine)
  5. Manjša zaveza ali samo rezervacija naslednjega obiska

Financiranje lahko dvigne prodajo (v enem predavanju navede okoli 35 %), slabega izdelka pa ne reši.

Shranjena kartica

Močno znižana ocena stanja ali majhna plačana vstopnica (v enem hotseatu okoli 99 $) služita predvsem temu, da shraniš kupčevo plačilno kartico (card on file). Pravi, da majhna pristojbina vnaprej navadno prinese 70–80 % udeležbe in olajša kasnejšo prodajo in nadgradnjo: naslednji nakup se bremeni na že shranjeno kartico.

Ko denar potrebuješ takoj

Njegov vrstni red za denar v 48 urah, iz enega Q&A v živo: najprej obstoječe stranke (predplačilo, raje za dodatno storitev kot za popust), nato bivše stranke (dobropis »dolgujem ti« za večji paket), nato seznam leadov (privabljalna ponudba). V denarni krizi izbere eno samo stavo z največjim denarnim potencialom v razpoložljivem času, najmočnejšo predplačniško ponudbo proda obstoječemu seznamu in denar takoj vloži v oglase. Kratkoročne denarne poteze so sprejemljive, če financirajo odpravo pravega ozkega grla.

Primeri

Primeri so povzeti in anonimizirani iz Alexovih videov. Primeri, označeni kot izmišljeni, so samo za ponazoritev.

Zagonsko podjetje za odvoz odpadkov iz gospodinjstev, z izgubo

Pred. Samo mesečna naročnina. Vsak nov naročnik stane delo, zabojnik in prodajno provizijo, preden je stranka sploh kaj plačala.

Po. Prvo plačilo sestavljeno iz trdih stroškov: približno 42 $ dela + 116 $ zabojnik + 50 $ provizija ≈ 208 $ ob podpisu. Ponudba A/B: plačaj leto vnaprej in dobiš zabojnik brezplačno, ali osnovni paket s pristojbino za zabojnik. Financiranje opreme kot rezerva, z vgrajenim letnim dvigom cene.

Denar ob podpisu pokrije strošek vsake stranke, zato lahko prodajalci od vrat do vrat, plačani samo s provizijo, rastejo brez dolga. Opozori, da se povprečen denar na prodajo dvigne, četudi sidro vzame le 20 % kupcev.

Notranji družinski zabaviščni park, nekaj tednov pred koncem denarja

Prihodek okoli 4,8 mio $, brez dobička, močno zadolžen. Alex je izbral eno samo stavo: predplačniško ponudbo poletnega tabora, prodano obstoječemu seznamu prek nagradne igre z rokom za prevzem in telefonskim sledenjem, denar pa takoj nazaj v oglase.

Coaching za pare, počasno povračilo

Dve 12-mesečni ponudbi in izzivi, gnani z oglasi, s počasnim povračilom. Zahteval je okoli 9.000 $ (prvo četrtletje) vnaprej, plačilo pred dobavo kot cenejšo možnost, bonuse za hitro odločitev pa vezal na plačilo vnaprej. Pričakoval je, da konverzijo bolj kot skupna cena poganja prvo plačilo.

Pogoste napake

  • Najprej posojilo ali investitorji. Denarno vrzel obravnava kot problem oblike ponudbe. Če podjetje z dobrimi donosi ne zbere denarja, krivi pogoje posla, ne predstavitve.
  • Popust brez zahteve po denarju. Popust, ki ni vezan na predplačilo ali zavezo, podari maržo in ne prinese ničesar nazaj.
  • Izraz »plačilo v celoti«. Prodajalski jezik. Predstavi ga kot popust za predplačilo, ki ga dobi kupec.
  • Spreminjanje plačevanja obstoječim strankam. Nove plačilne pogoje uvedi za nove stranke.
  • Plačilo prodajalcev po zaračunanem prihodku. Pravi, da plačilo redko spremeni vedenje prodajalcev, z eno izjemo: plačilo po pobranem denarju poveča pobrani denar.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Najprej vpraša za številko 30 dni. Koliko je stranka vredna v bruto dobičku v 30 dneh in koliko stane, da jo pridobiš in ji strežeš?
  • Ponudbo preoblikuje pred financiranjem. Pristojbine za nastavitev, predplačilo, letni paket kot privzeta izbira, plačilo pred dobavo. Dolg in kapital prideta šele, ko ponudbe ni več mogoče izboljšati.
  • Začne visoko in gre po stopnicah navzdol. Najprej obročna cena, nato popust za predplačilo, financiranje, delno predplačilo, obroki, manjša zaveza. Vsak nekaj kupi.
  • Ugodnosti uporabi pred odstotki. Hitreje, varneje, lažje ali bonus, ki je kupcu vreden več, kot tebe stane.
  • Ko je denar nujen, gre k obstoječim strankam. Predplačilo za dodatno storitev, dobropisi za večje pakete, ena močna ponudba seznamu.
  • Odstrani denarni strop, nato troši. Ko se pridobivanje strank financira samo, naj bo omejitev operativna, ne finančna.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Koliko denarja pobereš v prvih 30 dneh? Kakšen je CAC in v kolikšnem času se povrne? Ponujaš predplačilo ali financiranje in kolikšen delež ga vzame? Imaš shranjeno kupčevo kartico? Koliko denarja imaš in ali z njim lahko izvedeš vse, kar si že prodal?

Delovni list

Izpolni za zadnjih deset novih strank.

  1. Denar v 30 dneh: Koliko je posamezna stranka v povprečju plačala v prvih 30 dneh?
  2. Strošek v 30 dneh: Koliko je stala izvedba v teh 30 dneh in kakšen je bil CAC na stranko?
  3. Vrzel: Je pobrani denar nad stroškom ali pod njim? Za koliko?
  4. Nosilci: Kateri element vnaprej ustreza tvoji ponudbi: pristojbina za nastavitev, uvajanje, predplačano prvo četrtletje, letni paket kot privzeta izbira?
  5. Lestev: Zapiši svoje zaporedje korakov od celotnega predplačila do najmanjše zaveze, z natančnimi cenami.
  6. Nujni denar: Če bi denar potreboval v 48 urah, kaj bi ponudil obstoječim in bivšim strankam?
