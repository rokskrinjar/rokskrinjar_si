Hiring & TalentZaposlovanje in talenti

Alex's Playbook · Who, not how

Past the first stage, the speed of a company is the speed at which it recruits. Alex treats talent as the long-term constraint and runs hiring with the same funnel, metrics and standards as customer acquisition.Ko je prva faza mimo, podjetje raste tako hitro, kot zna zaposlovati. Alex vidi talent kot dolgoročno ozko grlo in zaposlovanje vodi z enakim lijakom, metrikami in standardi kot pridobivanje strank.

The gist

In almost every lecture on scaling, Alex ends up at the same place: once demand is solved, the business grows only as fast as it can find, train and keep good people. When an owner lists six problems, he often reads them as one: the company is missing around three senior hires. Above roughly $30M a year he describes his own bottleneck as nothing but talent.

Scaling speed = speed of recruiting talent. Ask "who" before "how": often the fastest answer is someone who has already done it.

He backs this with blunt economics. In his framing an A-player costs a bit more and produces several times as much, so paying properly for the right person is one of the largest arbitrages in business. His recurring rules of thumb: hire when the freed time or capacity returns several times the salary (he mentions 4 to 5x), and treat each new salary band as a new territory that reveals talent you did not know existed.

How it works

Recruiting is a second funnel

Across at least five videos he describes recruiting as a mirror of the customer pipeline: application generation, application nurture, the interview (which is a sales call), onboarding and training (fulfilment), then retention and ascension. You use the same channels you use for customers: ads, content, your own lists, direct outreach including poaching, referrals and word of mouth. You track the same kind of numbers: reach-outs per interview, finals per hire, cost and time to fill, net headcount after churn.

His rules of thumb: about 100 LinkedIn reach-outs per hire in one example, and strong candidates are gone within about 8 days, so a slow first reply loses them. Referral bonuses are sized against the gross profit a hire produces.

Two interview tests

These recur in several talks:

  1. Metrics. How many relevant metrics does the candidate use to describe their job, and how well do they know them? Someone who cannot define the outcome cannot deliver it. A strong HR candidate once quizzed him on cost per hire, time to fill and two-way fit at 90 days.
  2. Money. "How does what you do make the business money?" He rejects vague answers. A sales leader should talk about raising conversion across the team's leads; a finance lead about cutting costs and turning numbers into decisions.

For leadership roles he adds a third: if you are not learning from them in the interview, do not hire them. For specific roles he prefers a real problem over hypotheticals: show the actual stats and ask how they would attack it.

Interview volume is the method

He interviews far more people than feels sensible. Volume finds the outlier and trains your judgement: by the fiftieth interview for one role, you can tell. He cites a partner who interviewed about 600 developers to find a technical co-founder, and his own five sales managers before one worked. For front-line to manager roles he runs group interviews, where strong candidates speak clearly and help the nervous ones. For a role you do not understand, the interviews are your education.

Who to hire

  • Smallest skill gap. Every skill is trainable; the question is the cost of training it. Prefer the candidate whose missing skill is quickest to teach.
  • Experience you can buy. For senior roles, hire someone who has already taken a similar business from your size to the size you want, ideally with about ten years in the function. Their mistakes were paid for by someone else.
  • Leaders over managers. He grades people on three levels: individual contributor (knows what), manager (knows how, teaches it), leader (builds the system that trains people). He says leaders become necessary from about $3M a year.
  • First hires. The first five or so are extensions of you that take over repeated, low-value work: support and admin, then a setter or salesperson, marketing help, a fractional bookkeeper, a tech person. If you sell well, hire sales later.
  • Bar raiser. Hire only people who raise the average of the team they join, because people tend to hire slightly worse than themselves.

Promotion risk

His most repeated warning: the best closer is rarely the best sales manager. Hunters rarely want to coach. Assess each level separately, show a top performer the manager's day before they ask for it, and keep early titles modest so there is room to hire above. If a promotion fails, redefine their role with someone above them, or part ways. Own your mistake and frame the senior hire as an investment in their career.

Standards, tolerance and culture

The weakest team has the manager who tolerates the most. A long list of rules is, in his reading, a sign of low standards and wrong hires. The person with the highest standards should be in charge. His sales-team culture system: visible daily leaderboard, clear KPIs, a competition every 4 to 6 weeks, and the bottom 10% cut each quarter. A high-performance culture keeps attrition low even while firing often, because the strong stay.

Firing and pay

Two weeks below KPI means a performance plan; two more weeks without improvement means exit, and new hires hear this on day one. He rarely moves an underperformer sideways (he says nine times out of ten it is the wrong call). On pay: change it only to fix a problem attracting or keeping talent, because raises cannot be taken back. Before approving a new hire, ask for the team's hour-by-hour utilisation. Do not hire ahead of growth, with commission sales reps as the exception.

Examples

Cases are paraphrased and anonymised from Alex's videos. Any example marked as invented is for illustration only.

Used-car dealership with too many priorities

Before. A profitable dealership (about $500–600K profit) wants branding, social ads, a commercial-van line and a service shop, all at once, run by the owner.

After. Alex reads it as a missing-people problem: about three senior hires. First a finance lead, told to find their own salary in savings within 90 days. Then operators for service and the used lot. Only then does the owner chase the van line with freed time.

The finance hire funds the next hire, and each senior person removes a whole problem from the owner's list.

Wholesale business after a bad promotion

Before. Deals fall from about 70 to 40 a month after the sales manager and second-best closer leave. The top closer is promoted and coaches poorly. The owner wants to hire a sales director.

After. The owner learns sales personally, simplifies one script, enforces it for everyone and trains through call review and drills.

The wrong personality was promoted, and a sales-led company whose owner cannot run sales stays hostage to whoever does.

Solo agency afraid of the first hire

A solo ads agency with about $90K real profit asked how to make its first hire. Alex's diagnosis: the model is broken, and hiring would only scale the problem. Before hiring, either 10x the price per client or 10x delivery efficiency, because there is no margin to pay anyone.

Common mistakes

  • Hiring the first decent person. Without volume you have no comparison and no judgement.
  • Promoting the best performer by default. You can lose a great contributor and gain a weak manager.
  • Saving on salary for a key role. A few thousand saved on a person who moves the company years forward is a false economy in his view.
  • Recruiting with no system. No channels, no speed, no metrics, then concluding "good people don't exist".
  • Tolerating low standards to keep the peace. The tolerance becomes the culture.

How Alex would apply it

  • He checks whether talent is really the constraint. If the model has no margin, he fixes price or delivery before any hire.
  • He makes recruiting the owner's job when it is the bottleneck, compressing it hard: many interviews in one week, tracked like a sales pipeline.
  • He buys experience for senior roles and accepts a short-term profit dip to pay for higher-level leaders, framing it as skipping years of mistakes.
  • He interviews with the two tests (metrics, how the role makes money) and passes on leaders he cannot learn from.
  • He sets standards visibly and enforces them on everyone, including the top performer, with time-boxed performance plans.
  • He refuses sideways moves for failing people, inflated titles in small teams, and pay raises that do not solve a recruiting or retention problem.
  • Questions he would ask: If you had ten of your best person, would you make more money? What are you doing this week to recruit? How many candidates per hire, how fast is first contact? What metrics does this person use for their job? How does their role make money? Does this hire raise the team's average?

Worksheet

Answer in writing for your next or most important hire.

  1. Constraint: Is talent the thing that stops you from doing more, or is it price, demand or the model?
  2. Return: What will this person free up or produce, and is it at least several times their cost?
  3. Funnel: Which channels will you use, how many candidates do you need per hire, and how fast will you reply?
  4. Tests: Which metrics should a strong candidate name, and what real problem will you give them in the final round?
  5. Standards: What will you no longer tolerate, and what is the written performance-plan rule new hires will hear on day one?

Bistvo

Skoraj v vsakem predavanju o rasti Alex pride do istega: ko je povpraševanje rešeno, podjetje raste samo tako hitro, kot zna najti, izučiti in obdržati dobre ljudi. Ko lastnik našteje šest problemov, jih Alex pogosto prebere kot enega: podjetju manjkajo približno trije višji kadri. Nad približno 30 milijoni dolarjev letno svoje lastno ozko grlo opiše kot čisti talent.

Hitrost rasti = hitrost zaposlovanja talentov. Najprej vprašaj »kdo« (who), šele potem »kako« (how): najhitrejši odgovor je pogosto nekdo, ki je to že naredil.

To podpre z neposredno ekonomiko. V njegovem okviru A-igralec (A-player) stane malo več in ustvari večkrat toliko, zato je pošteno plačilo pravemu človeku ena največjih arbitraž v poslu. Njegova ponavljajoča se pravila palca: zaposli, ko sproščeni čas ali kapaciteta vrne večkratnik plače (omenja 4- do 5-krat), in vsak nov plačni razred obravnavaj kot novo ozemlje, ki ti pokaže talent, za katerega nisi vedel, da obstaja.

Kako deluje

Zaposlovanje je drugi lijak

V vsaj petih videih zaposlovanje opiše kot zrcalo prodajnega lijaka: pridobivanje prijav, negovanje prijav, razgovor (ki je prodajni pogovor), uvajanje in usposabljanje (izvedba), nato zadrževanje in napredovanje. Uporabljaš iste kanale kot za stranke: oglase, vsebino, lastne sezname, neposredno nagovarjanje, tudi prevzemanje ljudi iz drugih podjetij, priporočila in govorico. Spremljaš podobne številke: nagovore na razgovor, finalne kandidate na zaposlitev, strošek in čas do zapolnitve, neto število zaposlenih po odhodih.

Njegova pravila palca: v enem primeru približno 100 nagovorov na LinkedInu na eno zaposlitev, dobri kandidati pa so s trga v približno 8 dneh, zato te počasen prvi odziv stane kandidata. Bonuse za priporočila odmeri glede na bruto dobiček, ki ga nov človek ustvari.

Dva testa na razgovoru

Ponavljata se v več predavanjih:

  1. Metrike. Koliko relevantnih metrik kandidat uporabi, ko opisuje svoje delo, in kako dobro jih pozna? Kdor ne zna opredeliti izida, ga ne bo dosegel. Dober kandidat za kadrovsko ga je nekoč sam spraševal o strošku zaposlitve, času do zapolnitve in obojestranskem ujemanju po 90 dneh.
  2. Denar. »Kako to, kar delaš, podjetju prinaša denar?« Nejasnih odgovorov ne sprejme. Vodja prodaje naj govori o dvigu konverzije na vseh leadih ekipe, finančni vodja o zniževanju stroškov in o številkah kot podlagi za odločitve.

Za vodstvene vloge doda tretjega: če se od kandidata na razgovoru ne naučiš ničesar, ga ne zaposli. Za konkretne vloge raje kot hipotetična vprašanja pokaže pravi problem: dejanske številke in vprašanje, kako bi se ga lotil.

Število razgovorov je metoda

Opravi veliko več razgovorov, kot se zdi smiselno. Količina najde izjemo in trenira tvojo presojo: do petdesetega razgovora za isto vlogo že vidiš, kdo je dober. Navaja partnerja, ki je opravil razgovore s približno 600 razvijalci, da je našel tehničnega soustanovitelja, in svojih pet vodij prodaje, preden je eden deloval. Za vloge od prve linije do vodje uporablja skupinske razgovore, kjer močni kandidati govorijo jasno in pomagajo nervoznim. Pri vlogi, ki je ne razumeš, so razgovori tvoje izobraževanje.

Koga zaposliti

  • Najmanjša vrzel v veščinah (smallest skill gap). Vsaka veščina je naučljiva; vprašanje je, koliko stane, da jo naučiš. Izberi kandidata, ki mu manjka veščina, ki se jo najhitreje nauči.
  • Izkušnje, ki jih lahko kupiš. Za višje vloge zaposli nekoga, ki je podobno podjetje že pripeljal od tvoje velikosti do velikosti, ki jo želiš, idealno z okoli desetimi leti v funkciji. Njegove napake je plačal nekdo drug.
  • Voditelji pred menedžerji. Ljudi oceni na treh ravneh: posameznik (ve, kaj), menedžer (ve, kako, in to uči), voditelj (zgradi sistem, ki uči ljudi). Pravi, da voditelje potrebuješ od približno 3 milijonov dolarjev letno.
  • Prve zaposlitve. Prvih pet ali tako je podaljšek tebe in prevzamejo ponavljajoče se delo nizke vrednosti: podporo in administracijo, nato setterja ali prodajalca, pomoč pri marketingu, zunanjega knjigovodjo, tehnika. Če dobro prodajaš, prodajo zaposli kasneje.
  • Dvig povprečja (bar raiser). Zaposli samo ljudi, ki dvignejo povprečje ekipe, v katero pridejo, ker ljudje radi zaposlijo malo slabše od sebe.

Tveganje napredovanja

Njegovo najpogostejše opozorilo: najboljši closer je redko najboljši vodja prodaje. Lovci (hunters) redko želijo učiti. Vsako raven oceni posebej, najboljšemu pokaži menedžerjev dan, še preden zanj prosi, in zgodnje nazive drži skromne, da ostane prostor za zaposlitev nad njimi. Če napredovanje spodleti, mu preoblikuj vlogo in postavi nekoga nadenj ali pa se razidita. Prevzemi svojo napako in izkušenega človeka predstavi kot naložbo v njegovo kariero.

Standardi, toleranca in kultura

Najšibkejša ekipa ima vodjo, ki največ tolerira. Dolg seznam pravil je po njegovem znak nizkih standardov in napačnih zaposlitev. Glavni naj bo človek z najvišjimi standardi. Njegov sistem kulture prodajne ekipe: vidna dnevna lestvica, jasni KPI-ji, tekmovanje vsakih 4 do 6 tednov in vsako četrtletje odhod spodnjih 10 %. Visokozmogljiva kultura ima kljub pogostemu odpuščanju nizko fluktuacijo, ker močni ostanejo.

Odpuščanje in plače

Dva tedna pod KPI pomenita načrt izboljšanja (PIP); še dva tedna brez napredka pomenita odhod, in novi zaposleni to slišijo prvi dan. Slabšega izvajalca redko premakne v drugo vlogo (pravi, da je to v devetih primerih od desetih napaka). O plačah: spremeni jih samo, če rešuješ problem privabljanja ali zadrževanja talentov, ker povišic ne moreš vzeti nazaj. Preden odobriš novo zaposlitev, zahtevaj urno izkoriščenost ekipe. Ne zaposluj pred rastjo, z izjemo prodajalcev na proviziji.

Primeri

Primeri so povzeti in anonimizirani iz Alexovih videov. Primeri, označeni kot izmišljeni, so samo za ponazoritev.

Prodajalec rabljenih avtomobilov s preveč prioritetami

Pred. Dobičkonosna prodaja rabljenih avtomobilov (okoli 500–600 tisoč dolarjev dobička) hoče hkrati blagovno znamko, oglase na družbenih omrežjih, linijo gospodarskih vozil in servis, vse pa vodi lastnik.

Po. Alex to prebere kot problem manjkajočih ljudi: približno tri višje zaposlitve. Najprej finančnik, ki mora v 90 dneh najti prihranke v višini svoje plače. Nato operativca za servis in prodajni plac. Šele potem se lastnik s sproščenim časom loti linije gospodarskih vozil.

Finančnik financira naslednjo zaposlitev, vsak višji kader pa z lastnikovega seznama odstrani cel problem.

Veleprodajni posel po slabem napredovanju

Pred. Število poslov pade s približno 70 na 40 na mesec, ko odideta vodja prodaje in drugi najboljši closer. Najboljši closer napreduje in slabo vodi. Lastnik hoče zaposliti direktorja prodaje.

Po. Lastnik se prodaje nauči sam, poenostavi en scenarij, ga uveljavi za vse in uči s pregledom posnetih klicev in vajami.

Napredoval je napačen tip osebnosti, prodajno vodeno podjetje, katerega lastnik ne zna voditi prodaje, pa ostane talec tistega, ki jo vodi.

Samostojna agencija, ki se boji prve zaposlitve

Samostojna agencija za oglase z okoli 90 tisoč dolarjev dejanskega dobička je vprašala, kako narediti prvo zaposlitev. Alexova diagnoza: model ne deluje in zaposlitev bi problem samo povečala. Pred zaposlovanjem je treba ceno na stranko dvigniti 10-krat ali 10-krat izboljšati učinkovitost izvedbe, ker ni marže, s katero bi koga plačal.

Pogoste napake

  • Zaposliš prvega spodobnega. Brez količine nimaš ne primerjave ne presoje.
  • Samodejno napredovanje najboljšega. Lahko izgubiš odličnega izvajalca in dobiš slabega vodjo.
  • Varčevanje pri plači za ključno vlogo. Nekaj tisočakov prihranka pri človeku, ki podjetje pomakne leta naprej, je po njegovem lažna ekonomija.
  • Zaposlovanje brez sistema. Brez kanalov, hitrosti in metrik, nato pa sklep, da dobrih ljudi ni.
  • Toleriranje nizkih standardov zaradi miru. Toleranca postane kultura.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Preveri, ali je talent res ozko grlo. Če model nima marže, pred vsako zaposlitvijo popravi ceno ali izvedbo.
  • Ko je zaposlovanje ozko grlo, postane lastnikova naloga, močno zgoščena: veliko razgovorov v enem tednu, spremljanih kot prodajni lijak.
  • Za višje vloge kupi izkušnje in sprejme kratkoročen padec dobička, da plača višje voditelje, kar razume kot preskok let napak.
  • Na razgovoru uporabi oba testa (metrike in kako vloga prinaša denar) in zavrne voditelje, od katerih se ne more naučiti ničesar.
  • Standarde postavi vidno in jih uveljavi za vse, tudi za najboljšega, s časovno omejenimi načrti izboljšanja.
  • Zavrača premeščanje neuspešnih ljudi v druge vloge, napihnjene nazive v majhnih ekipah in povišice, ki ne rešujejo problema zaposlovanja ali zadrževanja.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Če bi imel deset takih, kot je tvoj najboljši, bi zaslužil več? Kaj ta teden delaš, da zaposluješ? Koliko kandidatov potrebuješ na zaposlitev in kako hitro jih prvič kontaktiraš? Katere metrike ta človek uporablja pri svojem delu? Kako njegova vloga prinaša denar? Ali ta zaposlitev dvigne povprečje ekipe?

Delovni list

Pisno odgovori za svojo naslednjo ali najpomembnejšo zaposlitev.

  1. Ozko grlo: Ali te pri rasti res ustavlja talent, ali pa cena, povpraševanje ali model?
  2. Donos: Kaj bo ta človek sprostil ali ustvaril, in ali je to vsaj večkratnik njegovega stroška?
  3. Lijak: Katere kanale boš uporabil, koliko kandidatov potrebuješ na zaposlitev in kako hitro boš odgovoril?
  4. Testi: Katere metrike bi moral našteti dober kandidat in kateri pravi problem mu boš dal v finalnem krogu?
  5. Standardi: Česa ne boš več toleriral in kakšno je zapisano pravilo o načrtu izboljšanja, ki ga novi slišijo prvi dan?
