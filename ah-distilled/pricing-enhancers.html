Cena in ojačevalci ponudbePricing & Offer Enhancers

$100M Offers · Pricing, Scarcity, Urgency, Bonuses, Guarantees, Naming

Zakaj visoka cena pogosto izboljša rezultat za obe strani, in pet orodij, ki dobro ponudbo spremenijo v ponudbo, na katero je težko reči ne.

Why a high price often improves results for both sides, and five tools that turn a good offer into one that's hard to refuse.

BistvoThe gist

Nizka cena ni prednost, je past: malo marže, malo denarja za dostavo, slabši rezultati, slabe stranke. Višja cena sproži krog rasti (virtuous cycle of price). Ko je cena postavljena, ponudbo ojačaš s petimi vzvodi, ki povečajo željo in zmanjšajo tveganje, ne da bi spremenil jedro.

A low price isn't an advantage, it's a trap: thin margin, little money for delivery, weaker results, worse clients. A higher price triggers the virtuous cycle of price. Once the price is set, you amplify the offer with five levers that raise desire and reduce risk without changing the core.

Cena in pet ojačevalcevPrice and the five enhancers

Premium cena in krog rastiPremium pricing and the virtuous cycle

Ko zaračunaš več, imaš več denarja za odlično dostavo: boljše ljudi, več pozornosti, boljša orodja. Stranka, ki plača več, se bolj potrudi, ker ji je mar za vložek, zato doseže boljši rezultat. Boljši rezultati prinesejo dokaze in priporočila, ki upravičijo še višjo ceno. Pri nizki ceni se isti krog vrti navzdol.

When you charge more, you have more money for excellent delivery: better people, more attention, better tools. A client who pays more tries harder because they care about the investment, so they get a better result. Better results bring proof and referrals that justify an even higher price. At a low price the same cycle spins downward.

  1. Višja cenaHigher price
  2. Več sredstev za dostavo in bolj zavzeta strankaMore resources for delivery and a more committed client
  3. Boljši rezultatiBetter results
  4. Več dokazov in priporočil, kar upraviči višjo cenoMore proof and referrals, which justifies a higher price

Pogoj: cena mora biti precej nižja od zaznane vrednosti. Visoka cena brez vrednosti ni premium, je samo draga.

Condition: the price must be well below perceived value. A high price without value isn't premium, it's just expensive.

1. Omejenost količine1. Scarcity

Ljudje bolj želijo tisto, česar ni dovolj za vse. Scarcity je omejitev količine: le toliko mest, le toliko projektov na kvartal, le toliko kosov. Najmočnejša je, ko je resnična in jo lahko pojasniš (»imam kapaciteto za tri implementacije na kvartal«). Oblike: skupna omejitev, omejitev na obdobje ali omejitev bonusov (prvih deset dobi dodatek).

People want more of what there isn't enough of. Scarcity limits quantity: only so many seats, so many projects per quarter, so many units. It's strongest when it's real and you can explain it ("I have capacity for three implementations a quarter"). Forms: an overall cap, a cap per period, or a cap on bonuses (the first ten get an extra).

2. Časovna nujnost2. Urgency

Urgency omeji čas: ponudba ali cena velja do določenega datuma. Brez roka kupci odločitev odlašajo za nedoločeno, ker je »ne zdaj« najlažji odgovor. Praktične oblike: kohorte z datumom začetka, sezonski popusti, cena, ki se po roku dvigne, ali bonus, ki velja le ob takojšnji odločitvi. Rok mora biti spoštovan, sicer ga naslednjič nihče ne verjame.

Urgency limits time: the offer or price is valid until a set date. Without a deadline, buyers postpone indefinitely, because "not now" is the easiest answer. Practical forms: cohorts with a start date, seasonal offers, a price that rises after the deadline, or a bonus that applies only to a quick decision. The deadline must be honoured, or nobody believes it next time.

3. Bonusi3. Bonuses

Bonus ni naključno darilo. Vsak bonus naj reši konkretno oviro, ki bi kupca sicer ustavila, in to oviro povej na glas (»ker se mnogi bojijo, da ekipa tega ne bo uporabljala, dobite …«). Pri vsakem bonusu navedi njegovo vrednost, da kupec vidi, koliko dobi. Več ločenih bonusov pogosto deluje bolje kot en velik, ker vsak nagovori svojo skrb. Odlični bonusi so poceni zate in dragoceni za kupca: predloge, posnetki, kontrolni seznami.

A bonus isn't a random gift. Each bonus should solve a specific obstacle that would otherwise stop the buyer, and you name that obstacle out loud ("because many worry the team won't use it, you get …"). State the value of each bonus so the buyer sees how much they're getting. Several separate bonuses often work better than one big one, because each addresses its own concern. Great bonuses are cheap for you and valuable to the buyer: templates, recordings, checklists.

4. Garancije4. Guarantees

Garancija prenese tveganje s kupca na prodajalca in neposredno dvigne zaznano verjetnost uspeha. Večina se boji zlorab, a v praksi garancija običajno prinese več novih kupcev, kot jih izgubiš z vračili. Glavne vrste:

A guarantee moves risk from buyer to seller and directly raises perceived likelihood of success. Most people fear abuse, but in practice a guarantee usually wins more new buyers than it loses in refunds. The main types:

VrstaTypePomenMeaningKdajWhen
UnconditionalVračilo brez vprašanjRefund, no questions askedNizka cena, malo truda kupcaLower price, little buyer effort
ConditionalVračilo ali dodatno delo, če kupec opravi svoj del in rezultata niRefund or extra work if the buyer does their part and the result doesn't comeRezultat je odvisen od sodelovanjaResult depends on cooperation
Anti-guaranteeIzrecno brez vračila, z jasnim razlogomExplicitly no refunds, with a clear reasonDostaviš nekaj, česar ne moreš vzeti nazajYou deliver something you can't take back
Implied / performancePlačilo vezano na rezultat (delež prihranka, provizija)Payment tied to results (share of savings, commission)Rezultat je merljivResult is measurable

Garancija naj bo specifična (kaj, do kdaj, kaj se zgodi) in ne napisana tako, da je nihče nikoli ne more uveljaviti.

Make the guarantee specific (what, by when, what happens) and not worded so nobody could ever claim it.

5. Poimenovanje5. Naming

Ime ponudbe je prvo, kar kupec vidi. Hormozi predlaga formulo MAGIC, ki je opomnik, katere sestavine lahko vključiš (ni treba vseh):

The offer's name is the first thing the buyer sees. Hormozi suggests the MAGIC formula as a reminder of which ingredients you can include (you don't need all of them):

M · Magnetic reason whyRazlog za ponudbo zdaj (otvoritev, sezona, obletnica)A reason for the offer now (launch, season, anniversary)
A · AvatarZa koga je (»za proizvajalce«, »za mamice«)Who it's for ("for manufacturers", "for new mums")
G · GoalSanjski izid, ki ga obljubljaThe dream outcome it promises
I · IntervalČasovni okvir (»v 30 dneh«, »6 tednov«)Time frame ("in 30 days", "6 weeks")
C · Container wordBeseda za paket: program, sistem, izziv, pospeševalnikA word for the package: programme, system, challenge, accelerator

Primer: »Jesenski 30-dnevni sistem za čista poročila za vodje proizvodnje« vsebuje razlog (jesen), časovni okvir, izid, avatar in vsebnik.

Example: "Autumn 30-Day Clean Reports System for Production Managers" contains a reason (autumn), interval, goal, avatar and container.

B2B · BI svetovanjeB2B · BI consulting

Paket za finančne direktorjeA package for CFOs

PredBefore

»Qlik poročila po meri. 70 €/uro.« Brez roka, brez garancije, brez imena.

"Custom Qlik reports. €70/hour." No deadline, no guarantee, no name.

PoAfter

»Program Zaprt mesec v 3 dneh za finančne direktorje« za fiksno ceno 9.800 €. Le dve stranki na kvartal. Ob podpisu do konca meseca bonus: predloga poročila za upravo (vrednost 1.200 €). Pogojna garancija: če po 60 dneh mesečno zapiranje ni krajše, delamo brezplačno, dokler ni.

"Month-End in 3 Days Programme for CFOs" at a fixed €9,800. Only two clients per quarter. Sign by month-end and get a bonus: board report template (value €1,200). Conditional guarantee: if month-end close isn't shorter after 60 days, we keep working for free until it is.

Kaj se je spremenilo: fiksna premium cena namesto urne postavke, resnična omejena kapaciteta (scarcity), rok za bonus (urgency), bonus, ki rešuje oviro »uprava ne bo razumela«, pogojna garancija in ime po formuli MAGIC (avatar, cilj, interval, vsebnik).

What changed: a fixed premium price instead of an hourly rate, real limited capacity (scarcity), a deadline for the bonus (urgency), a bonus solving the "the board won't understand it" obstacle, a conditional guarantee and a MAGIC name (avatar, goal, interval, container).

Svetovanje · AIConsulting · AI

Garancija na podlagi rezultataA performance-based guarantee

Svetovalec za avtomatizacijo ponudi nizko začetno plačilo in delež izmerjenega prihranka ur v prvih šestih mesecih (performance guarantee). Kupec skoraj ne tvega, svetovalec pa zasluži več kot s fiksno ceno, kadar rezultat pride. Pogoj: prihranek mora biti merljiv in dogovorjen vnaprej, sicer se spor o številkah začne že prvi mesec.

An automation consultant offers a low upfront fee plus a share of measured hours saved in the first six months (performance guarantee). The buyer takes almost no risk, and the consultant earns more than with a fixed price when the result arrives. Condition: the saving must be measurable and agreed upfront, or the argument over numbers starts in month one.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Lažna omejenost in lažni rokiFake scarcity and fake deadlines

    Odštevalnik, ki se vsak dan ponastavi, uniči zaupanje. Omejitve morajo biti resnične in spoštovane.

    A countdown timer that resets every day destroys trust. Limits must be real and honoured.

  • Bonusi brez namenaBonuses without purpose

    Bonus, ki ne rešuje nobene ovire, samo zamegli ponudbo. Vsak bonus potrebuje »ker …«.

    A bonus that solves no obstacle just muddies the offer. Every bonus needs a "because …".

  • Prenizka cena iz strahuPricing low out of fear

    Nizka cena privabi kupce, ki se najmanj potrudijo in največ pritožujejo, ter ti vzame denar za dobro dostavo.

    A low price attracts buyers who try least and complain most, and takes away the money for good delivery.

  • Garancija, ki je ni mogoče uveljavitiA guarantee nobody can claim

    Preveč pogojev in drobnega tiska kupec prebere kot nezaupanje vase. Pogoji naj bodo razumni in jasni.

    Too many conditions and fine print read as a lack of confidence. Keep conditions reasonable and clear.

  • Ime, ki opisuje procesA name that describes the process

    »Svetovalni paket B« ne pove ničesar. Ime naj pove, za koga je in kaj dobi.

    "Consulting Package B" says nothing. The name should say who it's for and what they get.

Vzemi svojo Grand Slam ponudbo in jo ojačaj. Odgovori pisno.

Take your Grand Slam Offer and amplify it. Answer in writing.

Cena: Kakšno ceno bi postavil, če bi bila zaznana vrednost desetkrat večja od cene? Kaj bi z dodatno maržo izboljšal v dostavi?

Price: What price would you set if perceived value were ten times the price? What would you improve in delivery with the extra margin?

Omejenost in nujnost: Katera resnična omejitev kapacitete ali časa obstaja pri tebi?

Scarcity & urgency: What real capacity or time limit exists in your business?

Bonusi: Tri najpogostejše ovire kupcev, za vsako en bonus in njegova vrednost.

Bonuses: The three most common buyer obstacles, a bonus for each and its value.

Garancija: Katera vrsta garancije ustreza tvoji ponudbi? Zapiši jo v enem stavku: kaj, do kdaj, kaj se zgodi.

Guarantee: Which type of guarantee fits your offer? Write it in one sentence: what, by when, what happens.

Ime: Sestavi tri imena po formuli MAGIC in izberi tisto, ki bi ga stranka sama povedala kolegu.

Name: Draft three names using MAGIC and pick the one a client would repeat to a colleague.
