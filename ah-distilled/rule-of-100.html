Pravilo 100 in Več–Bolje–NovoRule of 100 & More–Better–New

$100M Leads · Rule of 100, More Better New

Kako iz »vem, kaj bi moral delati« narediti sistem: najprej dovolj volumna, potem izboljšave in šele na koncu novi kanali.

How to turn "I know what I should be doing" into a system: enough volume first, then improvements, and new channels only at the end.

BistvoThe gist

100 dejanj / dan × 100 dni100 actions / day × 100 days → VečMore → BoljeBetter → NovoNew

Rule of 100: vsak dan opravi 100 osnovnih oglaševalskih dejanj, 100 dni zapored. Na primer 100 osebnih sporočil, 100 minut ustvarjanja vsebine ali 100 $ na dan za oglase. Ko kanal deluje, ga razvijaš po vrsti More → Better → New: najprej delaš več tega, kar deluje, nato to delaš bolje, šele potem poskusiš nekaj novega.

Rule of 100: do 100 primary advertising actions every day, 100 days in a row. For example 100 personal reach-outs, 100 minutes of content creation, or $100 a day on ads. Once a channel works, you grow it in order More → Better → New: first do more of what works, then do it better, and only then try something new.

Kako delujeHow it works

Pravilo 100The Rule of 100

Kaj: zaveza k fiksni dnevni količini dela v enem od kanalov Core Four. Število 100 ni magično; pomembno je, da je dovolj veliko, da se pozna, in dovolj konkretno, da ga ne moreš izigrati z izgovori. Zakaj: večina podjetnikov ne propade zaradi napačnega kanala, temveč zato, ker v kateremkoli kanalu nikoli ne naredi dovolj. Deset sporočil na teden ni test, je upanje.

What: a commitment to a fixed daily amount of work in one of the Core Four channels. The number 100 isn't magic; what matters is that it's big enough to show results and concrete enough that you can't excuse your way out of it. Why: most founders don't fail because of the wrong channel, but because they never do enough in any channel. Ten messages a week isn't a test, it's hope.

Kako: izberi en kanal in prevedi 100 v merljivo dejanje zanj. Topli ali hladni kontakti: 100 poslanih sporočil. Vsebina: 100 minut ustvarjanja in objavljanja. Oglasi: npr. 100 $ (ali evrov) na dan. Opravi to najprej, preden se lotiš vsega ostalega, in tako 100 dni.

How: pick one channel and translate 100 into a measurable action for it. Warm or cold outreach: 100 messages sent. Content: 100 minutes of creating and posting. Ads: e.g. $100 (or euros) a day. Do it first, before everything else, and keep it up for 100 days.

Volumen premaga spretnost (na začetku)Volume beats skill (early on)

Na začetku ne veš, katero sporočilo, naslov ali ciljna skupina deluje, in tega iz teorije ne moreš ugotoviti. Volumen ti da dve stvari: dovolj podatkov, da vidiš vzorce, in dovolj ponovitev, da postaneš boljši. Kdor pošlje 1000 povprečnih sporočil, se nauči več kot nekdo, ki je napisal 10 popolnih. Spretnost pride iz volumna, ne obratno.

At the start you don't know which message, headline or audience works, and you can't figure it out from theory. Volume gives you two things: enough data to see patterns, and enough repetitions to get better. Whoever sends 1,000 average messages learns more than someone who wrote 10 perfect ones. Skill comes from volume, not the other way round.

VečMore More

Kaj: ko kanal prinaša rezultate, najprej povečaš količino. Iz 100 sporočil na dan na 200, iz enega objavljenega videa na tri, dvigneš proračun oglasov. Zakaj: to je najcenejša in najzanesljivejša rast, ker ponavljaš nekaj, kar je že dokazano. Kako: povečaj postopno in opazuj, ali se rezultati na enoto ohranjajo. Ko se ne več ali ko si dosegel mejo časa ali trga, si pripravljen na naslednji korak.

What: once a channel produces results, you first increase volume. From 100 messages a day to 200, from one video to three, raise the ad budget. Why: it's the cheapest and most reliable growth because you're repeating something already proven. How: scale gradually and watch whether per-unit results hold. When they don't, or you hit a time or market limit, you're ready for the next step.

BoljeBetter Better

Kaj: izboljšuješ konverzijo na vsakem koraku: delež odgovorov, delež prijav na posvet, delež zaključenih prodaj. Zakaj: ko je volumen na meji, enak trud z boljšo konverzijo prinese več. Kako: spremeni eno stvar naenkrat (prva vrstica, ponudba, hook, ciljna skupina) in primerjaj s prejšnjo različico na dovolj velikem vzorcu. Ohrani zmagovalca, preizkusi naslednjo spremembo.

What: you improve conversion at each step: reply rate, call-booking rate, close rate. Why: once volume is maxed out, the same effort with better conversion yields more. How: change one thing at a time (first line, offer, hook, audience) and compare it with the previous version on a large enough sample. Keep the winner, test the next change.

NovoNew New

Kaj: šele ko si iz obstoječega kanala iztisnil »več« in »bolje«, dodaš nov kanal, novo platformo ali novo ciljno skupino. Zakaj zadnje: nov kanal pomeni začeti znova pri nič, z novim učenjem in novim obdobjem nizkih rezultatov. Kako: za nov kanal spet uporabi pravilo 100 in spet ponovi zaporedje Več → Bolje → Novo.

What: only once you've squeezed "more" and "better" out of the current channel do you add a new channel, platform or audience. Why last: a new channel means starting from zero, with new learning and a new period of poor results. How: apply the Rule of 100 again to the new channel, and run More → Better → New again.

MerjenjeKeeping metrics

Brez številk ne veš, ali potrebuješ »več« ali »bolje«. Vsak dan zapiši vsaj dejanja, odzive in angažirane leade, tedensko pa še posvete in prodaje. Dovolj je preprosta tabela.

Without numbers you can't tell whether you need "more" or "better". Every day, log at least actions, responses and engaged leads, and weekly add calls and sales. A simple table is enough.

Volume is steady, reply rate climbs: your messages are getting better. Once the response is stable, it's time for "more".

Examples are made up for illustration.

Svetovanje · AIConsulting · AI

AI svetovalec za proizvodna podjetjaAI consultant for manufacturers

PredBefore

LinkedIn objava ob torkih, newsletter enkrat na mesec, nekaj hladnih e-mailov, ko je čas, in poskusni Google oglasi. Vse po malem, nič merjeno. Po treh mesecih nejasno, kaj deluje.

A LinkedIn post on Tuesdays, a monthly newsletter, a few cold emails when there's time, and trial Google ads. A bit of everything, nothing measured. After three months, unclear what works.

PoAfter

Samo hladni kontakti: vsako jutro do 10. ure 100 personaliziranih sporočil vodjem proizvodnje, ponudba je brezplačen pregled ročnih procesov. Vse zabeleženo v tabeli. Po 6 tednih stabilno 5 posvetov na teden.

Cold outreach only: every morning before 10 a.m., 100 personalised messages to production managers, offering a free review of manual processes. Everything logged in a table. After 6 weeks, a steady 5 calls a week.

Nato: Več — doda asistenta za pripravo seznamov, 200 sporočil na dan. Bolje — preizkuša prvo vrstico in ugotovi, da omemba konkretnega ERP sistema podvoji odziv. Novo — po štirih mesecih začne objavljati primere strank na LinkedInu, kjer zdaj že ima zgodbe za vsebino.

Then: More — adds an assistant to build lists, 200 messages a day. Better — tests the first line and finds that naming the specific ERP system doubles replies. New — after four months starts posting client stories on LinkedIn, where he now has material for content.

Lokalno · zobozdravnikLocal · dentist

Zasebna zobna ordinacijaPrivate dental practice

Ordinacija se odloči za plačane oglase za beljenje zob: 100 € na dan, 100 dni. Prvi teden je cena za rezervacijo previsoka, a ne ustavijo. Merijo ceno na lead in delež prihodov na termin.

The practice commits to paid ads for teeth whitening: €100 a day for 100 days. The first week, cost per booking is too high, but they don't stop. They track cost per lead and show-up rate.

What matters: because they didn't stop after one bad week, they had enough data to find the winning ad. The "better" improvement wasn't in the ad at all but in show-ups, which is just as much a conversion gain.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Prezgodnje »novo«Jumping to "new" too soon

    Ko kanal ne prinese rezultatov v dveh tednih, gremo na drugega. Tako vedno ostanemo začetniki v vseh kanalih.

    When a channel doesn't deliver in two weeks, people switch to another. That way you remain a beginner in every channel.

  • Popolnost namesto volumnaPerfection instead of volume

    Ure za piljenje enega sporočila ali videa. Na začetku se več naučiš iz stotih poslanih kot iz enega popolnega.

    Hours polishing one message or video. Early on you learn more from a hundred sent than from one perfect one.

  • Brez beleženjaNot tracking

    Brez številk ne veš, ali je problem v količini ali v konverziji, in zato ne veš, ali potrebuješ »več« ali »bolje«.

    Without numbers you can't tell whether the problem is volume or conversion, so you don't know whether you need "more" or "better".

  • Oglaševanje kot ostanek dnevaAdvertising as leftover time

    Če 100 dejanj pride na vrsto šele po vsem ostalem, se ne zgodi. Najprej oglaševanje, potem ostalo.

    If the 100 actions only happen after everything else, they don't happen. Advertising first, everything else after.

  • Spreminjanje vsega naenkratChanging everything at once

    Pri koraku »bolje« spremeniš ponudbo, besedilo in ciljno skupino hkrati, zato ne veš, kaj je pomagalo.

    In the "better" step you change offer, copy and audience at once, so you don't know what helped.

Delovni listWorksheet

Pripravi svoj 100-dnevni načrt. Odgovori pisno in si nastavi tabelo za beleženje.

Prepare your 100-day plan. Answer in writing and set up a tracking table.

Kanal: Kateri en kanal iz Core Four izberem za naslednjih 100 dni in zakaj?

Channel: Which one Core Four channel do I pick for the next 100 days, and why?

Moje »100«: Kaj točno je moje dnevno dejanje in kako ga štejem (sporočila, minute, evri)?

My "100": What exactly is my daily action, and how do I count it (messages, minutes, euros)?

Urnik: Kdaj v dnevu to naredim, preden se lotim česarkoli drugega? Kaj odstranim, da najdem čas?

Schedule: When in the day do I do it, before anything else? What do I drop to make the time?

Meritve: Katere 3–5 številk beležim vsak dan ali teden? Kje jih bom zapisoval?

Metrics: Which 3–5 numbers do I log daily or weekly? Where will I record them?

Več / Bolje: Po kakšnem rezultatu bom vedel, da je čas za »več«? Katero eno stvar bom najprej preizkusil pri »bolje«?

More / Better: What result will tell me it's time for "more"? What one thing will I test first under "better"?
