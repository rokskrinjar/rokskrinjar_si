The ConstraintOzko grlo

Selling & Scaling · Constraint (bottleneck)

At any moment a business has one main constraint that limits its growth. Work on anything else has little effect until that one is fixed, and then the constraint moves.Podjetje ima v vsakem trenutku eno glavno omejitev, ki zavira rast. Delo na čemer koli drugem ima malo učinka, dokler je ne odpraviš, nato pa se omejitev premakne.

The gist

A business works like a pipe with several sections: leads come in, some become sales, sales become delivered work, and delivered clients stay or leave. Only as much flows through the whole pipe as the narrowest section lets through. Widening any other section changes nothing, because the flow still waits at the same point.

One constraint at a time → fix it → the constraint moves → find the next one

The idea comes from Eliyahu Goldratt's Theory of Constraints, set out in his book The Goal. Alex uses it as a simple operating rule for founders: find the one thing that limits growth right now, put your effort there, and say no to good ideas elsewhere until the constraint has moved.

How it works

There is always one main constraint

A business always has many problems, but usually one of them matters most: the one whose improvement would raise the result of the whole business the most. The common places are:

  • Leads. Too few of the right people hear about you.
  • Sales conversion. People come, but they don't buy.
  • Delivery or capacity. You sell more than you can fulfil well.
  • Retention. Clients leave too soon (churn).
  • Sometimes cash or people. Growth stalls on cash flow, or on one key person who is missing or overloaded.

Work elsewhere is low leverage

If delivery is the constraint, another ad campaign brings more disappointed clients and longer queues. If retention is the constraint, a new website fills a leaking bucket. This work looks productive but doesn't move the result, and it can make it worse. Hence the rule: say no to anything that isn't the constraint, good ideas included.

Find it with a thought experiment

The quickest test is to ask: "If my leads doubled tomorrow, what would break first?"

  • "Nothing, we'd handle them happily" means leads are the constraint.
  • "We wouldn't have time for all the calls" or "most wouldn't buy" means sales.
  • "We couldn't deliver on time" means delivery or capacity.
  • "We'd sign them, but they'd be gone in a few months" means retention.
  • "We couldn't fund the extra work" or "it all goes through me" means cash or people.

Confirm it with funnel numbers

Write down the numbers for each step of the last quarter: leads, calls held, new clients, onboarded clients, clients still active after a set period. Calculate the conversion at each step and compare it with a reasonable expectation for your market. The step that falls furthest short, or where work and people pile up in a queue, is the candidate. The thought experiment gives you a hypothesis. The numbers confirm or reject it.

Fix it, then it moves

When you remove a constraint, it doesn't disappear. It moves. More leads make sales the new bottleneck. Better sales overload delivery. Better delivery reveals that clients don't stay. That is progress, not failure. Finding the constraint is a standing habit, for example a monthly review, rather than a one-off project.

Goldratt's five focusing steps, simplified

  1. Identify the constraint.
  2. Exploit it: get the most out of it with what you already have, so it never sits idle.
  3. Subordinate everything else to it.
  4. Elevate it with new people, systems or money.
  5. Repeat once it has moved.

Examples

Examples are invented for illustration.

One quarter's funnel (B2B analytics SaaS)

A small company sells an analytics platform to manufacturers. The founder wants to spend more on ads, but first looks at the numbers.

Step Count Conversion Expected
Leads 240
Calls held 150 63 % 60 %
New clients 30 20 % 20 %
Onboarded within 30 days 27 90 % 85 %
Active after 6 months 12 44 % 80 %

Every step meets expectations except retention. More ads would bring more clients, and the company would lose more than half of them within six months. The constraint is retention. The first move is a monthly usage review with each client and an alert when someone hasn't opened the platform for two weeks. Only once retention rises does it pay to scale ads.

Full calendar, flat revenue (car repair shop)

Before. The owner thinks he needs more customers and pays for social ads. Calls go up, but the wait for an appointment grows to three weeks and some regulars go elsewhere.

After. The "if leads doubled" test shows it at once: the workshop would break. The constraint is one lift and one diagnostic technician. The owner pauses the ads, adds a second shift on Thursdays and Fridays, and pre-orders parts so cars don't sit on the lift waiting.

He had been improving a step that wasn't the constraint and made the customer experience worse. Once the workshop can handle more, leads become the constraint again, and that is when ads make sense.

Diagnostic table (AI consultancy)

A general aid for locating the constraint. Compare the symptoms with your business.

Constraint Symptoms Number to watch First move
Leads Empty calendar, sellers waiting New qualified leads per week More volume on one channel
Sales Many calls, few signatures Close rate Call reviews, a script, a stronger offer
Delivery Delays, queues, overloaded team Time to first result Standardise, automate, hire
Retention Clients leave after a few months Monthly churn Fast first win, regular check-ins
Cash or people Growth stalls on cash or one person Months of runway, owner hours in delivery Upfront payments, delegation

An AI consultancy has enough leads and a good close rate, but the founder runs every project personally. The symptoms point to delivery and people. Instead of new campaigns, the founder writes a standard process for the first project and brings in a project lead.

Common mistakes

  • Working on everything at once. Ten small improvements move little, because the constraint gets only a tenth of the attention.
  • Defaulting to "we need more clients". Leads are the most common answer and often the wrong one. More clients into leaky retention or overloaded delivery does harm.
  • Choosing by gut instead of numbers. Founders like working on what they enjoy. Without funnel numbers, picking the constraint often means picking a hobby.
  • Forgetting that it moves. After one constraint is solved, some keep doing the same work. Re-run the test after every improvement.

How Alex would apply it

  • He asks what the constraint is before giving any advice. Most business questions have a different right answer depending on where the bottleneck sits.
  • He runs the doubling test first, then checks it against the funnel numbers.
  • He puts the owner's time on the constraint and cuts or pauses projects that don't touch it, even promising ones.
  • He is wary of adding leads to a broken funnel. Fixing conversion, delivery or retention first makes every future lead worth more.
  • He exploits before he elevates: squeeze more out of the current team, process and offer before hiring or spending.
  • He expects the constraint to move and reviews it regularly, treating a new bottleneck as a sign of progress.
  • Questions he would ask: If leads doubled tomorrow, what breaks first? What are your numbers at each step? Where do people or work queue up? How much of delivery still depends on you? What would you stop doing this month if you took the answer seriously?

Worksheet

Take the last three months of your business and answer in writing.

  1. Test: If your leads doubled tomorrow, what would break first?
  2. Funnel: Write down the numbers for each step (leads, calls, deals, onboarding, retention). Where is the biggest gap against what you'd expect?
  3. Queues: Where do work or clients pile up and wait?
  4. Focus: Which three projects that don't touch the constraint will you pause this month?
  5. Action: What one action widens the constraint within 30 days, and how will you measure it?
  6. Next: Where is the constraint likely to move once you fix it?

Bistvo

Podjetje deluje kot cev z več odseki: povpraševanja (leads) pritečejo noter, del jih postane prodaja, prodaja postane opravljeno delo, stranke pa nato ostanejo ali odidejo. Skozi celo cev steče le toliko, kolikor prepusti najožji odsek. Če razširiš katerikoli drug odsek, se ne spremeni nič, ker tok še vedno čaka na istem mestu.

Ena omejitev naenkrat → odpravi jo → omejitev se premakne → poišči naslednjo

Ideja izhaja iz teorije omejitev (Theory of Constraints) Eliyahuja Goldratta, ki jo je predstavil v knjigi The Goal (Cilj). Alex jo uporablja kot preprosto delovno pravilo za ustanovitelje: poišči eno stvar, ki ta trenutek omejuje rast, tja usmeri svoj trud in reci ne dobrim idejam drugje, dokler se omejitev ne premakne.

Kako deluje

Vedno obstaja ena glavna omejitev

Podjetje ima vedno veliko težav, a navadno je ena med njimi najpomembnejša: tista, katere izboljšava bi najbolj dvignila rezultat celega podjetja. Običajna mesta so:

  • Povpraševanja (leads). Premalo pravih ljudi izve zate.
  • Prodajna konverzija. Ljudje pridejo, a ne kupijo.
  • Izvedba ali zmogljivost. Prodaš več, kot lahko dobro izvedeš.
  • Zadržanje (retention). Stranke odidejo prezgodaj (churn).
  • Včasih denar ali ljudje. Rast se ustavi zaradi denarnega toka ali enega ključnega človeka, ki manjka ali je preobremenjen.

Delo drugje ima nizek vzvod

Če je omejitev izvedba, nova oglaševalska kampanja prinese le več razočaranih strank in daljše čakalne vrste. Če je omejitev zadržanje, nova spletna stran polni vedro, ki pušča. Takšno delo je videti produktivno, a ne premakne rezultata in ga lahko celo poslabša. Od tod pravilo: reci ne vsemu, kar ni ozko grlo, tudi dobrim idejam.

Najdi ga z miselnim poskusom

Najhitrejši test je vprašanje: »Če bi se mi jutri povpraševanja podvojila, kaj bi se prvo zlomilo?«

  • »Nič, z veseljem bi jih obdelali« pomeni, da so omejitev povpraševanja.
  • »Ne bi imeli časa za vse klice« ali »večina ne bi kupila« pomeni prodajo.
  • »Ne bi mogli izvesti pravočasno« pomeni izvedbo oziroma zmogljivost.
  • »Podpisali bi jih, a čez nekaj mesecev bi jih ne bilo več« pomeni zadržanje.
  • »Dodatnega dela ne bi mogli financirati« ali »vse gre skozi mene« pomeni denar ali ljudi.

Potrdi ga s številkami lijaka

Zapiši števila za vsak korak zadnjega četrtletja: povpraševanja, opravljeni klici, nove stranke, uvedene stranke in stranke, ki so po določenem času še aktivne. Za vsak korak izračunaj pretvorbo in jo primerjaj z razumnim pričakovanjem za tvoj trg. Korak, ki najbolj zaostaja, ali tisti, kjer se delo in ljudje nabirajo v čakalni vrsti, je kandidat. Miselni poskus ti da hipotezo. Številke jo potrdijo ali ovržejo.

Odpravi ga, nato se premakne

Ko omejitev odpraviš, ne izgine. Premakne se. Več povpraševanj naredi prodajo za novo ozko grlo. Boljša prodaja preobremeni izvedbo. Boljša izvedba razkrije, da stranke ne ostanejo. To je napredek, ne neuspeh. Iskanje omejitve je stalna navada, na primer mesečni pregled, in ne enkratni projekt.

Goldrattovih pet korakov, poenostavljeno

  1. Poišči (identify) omejitev.
  2. Izkoristi (exploit) jo: z obstoječimi viri iz nje iztisni največ, da nikoli ne stoji.
  3. Podredi (subordinate) ji vse ostalo.
  4. Razširi (elevate) jo z novimi ljudmi, sistemi ali denarjem.
  5. Ponovi (repeat), ko se premakne.

Primeri

Primeri so izmišljeni in služijo ponazoritvi.

Lijak v enem četrtletju (B2B analitični SaaS)

Majhno podjetje prodaja analitično platformo proizvodnim podjetjem. Ustanovitelj bi rad več porabil za oglase, a najprej pogleda številke.

Korak Število Pretvorba Pričakovano
Povpraševanja 240
Opravljeni klici 150 63 % 60 %
Nove stranke 30 20 % 20 %
Uvedene v 30 dneh 27 90 % 85 %
Aktivne po 6 mesecih 12 44 % 80 %

Vsi koraki dosegajo pričakovanja razen zadržanja. Več oglasov bi prineslo več strank, podjetje pa bi jih v pol leta izgubilo več kot polovico. Omejitev je zadržanje. Prvi ukrep je mesečni pregled uporabe z vsako stranko in opozorilo, ko nekdo dva tedna ne odpre platforme. Šele ko zadržanje zraste, se splača povečati oglaševanje.

Poln koledar, ploski prihodki (avtoservis)

Pred. Lastnik misli, da potrebuje več strank, in plača oglase na družbenih omrežjih. Klicev je več, a čakalna doba za termin naraste na tri tedne in nekateri stalni kupci odidejo drugam.

Po. Test »če bi se povpraševanja podvojila« takoj pokaže: zlomila bi se delavnica. Omejitev sta en dvižni most in en diagnostik. Lastnik ustavi oglase, ob četrtkih in petkih uvede drugo izmeno in dele naroča vnaprej, da avto ne stoji na mostu in čaka.

Izboljševal je korak, ki ni bil omejitev, in s tem poslabšal izkušnjo strank. Ko delavnica zmore več, postanejo omejitev spet povpraševanja, in takrat imajo oglasi smisel.

Diagnostična tabela (AI svetovalnica)

Splošen pripomoček za iskanje omejitve. Simptome primerjaj s svojim podjetjem.

Omejitev Simptomi Številka za spremljanje Prvi ukrep
Povpraševanja Prazen koledar, prodajalci čakajo Nova kvalificirana povpraševanja na teden Več obsega na enem kanalu
Prodaja Veliko klicev, malo podpisov Delež zaključenih poslov (close rate) Pregledi klicev, skript, močnejša ponudba
Izvedba Zamude, čakalne vrste, preobremenjena ekipa Čas do prvega rezultata Standardiziraj, avtomatiziraj, zaposli
Zadržanje Stranke odidejo po nekaj mesecih Mesečni churn Hitra prva zmaga, redni pregledi
Denar ali ljudje Rast se ustavi zaradi denarja ali enega človeka Meseci rezerve, ure lastnika v izvedbi Predplačila, delegiranje

AI svetovalnica ima dovolj povpraševanj in dober delež zaključenih poslov, a vsak projekt vodi ustanovitelj osebno. Simptomi kažejo na izvedbo in ljudi. Namesto novih kampanj ustanovitelj zapiše standardni postopek za prvi projekt in zaposli vodjo projektov.

Pogoste napake

  • Delo na vsem hkrati. Deset majhnih izboljšav premakne malo, ker ozko grlo dobi le desetino pozornosti.
  • Samodejno »potrebujemo več strank«. Povpraševanja so najpogostejši odgovor in pogosto napačen. Več strank v podjetje z luknjastim zadržanjem ali preobremenjeno izvedbo škodi.
  • Izbira po občutku namesto po številkah. Ustanovitelji radi delajo na tem, kar jih veseli. Brez številk lijaka je izbira omejitve pogosto izbira hobija.
  • Pozaba, da se omejitev premakne. Ko je ena omejitev rešena, nekateri nadaljujejo z istim delom. Po vsaki izboljšavi ponovi test.

Kako bi to uporabil Alex

  • Preden svetuje, vpraša, kaj je omejitev. Pravilen odgovor na večino poslovnih vprašanj je odvisen od tega, kje je ozko grlo.
  • Najprej naredi test podvojitve, nato ga preveri s številkami lijaka.
  • Lastnikov čas usmeri v omejitev, projekte, ki je ne zadevajo, pa odreže ali ustavi, tudi obetavne.
  • Previden je pri dodajanju povpraševanj v pokvarjen lijak. Ko najprej popraviš konverzijo, izvedbo ali zadržanje, je vsako prihodnje povpraševanje vredno več.
  • Najprej izkoristi, šele nato razširi: iz obstoječe ekipe, procesa in ponudbe iztisne več, preden zaposluje ali troši.
  • Pričakuje, da se omejitev premakne, jo redno pregleduje in novo ozko grlo razume kot znak napredka.
  • Vprašanja, ki bi jih postavil: Če bi se povpraševanja jutri podvojila, kaj bi se prvo zlomilo? Kakšne so tvoje številke na vsakem koraku? Kje se ljudje ali delo nabirajo v čakalni vrsti? Koliko izvedbe je še odvisne od tebe? Kaj bi ta mesec nehal delati, če bi odgovor vzel resno?

Delovni list

Vzemi zadnje tri mesece svojega podjetja in odgovori pisno.

  1. Test: Če bi se ti jutri povpraševanja podvojila, kaj bi se prvo zlomilo?
  2. Lijak: Zapiši števila za vsak korak (povpraševanja, klici, posli, uvedba, zadržanje). Kje je največje odstopanje od pričakovanega?
  3. Čakalne vrste: Kje se delo ali stranke nabirajo in čakajo?
  4. Fokus: Katere tri projekte, ki ne zadevajo ozkega grla, boš ta mesec ustavil?
  5. Ukrep: Kateri en ukrep v 30 dneh razširi omejitev in kako ga boš izmeril?
  6. Naslednja: Kam se bo omejitev verjetno premaknila, ko jo odpraviš?
