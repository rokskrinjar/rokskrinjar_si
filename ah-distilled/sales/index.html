This collection in one page

This is not a book. Alex has not published a book on these topics, so this collection gathers ideas he teaches repeatedly in his talks, trainings and podcast episodes. They fill the gap the three books leave: what happens once a good offer and a steady flow of leads are in place.

The first idea is the CLOSER framework for sales conversations. A sales call works best as a diagnosis: the buyer explains why they came, confirms the problem, admits what hasn't worked, and only then hears about the destination your offer leads to. Concerns are handled by returning to the buyer's own words, and the decision is reinforced right after the purchase.

The second idea is the constraint, borrowed from Eliyahu Goldratt's Theory of Constraints. At any moment one part of the business limits growth: leads, sales, delivery, retention, and sometimes cash or people. Effort spent anywhere else has little effect. Fix the constraint, and it moves to the next place.

The flow:

  1. Make the offer and get the leads (the three books).
  2. Close the conversations with CLOSER, so leads become clients.
  3. Find the constraint with the doubling test and your funnel numbers.
  4. Fix only that, saying no to everything else for now.
  5. Repeat when the constraint moves.

How Alex would use these ideas

  • He checks the constraint before any other advice, because the right next step depends on where growth is stuck.
  • When sales is the constraint, he reviews recorded calls against CLOSER and trains the weakest step.
  • He fixes conversion, delivery or retention before buying more leads, so each new lead is worth more.
  • He keeps the owner's time on the constraint and pauses projects that don't touch it.
  • He re-runs the diagnosis regularly and treats a moved constraint as progress.

Zbirka na eni strani

To ni knjiga. Alex o teh temah ni izdal knjige, zato ta zbirka združuje ideje, ki jih vedno znova uči v predavanjih, treningih in epizodah podcasta. Zapolnijo vrzel, ki jo pustijo tri knjige: kaj se zgodi, ko imaš dobro ponudbo in stalen pritok povpraševanj.

Prva ideja je okvir CLOSER za prodajne pogovore. Prodajni klic najbolje deluje kot diagnoza: kupec pojasni, zakaj je prišel, potrdi problem, prizna, kaj ni delovalo, in šele nato sliši o cilju, do katerega ga pelje tvoja ponudba. Pomisleke rešuješ tako, da se vračaš k kupčevim lastnim besedam, odločitev pa utrdiš takoj po nakupu.

Druga ideja je ozko grlo (constraint), izposojeno iz teorije omejitev (Theory of Constraints) Eliyahuja Goldratta. V vsakem trenutku rast omejuje en del podjetja: povpraševanja, prodaja, izvedba, zadržanje, včasih pa denar ali ljudje. Trud kjer koli drugje ima malo učinka. Ko omejitev odpraviš, se premakne na naslednje mesto.

Tok:

  1. Naredi ponudbo in pridobi povpraševanja (tri knjige).
  2. Zaključi pogovore z okvirom CLOSER, da povpraševanja postanejo stranke.
  3. Poišči omejitev s testom podvojitve in s številkami lijaka.
  4. Odpravi samo njo in za zdaj reci ne vsemu drugemu.
  5. Ponovi, ko se omejitev premakne.

Kako bi knjigo uporabil Alex

  • Pred vsakim drugim nasvetom preveri omejitev, ker je pravi naslednji korak odvisen od tega, kje se je rast zataknila.
  • Ko je omejitev prodaja, posnete klice pregleda po korakih CLOSER in trenira najšibkejši korak.
  • Konverzijo, izvedbo ali zadržanje popravi, preden kupi več povpraševanj, da je vsako novo povpraševanje vredno več.
  • Lastnikov čas drži na omejitvi in ustavi projekte, ki je ne zadevajo.
  • Diagnozo redno ponavlja in premaknjeno omejitev razume kot napredek.
