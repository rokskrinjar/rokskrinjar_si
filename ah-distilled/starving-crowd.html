Izbira trgaStarving Crowd

$100M Offers · Starving Crowd (market selection)

Preden izboljšuješ ponudbo, preveri, ali prodajaš pravim ljudem. Dober trg odpusti povprečno ponudbo; slab trg pokoplje odlično.

Before you improve your offer, check that you're selling to the right people. A good market forgives an average offer; a bad market buries a great one.

BistvoThe gist

TrgMarket > PonudbaOffer > PrepričevanjePersuasion

Ideja starving crowd je preprosta: najdi lačno množico. Prodaja hrane lačnim ne zahteva vrhunske prodajne veščine. Vrstni red pomembnosti je trg, nato ponudba, šele nato prepričevanje (persuasion skills). Večina ljudi vlaga obratno: v prodajne skripte in oglase, medtem ko prodajajo ljudem, ki niso ne lačni ne plačilno sposobni.

The starving crowd idea is simple: find a hungry crowd. Selling food to the hungry doesn't take elite sales skill. The order of importance is market, then offer, and only then persuasion. Most people invest the other way round: in sales scripts and ads, while selling to people who are neither hungry nor able to pay.

Štirje kriterijiThe four criteria

1. Velika bolečina1. Massive pain

Trg mora problem čutiti in ga nujno želeti rešiti. To je massive pain: ne »bilo bi koristno imeti boljša poročila«, ampak »vsak mesec izgubljamo naročila, ker ne vemo, kje je zaloga«. Bolečina ni le želja po nečem lepšem, je cena, ki jo kupec plačuje že danes, ker problema ni rešil.

The market must feel the problem and urgently want it solved. That's massive pain: not "better reports would be useful", but "we lose orders every month because we don't know where the stock is". Pain isn't a wish for something nicer, it's a cost the buyer is already paying today because the problem is unsolved.

Test: ali stranka problem že rešuje sama, slabo in drago (Excel, nadure, zunanji izvajalci)? Če da, je bolečina resnična.

Test: is the client already solving the problem themselves, badly and expensively (Excel, overtime, contractors)? If so, the pain is real.

2. Kupna moč2. Purchasing power

Lahko imajo ogromno bolečino in nič denarja. Študentje, ki bi radi zagnali podjetje, so lačni, a ne morejo plačati. Purchasing power pomeni, da imajo proračun in da oseba, s katero govoriš, lahko odobri nakup. V B2B je to ključno: vodja oddelka z bolečino, a brez pooblastila, ni kupec.

They can have enormous pain and no money. Students who want to start a business are hungry but can't pay. Purchasing power means they have budget and the person you're talking to can approve the purchase. In B2B this is crucial: a department head with pain but no authority isn't a buyer.

3. Lahko dosegljivi3. Easy to target

Moraš vedeti, kje so in kako jih doseči: združenja, sejmi, skupine na LinkedInu, specifični portali, seznami podjetij po dejavnosti (SKD/NACE). Easy to target pomeni, da jih lahko poimensko najdeš, ne da upaš, da bodo oni našli tebe. »Vsa mala in srednja podjetja« niso dosegljiva; »proizvajalci plastike v Sloveniji z 50+ zaposlenimi« so.

You need to know where they are and how to reach them: associations, trade fairs, LinkedIn groups, niche portals, company lists by industry code (NACE). Easy to target means you can find them by name, not hope they find you. "All SMEs" isn't targetable; "plastics manufacturers in Slovenia with 50+ employees" is.

4. Rastoč trg4. Growing market

Na rastočem trgu raste število kupcev in njihova pripravljenost plačati, ne glede na tvojo spretnost. Na upadajočem se boriš za vedno manjši kos. Growing market je kot plavanje s tokom. Danes so to na primer podjetja, ki morajo uvajati AI, poročati o trajnosti ali se prilagajati novim regulativam.

In a growing market the number of buyers and their willingness to pay rise regardless of your skill. In a shrinking one you fight for an ever smaller slice. A growing market is like swimming with the current. Today that includes, for example, companies that have to adopt AI, report on sustainability or adapt to new regulation.

Zaveza nišiCommit to a niche

Ožja niša izgleda kot manjši trg, a v praksi poveča vrednost. Ponudba »za vse« je splošna, zato šibka. Ponudba »za proizvodna podjetja, ki prehajajo na nov ERP« zveni, kot da je narejena prav zame, zato kupec verjame, da bo delovalo (višja zaznana verjetnost iz enačbe vrednosti), in je pripravljen plačati več. Isti produkt, ožje pozicioniran, prenese bistveno višjo ceno.

A narrower niche looks like a smaller market, but in practice it raises value. An offer "for everyone" is generic, so it's weak. An offer "for manufacturers moving to a new ERP" sounds made exactly for me, so the buyer believes it will work (higher perceived likelihood from the value equation) and is willing to pay more. The same product, positioned more narrowly, supports a much higher price.

Druga polovica je zaveza: ne menjaj niše vsak mesec. Šele ko vztrajaš, se naučiš jezika trga, zbereš dokaze in postaneš znan.

The other half is commitment: don't switch niches every month. Only by sticking with it do you learn the market's language, collect proof and become known.

Trije osnovni trgiThe three core markets

Kot izhodišče se večina potreb, za katere ljudje plačajo, uvrsti v tri velike trge: health (zdravje, videz, energija), wealth (denar, kariera, posel) in relationships (partnerstvo, družina, ugled). Ti trgi ne bodo nikoli izginili. Začneš pri enem in se spuščaš navzdol: wealth → B2B storitve → poslovna analitika → analitika za proizvodnjo → OEE in izmet za srednje velike proizvajalce.

As a starting point, most needs people pay for fall into three big markets: health (wellbeing, looks, energy), wealth (money, career, business) and relationships (partnership, family, reputation). These markets will never disappear. You start in one and drill down: wealth → B2B services → business analytics → manufacturing analytics → OEE and scrap for mid-sized manufacturers.

The third is narrower but has sharp pain (order errors and delays), budget and clear places to find them (chamber of commerce, trade fairs, business registers). A consultant who commits to the third can charge more because they speak exactly this buyer's language.

Lokalna storitev · zobozdravnikLocal business · dentist

Od »vsi z zobmi« do jasne nišeFrom "everyone with teeth" to a clear niche

PredBefore

Zasebna ordinacija oglašuje »splošno zobozdravstvo za vso družino« in tekmuje s koncesijskimi ambulantami po ceni.

A private practice advertises "general dentistry for the whole family" and competes on price with publicly funded clinics.

PoAfter

»Implantati za ljudi 50+, ki se sramujejo nasmeha.« Velika bolečina (sram, težave pri žvečenju), kupna moč (prihranki), dosegljivost (lokalni mediji, društva upokojencev), trg raste s staranjem prebivalstva.

"Implants for people 50+ who are ashamed of their smile." Massive pain (shame, trouble chewing), purchasing power (savings), reachable (local media, retiree associations), and the market grows as the population ages.

Ordinacija še vedno sprejme vse paciente, a vse sporočanje, dokaze in ponudbo gradi okoli ene skupine. Primerjava s splošnimi ambulantami odpade, cena na pacienta pa naraste.

The practice still accepts all patients, but builds all messaging, proof and offers around one group. Comparison with general clinics falls away, and revenue per patient rises.

SaaS · poročanjeSaaS · reporting

Niša kot dvig ceneNiche as a price lever

Splošno orodje za poročila stane 30 € na uporabnika in se primerja z Power BI. Ista platforma, zapakirana kot »mesečno poročilo za lastnike franšiz fitnes centrov« (vnaprej povezani viri, KPI-ji panoge, primerjava z drugimi lokacijami), se prodaja po 300 € na lokacijo. Trg je manjši, a lačnejši in brez neposredne primerjave.

A generic reporting tool costs €30 per user and gets compared with Power BI. The same platform packaged as "monthly reporting for gym franchise owners" (pre-connected sources, industry KPIs, benchmarking across locations) sells for €300 per location. The market is smaller, but hungrier and without direct comparison.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Strah pred ožanjemFear of narrowing down

    Občutek, da ožja niša pomeni manj strank. V resnici pomeni boljše stranke, višjo ceno in lažjo prodajo.

    The feeling that a narrower niche means fewer clients. In reality it means better clients, higher prices and easier sales.

  • Bolečina brez denarjaPain without money

    Najlepše povratne informacije pogosto dajo tisti, ki ne morejo plačati. Navdušenje ni kupna moč.

    The warmest feedback often comes from people who can't pay. Enthusiasm isn't purchasing power.

  • Trg, ki ga ne znaš najtiA market you can't find

    Če ne moreš sestaviti seznama 100 konkretnih podjetij ali ljudi, trg ni dovolj definiran.

    If you can't put together a list of 100 specific companies or people, the market isn't defined enough.

  • Menjavanje niše vsak mesecSwitching niche every month

    Rezultati pridejo z vztrajnostjo. Vsaka menjava ponastavi dokaze, ugled in znanje o trgu.

    Results come from persistence. Every switch resets your proof, reputation and market knowledge.

  • Popravljanje prodaje namesto trgaFixing sales instead of the market

    Če nič ne deluje, najprej preveri trg, šele nato ponudbo in skripte.

    If nothing works, check the market first, then the offer, and only then scripts.

Write down 2–3 candidate markets and score each 1–5 on the four criteria.

Bolečina: Kaj ta trg danes stane, ker problem ni rešen? Kako ga zdaj rešujejo sami?

Pain: What does the unsolved problem cost this market today? How are they solving it themselves now?

Kupna moč: Kdo odobri nakup in kakšen proračun ima za tak problem?

Money: Who approves the purchase and what budget do they have for this kind of problem?

Dosegljivost: Kje lahko najdeš prvih 100 imen? Navedi tri konkretne vire.

Reach: Where can you find the first 100 names? Name three concrete sources.

Rast: Zakaj bo ta trg čez tri leta večji ali bolj potreben tvoje rešitve?

Growth: Why will this market be bigger, or need your solution more, in three years?

Niša: Pot od zdravja, bogastva ali odnosov do tvoje niše v 4–5 korakih. Kje se ustaviš?

Niche: Path from health, wealth or relationships down to your niche in 4–5 steps. Where do you stop?
