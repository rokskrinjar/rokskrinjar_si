Ekonomika strankeCustomer Economics

$100M Money Models / $100M Leads · LTGP:CAC, payback

Tri številke, ki povedo, ali se ti rast splača in kako hitro jo lahko poganjaš: koliko stane stranka, koliko dobička prinese v življenjski dobi in kako hitro se strošek povrne.

Three numbers that tell you whether growth pays and how fast you can push it: what a customer costs, how much profit they bring over their lifetime, and how fast the cost comes back.

Podjetje je zdravo, ko stranka v svoji življenjski dobi prinese vsaj trikrat več bruto dobička, kot je stala njena pridobitev. A razmerje samo ni dovolj: če se strošek povrne šele v letu dni, ti zmanjka gotovine, preden rast sploh zaživi. Zato je drugi ključni podatek doba povrnitve. Ko se CAC povrne v 30 dneh, lahko rast financiraš z denarjem strank, ne s krediti.

A business is healthy when a customer brings at least three times more gross profit over their lifetime than it cost to acquire them. But the ratio alone isn't enough: if cost pays back only after a year, you run out of cash before growth takes off. So the second key number is the payback period. When CAC pays back within 30 days, you fund growth with customers' money, not loans.

Kako izračunaš vsako številkoHow to compute each number

1. CAC: strošek pridobitve stranke1. CAC: Customer Acquisition Cost

Seštej vse, kar si v obdobju porabil za pridobivanje strank: oglase, orodja, plače ali čas prodajalcev, provizije partnerjev, dogodke. Deli s številom novih strank v istem obdobju. Najpogostejša napaka je, da štejemo samo oglase. Če prodajaš sam, oceni vrednost svojega časa, sicer bo CAC videti nerealno nizek in se bo ob prvem zaposlenem prodajalcu nenadoma podvojil.

Add up everything spent in a period on getting customers: ads, tools, salaries or sales time, partner commissions, events. Divide by the number of new customers in the same period. The most common error is counting ads only. If you sell yourself, put a value on your time, or CAC will look unrealistically low and suddenly double when you hire your first salesperson.

2. Bruto dobiček, ne prihodek2. Gross profit, not revenue

Bruto dobiček je denar, ki ostane po neposrednih stroških izvedbe: delo ljudi, ki storitev opravijo, material, licence, gostovanje, podizvajalci. Le ta denar lahko vložiš nazaj v pridobivanje strank. Dve podjetji z enakim prihodkom imata lahko povsem različno ekonomiko: 1.000 € prihodka pri 80 % marži je 800 € za rast, pri 20 % marži le 200 €. Zato Hormozi vse te kazalnike gradi na bruto dobičku (gross profit).

Gross profit is what's left after the direct costs of delivery: the people doing the work, materials, licences, hosting, subcontractors. Only this money can be reinvested in acquiring customers. Two businesses with the same revenue can have very different economics: €1,000 of revenue at 80% margin is €800 for growth, at 20% margin only €200. That's why Hormozi builds these metrics on gross profit.

3. LTGP: življenjski bruto dobiček3. LTGP: Lifetime Gross Profit

Koliko bruto dobička povprečna stranka ustvari, dokler ostane. Za naročnine je praktična ocena: povprečna življenjska doba ≈ 1 ÷ mesečni delež odhodov (churn). Pri 5 % odhodih na mesec stranka v povprečju ostane 20 mesecev. LTGP = mesečni bruto dobiček × 20, plus enkratni nakupi (nastavitev, nadgradnje). Pri projektnem delu namesto tega seštej povprečen bruto dobiček vseh projektov, ki jih stranka naroči.

How much gross profit the average customer generates while they stay. For subscriptions a practical estimate: average lifetime ≈ 1 ÷ monthly churn rate. At 5% monthly churn a customer stays 20 months on average. LTGP = monthly gross profit × 20, plus one-off purchases (setup, upsells). For project work, instead add up the average gross profit of all projects a client orders.

4. Razmerje LTGP:CAC4. The LTGP:CAC ratio

LTGP deli s CAC. Pod 1:1 vsaka nova stranka izgublja denar. Okoli 3:1 je pogosto uporabljeno pravilo za zdravo podjetje, ker pokrije pridobitev in še ostane za režijo in dobiček. Precej nad tem je lahko znak, da premalo vlagaš v rast. Številka je ocena, ne resnica: posebej pri mladih podjetjih je življenjska doba strank še domneva, zato računaj previdno.

Divide LTGP by CAC. Below 1:1 each new customer loses money. Around 3:1 is a widely used rule of thumb for a healthy business, because it covers acquisition and leaves room for overhead and profit. Far above that can be a sign you're under-investing in growth. The number is an estimate, not truth: especially in young businesses customer lifetime is still an assumption, so calculate conservatively.

5. Doba povrnitve5. Payback period

CAC deli z mesečnim bruto dobičkom, upoštevaj pa tudi enkratna plačila na začetku. Če se povrne v 30 dneh, imaš na koncu meseca že nazaj denar za naslednjo stranko; rast je omejena le z zmogljivostjo, ne z gotovino. Hitro dobo povrnitve dosežeš s plačili vnaprej, nastavitvenimi pristojbinami, letnimi predplačili in nadgradnjami v prvem mesecu, kar je točno naloga money modela.

Divide CAC by monthly gross profit, and include any one-off payments at the start. If it pays back within 30 days, by month-end you already have the cash back for the next customer; growth is limited only by capacity, not cash. You get fast payback through upfront payments, setup fees, annual prepayment and first-month upsells, which is exactly the job of a money model.

Kako izboljšaš vsak vzvodHow to improve each lever

Nižji CAC: boljša ponudba, ki jo več ljudi sprejme; boljša konverzija na vsakem koraku (več odgovorov, več sestankov, več zaprtih poslov); več priporočil in partnerjev, ki prinašajo leade z izposojenim zaupanjem. Višji bruto dobiček: višja cena (gre skoraj v celoti v dobiček), nižji stroški izvedbe s standardizacijo in avtomatizacijo. Daljša življenjska doba: hiter prvi rezultat, dober začetek sodelovanja (onboarding), ponavljajoča se vrednost, nadgradnje. Hitrejša povrnitev: denar na začetku namesto na koncu.

Lower CAC: a better offer more people accept; better conversion at each step (more replies, more meetings, more closes); more referrals and partners bringing leads with borrowed trust. Higher gross profit: higher price (falls almost entirely to profit), lower delivery cost through standardisation and automation. Longer lifetime: a fast first result, good onboarding, recurring value, upsells. Faster payback: cash at the start instead of the end.

B2B · SaaS / analitikaB2B · SaaS / analytics

Naročnina na upravljano BI rešitevManaged BI subscription

V četrtletju porabijo 6.000 € za oglase in dogodke ter 4.000 € za prodajni čas in pridobijo 5 strank. Naročnina je 800 € na mesec, neposredni stroški (licence, podpora) 200 €. Nato uvedejo plačano nastavitev 1.800 € (stroški 600 €), boljši onboarding prepolovi odhode, priporočila pa ob istem strošku prinesejo še eno stranko več.

In a quarter they spend €6,000 on ads and events and €4,000 on sales time and win 5 customers. The subscription is €800/month, direct costs (licences, support) €200. Then they introduce a paid setup of €1,800 (cost €600), better onboarding halves churn, and referrals bring one extra customer at the same spend.

Arithmetic: CAC before 10,000 / 5 = €2,000, after 10,000 / 6 ≈ €1,667. LTGP before 600 × 10 = €6,000, after 1,200 + 600 × 20 = €13,200. Ratio after 13,200 / 1,667 ≈ 7.9. Because €1,800 of first-month gross profit exceeds the €1,667 CAC, acquisition pays back within 30 days. Before, the business was "healthy" on paper (3:1) but waited more than three months for its money on each customer.

Lokalna storitev · avtopralnicaLocal service · car detailing

Zakaj prihodek zavajaWhy revenue misleads

Detajling studio prodaja dve storitvi po 100 €. CAC iz lokalnih oglasov je 50 € na novo stranko.

A detailing studio sells two services at €100 each. CAC from local ads is €50 per new customer.

StoritevServicePrihodekRevenueNeposredni stroškiDirect costsBruto dobičekGross profitObiskov do povrnitve CACVisits to pay back CAC
Notranje čiščenjeInterior clean100 €30 €70 €1
Keramična zaščitaCeramic coating100 €75 €25 €2

Na računu sta storitvi enaki, v ekonomiki pa ne. Notranje čiščenje povrne CAC že ob prvem obisku (70 € ≥ 50 €), keramična zaščita šele ob drugem (2 × 25 € = 50 €). Studio zato oglašuje čiščenje kot vstopno storitev, zaščito pa ponudi kot nadgradnjo obstoječim strankam, kjer CAC ni več treba plačati, ali ji dvigne ceno.

On the invoice the two look identical; in the economics they aren't. Interior cleaning pays back CAC on the first visit (€70 ≥ €50), ceramic coating only on the second (2 × €25 = €50). So the studio advertises cleaning as the entry service and offers coating as an upsell to existing customers, where no CAC has to be paid again, or raises its price.

Svetovanje · AIConsulting · AI

Moč ceneThe power of price

PredBefore

Projekt avtomatizacije 10.000 €, stroški izvedbe 6.000 €, bruto dobiček 4.000 €. CAC 2.000 €. Razmerje na en projekt 2:1.

Automation project €10,000, delivery cost €6,000, gross profit €4,000. CAC €2,000. Ratio on one project 2:1.

PoAfter

Z jasnim izračunom prihranka in garancijo cena 12.000 €, stroški ostanejo 6.000 €, bruto dobiček 6.000 €. Ob istem CAC razmerje 3:1.

With a clear savings case and a guarantee the price is €12,000, costs stay €6,000, gross profit €6,000. At the same CAC the ratio is 3:1.

Cena se je dvignila za 20 %, bruto dobiček pa za 50 % (s 4.000 € na 6.000 €), ker gre ves dodatek neposredno v dobiček. Zato je cena praviloma najmočnejši in najhitrejši vzvod LTGP, ki ga podpre boljša ponudba.

Price went up 20%, gross profit by 50% (from €4,000 to €6,000), because the entire increase drops straight to profit. That's why price is usually the strongest and fastest LTGP lever, backed by a better offer.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • LTV na prihodkuLTV on revenue

    Življenjska vrednost iz prihodka napihne razmerje in skrije slabo maržo. Vedno računaj z bruto dobičkom.

    Lifetime value based on revenue inflates the ratio and hides poor margins. Always use gross profit.

  • Nepopoln CACIncomplete CAC

    Samo oglasi brez prodajnega časa, orodij in provizij dajo prenizek CAC in lažen občutek varnosti.

    Ads alone, without sales time, tools and commissions, give a CAC that's too low and a false sense of safety.

  • Dobro razmerje, počasna povrnitevGood ratio, slow payback

    Tudi 5:1 ti ne pomaga, če se denar vrne v enem letu in ti gotovina zmanjka v treh mesecih.

    Even 5:1 doesn't help if the money comes back in a year and you run out of cash in three months.

  • Optimistična življenjska dobaOptimistic lifetime

    Pri mladem podjetju je življenjska doba domneva. Računaj s krajšo dobo, dokler podatki ne pokažejo drugače.

    In a young business lifetime is a guess. Use a shorter one until the data says otherwise.

  • Povprečje, ki skriva segmenteAn average that hides segments

    Skupni CAC lahko skrije kanal, ki izgublja denar. Računaj ločeno po kanalu in tipu stranke.

    A blended CAC can hide a channel that loses money. Calculate separately by channel and customer type.

Delovni listWorksheet

Vzemi podatke zadnjih treh mesecev. Kjer številke nimaš, zapiši previdno oceno in označi, da je ocena.

Use data from the last three months. Where you lack a number, write a conservative estimate and mark it as such.

CAC: Koliko si skupaj porabil za pridobivanje strank (vključno s svojim časom) in koliko novih strank si dobil?

CAC: How much did you spend in total on acquiring customers (including your time) and how many new customers did you win?

Bruto dobiček: Kakšen je neposredni strošek izvedbe na stranko? Kakšna je tvoja bruto marža?

Gross profit: What is the direct delivery cost per customer? What is your gross margin?

LTGP: Kako dolgo stranka v povprečju ostane in koliko vsega kupi? Izračunaj LTGP in razmerje s CAC.

LTGP: How long does a customer stay on average and how much do they buy in total? Compute LTGP and the ratio to CAC.

Povrnitev: Koliko bruto dobička dobiš v prvih 30 dneh? Kaj bi moral spremeniti, da preseže CAC?

Payback: How much gross profit do you collect in the first 30 days? What would need to change for it to exceed CAC?

Vzvod: Kateri en vzvod (CAC, cena, stroški izvedbe, življenjska doba, plačilo vnaprej) bi najbolj izboljšal številke? Kaj narediš ta mesec?

Lever: Which single lever (CAC, price, delivery cost, lifetime, upfront payment) would improve the numbers most? What will you do this month?
