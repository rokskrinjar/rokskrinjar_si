Enačba vrednostiThe Value Equation

$100M Offers · Value Equation

Kako kupec v glavi oceni, ali je nekaj vredno svoje cene. Štirje vzvodi: dva povečuješ, dva zmanjšuješ.

How a buyer judges, in their head, whether something is worth its price. Four levers: two you push up, two you push down.

BistvoThe gist

Vrednost =Value =
Sanjski izidDream Outcome × Zaznana verjetnost uspehaPerceived Likelihood of Achievement
Časovni zamikTime Delay × Trud in žrtvovanjeEffort & Sacrifice

Ljudje ne kupujejo izdelka, kupujejo spremembo. Ponudba je vredna toliko, kolikor je ta sprememba velika in verjetna, deljeno s tem, koliko časa in muke stane, da do nje pridejo. Če je imenovalec blizu nič, postane vrednost ogromna.

People don't buy a product, they buy a change. An offer is worth as much as that change is big and believable, divided by how much time and pain it costs to get there. If the denominator approaches zero, value explodes.

Štirje vzvodiThe four levers

1. Sanjski izid1. Dream Outcome ↑

Rezultat, ki si ga kupec v resnici želi, izražen z njegovimi besedami in čustvi, ne s tehničnimi lastnostmi. Pogosto gre za status: kako ga bodo videli drugi. »Nadzorna plošča« ni sanjski izid; »na sestanku uprave imaš odgovor na vsako vprašanje v 10 sekundah« je.

The result the buyer actually wants, in their own words and emotions, not technical features. Often it's about status: how others will see them. "A dashboard" isn't a dream outcome; "you answer every board question in 10 seconds" is.

2. Zaznana verjetnost uspeha2. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement ↑

Koliko kupec verjame, da bo pri njem delovalo. Ključna beseda je »zaznana«: ni dovolj, da deluje, kupec mora to verjeti. Dvigujejo jo dokazi (primeri strank, številke), garancije, specifičnost (»za proizvodna podjetja s 50–200 zaposlenimi«) in jasno opisan proces.

How much the buyer believes it will work for them. The key word is "perceived": it isn't enough that it works, they have to believe it. Raised by proof (case studies, numbers), guarantees, specificity ("for manufacturers with 50–200 employees") and a clearly described process.

3. Časovni zamik3. Time Delay ↓

Čas od plačila do prvega občutenega rezultata, ne do končnega. Ljudje plačajo bistveno več za hitrost. Trik: končnega rezultata pogosto ne moreš pospešiti, lahko pa dosežeš hitro prvo zmago (»quick win«) v prvih dneh, ki dokaže, da je odločitev bila pravilna.

Time from payment to the first felt result, not the final one. People pay far more for speed. The trick: you often can't speed up the end result, but you can deliver a quick win in the first days that proves the decision was right.

4. Trud in žrtvovanje4. Effort & Sacrifice ↓

Vse, kar mora kupec narediti ali se čemu odpovedati: učenje, njegov čas, sprememba navad, pa tudi čustveni napor (strah pred napako, nerodnost). Zato »naredimo namesto vas« (done-for-you) stane več kot »naredite sami« (do-it-yourself), čeprav je znanje isto.

Everything the buyer has to do or give up: learning, their time, changing habits, plus emotional cost (fear of failing, awkwardness). That's why done-for-you costs more than do-it-yourself, even when the knowledge is identical.

Examples are made up for illustration.

B2B · analitikaB2B · analytics

Implementacija BI nadzorne ploščeBI dashboard implementation

PredBefore

»Postavimo vam Qlik nadzorne plošče. Projekt traja 3 mesece. Potrebujemo dostop do sistemov in vaše ljudi na delavnicah.«

"We'll build you Qlik dashboards. The project takes 3 months. We need system access and your people in workshops."

PoAfter

»V 10 dneh imate prvo poročilo o marži po kupcih, ki ga zdaj delate ročno v Excelu. Vaša ekipa porabi skupaj 4 ure. Če v 10 dneh ni v uporabi, ne plačate prve faze.«

"In 10 days you have your first margin-by-customer report, the one you now build by hand in Excel. Your team spends 4 hours total. If it isn't in use within 10 days, you don't pay for phase one."

Kaj se je spremenilo: sanjski izid je konkreten (poročilo, ki ga že poznajo), časovni zamik pade s 3 mesecev na 10 dni, trud s »vaših ljudi na delavnicah« na 4 ure, verjetnost pa dviguje pogojna garancija.

What changed: the dream outcome is concrete (a report they already know), time delay drops from 3 months to 10 days, effort from "your people in workshops" to 4 hours, and a conditional guarantee raises likelihood.

Storitev za posameznike · fitnesConsumer service · fitness

Osebni trenerPersonal trainer

PredBefore

»Mesečna članarina z dostopom do fitnesa in planom vadbe.«

"Monthly membership with gym access and a workout plan."

PoAfter

»Do poletja -6 kg. Nakupovalni seznam in recepti za ves teden, 3 vadbe po 30 minut, tedensko tehtanje s trenerjem. Prve spremembe na tehtnici v 14 dneh.«

"Down 6 kg by summer. Shopping list and recipes for the whole week, three 30-minute workouts, weekly weigh-in with the coach. First change on the scale within 14 days."

Recepti in seznam znižajo trud (ni več razmišljanja, kaj jesti), 30 minut zniža žrtvovanje časa, tedensko tehtanje dviguje verjetnost, prvih 14 dni pa skrajša zaznani zamik.

Recipes and the list cut effort (no more deciding what to eat), 30 minutes cuts time sacrifice, weekly weigh-ins raise likelihood, and the 14-day marker shortens perceived delay.

Pogoste napakeCommon mistakes

  • Napihovanje obljubeInflating the promise

    Večji sanjski izid brez dokazov zniža verjetnost. Obljuba, ki ji kupec ne verjame, ima nično vrednost.

    A bigger dream outcome without proof lowers likelihood. A promise the buyer doesn't believe is worth zero.

  • Prodaja procesa namesto rezultataSelling the process, not the result

    Število ur, modulov ali funkcij so stroški za kupca, ne vrednost. Več korakov pogosto pomeni več truda.

    Hours, modules or features are costs to the buyer, not value. More steps often means more effort.

  • Ignoriranje imenovalcaIgnoring the denominator

    Vsi tekmujejo na izidu. Hitrejši začetek in manj dela za stranko je pogosto cenejše za tebe in vrednejše za kupca.

    Everyone competes on outcome. A faster start and less work for the client is often cheaper for you and worth more to them.

  • Mešanje dejanske in zaznane verjetnostiConfusing actual and perceived likelihood

    Odlični rezultati, o katerih nihče ne ve, ne dvigujejo vrednosti ponudbe. Dokaze moraš pokazati.

    Great results nobody knows about don't raise your offer's value. Proof has to be shown.

Delovni listWorksheet

Vzemi svojo glavno ponudbo in odgovori pisno. Pri vsakem vzvodu oceni trenutno stanje 1–10.

Take your main offer and answer in writing. Score each lever 1–10 as it stands today.

Izid: Kako bi stranka z lastnimi besedami opisala, kaj želi? Kako jo bodo po tem videli drugi?

Outcome: How would the client describe what they want, in their own words? How will others see them afterwards?

Verjetnost: Kateri trije dokazi bi skeptiku najbolj pomagali verjeti? Katere imaš že danes?

Likelihood: Which three proofs would most help a sceptic believe? Which do you already have?

Zamik: Kaj je najhitrejša opazna zmaga, ki jo lahko stranka dobi v prvem tednu?

Delay: What is the fastest visible win the client can get in week one?

Trud: Naštej vse, kar mora stranka narediti. Kaj od tega lahko prevzameš ti ali odpraviš?

Effort: List everything the client has to do. Which of it can you take over or remove?
