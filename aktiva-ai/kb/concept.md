# THE CONCEPT: Hiško (what this site is)

- "Hiško" is a concept by Rok Skrinjar: a working AI assistant ("Hiško") for apartment owners and tenants in buildings run by Aktiva upravljanje d.o.o., plus the upravnik's side of it, a map of AI opportunities across Skupina Aktiva, and a page on responsible use. It is not affiliated with Skupina Aktiva. The building "Lipa", the apartment, the owner and all amounts are fictional demo data; work orders sent from the chat only go to a demo inbox in the visitor's own browser.
- Pages:
  - index.html (Asistent): the fictional owner's cost statement for September and October 2026, the reserve fund balance, and the chat with Hiško. Buttons ask Hiško to explain the statement, report a fault, or ask in another language.
  - upravnik.html (Pogled upravnika): the work-order inbox (seed orders plus the ones sent from the chat), simulated KPIs, topics and languages, and how quality and business effect are measured.
  - priloznosti.html (Priložnosti, "Zakaj ta koncept"): why the owners' assistant is a good first step, the opportunity map of AI use cases across security, cleaning and building management (value, feasibility, AI Act risk), and a 90-day pilot.
  - odgovorno.html (Odgovorna uporaba): the AI Act timeline and duties, GDPR, and how Hiško itself is built to be safe (transparency, no personal data, human decisions, evaluation, logging).
- Why the owners' assistant first (public facts): Aktiva upravljanje says it manages 350+ buildings and has 8,000+ clients; the billing office (obračun) answers the phone two hours a day (Mon, Tue, Fri 8:00–10:00; Wed, Thu 13:00–15:00), upravniki until 16:00. Most owner questions repeat (cost statement, heating, reserve fund, meetings, faults) and have clear answers in the law and the contract, so they can be answered at any hour, in the owner's language, with the source. Faults turn into structured work orders that the upravnik only confirms.
- What Hiško does not do: decide, promise on the company's behalf, give individual legal advice, handle debts or disputes, or process personal data beyond a first name and the apartment. It hands these to a person.
- How a real rollout would work: start with one region or 20–30 buildings for 90 days; connect read-only to the owner portal (iiPortal) data for the owner's own statement; send work orders into the existing system; measure against a baseline (calls, e-mails, handling time) collected in the first two weeks; weekly evaluation on a set of ~150 real questions answered by upravniki; extend only if the targets are met.
