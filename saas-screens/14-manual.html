Events / Webhooks

Receive webhooks

Relay sends an HTTP POST to your endpoint whenever something happens — a message is delivered, a user replies, a number opts out. This guide gets you from zero to verified events in about ten minutes.

Create an endpoint

  1. Open Dashboard → Webhooks and click Add endpoint.
  2. Paste a public HTTPS URL. For local development, use relay listen to tunnel events.
  3. Choose the event types you need. You can change these later.
ℹ
Each endpoint gets its own signing secret, starting with whsec_. Keep it out of your repo.

Handle the event

Respond with any 2xx within 10 seconds. Do slow work in a background job — Relay treats a timeout as a failure and retries.


  
Verify the signature
Every request carries a Relay-Signature header. Always verify it before trusting the payload.

  
tintegerrequired
Unix timestamp of when the event was signed. Reject anything older than 5 minutes.
v1stringrequired
HMAC-SHA256 of {t}.{raw_body} using your endpoint secret, hex-encoded.

  
⚠
Verify against the raw request body. Parsing JSON first changes whitespace and breaks the signature.

  
Retries
Failed deliveries retry with exponential backoff for 3 days: after 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, then every 6 h. After that the endpoint is disabled and we email your team.

  
Idempotency
Retries reuse the same event.id. Store processed IDs and skip duplicates.
