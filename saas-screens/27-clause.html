7. Limitation of Liability

7.1Except for breaches of Section 9 (Confidentiality), each party’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed three (3) times the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
7.2Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages, including loss of data, howsoever arising.

9. Confidentiality

9.1Each party shall protect the other’s Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own, and in no event less than reasonable care.

11. Term and Termination

11.2Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time upon ten (10) ninety (90) days’ written notice, with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.
11.3Upon termination, Provider shall make Customer Data available for export for thirty (30) days, after which it shall be deleted.

14. Governing Law

14.1This Agreement is governed by the laws of Norway, and the courts of Oslo shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
