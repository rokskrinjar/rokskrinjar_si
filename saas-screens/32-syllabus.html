Quick check · Lesson 3.2
Revenue “jumped” from €980k to €1.02M. The chart below makes it look like it tripled. Why?
y-axis: €970k → €1.03M
Exactly. A truncated axis turns a 4% rise into a skyscraper. When the bars’ heights matter, start at zero — or show the change as a number instead.
