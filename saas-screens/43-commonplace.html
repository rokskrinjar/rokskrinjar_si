How we ship

OwnerJoon ParkTagsprocesshandbookVerified✓ by Platform team · 12 Sep
＋⋮⋮

We ship small, ship often, and make it boring to roll back. Most changes reach production within a day of being merged.

＋⋮⋮
💡If a change can’t be rolled back in under five minutes, it needs a written plan and a second reviewer.
＋⋮⋮

Before you merge

Tests pass and coverage didn’t drop
Feature flag wraps anything user-facing
Migration is backwards-compatible with the previous release
＋⋮⋮

Release trains

＋⋮⋮

Trains leave at 10:00 and 15:00 CET. Missed one? Catch the next — no hotfix exceptions unless there’s an active incident.
