Poslovni primerBusiness case

Isti les, nov kupec.Same wood, a new buyer.

Solis Timber prodaja 87 % lesa v izvoz, na debelo. Domači mizar pa isti skobljan les kupi v trgovini DIY za približno štirikratno ceno, v dvometrskih kosih in brez nasveta. Ta koncept pokaže, kako bi žaga ta prepad zapolnila z vsebino, AI svetovalcem in načrtovalnikom, ki naročilo spremeni v delovni nalog.Solis Timber exports 87 % of its timber, wholesale. A home woodworker buys the same planed wood in a DIY store at about four times the price, in two-metre pieces and without advice. This concept shows how the sawmill could close that gap with content, an AI adviser and a planner that turns an order into a work order.

Kako deluje lijakHow the funnel works

  1. VsebinaContent

    Vodič, 13 projektov, »Les za začetnike«. Odgovarja na vprašanja, ki jih ljudje iščejo: koliko lesa za visoko gredo, kaj je 2x4.The guide, 13 projects, Wood 101. It answers what people search for: how much wood for a raised bed, what a 2x4 is.

  2. AI svetovalecAI adviser

    Skobi odgovarja iz baze znanja žage in iz Pinterestove ideje naredi kosovnik iz dejanskih izdelkov in prerezov.Skobi answers from the mill's knowledge base and turns a Pinterest idea into a cut list of real products and sections.

  3. NačrtovalnikPlanner

    Razporedi kose na deske 3 in 4 m, pokaže odpadek, ceno, težo in možne načine dostave.Nests the parts onto 3 and 4 m boards and shows offcuts, price, weight and the possible delivery modes.

  4. Delovni nalogWork order

    Žaga ne dobi e-pošte za razvozlavanje, ampak razrez za vsako desko. Prodaja ne izgublja časa z malimi naročili.The mill gets a cutting plan for every board, not an email to decode. Sales spends no time on small orders.

Pilot v 90 dnehA 90-day pilot

  1. Prevzem v Straži, trije izdelki (skobljan, radialen, peleti), 13 predlog projektov. Razrez za DIY enkrat na teden, v paketu.Pickup in Straža, three products (planed, quarter-sawn, pellets), 13 project templates. DIY cutting once a week, in a batch.
  2. Partner: lesarski laboratorij v Centru Rog (Ljubljana) za tečaje in prevzemno mesto.Partner: the Center Rog woodworking lab (Ljubljana) for courses and a pickup point.
  3. Paket do 2 m po vsej Sloveniji, ko se postopek ustali.Parcels up to 2 m nationwide once the process settles.
  4. BSH in KVH za pergole in nadstreške, s partnerskim statikom.Glulam and KVH for pergolas and carports, with a partner structural engineer.
  5. Merimo: povpraševanja, delež oddanih, povprečni m³, čas na naročilo, vprašanja za Skobija.Measure: inquiries, conversion, average m³, time per order, what people ask Skobi.

Kaj je tu narejeno z AIWhat AI did here

  • Raziskava: podatki o podjetju, cenah na trgu in uporabi lesa iz javnih virov, z viri za vsako številko.Research: company data, market prices and wood uses from public sources, with a source for every figure.
  • Vsebina: vodič in navodila za 13 projektov v slovenščini in angleščini.Content: the guide and instructions for 13 projects in Slovenian and English.
  • Svetovalec: Skobi odgovarja samo iz baze znanja (~30.000 besed) in vrne kosovnik v strukturirani obliki.Adviser: Skobi answers only from the knowledge base (~30,000 words) and returns a structured cut list.
  • Delovni nalog: iz kosovnika nastane razrez za proizvodnjo, brez ročnega prepisovanja.Work order: the cut list becomes a production cutting plan, with no retyping.

Enak pristop deluje tudi za B2B: ponudbe iz povpraševanj, izvozna dokumentacija, tehnični listi v več jezikih.The same approach works for B2B: quotes from inquiries, export documents, data sheets in several languages.
