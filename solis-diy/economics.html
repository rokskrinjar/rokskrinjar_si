Ekonomika in donosnostEconomics and profitability

Koliko je vreden trg in koliko lahko zasluži žaga.How big the market is, and what the mill can earn.

Ocena od zgoraj navzdol: trg DIY v vsaki državi, delež lesa, del, ki ga pokrijejo izdelki Solisa, regije v dosegu tovornjaka iz Straže in kupci, ki les naročijo prek spleta. Nato petletni izkaz poslovnega izida kanala DIY v treh scenarijih.A top-down estimate: the DIY market in each country, the timber share, the part Solis' products cover, the regions within truck reach of Straža and the buyers who order timber online. Then a five-year P&L for the DIY channel in three scenarios.

–trg DIY in vrta v Evropi na letoEuropean DIY and garden market per year
–porabijo za les domači mojstri v Sloveniji in sosednjih državahspent on timber by DIYers in Slovenia and its neighbours
–dosegljivi trg (SAM): naši izdelki, regije v dosegu, nakup prek spletaserviceable market (SAM): our products, reachable regions, online buyers
