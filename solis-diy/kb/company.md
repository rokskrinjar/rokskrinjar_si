# Solis Timber d.o.o.: company facts

Public facts for the Skobi adviser. Sources: solistimber.com (read 2026-10-28), AJPES via companywall.si, Sloexport, FSC certificate database, Delo (12 Jul 2018), EY Family Business Book Slovenia 2022, Si21 (Apr 2021), 24ur (Mar 2023), Kmečki glas (Feb 2025).

## Who they are
- Full name: Solis Timber, predelava lesa, d.o.o. (until 27 Jul 2021: Solis Straža d.o.o.). Address: Na žago 6, 8351 Straža, Slovenia (south-east Slovenia, 9 km from Novo mesto, 8 km from the Kočevski Rog forests). Railway station Straža nearby.
- An innovative family sawmill and wood-processing company. Main activity: sawmilling of softwoods (spruce, fir, pine; larch logs are also bought). Products: boards, beams, planks, battens, mostly for construction, glued panels, packaging, window and door lamellas, and planed timber. By-products: offcuts, chips, sawdust and pellets.
- Founded in 1990 by Jože Zoran as "Žagarstvo Jože Zoran s.p.", started in his garage. Management today: founder and director Jože Zoran, co-founder and procurist Betka Zoran, director Kaja Zoran Ledinek (MSc wood technology, Biel; earlier sales at Binderholz; led sales from 2017).
- Timeline (company site): 1999 first employee and sawing hall; 2001 first computer-controlled band saw; 2003 office building; 2007 Solis Straža d.o.o. founded; 2009 new production plant and product range; 2010 finished-goods warehouse; 2015 production moved to the former Novoles site in Straža; 2017 Kaja joined, staff grew from 7 to 20 in two years; 2018 investment in digitalisation, new sawing line, debarking with scanning and a boiler house (about €5 M); 2021 renamed Solis Timber; Dec 2022 bought 3.5 ha in the industrial zone for expansion; 2024 automatic planing line; 2025 new computer-controlled EWD band saw.
- EU/Slovenian co-financing "Strong&Solid" (decision 16 Feb 2023, up to €3,435,960.90): extension of the plant, a planing-and-sorting line, a finger-jointing line and dust extraction, to introduce KVH (finger-jointed structural timber).
- Size: more than 60,000 m³ of softwood logs a year (2025), which puts it in the top size class of Slovenian sawmills (only about 2 % of ~510 Slovenian sawmills saw more than 60,000 m³). About 30–40 employees. Revenue €17.1 M in 2025 (AJPES), about 87 % from exports.
- Markets: more than 30 countries in Europe, the Middle and Far East, Africa and America, mostly without middlemen (e.g. Saudi Arabia, UAE, Japan, Austria, Sweden, Türkiye, India). The company site says "33 years on the market, 234 satisfied customers, 33 countries".
- Today Solis sells B2B/wholesale. It has supplied planed timber to DIY stores abroad, but has no shop, price list or online sales for private customers in Slovenia. (This is the gap the Solis DIY concept explores.)

## Quality and certificates
- Logs from Slovenia, Austria, Italy, Croatia, Czechia and South-East Europe; sawn on German EWD technology. Log purchasing is all year round, logs measured electronically with Microtec 3D scanning; diameters 10–120 cm; standard log length 4 m.
- Kiln drying in controlled chambers, "perfect and even drying"; attention to grain orientation and appearance.
- FSC Chain of Custody FSC-C124693 (BV-COC-124693, valid to 30 Mar 2030): all products FSC 100 %.
- CE: visually strength-graded structural timber to EN 14081-1 (factory production control certificate).
- FITO certificate for ISPM-15 (heat treatment of packaging wood, HT).
- "Production 100 % without waste"; the company says it stores more than 60,000 t of CO₂ a year.
- Motto on the site: "Strong. Solid. Solis."

## Products (as published by Solis)
- Dry sawn timber (suh žagan les): spruce/fir/pine; centre or side cut; boxed-heart-free possible; custom dimensions; kiln dried KD 8–20 %; lengths 3–4 m; graded to DIN. Uses: glulam, CLT and KVH production, construction, packaging, furniture.
- Planed timber (skobljan les): spruce/fir; sharp or rounded edges; profiled timber possible; centre or side cut; custom dimensions; KD 8–20 %; 3–4 m. Uses: BSH/CLT/KVH production, DIY products, garden furniture, floor and wall cladding.
- Radial timber (radialen les): spruce/fir; side cut + radial; with or without knots; no pith; widths 50–150 mm; KD 8–20 %; 3–4 m. Uses: windows and doors, furniture, garden furniture, saunas, wall cladding, parquet. Shrinks and warps less, lasts longer.
- Fresh sawn timber (svež žagan les): spruce/fir/pine; optional bio-protective dip; IPPC (HT) and CE possible; 3–4 m. Uses: wooden packaging, support in concrete construction, simple structures.
- Glulam BSH: spruce, visible and industrial quality, GL24, widths 80–240 mm, heights 120 mm+, lengths up to 13.5 m, KD 10–12 %. Uses: houses, halls, carports, lintels, beams and posts.
- Biomass: sawdust (fresh or dry; pellets, animal bedding), chips G30 without bark, offcuts 7–40 cm (firewood), ground bark (fuel, fertiliser), A1 spruce/fir pellets in 15 kg bags.
- Coming: KVH finger-jointed structural timber.
