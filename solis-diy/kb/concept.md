# The Solis DIY concept and the market

## What this site is
- "Solis DIY" is a concept by Rok Skrinjar: a guide, an AI adviser (Skobelj) and an order planner that shows how Solis Timber could sell its timber directly to hobby woodworkers in Slovenia. It is not affiliated with Solis Timber. No orders are sent; the inquiry button only shows what the customer and the sawmill would receive.
- Pages: the guide (/solis-diy/index.html: projects, Wood 101, by-products, the company), the planner (planner.html: 13 project templates or an own cut list, nesting into 3 m / 4 m stock, price, delivery, inquiry preview) and the business case (business.html).
- The planner: choose a project, set sizes, choose the wood variant, or load a cut list from the chat. It groups parts by product and section, nests them into stock lengths (3 or 4 m, about 20 mm overmeasure, 4 mm kerf), counts cuts (0.30 € each), computes m³, weight, an illustrative price and the DIY-store comparison. Delivery: pickup in Straža (free), parcel for pieces up to 2 m (5.99–29.99 € by weight), van up to 4 m and 1 t (25 € up to 20 km, then 0.70 €/km), truck for long beams (60 € up to 20 km, then 1.20 €/km). Orders under 100 € have an 8 € small-order fee.

## Market facts (October 2026)
- Slovenian sawmills' median price for kiln-dried joinery boards: 415 €/m³ net (Slovenian Forestry Institute, Feb 2026). Construction timber C24: 285–300 €/m³ net.
- Slovenian DIY stores sell planed spruce boards at about 1,800–2,300 €/m³ incl. VAT (OBI 19 mm boards 2.89–6.29 €/m in 2 m lengths; Slovenijales planed battens cut to length 2,090–2,330 €/m³). KVH 100×100 / 120×120 at OBI about 1,460–1,500 €/m³, special order with about 3 weeks' delivery. Glulam beams 1,060–1,830 €/m³. Pellets 6.89–7.49 € per 15 kg bag (national average 456 €/t, Aug 2026).
- So hobbyists pay about 3.5–4 times the sawmill price for dry planed timber, mostly in short (2 m), thin sections. No Slovenian sawmill was found that sells to hobbyists online with a cut-to-size configurator; Slovenijales (a merchant) cuts planed battens to 300–2,000 mm for pickup only.
- Abroad: sawmills and merchants such as Holz-Shop.com (AT), baustoffshop.de, Zuschnittprofi (DE), Holz Gar (Bavarian sawmill with web shop) and Scottish Wood (UK sawmill) sell direct with configurators or cut lists.
- Center Rog in Ljubljana runs a public woodworking lab (membership 15 €, monthly 60 €); a natural partner for courses and pickups.

## Concept prices (illustrative, incl. 22 % VAT, ex works Straža)
- Planed spruce/fir 1,190 €/m³ (larch to order ×1.45). Quarter-sawn 1,450 €/m³ (knot-free +20 %). Dry sawn 640 €/m³. Fresh sawn 390 €/m³ (+25 €/m³ HT). Glulam BSH GL24h 1,050 €/m³. KVH (coming) 890 €/m³.
- These are about 35–45 % below DIY-store prices and still 2–3 times the wholesale value. They are estimates for the concept, not Solis Timber prices.
- Examples from the planner with default sizes: raised bed 200×100×60 cm in planed spruce about 130 € of timber; workbench 160×60 cm about 200 €; picnic table 180 cm about 230 €; pergola 3×3 m about 560 €.
