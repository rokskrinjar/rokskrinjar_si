Solis DIY je koncept Roka Skrinjarja in ni povezan s Solis Timber d.o.o. Cene so ilustrativne ocene z DDV, prevzem v Straži; naročila se ne pošiljajo. Prerezi pri večjih gradnjah so tipični za oceno količin, ne statični izračun.Solis DIY is a concept by Rok Skrinjar, not affiliated with Solis Timber d.o.o. Prices are illustrative estimates incl. VAT, ex works Straža; no orders are sent. Sections for larger builds are typical sizes for estimating, not a structural calculation.
