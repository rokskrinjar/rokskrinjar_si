Razpisni radar · AI za podjetjeFunding radar · AI for the company

Vsak ponedeljek: razpisi, ki ustrezajo žagi.Every Monday: the calls that fit the mill.

Agent vsak teden pregleda razpise domačih in evropskih virov, prebere pogoje in jih primerja s profilom podjetja. Ko nekdo razpis izbere, nastane projekt prijave: Skobi iz baze znanja podjetja napiše osnutke poglavij, kar manjka, pa vpraša.Every week an agent scans Slovenian and EU funding calls, reads the conditions and compares them with the company profile. When someone picks a call, it becomes an application project: Skobi drafts the sections from the company's knowledge base and asks for whatever is missing.

    Kako bi to delovalo na žagiHow it would work at the mill

    1. PregledScan

      Vsak ponedeljek zjutraj agent prebere nove objave na straneh skladov, ministrstev in EU. Vsakemu razpisu prebere pogoje in upravičene stroške.Every Monday morning the agent reads new postings from funds, ministries and the EU, and reads each call's conditions and eligible costs.

    2. UjemanjeMatch

      Razpis primerja s profilom: velikost podjetja, regija, dejavnost, načrtovane naložbe. Pove, zakaj ustreza in kaj bi lahko financiral.It compares the call with the profile: company size, region, activity, planned investments. It says why it fits and what it could fund.

    3. OdločitevDecision

      Povzetek pride po e-pošti ali v Teams. Vodstvo izbere razpis; agent nikoli ne odloča in nikoli ne odda prijave sam.The summary arrives by email or in Teams. Management picks the call; the agent never decides and never submits.

    4. PrijavaApplication

      Skobi napiše osnutke iz baze znanja (podatki podjetja, pretekle prijave, letna poročila, ponudbe), odpre vprašanja za manjkajoče in vodi seznam pogojev, prilog in rokov.Skobi drafts from the knowledge base (company data, past applications, annual reports, quotes), opens questions for what is missing and tracks conditions, attachments and deadlines.
