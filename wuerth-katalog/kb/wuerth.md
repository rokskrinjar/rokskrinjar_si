# WÜRTH: public facts (checked 2026-11-02)

## Würth in Slovenia
- Legal name Wurth, trgovina z montažno opremo, d.o.o. (Wurth d.o.o.), registered 7 September 1992, part of the Würth Group since 1993, owned by Würth International AG.
- Head office and logistics centre: Cesta ob Bregu 6, 1291 Škofljica. They moved there in 2026; the base was in Trzin until 2025.
- More than 100 employees, more than 13,000 customers, about 150,000 products.
- 2025 revenue about EUR 17.2 million and about 101 employees (AJPES data through CompanyWall; not checked at AJPES itself).
- Seven stores: Maribor, Šenčur, Novo mesto, Koper, Celje, Ljubljana, Slovenj Gradec.
- Sales channels: field sales advisers specialised by trade and industry, stores, e-shop eshop.wuerth.si with an app, call centre. Business only with legal entities; prices appear in the e-shop after login or registration.
- E-shop sectors: Auto, Cargo, Gradbeništvo, Inštalacije. Groups: technical chemistry, material processing, fastening and anchoring, automotive.
- ORSY systems: ORSY trolleys (empty or equipped), ORSYmobil van fit-out with a free configurator, ORSY MAT automatic replenishment.
- Toll-free number 080 13 00, Monday to Friday 8:00–15:00. The field sales team is not counted on any public page.

## Würth Group
- 2025: sales about EUR 20.7 billion, operating result EUR 970 million, 86,415 employees, about 44,000 of them in sales, more than 400 companies in 80 countries, over 2,800 shops, about 4.5 million customers, e-business EUR 5.2 billion or 25.2%.

## Evidence on AI in distribution
- No independent controlled study of AI search or churn models at a distributor of this type was found. Published figures are mostly modelled, vendor-reported or correlations.
- Deloitte models 75–100 basis points of EBIT from generative AI in sales, quoting and post-sales, and reports an unnamed distributor cutting complicated product search tasks by more than half (early result).
- DSG survey of 233 distribution executives: 93% call AI a priority, 16% have deployed it across functions; 81% of those with strong data are confident in returns, against 18% with poor data.
- Grainger uses RAG search over 2.5 million products for about 4,000 sales staff. Rexel reports a 4% productivity gain in 1H26, attributed to improvement programmes and AI.

## Law, short version (a summary, not legal advice)
- AI Act after Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: Annex III high-risk rules apply from 2 December 2027. Article 50 transparency has applied since 2 August 2026: a chatbot must say it is AI at the first interaction at the latest. A tradesman is a natural person, so a B2B audience is no exemption, as far as the sources read show.
- Annex III point 4(b): allocating tasks based on individual behaviour or traits, or monitoring and evaluating workers' performance, is high-risk. Commission guidelines on this are still a draft. Rep rankings feeding pay or reviews sit on the wrong side; customer-level suggestions a rep may ignore sit on the safer side (the author's reading).
- Article 5(1)(f): inferring emotions at work has been prohibited since 2 February 2025, with medical and safety exceptions. This matters for call analysis.
- GDPR does not cover legal persons, but sole traders (s.p.) and named contact persons are personal data.
